Ouranxo is a region north of the Cadole ocean. It's geography is predominantly temperate, slowly getting colder in the north as it touches a polar circle. 
![climate.png](/wastelife/worldproject/climate.png)
It is believed that the primary inhabitants of Ouranxo are a type of Muz, a species of bear-person, taurus folk, badger guys, and a few fish people.
