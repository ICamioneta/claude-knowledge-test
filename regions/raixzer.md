Raixzer is a temperate continent inhabited by unique species of person yet properly defined, but it is known that [Caran](/wastelife/species-of-person/ran/caran) make up a large portion of the population. 

Raixzer isn't large enough to have defined sub-regions, but there is distinction to be made between the south, north-east, and north-west. 

The east is a rainforest with similar geography to Vietnam. The west is a 1000m highland, and the south is a flatter temperate region. 

# Name Origin

The origins of this name comes from the Ghzish word ‘Silver Comet’. Daklyn named this continent that due to the lore. There are several silver ruins on the vast highlands of Raixzer, and it is trailed by a number of islands spanning from its own east coast to the western mouth of Ayansh. These are called the Silver Islands, this time because of the abundance of silver on those islands and the importance of that element to the people who live there. ‘Silver Comet’ is ‘rajanhochyzer’ in Ghzish, and it was heavily ouranxized.
