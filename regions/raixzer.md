Raixzer is a temperate continent inhabited by unique species of person yet properly defined, but it is known that [Caran](/wastelife/species-of-person/ran/caran) make up a large portion of the population. 

![Raixzer.png](/wastelife/region-maps/cropped/raixzer.png)

The image is a screen grab from the globe, without the distortion that makes it look way longer than it actually is. 

Raixzer isn't large enough to have defined sub-regions, but there is distinction to be made between the south, north-east, and north-west. 

The east is a rainforest with similar geography to Vietnam. The west is a 1000m highland, and the south is a flatter temperate region.
