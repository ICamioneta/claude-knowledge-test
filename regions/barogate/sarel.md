Sarel is a transitionary land between the green civilisation of [Shildo](/wastelife/regions/barogate/shildo) and the uninhabitable wilderness of [Kyenel](/wastelife/regions/barogate/kyenel). It's deemed ungovernable. Although historically fitting in with warlord-like feudal and dynastic structures, with the advent of modern Empire the region has been incredibly resilient to authority. 

# Geography and Climate

Sarel is primarily a dry savannah that transitions into arid desert towards the north. Its elevation steadily rises from sea level to Kyenel's 1200m-tall plateau. 

Despite its low population, Sarel has decent development with sparse corn and soybean farming. Besides this, the land is used to grow goats.
