Elvas Eldu, or Continent 6 until recently as of writing, is a proposed subequatorial continent extending down to the south pole but coming up to the equator near [Annwvyn](wastelife/regions/Annwvyn.md). It is present on the World map.

![World Map.png](/wastelife/worldproject/world-maps/world-map.png)
Lying mostly beneath the tropic is dominated by desert and tundra, with only a few habitable regions near the coast depending on where the mountains are. No doubt, it's a lot less habitable than other continents

The inhabitants of Elvas Eldu are primarily the [[Ryr]], but on the tundra itself there likely lives a species of giant woolly person, getting along, making cheese, and what have you. Gonna call them the "Elvasans" or maybe just the "Elvas" to throw people off.
