# Lynlanes
![[Lynlanes tiny.png]]
Lynlanes, or Llynllanes, is a large archipelago consisting of 5 major islands and 11 minor islands. It is a place of high conflict between the factions of [Baron-Gazi](/wastelife/factions/raidal/baron-gazi) and [Kingdom Naxdyme](/wastelife/factions/raidal/kingdom-naxdyme). Baron-Gazi owns the 3 westmost major islands alongside the 7 minor islands attached, while Red Storm owns the 2 eastmost major islands alongside the 3 minor islands north of those. In between the two regions of control sits islands of high contention. The Baron-Gazi island of Blava, or the Naxdyme island of Texo, is in disputed control. Seen below are the names of every island that is part of the archipelago.

![Llynllanes Labeled.png](/wastelife/region-maps/llynllanes/llynllanes-labeled.png)
The bay created by the three Baron-Gazi islands is called Lynlanes, and the other islands inheret that name as a proximal group. Geographically, Acadym, Ceseison, and Suribls are flat or hilly, while Tindenfara and Tijil are mountainous. In the hand-drawn map, there are three shades of green. The bluemost one represents low, flat, and fertile lands. The greenest colour represents a hilly environment. The darker green represents a mountainous environment dominated by forests.

## Demography
![Llynllanes Settlements.png](/wastelife/region-maps/llynllanes/llynllanes-settlements.png)
There are three types of people present on the islands. These are [Domuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/domuz), [Kaemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/kaemuz), and [Caran](/wastelife/species-of-person/ran/caran). Of course, there are all types of people present on the islands, but those three are the peoples present with populations above 50 peoples. The population of Domuz is a recent one, with the [[Domuz Republic#Colonies|Lale Darie colonisation]] of Raidal in the early 3420s. The local government of Baron-Gazi (literally new Gazi) was established to govern the settled Domuz populace. Here, on this island, in Baron Xillall, is where the government was established. The city remains as the capital city of Baron-Gazi.

All of the islands controlled by Baron-Gazi are dominated by a Domuz populace, save Baron Synset which is dominated by Kaemuz settled from Synset in accordance with 3432’s Ursan Resettlement in Domuz Raidal treaty made between the provincial government of Synset and the Lale Darie government. The islands controlled by Kingdom Naxdyme are dominated exclusively by Caran peoples. The contested island, Blava/Texo, contains a single settlement that permanently houses a military populace of Domuz.

The largest island is the most populated, while the second most populated is the Naxdyme island of Suribls. Then, Ceseison, followed by Tindenfara, and Tijil. Most minor islands have populations below 500 peoples, save the island of Old which has a town.

## Political
![Llynllanes Geopolitical.png](/wastelife/region-maps/llynllanes/llynllanes-geopolitical.png)
Above is a political map of Llynllanes, with the island of Blava/Texo There are two factions present on the islands, both are island conglomerates. There is Baron-Gazi, often known as Ron-Gaz, and Kingdom Naxdyme, often known as Red Storm. The Llynllanes Islands are a point of conflict between these two nations, with the island of Blava/Texo being claimed by both islands. Currently, the island is occupied by the Baron-Gazi military and contains a few Domuz settlers, alongside a suppressed Caran population.
