Neble is the westmost region of [Anula](/wastelife/regions/anula) and homeland of the [Nebdos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nebdos). 

![Neble.jpg](/wastelife/region-maps/neble/neble.jpg)

The region is dominated by two main biomes: a large and expansive temperate forest and the managed farmlands and forestry. 
# Geography

Neble is a temperate region that receives a good amount of rain and sunlight, playing into its very green visage. It has four major geographical regions: the coast, Codia, the clearing, and Plaen.

## The Coast

The coast is a largely cleared region with a few highly managed forestries. It includes all territories held by the [Western Neble Imperia](/wastelife/factions/anula/western-neble-imperia) (WNI) and [Carehope](/wastelife/factions/anula/carehope). The region's borders are defined by the everlasting conflict between the WNI and [Codia](/wastelife/factions/anula/codia). 

## Codia

Codia is the name of the forest that takes up at least a third of Neble's area. It is also the name of the largest Ews community on Barogate and Anula. It's a complete forestry where the only clearings are those of the communities of the Ysgews. The trees that make it up are a mix of ancient oak-like trees and younger, shorter lifespan trees. 

## The Clearing

The clearing, also called Eastern Neble, is an area cleared from Codia by the [Diacut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/diacut) peoples, and presently inhabited by the Governance of [Brwrd](/wastelife/factions/anula/brwrd) and the Nebcholes half of [Nebcholes-Remen](/wastelife/factions/anula/nebcholes-remen). It's similar to Codia in climate, but the forest has been demolished and replaced with industrial farms.

## Plaen

Plaen, or The Plains, is an area of predominantly rolling hills with some wild forests, but it's primarily managed farmland.

# Name Origin

The name 'Neble' is the actual name of the region in Adal that Neble in Wastelife is based on. Daklyn probably used the direct name because he spent most of his time on Adal here. It is more traditionally called 'Adal Neb' instead of Neble, so there is some disconnect between the real name and the Wastelife name.
