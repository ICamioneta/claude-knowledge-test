Thagryene is the south-eastern region of Anula. it is homeland to the [Nudos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nudos), [Dancut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/dancut), and [Akiiogan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/akiiogan) peoples.

# Name Origin

Thagryene is where Adal Nu would be on Adal. Daklyn on his travels was likely amazed by the co-habitation of the Kithral Nu and Akiio-gan people, and how they both got along. This aspect was adapted into Wastelife, with the Nudos and the Akiiogan were directly used in the world with a similar relationship. The area name comes from 'Thugeddo Mysag Rynenae' which translates to 'Peace among People'. This was shortened and ouranxised to 'Thagryene'.
