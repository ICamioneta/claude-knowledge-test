Belkis is a region in central [Anula](/wastelife/regions/anula). It borders [Vezoen](/wastelife/regions/anula/vezoen) to the north across the calaerula river, [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene) to the south, and [Calaeddodd](/wastelife/regions/anula/calaeddodd) to the east. It is a dry steppe in the west and an arid savanna in the east. It is primarily inhabited by the [Lescut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/lescut) the [Cafcut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/cafcut), and the [Nudos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nudos). It is geographically quite flat. 

Politically, it is divided between the dual monarchy o[Nebcholes-Remen](/wastelife/factions/anula/nebcholes-remen) and the County of Eynon in the [Union of Thagryene Countries](/wastelife/factions/anula/uthec).
