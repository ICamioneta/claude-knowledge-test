Vezoen is the north-eastern region of Anula. It is homeland to the [Nidos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nidos). It consists of two major sub-regions: Roughwalk and Seliene. 

# Name Origin

The land Vezoen is based on, Adal Ni, actually has an area like Roughwalk. This is where Daklyn spent his time in Adal Ni. They would call their area 'Vezpop', the proper name for Roughwalk, but Daklyn would use this as the name for the region as a whole and use his native Ouranx language's translation for the subregion.
