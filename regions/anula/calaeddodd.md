Calaeddod is the mountain range that cuts through the centre of Anula. It is homeland to the [Cut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut) peoples, namely the [Chōcut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut). It is also home to the fierce [llofyn](wastelife/creatures/misc/llofyn.md), colossal dragon-like butterflies. 

# Name Origin

The name of Calaeddodd comes from the historical outlook on the mountains, which on Adal were seen as a defence people both sides of the mountain could use to defend against attackers. 'Calae ddodd' literally translates to 'border defend'.
