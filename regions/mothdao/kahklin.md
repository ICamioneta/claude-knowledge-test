Kahklin is a temperate region with some inland steppe. It is primarily inhabited by [Kahrçin](/wastelife/species-of-person/cin/kahrcin) living in large family-based towns and cities. The inner parts of the region are a dry steppe. While still populated, the steppe is the beginning of [Mothnial](/wastelife/regions/mothnial) and has many dangerous creatures. Hunting packs and lone hunters take pride in hunting these creatures. 

The region's pre-truth religion still has a strong presence on Kahklin culture. It worshipped [Fflam](/wastelife/eternals/fflam) and [Ammehdytha](/wastelife/eternals/ammehdytha) as total opposites while still considering them both morally good. Post truth, the cultural elements of the religion persisted but worship of the gods turned into reverence.
