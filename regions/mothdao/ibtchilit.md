Ibtchilit is a rainforest of very tall trees. It's primarily inhabited by the Eximipri, who live in large sophisticated town-sized web colonies. Its north is a dry woodland forest known as a wild land with no sophistication. 

To its east sits the mountains of Iggijoq and [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd). Touching its north-eastern tip is [Varan Del](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/varan-del). To its south is [Pzthrisdon](wastelife/regions/mothdao/pthrisdon.md).
