Varan Del is a region of [Mothdao](/wastelife/regions/mothdao). Its northern half is a comfortable and hilly temperate region. In the south, the mediterranean climate leads comfortably warm beaches and massive redwood forests. It's inhabited by pri.

It is also supposed to have a large island encapsulating a bay and contributing to the Mediterranean climate of the south.

To its south sits the impassable [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd). To its east, the mountains of Zuned and [Kahklin](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/kahklin). To its west, northmost [Ibtchilit](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/ibtchilit).
