Aerlada is a land in [Mothdao](wastelife/regions/mothdao) with many large and noteworthy rivers. 

The land is littered with [Bracai](/wastelife/creatures/misc/bracai) who make its culture noteworthy. It is otherwise relatively sparsely populated. 

[Jetty of Lihle Isle](/wastelife/characters/creatures/jetty-of-lihle-isle) lives in Aerlada around the mouths of its rivers, and acts as a sort of elder to all communities that live there. 

It is primarily inhabited by Kaemuz that migrated here and out-populated the native Pri during the early and middle medieval era. 

# Name Origin

Aerlada comes from  “Paerulado” which translates to 'River Land'. The name comes from the fact that the land is just covered in rivers, head to toe. Other candidates for the name include “Paerul”, “Ruldo”, “Parldo”, “Parlo”, “Arela”, “Aerlo”, “Aerlad”.
