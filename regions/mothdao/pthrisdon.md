Pzthrisdon is a very mountainous region in [Mothdao](/wastelife/regions/mothdao) highly populated by the native [[Pri]] peoples. They are stoic and bellicose, building immense stone-and-silk castles, donning steel and gambeson armour, and shooting each other with crossbows. 

Pthrizdon is built around the Pthrisjoq, the mountains that dominate its centre. The coastal side of the mountains, west and south, is the primary lands of Pthrisdon. Hilly, dominated by valleys that are protected from the frequent monsoons. Inner Pthrisdon describes a vast dry steppe north and east of the mountains. 

Also included in Pthrizdon is the south-most Icsuxjoq in which lies the [Volcanic](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic) communities who fuel much of Pthrisdon's metallurgy industry. 

To its north sits the Einohrac mountains and [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd). To the east, [Dendrene](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/dendrene). North-west is [Ibtchilit](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/ibtchilit).
