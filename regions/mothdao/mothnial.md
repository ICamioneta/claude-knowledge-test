Mothnial is the larger desert region of Mothdao. It is a megaregion that consists of:
- [Haerul](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/haerul)
- [Asenhal](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/asenhal)
- [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd)

# Name Origin
The name comes from the word “Big Desert”, or “Mothan Nihal”. That simple.
