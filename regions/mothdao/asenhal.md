This region of [Mothnial](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/mothnial) is primarily a shrub-land and savanna. It is generally inhospitable.

It's primarily inhabited by nomadic [Çin](cin.md).

It harbours very hostile fauna including the notorious [Fruchews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/fruchews). 

To the west are the Kassakorac mountains and [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd). The desert topology in southern Asenhal is dominated by the sheer desert of Asenhal-tol, Etoktol, and Tukohtol, as well as the Kogonrac, Momarac, and Onosrac mountains. To the south is [Haerul](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/haerul), and to the north is [Kahklin](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/kahklin). 

# Name Origin

It comes from 'Adosenihal' which translates to 'The Great Desert'. Another alternate name could be 'Donha'.
