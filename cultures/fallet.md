Fallet is a language family originating in [Duela](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/duela) and then carried across [Barogate](barogate.md) by the [Rutefall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/rutefall). In a time long gone, Fallet was present across the entirety of the continent. However, in modernity it barely survives. Its greatest impact is lexical additions to nearly every continental language. Nowadays, the language is primarily spoken by lowborn Duela communities as well as more organised Edlyue and Barthe.
