Kiyvan is a culture present in [Vania](vania.md), in southern [Ereso](ereso.md). It is in the [Llofil Shildoan](wastelife/cultures/llofil-shildoan/index.md) group, and is generally highly isolated from the other Eresonian cultures. 

It's well understood when split further between localities. 

Kiu is the Kiyvan of the marshes.

Estateer culture is a very modern, capital-focused culture spreading from south-western Vania. Labour culture is the more traditional Vanian culture, being the prevalent culture during Vania's councilism days. 

Also present on Vania are Shildoan cultural regions.
