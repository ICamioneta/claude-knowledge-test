Llofil [Shildoan](shildoan.md) is a cultural family present across [Ereso](ereso.md). It's modern members follow:

- [[Llofil]]
- [Kiyvan](kiyvan.md)
- [[Sapyat]]
- [[Reipo]]
- [[Ursan]]
- [[Vevran]]
- [[Bistcran]]

 Llofil is technically a hybrid, but its modern version is significantly closer to [Fellan](fellan.md) cultures. The culture of Clan Bistcran is a hybrid with the [Laurien](laurien.md)-family cultures of southern Kyeron. Ursan is a local hybridisation of migrated [Aerlada](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/aerlada) culture and the previous more untouched Ursan culture. The [Kiyvan](kiyvan.md) culture also incorporates parts of a Raixzer migration specifically in the however it is not considered a hybrid.
