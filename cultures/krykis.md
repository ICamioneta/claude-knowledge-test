Krykis is a culture family present in [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao). It is prehistoric; originating from the [Phax](phax.md) people present in Kusao and [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar). Prior to the [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/index) migration to Cronar, a sub-family Alludwathium Krykis was popular in the region. It now is preserved in the semi-hybrid culture of modern Ataken.

Krykis sub-cultures are largely based on which valley of Kusao they are in. The cultures are fairly homogenous, sharing a local diversity. There are also only so many valley populations, small enough to group here.

- Dorkosfail of Kosfail, Benyg Twynir, Illtud
- Doraf of Funaf, Heveth, Dodi, Koraridd, Selleiaf
- Dorflanyd of Fynfil, Fynei, Fynhoi, Fynehw, Argyd
- Dorhoi of Fynhoi, Argyd, Daercenyd, Koravaen
- Doreai of Daerai
- Dorcaryx of Ryban, Caryx, Bamtho
- Doryr or Callyr and the Valley of Life

The prefix "dor" probably just means "people of" or some sh.
