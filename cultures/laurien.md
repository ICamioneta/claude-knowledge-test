Laurien is a cultural family present in [Kyeron](kyeron.md). Although almost entirely [Ximuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/ximuz)-dominated in modernity, its roots come from prehistoric [Laurafall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/laurafall) communities in the area.

I plan for the Kyeric cultures to be incredibly diverse outside of certain cultures centralised by traversable features such as the Kiasv, Llesndijti, and Abdigszi rivers. I am yet to actually name these, so I've losted them as "Southern Kyeric" of which there are 3 (including Bistcran), and "Northern Kyeric" of which there are likely 6. The Central Kyeric cultures are supposed to be very specifically wildly varied, perhaps with dozens of recognisable unique members. However, all of these members share that they are part of this chaotic diversity.
