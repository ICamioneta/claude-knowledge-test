Londa is a cultural group present in eastern [[Senolado]]. It is part of the [Fygh Shildoan](fygh-shildoan.md) group, and arguably has the closest language to this original root. 

It is tied to the Fygh river. It is somewhat influenced by [[Ataken]] cultures following the Empire of Kan-Koronar's occupation of the area throughout medieval history. Eastern areas have large trade cultures tied to the Obsidian Road, giving them certain elements of [Nyrmish](nyrmish.md) culture.
