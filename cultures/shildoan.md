Shildoan is a massive culture and language family with two vaguely connected branches. 

It is somewhat based in the prehistoric presence of Ews in Shildo. However, it is primarily Muz-driven, with Ews cultural aspects showing their clear importance in both the practices and language. 

The two branches follow:

- [Fygh Shildoan](fygh-shildoan.md)
- [Llofil Shildoan](wastelife/cultures/llofil-shildoan/index.md)

Culturally, these two are almost entirely distinct. They share more similarities from modern exchange than they do from their historical emergence among Ews. Phonetically, they are entirely unique. The most interesting similarity is their similar grammatical structure, which shares similarities to the Ews non-verbal language.
