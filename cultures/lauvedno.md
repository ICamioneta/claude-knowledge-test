The Lauvedno culture group is a large culture present in southern [[Senolado]]. It is part of the [Fygh Shildoan](fygh-shildoan.md) family. 

It's tied to the lower Niez river, the cities of Rejedmana and Kovedlajo. It also has a hybrid culture [[Vadeia]] with Plaen Neble. 

Lauvedno culture is known for its strong sense of collectivism and shared purpose.
