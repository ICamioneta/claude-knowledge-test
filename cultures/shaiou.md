Shaiou is a family of cultures primarily present across [Barogate](barogate.md)'s wastes. 

It's origins are in the prehistoric [Mad](mad.md) peoples and their language. It was greatly transformed by the inclusion of [Difmuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/difmuz) in the culture.

Shaiou family cultures are highly diverged from their origins. Spreading across the Wastes, they adopted parts of the pre-existing cultures into their own. It geographically originates in Dithas, however is now significant across all of the Wastes.

Notable groups of Shaiou languages follow:

- Dithas Shaiou
- Vuga
- Duela Shaiou
- Aredvic Shaiou
