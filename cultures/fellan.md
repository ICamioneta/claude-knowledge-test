The Fellan cultural group is perhaps the most untouched [Muz](muz.md)-based cultural group. It geographically originated in [Terlaye](terlaye.md). While Terlaye did have some prehistoric [Ews](ews.md) communities, the Fellan language and culture evolved separately from them. Instead, it depended on the local [Bracai](/wastelife/creatures/misc/bracai) of Rullan and Naters' River for its grammatical and lexical roots. Fellan likely tied to the Bracai rather than the Ews because the Bracai language is verbal, whereas the Ews language is non-verbal and therefore struggled to exert influence when other easier options were available. 

Fellan is a relatively small group, however it has lexically left significant marks on [Shildoan](shildoan.md) languages.

# Culture
![Ralean Map Cultures.jpeg](/wastelife/region-maps/ralean-and-terlaye/ralean-map-cultures.jpeg)

There are six cultures present in Terlaye. There’s the Llofilot culture. There’s the southern culture of Abas which arises in the small part of Terlaye where there are no actual rivers, at the end of Taper. Theren there are the ultimately similar cultures of Gordan, Maeves, and Naotec. Each of these inhabit almost identical land. It’s important to note that the culture Naotec and the neighbouring [Ralean](ralean.md) culture of Rodxan have a hibrid culture on their border, in the mountains and hills that sit between them.

Then there’s the culture marked as Shildoa that surrounds Nater’s End. Many people see Nater’s End not as a Terlayan city, but a city of the larger and less accepted [Shildo](shildo.md) region that consists of the regions of Terlaye, Ereso, and Ralean, often including the lands held by Clan Bistcran. The main reality of this region is the super hybrid culture present in Nater’s End, where aspects of every culture in the region can be seen, except Vadeiea for resons that are aparent in Ralean’s culture explanations.
