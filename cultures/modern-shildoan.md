Not to be confused with [Shildoan](shildoan.md), which is a language family rather than a specific culture. 

Modern Shildoan is understood as the great cultural blending of all Shildoan cultures, therefore it would belong to all three groups of [Fygh Shildoan](fygh-shildoan.md), [Llofil Shildoan](wastelife/cultures/llofil-shildoan/index.md), and [Fellan](fellan.md). It is primarily present in Naternegorna, however it is also spread sparsely across the bay's area.
