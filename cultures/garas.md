Garas is a language and cultural family originating in the prehistoric [Ens](ens.md) communities coming from [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/the-heabe) and [Gordolmonre](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/gordolmonre). As a culture, it prevails primarily in nomadic Heabe cultures that are organisationally subordinate to the [Aredvic](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/aredvic.md) peoples. Gordolmonre Garas culture is significantly varied from Garas, but is still considered part of the family. Many Garas cultural aspects have also influenced northern Shildoan cultures such as the [[Gordan]] Terlayan and those of Ralean's Sefare Eysenre. The language family mirrors the influence of the culture.
