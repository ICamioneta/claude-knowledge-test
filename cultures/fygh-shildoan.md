Fygh [Shildoan](shildoan.md) is a cultural family present across [Ralean](ralean.md). It's modern members follow:

- [Rodxan](rodxan.md)
- [Lauvedno](lauvedno.md)
- [Londa](londa.md)
- [[Vadeiea]]
- [[Meison]]
- [[Ithun]]
- [[Naoxan]]
- [[Sefars]]
- [[Eysen]]

Notably, Sefars and Eysen are arguably [Garas](garas.md)-Fygh hybrids, and Naoxan is a hybrid culture with the [Fellan](fellan.md) cultural family.
