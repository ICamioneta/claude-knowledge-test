The Rodxan culture group is a large culture present in western [[Senolado]]. It is part of the [Fygh Shildoan](fygh-shildoan.md) family. 

It is tied to the upper Niez river and the city of Canita and Rodxan Heire. It is noted for a strong sense of cultural identity, however after Canita was made the capital of [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md), the city itself has greatly diversified.
