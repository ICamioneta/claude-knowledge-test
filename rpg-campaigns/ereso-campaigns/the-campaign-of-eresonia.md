Central Ereso is a "level 0" area. It is densely populated, extremely developed, and the government is social and friendly. For this reason, it's the starting area of WTTTRPG. 
To take this information and conclude that there is no danger or nothing to do in Ereso is harshly incorrect. The councilist government of Eresonia is too far ahead for its time, creating contradictions that, in 3488, are finally coming to boil. 

Eresonia is a truly democratic state well before the Industrial Revolution. It somehow manages to unify Worker's councils, Artisan guilds, Peasantry, and Private Companies all together at once. This is an unholy unison, and since its formation, the majority worker-and-peasant run councils have consistently come into conflict with the Artisans and the capitalists who own private companies.

It's this political conflict that drives Eresonia's main campaign, the rise of the small business owner and the establishment of Eresonian Capitalist political system. This conflict was sped up in Vania, where the devastation of the necromancer created the economic conditions for the bolstering of the small business owner in Western Vania, which then allowed for the rapid and coordinated attack against the working councils of Vania and the eventual establishment of the 3456 constitution, which showed the businesses of Eresonia how miniscule changes to the system of democracy could violently upheave the status quo in their favour, if they were ever to gain a majority control over the economy.

Eresonia is much more stable than Vania, but still the distribution of the socio-economic classes is uneven. Central Ereso, the dominant Sapyat areas, are majority worker-owned areas. Reipan areas, in comparison, are majority business owned, with Reipoten famously being the location of large manor houses in which many business owners live, far from the locations of their actual workplaces. Vevran areas are majority guild-owned, and although the exact structure of the guild differs from a private company, the slow transformation of a guild into a company is clearly occurring. And then there's majority Ursan areas, which are predominantly independent, with independent farmers, a mix of business and worker controlled resource extraction, and independent artisans permeating the towns and cities.

So the ultimate campaign of Ereso revolves around this conflict, and the rise of certain independence movements driven by the ability for a certain economic class to take control of government in their local area, but without the support of the worker-majority national council these economic classes will seek to make their dominated region independent from Ereso. 

The only class that really seeks to benefit from control of the government is the working councils and the business owners, and since in Reipo-majority areas the business owners are usually owners of businesses in worker-majority areas, Vevran guilds are based in Vevran-majority areas, and largely operate globally. 

# Karsas Hockey and the Independence of Vevran

The figure is **Karsas Hockey**, a higher-up in the Mia Mesten guild which is one of Ereso's most powerful resource extraction and construction guilds, based in a town of the same name. Although Vevranian independence is a somewhat popular movement among the Vevran wealthy. The most moderate of the independence movement only wants independence for Vevran itself, the land between the rivers that define the Daraerula region. Karsas is a bit of a fanatic in Vevranain independence, seeking to restore "old borders" and bring the Iavan coast into this new Vevranian nation.

This irridentist movement lead by Karsas Hockey hinges on somewhat false historical information that the Iavan coast was once a Vevran cultural area. The truth in this belief is that the Iavan Coast was once the location of a "lost" Eresonian culture that was more close in tradition to the Vevran than the other closest Sapyat, but this culture was somewhat lost as the area became culturally mixed during the Second Eresonain Empire. The original culture was lost as its unique traditions were overcame and regularised into the Vevran peoples starting to inhabit the area.

Despite this irridentism being largely unpopular in the Vevran independence movement, Karsas is an incredibly influential and active figure in the movement, and has gained a huge following. He states, in opposition to those who are against his irridentism, that the inclusion of extra-territorial gains are important to the independence movement to push their movement beyond its current passive nature and to make their claims known, even if the eventual independent state doesn't include this territory.

The Iavan coast is also the location of Eresonia's capital and much of Eresonia's developed region, so inclusion of this influential are is very controversial. 

## Other influential Vevranian Irridentists

Vevranian irridentism, being the claim that Iavan Coast is a Vevran cultural homeland, is quite popular in other higher-ups. Karsas has gained the position of leader of these irridentists, and those who follow him also wish for him to be the proactive leader of the independence movement as a whole, which he more or less is at this point.

## Military Operation

Karsas and Iketa have prepared amongst themselves for a potential armed conflict between Eresonia and the Vevran Independence Movement. This conflict is especially likely to occur with the ideas of Vevran irridentism that Karsas leads. 

Iketa claims he can raise 4000 trained troops from across Reipoten and Vevrania loyal to an anti-councilist cause. He also claims that an additional 10000 recruits could be made in Vevran itself. With his insider knowledge, he knows that the maximum Eresonian force is around 20000 trained troops with an unlikely possibility of commoner conscript if the military is strained.

Karsas hopes to use other means to weaken any Eresonian military response, as well as gain immediate allies for the anti-Eresonian war. He believes the OPB and other covert action groups are skilled enough to trap large parts of the Eresonian military behind the Anlyrer strait. He also hopes that the military of Vania would be able to assist in part with the victory of Vevranian independence, although he also notices that the Vanian military is an inherently defensive structure and Vania's own insecurity might pose a problem for Vanian anti-Eresonian sentiment.
