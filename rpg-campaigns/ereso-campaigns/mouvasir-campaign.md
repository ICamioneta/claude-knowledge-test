This racist has been bestowed the name Malaxihas

# Nationalism, Culturalism, and the Subjugation of Peoples
So what is Culturalism?

Culturalism, or Cultural Supremacy, is the idea that cultures are fundamentally different, and that a culture defines ones self on an integral level. The more radical ideas that proponent culturalism is that some cultures are better than others, culturalism asserts that every culture has a particular set of qualities and values, and that certain cultures values are superior.

Nationalism is identical to the real world. It is a belief system that prioritises the interests, culture, and identity of a nation as a state. It emphasises the unity and sovereignty of a nation state. Nationalism can lead to a sense of pride and loyalty from its members, but also fosters hostility and exclusivity to people from other nations and works in hand with racism (or cultural supremacy) to exclude and other people to further the interests of those who actually control the nation.

Nationalism is a tool of the ruling classes of a nation to subjugate the overlorded classes of the nation. By fostering a belief that a nation is sovereign, supreme, and righteous, they can bring the belief from the people that the nation and its organisations are inherently good.

Anyway, that’s enough about that. Time to talk about what this means for Malaxihas, or just Mal for short.

# Malaxihas
Malaxihas is a Sapyat Kaemuz, a fervorous culturalist, and the first racist in Wastelife.

## Malaxihas' beliefs
Mal, as a Sapyat, is a full proponent of cultural supremacy. This idea is foreign to a councilist Ereso, but before the revolution it was the primary driving force for the subjugation of peoples. The first and especially the second Eresonian Empires believed that wealth should be funnelled to the centre of a country in part because the rulers of these places were from that centre and benefited from the movement of wealth there. Councilism however emphasises against this, actively distributing wealth from the richer parts of the country towards the poorer, albeit at a slow pace.

Mal is probably about 50 years old. He doesn’t remember the times before Councilism, but he has been in some pretty weird forums and picked up on the idea of culturalism. His life must have been going downhill, and while in reality this is probably because he has a bad character. But how do I make a bad character that doesn’t live in a bad society?

I think the only real way is to have a character who fights for their interests, and these interests are selfish and antisocial. Perhaps I bring out these character flaws not as ones that go ignored by society, but as ones that society actively tries to remedy but his own free will he rejects society’s help and falls back on the ideas of culturalism to further his own interests. Perhaps these are interests to be liked? I should also make him incompetent to a certain degree.

Malaxihas believes that Sapyat culture is supreme and this is evident because of how wealthy it is compared to other cultures. He believes that the Sapyats should rightfully rule over Eresonia and be the only people delegated to councils across the culture, as he believes that Sapyat folk are better at managing the land than people of other cultures. People of other cultures are worse, and for each he has his own flaws.

For the Vevrans, he shares some respect as their industrious and productive nature give them a strong “sapyat-like nature”. Kiyvan culture too shares this industrious nature, how “they carved their own path away from Ereso” he admires, but he sees that “they need to be rid of the muddy parts of their culture, abandon the unproductive side of the Vania and Sapyat will take care of it.” His real prejudice is with the other two cultures. He would say “Ursan culture is filthy, the rainforest does them no good and they’ve no idea how to develop it. They need to abandon their foreign traditions and lean in on their good Eresonian fundamentals.” And Reipan he would call “Uptight and arrogant, the Reipan culture is full of money but for all the wrong reasons, they leech from the hard-working Sapyat individuals”.

So, with these clear culturalist ideas, how did he then invent racism?

He sees that modern Sapyat culture only gives to other cultures, and instead of this being a contradiction to his world view, he scapegoats these “issues” onto actual goats (the Ens) and the other non-Kaemuz peoples that live in Sapyat majority lands. He then extends these prejudices to the people of other species who are actually part of the Sapyat culture. His rationale would be like this:

“What is a Sapyat? The people of the culture? And who are the people? Are they the goats who invade from the Heabe? Are they the birds who attack from Anula? Are they the Muz from other countries that land here and have children here and have the audacity to call themselves ‘Sapyat’ as they sit on our councils and give away our money to the deprived (derogative) areas of this country? I say no, it is the Sapyat peoples who must drive Ereso to victory as it has before! It is the Kaemuz who make this Sapyat country with our minds that are fit to be the architects of society!”

## Malaxihas' words
Malaxihas is a racist. He doesn’t like people who aren’t Kaemuz. He attributes the restoration of Mouvasir from its second Eresonian empire as a success of the Kaemuz Sapyat ruling the place. Currently though he wants to blame everything wrong with Mouvasir on the non-Kaemuz people in council.

Specifically the delegates from the University, Backstreet, Newroad, and Mavnen Town. Although all of these areas are majority Kaemuz and realistically have no more non-kaemuz than any other part of the city, they have chosen non-kaemuz delegates to the council. Specifically, Colsens, Danmuz, Korongan, and Lemuz. This is probably what Mal has to say about these people, under his racist and culturalist presumptions:

“Why is an Ens in delegate? Who in their minds would look at the Ens who are known for their physically smaller brains and decide that they should be in council? The Ens, who are known for their stupidity and need to be ruled by a Muz peoples in council is sure to sow disaster.”

“The Terlayans are a conquered people, their supremacy disproven by the superior Ralean nation state, and why are we letting a person of this failed region influence our city’s decision. This is the second downfall of Mouvasir.”

“Korongan are no different from their Akiiogan brothers, shy and sly. And the people of Mavnen Town has one as delegate! This is preposterous, they are sure to steal from our great city and cause its rust. The days of Kan-Koronar are long over, they struggle to rule their own nation, so why should they rule ours?”

“The Lemuz are a fine people with a great empire, is what I would have said eight years ago before the complete failure of their administration and the corruption of it by inferior cultures and peoples invaded the diverse south! If the Lemuz wanted a unified nation, they need only promote their greatest cultures – Eysen and Rodxan.”
## Malaxihas' Actions
Where exactly do these words spread? I’ll tell you where, in the most pretentious layers of society that believe that their places have been worsened by Councilism.

People that used to live in the Ancient City before it was made a historical preserve are targeted and told it was to further the interest of Danmuz and Nidos scholars who seek to use Kaemuz wonders for their own personal gain.

People that used to be in the private Fencing Academies that were recently forced to open to the public. They believe that being forced to accept people based on their skill and not also their finances is a ridiculous proposal and only waters the competitivity of Slic Academy and Mouvaside FA. Mal tells these people to look at how many non-kaemuz are now in these institutions.

 People from University, Backstreet, and Mavnen Town who disagree with the delegations. Although their reasons for disagreeing with the delegation initially has nothing to do with species and everything to do with policy or competency, Mal will convince them that this is in fact a species thing.

And everyone else who is in a position where they are susceptible to being told that they’re problems aren’t for them to fix with help, but they are someone else’s fault and removing that other will remove the problem.

And by sowing discord in these people, he would start rallying them and attacking layers of society unprepared for the new and deadly ideas of racism.

These rallies might target bigotry at the non-kaemuz elements in the FAs, in the town streets, or eventually targeting the university and trying to move people back into the ancient city.

Whispers on the streets about a guy who hurled a bunch of new and unique insults at a bird who walked into town will turn to a rally of 20 guys protesting at government that Slic Academy and Mouvaside FA should remain paid and private places.
## Karsas and Malaxihas
Malaxihas' story of racism is inherently tied to the current political situation in Ereso, and the independence of the Vevran and Karsas' Vevran irridentism present as the [The Campaign of Eresonia](/wastelife/tabletop/rpg-campaigns/ereso-campaigns/the-campaign-of-eresonia). 

Mal's actions are in the express interests of Karsas and Vevran Irridentism. Mal is likely to receive a visit from the OPB, including him in the plan to hamper Eresonia's military come a conflict between an independent Vevrania and Eresonia. Mal's active division of the Eresonian people is exactly the type of thing Karsas needs to destabilise Eresonia.

Mal might even agree with the Vevran independence movement, and the general capitalisation of Ereso. The idea is that Mal would support these cultures being institutionally separate so that the sapyats can be in their own great nation, and he might support the capitalist saying that the Sapayts need to return to their roots of economic development and make their land and people productive once more. He might even speculate about a future independent Sapyatania under a similar sort of governance.
