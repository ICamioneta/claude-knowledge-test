The Redel campaign is a difficult topic. It is such an eventful city:
- The current administrational capital of Eresonia, and the seat of the National Eresonain Council
- The centre of Eresonian technological innovation, housing Ereso's first steam engine and the secret invention of the loom
- The target of a Vevran irridentist campaign, claiming Redel as a lost city of Vevran culture
- A huge ship trade centre, acting as the main port in international trade with the small state Llofilot.
- The town is literally named after the Spire. Re Del, spire town. Its proximity is of massive significance.
So somehow bringing all of these elements together is a bit of a challenge.

immediately, some of these elements are related. Like it being the target of Vevran irridentism, the growing Vevran independence movement, and it being the seat of the national Eresonian council. 

Here's some story beats:

## The Inventor's Guild
The Inventor's Guild of Redel is an organisation that links back to an old second-empire artisan guild, but it has since turned into a sort of mutual association of well-funded inventors who try to move technology forwards. These are random experimentalists fighting for funding provided by private corporations and worker council surplus who want to improve their methods of production and show off Eresonian wonder. Although strictly based in Redel, members of the Inventor's guild are Eresonia-wide, which was a recent Councilist intervention in attempts to decentralise Ereso's wealth.

Here are some things members of the Inventor's Guild are working on:

### Steam Engine
First and foremost: the idea that fire's energy can be transferred into mechanical movement, and the efficient capture of it. Steam Engines are already in Wastelife, more accurately the [Atmospheric Engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcomen_atmospheric_engine) being used to pump water out of mines across Shildo and Lale Darie. But a more modern and improved Steam Engine is being worked on by Shames Detrel. At the moment, he's just working on an atmospheric engine in use by a colliery near Redel.

**Story Hook:** Shames is being threatened by the OPB to deliver his prototypes to Lesten to bolster the Vevran economy.
### Longitude Watch
The spring-based clock was actually invented by the Domuz a century prior in 3370 with the idea that it would aid colonial sea navigation. The watch was also a Domuz perfection on this, and the Eresonian co-invention of a Longitude watch is being done with the express understanding that it's in competition with leading naval technologists in Lale Darie.

**Story Hook:** The party is asked by an inventor to procure a Domuz longitude watch so that the inventor can reverse-engineer it.
### Automaton
Automaton are moving mechanical things. In Wastelife, they are generally inspired by Golems and the philosophy among some inventors that animals and creatures are complex machines. 
Automaton invention in Ereso is very small, with no rich fannies to sell needlessly expensive toys to, but in collaboration with the Hidol Research Institute in the Redel University, enchantments are being developed in conjunction with mechanical bodies to create autonomous machines, similar to the Bone Binding enchantment which was made common in the 2680s when the Dictatorship of the Dead was being trivialised.
**Story Hook:** an enchanted automaton has gone rogue causing chaos in Redel.
### Personal Flight
Flight in Wastelife is actually a surprisingly common invention, with enchantments existing to make objects "float" dating back to Nao Techia in the 1200s. Controlled flight has been another subject, with certain mages managing to perform sequential claps that produce bursts of air that propel them in the opposite direction. Such an action is more a "jump" and becomes less effective the further away the caster is from a solid object.

The ability to move the physical world using the conscious interaction with the Hidol has always been a possibility, and with the scientific knowledge of why exactly air jumping works, and why certain objects might float when enchanted in certain ways, the concept of "Aether Flight" emerged in 3270 in Ralean. The idea was that a person sufficiently in-touch with their Torfol and its ability to manipulate objects within it could use it to push the air around them downwards - and them upwards. This concept was proven later that year, and quickly became a staple of a powerful mage's toolkit.
Aether Flight was popularised in Ereso by the Hero of Vania, who famously used this tactic of flight, as well as some other experimental uses of the Hidol, in his fight against the necromancer.
But such a method of flight is completely inaccessible to the layman, but inventors everywhere have devised ways to mechanically move air downwards working on the same principle as Aether Flight.

Perhaps a more successful method of flight that's being devised is the idea of a floating flight. The same enchantments that have been making objects float since 1200 are being proposed to lift baskets, then a wizard can use enchantments to ferry passengers above the land.

In Neble, the idea of rising hot air had been devised by its inventors to create the first hot air baloons, and Eresonian inventors are challenging each other to replicate it without the phenomena of the Hidol.

**Story Hook:** An inventor is close to a breakthrough in hot air lifting, but is worried that a rival inventor who is sure that their method of personal flight will succeed will sabotage their efforts.
### Water Power
Seeking ways to make production easier for companies and workers, the natural flow of water is already a popular natural phenomenon to draw from. Terlaye is generally the centre for water power innovation, but Ereso has its fair share of high-capacity rivers and hills. The more experimental elements of water power at this time are pumping it to higher elevations, hydro-mechanical devices, and better water-based transportation for goods.
### Explosive Powder
Explosive Powder is a very new thing in Barogate and Anula. Generally seen as a technology from Mothdao, a particular blend of powders that when set alight produces an explosion. Explosive powder is generally untouched in Wastelife because any use it does have is completely overshadowed by the explosive alchemical properties of Torfol and Torfol Overlap. But, with a modern focus on the layman's ability to experience wonders, explosive powder is somewhat being revisited by Eresonian inventors

A crucial part of the invention of Explosive Powder in Ereso revolves around the foundation of Chemistry and its separation in science from the alchemy that surrounds the Torfol and its reactions. While Alchemy is strictly interested in the properties of living thing's Torfol, Chemistry is concerned with the properties of non-living things.

Explosive powder is a chemist's way of saying that the phenomena of the Torfol are replicable using other means. Although currently limited, the introduction of explosive powder as a mix of brimstone, charcoal, and saltpeter. These elements are slowly entering the scientific consciousness of Wastelife.

**Story Hook:** Barrels of experimental explosive powder have gone missing, and the party must track the barrels down. 
### Firearms
Inspired by the recent invention of Springburst Pneumaflux Confluence Catalyst (SPCC) in Brwrd and the discovery of Archetech Repeater and its copy by Lale Darie with the Republica Firearm, Eresonian inventors are also looking for ways to shoot things real good. Effective ranged combat has been dominated for centuries by enchanted arrows and clapped projectiles, so the creation of a similar weapon that anyone with minimal training could use is taking the world by storm.

Eresonain variants are in a very early invention phase. The claps dominant in Ereso produce spikes, lightning, and other detriment and illusion-based effects. Although designs similar to the SPCC are effective in casting and directing lightning-based reaction effects, spike effects prove especially difficult to direct and propel. When the kinks are sorted out, simple spellcasting is likely to be made available for anyone with such a device, and even the more complex Eresonian spells will eventually be made accessible by all.

**Story Hook:** an Eresonain inventor has ordered the delivery of an Archetech Repeater, but its been stolen by some unknown party (likely OPB or other Vevran Independence peeps). The same inventor might point out a frequent loss for SPCCs and his prototypes facing constant stealage, hinting towards a black market for these weapons.
### Printing Press
Printing technology and Ereso have always gone hand in hand. From the invention of the paper mill that defined the rise of the Second Eresonian Empire in 3091 to the invention of the printing that lead the height of that empire in 3200 - and the downfall of the Kingdom of the Domuz in 3189. The printing revolution saw the downfall of the Second Eresonian Empire in 3253, and now in 3488 the process of printing again is being challenged by Eresonain inventors.

Past all the past glory of Eresonian printing, the idea of a circular printer is being one of the main speculative technologies.

**Story Hook:** Irridentist and anti-Eresonaian materials are being produced and distributed, and none of the above-ground print houses in Redel are confessing to printing them.
### Diving
Eresonain diving technological invention is leaning heavily on inventions performed in Vezoen, but copying foreign technology and bringing it home is always a staple of inventors. Speculative submersibles and diving bells are generally seen as a novelty for exploration purposes. Keeping dry underwater is generally only a problem for non-Domuz.

**Story Hook:** A prototype submersible is stuck in Lake Daini, and a desperate inventor wants the party to recover it and the person inside safely so that he can avoid consequences of his catastrophically failed test.
### Sail Rigging
Much of the world has come to understand that there's no beating the Domuz when it comes to naval technologies. But, the Domuz try to keep their secrets to ensure their continued domination of the waters. So, inventors worldwide find themselves stumped trying to recreate the effectiveness of Domuz sails. 
Eresonia's navy is ranked third in the world, only behind the Domuz republic and Thagryene, and somewhat sharing an aptitude with fourth-place Edris Nation whose ability to keep up with Ereso is only because of their own technological divisions.

**Story Hook:** The party is hired as a mercenary group aboard a test rigging on open waters, but the rigging goes awry and sends them on an unknown path.

## Campaign Arcs
### A Looming Revolution
Rumour has it that somewhere in Redel a revolutionary new technology is being developed that weaves cloth mechanically. This mechanical loom could allegedly save hours of work each day, but the inventor company is keeping it to themselves. Players might look into a dodgy Redel-based private clothing company that reports to have workers weaving cloth, but these so-called workers have never been seen and no-one knows anyone who works for the company.

# The Main Campaign of Redel
At some point during their stay in Redel, perhaps during one of the other missions, they might hear from someone that Eresonia's in a spot of trouble. This person is on the National Eresonian Council, and has started to notice the rise of the Vevran Independence movement, and the rise of Karsas Hockey. This should be enough to tie the players in with the main campaign of Ereso, and its relation to Redel.

Another person might at some point ask a player character, or another NPC, what they think about the Vevran cultural claim on Redel is about. There are a few responses others might give if pressed.

There is the general idea that Redel and the Iavan Coast were once Vevran-majority areas, but since the Second Empire they have become heavily mixed between all the cultures of Ereso. 

There is also the more well informed idea that Redel and the Iavan Coast once hosted a culture of its own that was closer in tradition to the Vevran, but due to mixing under the Second Empire it has since been lost or integrated with normal Vevran culture.

Opinions on this will probably vary, but generally the people of Redel wouldn't really think that division along cultural lines is good, and those who are especially politically educated might point out the 3456 constitution and certain opinions surrounding that.

A likely thing to occur is the player party's introduction to the OPB during a side-quest with an inventor. The OPB are involved in the following story hooks: printing press, Firearms, Explosive Powder, Steam Engine. During this, the OPB will be certain in their goals and try not to kill anyone (meaning Miabi probably isn't present or is otherwise being held back). 

Then I think the third and final element would be the inclusion of the Spire in the campaign. Perhaps there's a simple underground cult dedicated to its worship and the eventual "activation" of the spire, although the cult definitely doesn't know what that's about, and if the players ever figure it out they might want to dissuade the cult from existing. Its an open-ended mystery, but even if people did find out what it was, no-one would ever want to re-activate it and the Eresonians might take it as a sort of trophy.
