As part of a WTTTRPG session, player characters managed to broker a sort of peace between some modified sentient rats and the people of Mouvasir. 

Previously, these rats were made subservient creatures of a mad man seeking to use the rats to conquer the land for himself. However, after being foiled, the players managed to find a mutated talking rat and broker with the Council of Mouvasir that the rat be placed on the council to represent rat-kind's interests and facilitate cooperation between the Rats of Movuasir and the People of Mouvasir. The rats would no longer be pests, but participate in Mouvasirian society as equals to the people.

All of this is likely much to the dismay of Malaxihas, in the [Mouvasir Campaign](/wastelife/tabletop/rpg-campaigns/ereso-campaigns/mouvasir-campaign)
