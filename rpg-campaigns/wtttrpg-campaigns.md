Wastelife: The Tabletop Roleplaying Game, or WTTTRPG, is a custom roleplaying game whose mechanics are a modified version of D&D 5E to fit the themes and the systems in Wastelife. The largest change is in the magic system, which was completely overhauled. Although Wastelife and WTTTRPG are integrally connected, the idea is that the worlds are also separate cannons, and the magic system outlined in the WTTTRPG rules aren't truly reflective of the Hidol in the worldbuilding of Wastelife. The magic in WTTTRPG is a gamified version of normal Wastelife, which is required for magic in gameplay.

The tabletop roleplaying is heavily reliant on the worldbuilding. It exists, as a concept, to let my friends explore the intricate world I've created in a consumable and recognisable form. Documents discussing campaigns and game ideas for WTTTRPG are essentially set in Wastelife, with the only caveat being advancements in the world's story due to players playing past the year 3488.

Currently, there are campaigns planned for Eresonia, Acvidaras, and Llynllanes. But, the issue is, Wastelife is well developed enough to make up campaings on the spot for essentially anywhere.
