More Adventures

So sure i've got the Peddler thing worked out but I've still got to think of proper adventures.

How about I start with a campaign?

The important campaign should make use of all 7 locations in Acvidaras. 

Hegrel
Mihu
Kentown
Seasig
Reytown
Gatesweep

Hegrel is easy to include, it's a base of operations for the entire region. Anything that happens there has to include it in some way.

Mihu's a bit harder to include. Perhaps there's trouble at the quarry, someone trying to take it over for themselves to disrupt the industry of Acvidaras. Perhaps it's the location of a battle, as a highly fortified and important town. Maybe it's where intrigue happens, with all of the workers being strong and the upper management mostly being mysterious?

Kentown is in the exact same situation, with it being an industry production location for Hegrel. This one might be easier to include, like what if there was a plot involving Hegrel's drinking water? Perhas the management here is also mysterious. Taking Kentown will have an immediate effect on Hegrel, stopping them from getting clean water. They'd have to use the unclean water from the river, or the dangerous water from the lake. In the end, even boiling it won't make it as pure as Kentown's waters.
