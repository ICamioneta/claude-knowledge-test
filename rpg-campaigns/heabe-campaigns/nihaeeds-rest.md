Nihaeed’s Rest
# Concept

This is the dungeon of Nihaeed, who took the Goat from one of Tanea’s dungeons. Nihaeed originally cleared the dungeon, but later came to use it as their own resting place. In life, Nihaeed was a very successful Reyens adventurer, but their most prised possession was one of Tanea’s 75. The Goat, with eyes so awful they could captivate a soul for hours.

Right now, a tribe of Lindis is using this well-made cave as their home. They use it to sleep in and to store things they get. The Peddler knows this tribe, and for days has tried to get access through them to the adventuring goods that Nihaeed had collected, but these Lindis are stubborn and believe the wealth of the caverns to be far more expensive than the Peddler deems them to be.

# The Construct of the Dungeon

This dungeon has 2 floors, the first and topmost is much larger than the second, which is only a few rooms. The entrance is well hidden by the Lindis, since they covered the decorated stone door with dirt, leaving a hole only small enough for them to crawl through.

![Nihaeed_s Rest.png](/wastelife/tabletop/rpg-campaigns/heabe-campaigns/dungeon-maps/nihaeed-s-rest.png)

The entrance leads into **room 1**, which is a good hall with a round table in the centre. There are usually Lindis around this table discussing tribal matters in Dirish. In here, there are a few displays of swords and shields on the walls, but otherwise nothing special. There are three doors on the walls of this hall. The one on the left leads to room 2, the one straight ahead leads to corridor 1 and the other on the right leads to corridor 2.

**Room 2** consists of a simple stone throne which the Lindis made to suit their tribal leader. On the floor there are several riches that they decided was theirs now. These are several worthless but nice-looking animal bones, 12 Tries (the local currency), several dirt ornaments such as plates and vases.

**Corridor 1** is a long corridor with several doors on it. It is an L-shape which, from the door to room 1, goes straight and then turns left. On the initial straight, there are two doors on the right wall, the first leading to room A1 and the next A2, and one door roughly in between those two doors but on the opposite side which leads to room 3. On the left turn, there is a door straight on at the end of the corridor that leads to room 4 and another door on the right that leads to room 5. This corridor is bland and contains nothing of note.

**Room 3** seems to be a food hall, but all that’s ever been eaten here are leaves and berries. There sits two tables with benches besides them, all made of stone of course. There is nothing of note in this room but maybe a few clay plates, bowls, and cutleries. On the opposite end of this room from the door to C1 is a door to room 4.

**Room 4** is a food preparation area, with tables lining the walls. The tables are stained and are covered in fresh berries, leaves, and veggies, the primary diet of this tribe of Lindis. There are a few items in this room, the most notable being an enchanted knife that the Lindis were using to cut their food. All of the loot is detailed elsewhere. On one of the walls sits the doors to C1 and room 3.

**Room 5** is yet another room with a throne in it. The Lindis avoid this room, knowing that it’s the room right next to the resting place of Nihaeed and the Goat. In the room is a chest holding a permanently enchanted weapon, detailed later.

**Room 6** is where Nihaeed’s body came to rest. In the centre of the room is a gigantic tomb, and above the tomb sits The Goat, Tanea’s 75. On the floor are 2 dead Lindis bodies, who allegedly entered the room and couldn’t escape the Goat. The Goat’s headpiece sits just above its eyes, making it a danger to any who enter the room and accidentally look at it.

**Room 7** is the location of the trapdoor to the lower level. The room is otherwise bland, with shelves holding bottles of unknown base liquid.

**Room 8** is a simple storeroom that the Lindis use.

**Rooms A1 and A2** are rooms where the Lindi do certain trades. A1 is their clay moulding room where they make bowls and things. A2 is the room where they make their clothes out of grass and other fibres.

**Rooms A3-A8** are bunk rooms that the Lindis use to sleep in. The floors are scattered with comfortable sleeping areas and the shelves are littered with personal belongings of the Lindis.

**Room B1** is the first underground room. In this room, the walls are heavily decorated and tell the story of Nihaeed and his many adventures.

**Room B2** is the location of barrels of water and other dried foods. It also holds 9 Tries, and a barrelful of coal.

**Room B3** is the location of Nihaeed’s trophy room. The shelves are lined with the fruits of their adventures. Several displays are missing.

# The Lindis of Nihaeed’s Rest

The Lindis of Nihaeed’s Rest is a tribe of Lindis living in the dungeon known as Nihaeed’s Rest. The Peddler often tries to trade with them for many of Nihaeed’s items, and is aware of The Goat inside. He has developed a fairly positive relationship with the Lindis of Nihaeed’s Rest and seeing them dead would greatly hurt him.

There are 30 Lindis living in Nihaeed’s rest. There are 10 hunters, 12 gatherers, 4 crafters, 1 chieftain and 3 children. Some of the hunter Lindis are outfitted with magical weapons the tribe found in the dungeon. Their cards are seen below.

The hunters and gatherers, as well as the children, sleep in rooms A3-A8. The potter sleeps in the pottery room, A1, and the clothier sleeps in A2. The other two crafters sleep in room 4, with all of the unprepared food.  The chieftain practically lives in room 2.

The lindis have locked away the doors to room 5. It is a forbidden room, as it leads to the deadly room where the Goat is. They make good use of the many enchanted items found in the basement, but it is difficult for them to go up and down often because the ladder is too large for them.

In the middle of the day, most of the hunters and the gatherers leave the dungeon to go do their thing. Some stay behind for several reasons, such as generic protection of the place or to look after the two children. The crafters stay home too. During the middle of the day, there are 9 adult Lindi around the dungeon. The children might be playing just outside the dungeon, with three of the adults looking out for them, in which case there won’t be any lindis in rooms A3-A8. The chieftain will be in his room sitting on his chair, or in the main hall talking to the 2 adults who aren’t looking after the children (or maybe they will all be in that room.) The crafters might be in that room too, but depending on the exact hour, they will probably be in their crafting rooms preparing food or making things.

When all of the Lindi are home in the afternoons and the mornings, almost all of them will be in room 1 or room 3, with the exception of a few who are standing guard outside of the dungeon.

When there are lindi outside of the dungeon, upon seeing another person they will approach them and ask what they are doing in this region. If they say that they are just passing by, then the lindis will resume doing what they were, be it playing with the children or just standing guard. If the people say that they seek entry to the dungeon, the Lindi standing guard might decline depending on how armed or dangerous the people look, or will get the chieftain for consultation.

The chieftain will ask why they seek entrance to their home, and if they say “to seek the treasures” the chieftain will object and say that the treasures are their belongings and to take them would be to steal. If the people push, or say that they seek the Goat, the chieftain might lead them to the doors to room 5, and explain that they locked the doors because of the danger behind them. The chieftain will say that if the adventurers remove the danger they can keep anything they find in that room.

The adventurers might enter Nihaeed’s Rest when there are no guards outside. During daytime, they will enter into a hall with many of the Lindis and perhaps the children as well. The lindis will back away and shout “Intruder” in their Dirrish or maybe in Hegrel’s Shaiou if they know that. That’s when the chieftain will enter from their room aggressively brandishing his enchanted spear and will say “why are you here”, then it’ll be the same as the guard situation but the chieftain will be harder to persuade and easier to intimidate.

If they enter during the night when everyone’s asleep, if they make any noise entering (such as opening the dirt hole) then the chieftain will awaken and quickly run to the room where some people sleep to wake them up, then they will go to the hall. The adventurer will have to succeed on a DC 10 dexterity saving throw to not make any noise while opening the door and another DC15 dexterity saving throw to not trip up the wire that the chieftain sets each night, which will awake the chieftain, who will then shout and wake up everyone else in the dungeon. Once the chieftain has threatened the adventurer in the same way as during the day, he will be much harder to persuade and intimidate, and might ask that the adventurer take nothing but what they seek from rooms 5 and 6.

If the adventurer decides to go charging in there, then practically all of the lindis present will take up arms and join in the attack except one, who will be caring for the crying children (if they can hear the battling.) The person protecting the children will fight if they think they have to, but the children will do nothing.
