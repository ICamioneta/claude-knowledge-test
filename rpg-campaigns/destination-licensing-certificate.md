A Destination Licensing Certificate (or DLC) is a type of visa that allows for free passage through country borders. It is a concept in use for WTTTRPG as a backronym play for DLC as in Downloadable Content, where the idea is that players start in a certain region and would have to purchase a DLC (destination licensing certificate) in order to play the other regions.
