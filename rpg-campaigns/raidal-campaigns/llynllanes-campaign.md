The Llynllanes campaign probably starts when the player party first enters the islands from an Eresonian ship departing from Synset to Baron Synset. After completing a short mission that’s plaguing the town of Baron Synset, the party will be hailed to Baron Xillall by the Baron Gazi government and will be told that they want to hire the player party as mercenaries to help them in the conflict they have against Kingdom Naxdyme.
