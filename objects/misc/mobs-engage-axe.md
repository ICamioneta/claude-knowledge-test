Mob was an expert warrior who served under the God-King Mwender during his conquest of Annwvyn. Skilled with the Bardiche in a capacity beyond any other mortal, he was a collaborator with Medr on that weapon's martial art. 

Towards the end of his life, he wished he would persist onwards. Believing such a thing impossible herself, Medr instead suggested Mob seek a Anulan master wizard by the name Ytwi Venx. Ytwi was working on a unique enchantment: one that could project ghostly apparitions. At the time, Ytwi was hoping to capture the very essence of a person to project it at will. 

Ytwi's final productions was the Engage enchantment. Difficult beyond belief to cast, they copied some notable characteristics of a person and sealed it in the enchant. These enchantments remain rare and locked to only be attempted by expert wizards. To this day, only two wizards were capable of permanent versions of this enchantment. 

Mob's axe is one such weapon. A bardiche that, when activated with the Engage enchantment, projects a ghostly apparition that projects some of his skills to the user.
