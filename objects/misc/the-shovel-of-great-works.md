---
title: The Shovel of Great Works
description: An ornate and useful shovel
---

The Shovel of Great Works is a shovel with an agueltine scoop and handle, and a wooden shaft. On the shaft, the shovel is engraved with a vine pattern. On two large engraved leaves, the names "Marion" and "Dixie" are etched. The handle of the shovel has the same vine pattern that makes the handle rather ergonomic for an averagely-sized hand to hold. 

There are two separate enchantments on the shovel: an active enchantment and a passive enchantment. The passive enchantment allows the scoop of the shovel to hold five times the amount of material that it should be able to, with the material floating off the side of the shovel seemingly supported by nothing. The shovel gets no heavier when stacking up to this five times of material. 

The active enchantment makes flowers, weeds, and grass rapidly grow around where the shovel is implanted. One must jerk the shovel when it is already in the ground to get this to work.
