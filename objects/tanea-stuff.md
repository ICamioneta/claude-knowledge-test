# Tanea's 75 Great Relics

The God of the Forge, [Tanea](/wastelife/eternals/tanea), started the creation of the 75 relics as a mortal. He had finished all of their mettalic forms and many of their enchantments, however his ambitions for the more powerful of his artefacts were impossible to realise as a mortal. When he ascended to Eternity and became an Eternal, he was able to complete his original vision for his 75 Great Relics. 

Part of Tanea's origin story is his mortal forging of the 75 relics and the ultimate completion of them as an Eternal. I haven't catalogued all of them, but I'll add to them whenever I find inspiration. 

I feel it also necessary to add that Tanea's mortal life was during the Wastelife equivalent of the Neolithic. However, metallurgy was not undeveloped. The Volcanic peoples only worked with metals, and they would inspire some who knew of them to forge, just like Tanea. So, if you see an object that seems out of place for the Neolithic era, blame the Volcanic people.
