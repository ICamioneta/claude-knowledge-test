---
title: "Calapeth Jar"
description: "Useful for containing friends or foes"
---

The Calapeth Jar and its many replicants are jars of varying sizes, the original wide and short, with corks on top. There is a symbol engraved into both the cork and the jar. 

When the jar is opened, the nearest calapeth is contained within a jar against their will, their ghostly form being sucked into the jar. The jar must be quickly closed as to prevent the calapeth escaping. 

When the jar is open, a calapeth inside may exit the jar freely. The calapeth may also choose to remain within the jar, occupying a space usually too small for their bodies to fit into. When within the jar, calapeth appear as a dense black mist or black liquid. The calapeth cannot act when the jar is closed.

# history

The Calapeth Jar enchantment was created by the master wizard [Droby Bib](/wastelife/objects/bibs-things). He did not create the jar to maliciously contain Calapeth. Instead, ig was intended to safely house one of their friends to shield them against the public, and for convenience of transport.
