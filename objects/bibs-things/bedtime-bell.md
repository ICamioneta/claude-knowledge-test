A relatively mediocre bronze bell that only sounds if everyone within its building is asleep. It has a soft and calming ring. It's a small bell.

# History

One of [Droby Bib's](/wastelife/objects/bibs-things) less exciting permanent enchants, oft-unreplicated. Despite this, it shows his early skill: building detection and conditional force absorption.
