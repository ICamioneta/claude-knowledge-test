---
title: "Tanea's 6th: Precise Boule"
description: "A pétanque boule that hopefully doesn't get lost"
---

The Precise Boule is a solid steel sphere roughly 10cm in diameter. When thrown, so long as the target of the throw was clear, the boule will always hit the target at the same speed it was launched. Usually, following through on a throw is enough to secure a target, only _really bad_ throws will not be enough for the boule. 

The boule is guaranteed to hit its target, although the time which it takes to do so depends on the target's motion. The boule will follow the target in a "locked-on" fashion, slowing down, speeding up, and changing directions in attempts to eventually hit its target. Despite varying speeds during travel, the final impact velocity of the boule will still be the same as thrown.

The impact velocity is set relative to the motion of the target: if a target runs, the boule will impact faster relative to the ground.

The boule sometimes hits other things in its path as it moves towards its target. It will only hit things if it will break through them. The boule avoids sturdy walls but will path through paper.
