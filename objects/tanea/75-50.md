Jobi's pronouns are it/its. 

Jobi is a shape-shifting mask made out of Agueltine. It's appearance generally conforms to its surrounding culture: if Jobi is amidst Domuz it will take the form of a familiar full-face mask from Domuz culture. If Jobi is amidst Nudos, it will take the form of a familiar full-face mask from Nudos culture.

When unattended, Jobi will float and move around its environment attempting to avoid contact with any person. Jobi will search for any area which is generally open and dark, with plenty of cover to hide behind. These areas are usually dense forests, or large cave systems such as it's home in Fesse Cave under the Iggijoq mountain range. 

When attended, the mask is seemingly inanimate. Despite this, it is known to sometimes talk or make noises, and move slightly when no-one is looking. Attendance to the mask involves general proximity and awareness, not a direct eyesight on Jobi. Jobi will slip away if left inconspicuously in a crowd of people, seemingly weaving unseen between people. Jobi will not go away if it is left on some tree stump while the possessing adventurers camp, even if they aren't looking at it. The small movements Jobi makes when unseen and in attendance are not seemingly with the intent of getting away.

When worn, Jobi will possess it's wearer. The original creature's mind is reportedly transported to a large, open, and grassy field from which a large and confusingly built metal construct can be seen in the far distance. Meanwhile, Jobi takes full control of it's host's mortal body.

When possessing a creature, Jobi will speak in its own mid-tone and raspy voice. When attended to, Jobi is absolutely cooperative with its host's company. Jobi will perform conceivably any task asked of it. 

Jobi is reportedly incredibly skilled and knowledgeable at all things. Its only limit is the body it inhabits, for example Jobi is capable of performing archery of absolute accuracy, provided the body it inhabits is physically capable of the strength and control required. 

Jobi will detach from its host if asked well enough. Jobi will usually issue objections to detaching, citing its relatively dull life on a grassy field. Jobi will also detach from it's host if it is left unattended, such as if Jobi is alone. Jobi will often be torn between wanting to be alone and wanting to continue possession. Jobi's want to stay attached usually overpowers its wanting to be alone.

For more meta detail, the flat grassy plain is well known among scholars of the Gods and their realms to be the realm of Tanea. The mind of Jobi's host is perfectly capable of moving around and even reaching the metal mega-structures in the distance, however their ability to interact is limited.

Jobi has no control over the enchantment of the mask. When not possessing someone, they reside in the realm of Tanea just as the mind of its host does when they switch places. When not possessing someone, the mask's enchantments determine it's behaviour rather than Jobi.

If a host is being possessed for a certainly long length of time, an Esten of Tanea or Tanea himself might find their consciousness in the realm and offer the creation of a body and company. This is known because a long time ago, Jobi was a King's chancellor. The body they possessed had its mind live in Tanea's realm for almost a decade before Jobi was caught alone and detached from the host. 

Jobi, similarly, may be interacted with by Tanea and his Esten. But, Jobi is ultimately an Esten itself, and exists for the explicit purpose of the mask. 

Jobi was inspired by Final Fantasy XIV's "Fesse Cave" unfinished area. Some caves were guarded by an NPC who has the dialogue "I've been given specific orders to prohibit all access to the Fesse using all necessary force. If you value your life, unbeliever, I suggest you withdraw at once". A video exploring the caves, once deep within, hears footsteps around them. Only when they reach the end of the cave, where it breaks and they leave bounds, to they see a floating head inside bounds.
