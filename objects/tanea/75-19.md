---
title: "Tanea's 19th: The Third Eye"
description: "Adaptable jewelry"
---

The Third Eye is an iron circlet with a peculiar shining eye-like design of unknown material on the front. It was made to fit an average head of a Muz, Dos, Gan, or similar average heads, and might not fit on other heads. While it is usually worn soundly on the head, smaller heads may wear it around their necks and still gain the full effects. Heads too large to keep it on them might need to tape it down or tie it on them.

When worn facing forwards, the wearer gains the ability to see things that are usually hidden: they will find themselves more perceptive and will suddenly have insight on objects that they would not otherwise know. 

When worn backwards, the wearer gains the ability to see behind themselves. 

When worn sideways, the Third Eye becomes a weapon, shooting a deadly beam of light from the forward-part of the circlet when the eye is touched.

When worn, the Third Eye attunes to that creature. If put down, the creature the Third Eye has attuned to will be able to see out of the eye at all times. This vision has a practically infinite range. If either the Third Eye or its attuned host leave the realm, the attune breaks.
