---
title: "Tanea's 14th: Cannae Lafter"
description: "Funny stuff"
---

The Cannae Lafter is a small thick tube container a small hand's height and of a diameter that fits nicely into the hand. It is made of a thin aluminium that is easily dented and cut. 

When shaken, a vague sound of laughter is heard from within. When dented, a sharper and louder laugh is made from within the can. When the can is pierced or cut open in any way, a wild gush of wind will force its way through the opening, with an overwhelming sound of laughter being heard from all around the can. 

The can self-repairs when no-one is looking.
