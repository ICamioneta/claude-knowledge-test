The Glass Trombone is as it's described, a trombone made of a tempered sturdy glass. The Glass Trombone was originally an oddly shaped horn, until the modern invention of the Trombone by some Annwvian musician in the 2000s made Tanea realise that this was his vision for the instrument all along, inspiring him to re-forge "The Glass Horn" into the Glass Trombone. 

The trombone is playable by anyone, but only those who have mastered the trombone can use it's enchantments. 

It is known that baboons of all types revere masters of this trombone, but not much else is know about the enchantments on the glass Trombone apart from that they have yet to be fully utilised. The enchantment present is incredibly powerful, and wizards who have tried to analyse it have only speculated on what it might be able to do if wielded by someone who has truly mastered the instrument. 

Some distinguished and otherwise trustworthy enchantment analysts state that the strength of the enchantment is akin to that which could level cities. Tanea has stated that his original enchantment has not changed, but could not have been fully utilised prior to the reforge as the shape of the instrument did not match the expectations of the enchantments. Tanea claims to have forgotten on the exact details of the enchantment and refuses to check. He states that the time will come when the Trombone's Champion arises and wields the glass Trombone with conviction.

Only time can tell for this transparent instrument. Currently, in the present year of 3488, the trombone is in the care of the Church of the Prophet in the city of Aroru, Kyeron. Many claimants to its power have tried to use it in the past, but they have all failed to impress it's caretakers. Its caretakers are church folk and an Esten.
