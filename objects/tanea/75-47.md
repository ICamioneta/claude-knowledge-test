---
title: "Tanea's 47th: The Emotional Banisher"
description: "Comedic timing that gets old"
---

The emotional banisher is a silver necklace with a small silver chain that contains a single blue quartz gemstone is engraved in its centre. It is unnaturally heavy for its size and material, but does not weigh so much as to make it unwearable. The symbol "ʘ" is engraved on the quartz, and this symbol radiates a red light.

Whenever a creature within around 20 metres of the emotional banisher gets unexpectedly insulted to a point where it causes (even a small amount of) psychological harm, that creature will let off a small scream cut off by them vanishing. After around a minute, the creature re-appears at the spot they vanished, or a nearby space occupied by a fluid if the space where they once was is now occupied by solid matter.

The disappearing creature appears in a metal room. This room has a crude bed, a cotton-stuffed mattress on top of a sleek metal platform. There is also a metal sink with two taps that expel clean cold and warm water. There is a metal table besides which a metal chair and a sofa of cotton pillows lay. Atop the table is a bowl (made of wood. Just kidding, it's a metal bowl) that contains various dried fruits that immediately re-appear once eaten. 

The room has a large square glass window that reveals a vast, empty grassland. 

The creature will not be able to bring anything from this room back to where they were when they vanished.
