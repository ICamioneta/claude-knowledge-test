title: "Tanea's 75th: The Armour of Plyt"
description: "It's plot armour but spelt weirdly"
---

The armour of Plyt (rhymes with hit) is a collection of armour pieces forming a set of plate armour made out of a special material called Plyt. Plyt is a translucent, active material with a ghostly appearance. 

The enchantments of the armour sometimes give the effect alone, or need other pieces of the set to provide the effect. The rules are slightly complicated. 

The pieces of the armour of Plyt follow:

- The Helmet of Plyt, which gives the full effects alone. 
- The Gorget of Plyt, can be worn with any other piece to give the full affects.
- The Cuirass of Plyt, gives the full affects alone.
- The Pauldrons of Plyt, when a pauldron of one arm is worn with the rerebrace, couter, and vambrace of the same arm, it gives the full effects.
- The Rerebrace of Plyt, the upper arm piece.
- The Couter of Plyt, the elbow arm piece
- The Vambrace of Plyt, the forearm arm piece.
- The Gauntlets of Plyt. Gives the full effects when worn with the other gauntlet, or with the same side’s pauldron as the gauntlet is an armpiece.
- The Tassets of Plyt. Wearing the full set of legwear gives full affects. This is a part of legwear.
- The Greaves of Plyt. Part of legwear
- The Cuisse of Plyt. Part of legwear
- The Poelyn of Plyt. Part of legwear
- The Sabaton of Plyt. Footwear, gives no benefits, but looks really cool. 

The armour reshapes itself to fit the wearer, however the armour does not come bundled with padding so it is recommended you source your own. 

When the effect of the armour is given, the ghostly form of the rest of the armour appears on the wearer. This ghostly armour is impenetrable. Provided with sufficient padding, it is capable of withstanding almost every attack. The ghostly armour also weighs around 30 kilograms. The armour weighs as a feather when the effect isn't given.

In addition to being a powerful set of armour, Esten of Tanea track every piece of the armour. Sometimes, they will be sociable and interact with the owner of the armour piece, but other times they will observe silently from afar. Legend has it that that Esten who watches the Sabaton of Plyt is a really cool guy.

When a wearer of the armour of plyt is taken to near-death while wearing the armour, the Esten is obliged by Tanea to step in and offer the wearer's life in exchange for the piece of armour. If the wearer agrees, the Esten will do whatever is in their power to save the life of the wearer, often by moving them to a safe place and healing them with potions. The Esten will take the Armour of Plyt and stay with the wearer until they are fully recovered. Once the recovery is successful, the Esten will vanish alongside the piece of the armour. The Esten will probably hide it someplace new.

Legend has it that if someone somehow obtains the full set of the armour, Tanea himself will drop whatever they are doing and watch the wearer. Once the wearer is near death, even if they are not wearing the armour, Tanea has suggested that they will take a commission from the wearer in exchange for the armour and their life.
