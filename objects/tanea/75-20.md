---
title: "Tanea's 20th: The Piercing"
description: "Jewelry with perks"
---


The Piercing is a small silver artefact with a thin rod connecting two balls. One of the balls can be screwed off, and the rod is supposed to be inserted into piercings around the body. The Piercing is only identifiable by Tanea’s mark imprinted on the main ball.

When the piercing is inserted onto the tongue or around the mouth, it allows its user to speak and understand any language, including languages that a creature wearing this artefact wouldn’t be able to articulate or sense otherwise. For creatures with an irregular communication method, for example the Phax, the piercing around their pheromonal openings will produce this affect.

When the piercing is inserted onto the ear, the creature wearing it gains perfect hearing up to a hundred meters and can hear clearly regardless of physical barriers. The wearer will also gain the ability to isolate particular sounds by focusing on them, and can distinctly hear individual voices from within a crowd.

When the piercing is inserted in or around the nose, the creature wearing it gains the ability to perfectly distinguish the compositions of things they smell, provided they are aware of what the elements of the composition is. For example, someone aware of carbon monoxide would be able to smell it, but someone not aware of DNA would be able to tell there's something they don't recognise, but not know it's DNA.

When the piercing is inserted in or around the cheek area including the mouth, the creature wearing it will be able to blow air powerfully. 

When the piercing is inserted in or around the forehead or upper head (including around the eyebrows) then by staring at another creature, the creature wearing the piercing will be able to hear some of their thoughts. Target creatures can only notice the piercing reading their thoughts if they are aware of the piercing's presence and effects or if they have some mind sense.

When the piercing is inserted into the bellybutton, or some other familiar feature for non-mammalian species, the creature gains unwavering confidence in themselves and their abilities.
