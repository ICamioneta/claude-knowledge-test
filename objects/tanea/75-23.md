---
title: "Tanea's 23rd: Disgusting Jambyia"
description: "Ew!"
---

The Disgusting Jambyia is a type of cutting dagger with an ivory handle and golden fixtures. The handle has a crescent curve to both sides of it. The dagger has no crossguard. The blade is short and curves a sixth radian (30°) halfway through the blade. The blade is about a hand and a half long. The blade is made out of Oshias, which originates from Ouranxo.

Whenever the Disgusting Jambyia breaks skin, the wounded creature begins vomiting uncontrollably, emptying the contents of its stomach until their wound heals.
