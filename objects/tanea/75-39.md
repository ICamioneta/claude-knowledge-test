---
title: "Tanea's 39th: Jewelled Plate"
description: "Life-stealing and confusingly ornamented"
---

The jewelled plate is a 7mm thick round silver plate with small divots at 45 degree and 225 degree angles to the vertical. On the front of the plate are several imbedded gems. the first is above the centre, a blue trapezoid with its shortest edge against the centre. To the left of the centre is a rectangular gem of a magenta colour, and this gem is mirrored on the right in a darker deep pink. In the centre of the plate sits a circular gemstone that changes colour depending on how you look at it. Underneath the centre of the plate is a golden oval shaped area.

When activated through unclear means, an incorporeal rope-like tether extends from the plate to some nearby creature, who can be selected by the means of activation. The jewelled plate drains the life out of whatever creature it tethers to, first making them tired, then malnutritional, then a near-skeleton, before ultimately killing them. The length of this process depends on the size of the creature; regular people die after around six hours tethered.

The tethered creature will immediately be too fatigued to move once the tether hits. The tether stays connected until it is activated on another creature or until the tethered creature moves around 15 metres away from the plate. The tether is clear and light-like, it floats in the air and reroutes around new obstructions.
