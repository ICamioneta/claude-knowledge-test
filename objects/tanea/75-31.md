title: "Tanea's 31st: The High Bell"
description: "Probably inspied by a tumblr post."
---

The Bell is an aluminium-silicone bell of roughly 15mm diameter that makes an F7 or 2800Hz note when properly struck with the accompanying High Bell Hammer. 

When struck, a means of transportation is presented to the striker. This means of transport is highly dependent on environment. It will be the fastest, most environment-suitable transport available. This also adheres to cultural norms, for example if rung in an area where chariots are common you will get a speedy chariot instead of the more common speedy post-horse. 

Often, this fastest transport is a means that was already present made to summon to you. For example, a fast horse in a nearby field that is rapidly moved to the Bell's position when rung. The exact range of this abduction isn't well defined. Sometimes, this abduction can boot individuals previously using said means of transport. 

If there is no available transport within range, the high Bell's esten will offer themselves as a appropriate transportation method. 

If the first transport offered by the ring of the bell is unsuitable, for example if you have a lot of things to carry and a horse is insufficient, a second ring will return the first summoned means of transporatation to its original position and summon the next available means of transportation, who will have a greater carrying capacity but will necessarily be slower. The bell can be rung as many times until it seems that the ringer should just walk.

The summoned means of transportation is not necessarily biological or land-based. Entire ships have been summoned in the records of the bell's usage. Using the bell in ways this conspicuous are not without their consequences. The owner of the ship might be a bit annoyed if this happens, as generally the ship's crew and cargo will be left in their original position for gravity to act upon. When the ship returns to its original position, these other things do not magically re-board the ship. The bell will summon galleons. It may summon whales. 

The means of transportation presented, once boarded by the ringer, is now "selected", and can be controlled by the ringer and their team as they see fit, to move wherever they must. When the ringer is done with the means of transportation, they may ring the bell once more to have the means of transportation moved back to it's original position.
