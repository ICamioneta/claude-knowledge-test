---
title: "Tanea's 12th: The Goat"
description: "A petrifying goat statue"
---

The Goat is a small tungsten ornament in the shape of a goat in an emotionless pose, with all its legs straight and its head looking forwards. The model is incredibly detailed, hair being well depicted and even the horns have a perfect texture. The goat is about the size of a hand, the feet being able to stand entirely on the palm, with the head reaching over the fingers when held so. The goat’s eyes are made out of red volcanic glass. When found, and when stored neutrally, the goat will have a small headpiece that covers the eyes.

Whenever a creature looks into the red volcanic glass eyes of The Goat, they feel incredibly uneasy and are paralysed. The creature must have great willpower to escape The Goat's gaze and become unparalyzed. If The Goat is moved so that the paralyzed creature is no longer staring in its eyes, the creature is no longer paralyzed.
