---
title: "Tanea's 30th: The Cauldron"
description: "If you're dead, just take a dip"
---

The cauldron is a large cauldron about a meter tall and with suitable width dimensions. The cauldron is somewhat round, and stands on five metal legs which keep the bottom of the cauldron about 30 centimetres off the ground. The cauldron is made out of cast iron, and has some druidic pattern silver engravings on its exterior.

When a dead body is placed within, so long as it died no more than a day ago upon its placement within the cauldron, it revives the next morning into a living thing again. This creature will be as strong and fierce as ever, but will not be able to communicate with anything beyond simple gestures.
