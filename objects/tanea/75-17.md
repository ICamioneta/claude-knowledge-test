---
title: "Tanea's 17th: The Teetgong"
description: "Fast enough to meet the stoor worm"
---

The Teetgong is an Oshias model of the windpipe of a goose. When the teetgong is blown into, like one would with a flute, it makes a multi-tonal sound. While the teetgong is blown, all Equidae creatures within ten metres are capable of moving four times faster than usual. This effect persists for as long as the creature runs continuously. They must not remain near the Teetgong for effects to persist.
