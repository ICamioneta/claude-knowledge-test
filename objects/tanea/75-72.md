---
title: "Tanea's 72nd: The Telescope of Time"
description: "All telescopes look back in time, but this one especially"
---

The Telescope of Time is a brass telescope roughly a metre long, with the wider end having a diameter of roughly 15cm while the eyepiece is only around 4cm. The telescope of time comes with the tripod of time, a brass tripod which can be attached to the telescope of time to brace it against the ground. The tripod of time comes with mechanical means to precisely set the latitude and azimuth of the telescope of time.

There are two small dials on the side of the eyepiece. These dials allow the user to tune how far back in the past the telescope sees. The first dial provides a general timescale, and the second dial allows the user to fine-tune the time. The dials are logarithmically scaled, so the further back in time you go the less precise you can tune the dials. It can be tuned for individual seconds when looking a week ago, minutes for months ago, hours for years ago, and days for decades ago. When set to a time, the telescope of time views what it would have seen if it were placed in that exact position (relative to the Mortal World) at the time set. Time moves normally when viewed through the telescope, so if you tune a little far back and don't have the accuracy to get the exact hour you want, you can wait for that hour to come. 

The telescope of time's default zoom factor is 50 times. Upon a mortal request a long time ago, Tanea has also created an alternate lens for the telescope of time that provides 10 times zoom. Legend has it that if you find an esten who has contact with Tanea, they will make a request for a new lens on your behalf. Alternatively, you can make this request yourself if you are presented with the company of a god.
