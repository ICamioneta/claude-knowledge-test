Tanea’s 1st Great Relic is a collection of 1000 iron daggers, each somewhat indistinguishable in quality from the one forged before and after it, but overall the daggers start at a poor amateurish quality and end in a beautifully forged and impressive dagger.

Many of these daggers are unenchanted, with a random selection of 250 of the topmost daggers containing enchants, varying themselves in quality from simple enchants to enchantments as complex as those in Tanea’s other 74 relics.

These daggers are scattered around the mortal realm and most are in the possession of rich folk or adventuring groups lucky enough to find them. Many of the daggers were permanently enchanted after being found, giving wizards who owned these daggers their chance to make a permanent mark on one thousandth of Tanea’s first great relic.

Legend says that if someone were to gather all thousand daggers in a big pile, they would ascend and become the Eternal of Collecting Daggers. Alternative forms of this legend say that they all combine to form one really big dagger, or they all join up into one big dagger-bound creature.
