Droby Bib was a master wizard alive between 3121 and 3240, and was considered a master wizard from the age of 43. Bib was a wizard of hat culture, owning am extravagant abode in Gordolmonre's Basinal Highland. His sizeable abode is the Palace of Bib, a great gathering place for Droby and their friends. 

Bib is most well known for their permanent enchants, which he shared freely for others to reproduce. 

# The Palace of Bib

The Palace of Bib was oddly laid out and fabulously decorated. The layout was optimised for play - running around, social deduction with an active component. Each room had a theme and the connections between them were closeable depending on activity. 

After Bib's death, his friends took first picks among his things, and the Palace was ransacked thereafter by common looters. Years later, in 3410, an amateur tower wizard came to control the building, restoring much of its structure and constructing an 8-storey tower alongside it and considering the original as a part of the low-rise complex despite its three storeys and two underground levels. 

Today, the Palace of Bib is inhabited by most of the wizard's servants. The wizard's occupation is appreciated by Hegrel for its trade, and by historians for the preservation of Bib's history.
