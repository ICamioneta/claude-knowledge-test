This document is dedicated to all of the fake materials I made up for fanciness. You can imagine that these are special alloys or mixtures, or that they are just a regular metal treated in a fanciful way. These materials are said to be "enchant catalysts".

Plus, some of these are real, but they have additional properties for enchanting. 

# Metals

## Agueltine

A brownish metal from the [Volcanic](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic) communities of Icsuxjoq. It is a potent enchant catalyst. Materially, it is a unique form of Beryllium Copper.

## Vocriene

Vocriene is a rare metal that is found deep in the Gordolmonre mountains. It is a bright, reflective material. It is a good material to make flashy weapons and tools from. Materially, it is chromium-based.

## Oshias

Oshias is a metal from [Ouranxo](/wastelife/regions/ouraxo). It is incredibly durable and holds edges very well. It is an exotic bronze.

## Gorg

Gorg is a unique super-alloy created by [Tanea](/wastelife/eternals/tanea) in collaboration with a Volcanic metallurgist in the 1900s. Its chemical composition is primarily cobalt, nickel, and chromium. 

## Rhenium

A real metal that's a enchant catalyst. It is most practically used in an alloy with nickel. The nickel alloys containing Rhenium are enchant catalysts, proportionally potent to the proportion of Rhenium in the alloy.

## Silver

This well known rare metal is a good enchant catalyst.

## Titanium

A lesser known rare metal has minor enchant catalyst properties.

# Transmutations

## Aeroquartz

It's a type of silica aerogel matrix often constructed by hidosis that is tough and light as air. Once broken or given enough time, the structure collapses into a harmless fine invisible dust. 

# Hyper advanced

These materials were recently invented by Tanea, [Hell](/wastelife/eternals/hell), and [Cnthon Dan](/wastelife/characters/creatures/cnthon-dan). 

## Star Weave

Star weave is a material pioneered by Hell. Its components are found in small but dense stars in the Mortal Realm and realms alike. It is made out of the fundamental materials of realms, arranged in a microscopic weave-lattice structure. It is practically indestructible and comes in densities from a manageable steel to catastrophically unmovable. 

## Sun Steel

Made from the burning material of the sun itself, sun steel is superconductive, impossibly lightweight, and immensely tough and strong. When it breaks, it unleashes unfathomable destructive energy. Weapons will explode with enough force to destroy a house. Larger objects get impossibly destructive. Sun steel cannon balls rigged to break on impact can level an entire city block. 

Luckily, it is impossible to produce enough energy to break a well forged Sun Steel object. The only things in the mortal realm that will break it are other god-forged weapons capable of focusing pressure to small points, high-frequency vibrations, and thermal instability.

There exists a unique enchantment made to remove this danger from sun steel. It is a complex enchantment, but if the enchanted object explodes it will absorb that energy into the Hidol. It is imperfect, exploded weapons will still cause fatal blasts. 

## Aythre

Aythre is a superheavy metal that's extremely dense and tough. It is almost completely impervious to heat, corrosion, and fatigue. 

## Void Diamond

Void diamond is a nanoscopic material that cannot chip or deform. It is either impossibly black or crystal clear, and can flicker between the two under stress. It also does not retain heat.
