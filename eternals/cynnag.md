**Domain:** Growth

**Identity:** Agender (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** A Mad, preferably an exceptionally large Guarmad

## Introduction

Growth and Decay are not mutually exclusive topics. With growth, decay is necessary to facilitate more growth and the development of that which exists. Without growth, decay is nothing. Despite this, Cynnag and Pydred hate each other. They fail to see the reliance that their domains have on each other and wage the Everlasting War in the mortal realm between the mushroom forest of Vugaix and the deserts of Kyenel.

## Social#

Cynnag is an alright person, although ultimately, they are a bit of a fanatic. Their insistence on growth often opposes the natural cycle that’s present across life, and that other gods can’t avoid bringing up. Rather than having a gentle approach to growth, Cynnag’s ideas are harmful and for that reason he often has complex relationships with the other gods. He is generally liked, despite his fanatic flaws.

## Domain

There are lots of aspects of growth that Cynnag could focus on, such as the beautiful cultivation of life, or the development of society, but instead he choses to focus on excessive growth, and the consummation of everything under the growth of biomass. Its for this reason that he likes mushrooms, they represent this unending growth.
