**Domain:** Communication

**Identity:** Androgenous (They, Them)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Parrots

## Introduction

Cyfapheb is the Eternal of Communication, it's sciences, and what it can mean to people. It's a scientific interest; Cyfapheb is a master of etymology, dictionary, and whatever else you can think of that pertains to two things communicating. 

Cyfapheb is the God of Communication, and for a God of Communication, her name is annoyingly difficult to pronounce. She’s into communication, its sciences, it’s meaning to people. How it changes, how it impacts lives, the exact meanings of it in society. She studies networks, relationships, languages. She’s not the creator of them, although she does create a few interesting ‘experimental’ ones from time to time, she’s just the watcher and learner of them.

## Social

Cyfapheb is known for clearly communicating when they aren't exploring their domain, for example in the well-understood Eternal meetings. However, when exploring their domain, Cyfapheb can socialise to an unnatural extent and without bore. Recognising that rest from communicating is important for many. 

## Domain

Communication isn't just spoken language. In fact, spoken language is one of the most explored in Cyfapheb's domain, although there is always work to be done. People's speech patterns change wildly over time, new words pop up, vowels and phonemes shift. The meanings of words change as time changes the contexts. Cyfapheb tracks all of these across the mortal world, with the help of their Esten. 

Much more fascinating to Cyfapheb is non-verbal communication. Dance, pheromone, body language, cryptography and made-up languages. 

Their realm is on the smaller side, only ever getting traffic from themself and their returning Esten. Often, the realm's space isn't even material.

## Manifestation

Cyfapheb’s manifestations are parrots when fit for purpose. They find them fascinating in how they communicate. It seems like a current obsession of hers rather than a long-term thing, but she seeks to guide the parrots in developing their own language, easy to understand and similar to those created by the people of the mortal realm. She does not like to be big, as she claims it interferes with her vocals.

## Esten

Cyfapheb's Esten are the Sardier. They do pretty much the same thing as Cyfapheb, going out and exploring the Mortal Realm's many methods of communication.
