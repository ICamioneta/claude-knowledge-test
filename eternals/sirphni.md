**Domain:** Lethargy and Laziness

**Identity:** Feminine (She, Her)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Anything really, whatever requires the least effort.

## Introduction

Sirphni is colloquially known as the God of Lethargy and Laziness, which is an entirely fair interpretation of her character. She is often blamed for coaxing people into laziness, that feeling we get that tells us to not do anything on a day.She herself obeys her own doctrine to a tea, and doesn't do things as a religion.

## Social

Although Sirphni is subscribed to the doing of nothing, she still gets up and experiences Eternity. Her social efforts are still half-arsed and lethargic. She does actually enjoy hanging out with people so long as they're also doing very little. 

## Domain

Pre-enlightenment, Sirphni was often interpreted as a temptress. In reality, she just advocates for taking breaks and pacing yourself. Perhaps she advocates her things a little too much. If everyone followed her recommendations, not much would ever get done. But she's not pushy and actually admires mortals who do things from time to time, provided they aren't overworking themselves. 

Her realm is often the most minimal thing she needs at the moment. Sometimes, it's even been just a single interior, until she wanted to get up and look out of a window. It doesn't take the same understanding of effort that Mortals have to maintain a realm, but she often removes parts when she can't be bothered to think about it. 

At its maximum, around the year 600, Sirphni's realm became a beautiful concrete city. This uncharacteristic effort, mostly offloaded to temporary Esten. The entire purpose of this city was some spas and baths, and some nice views, for Sirphni and some mortal friends to relax in. 

## Manifestation

Sirphni doesn’t manifest as anything in particular. When she can be bothered to manifest, be it for an inspiration or to chill with some mortals, she usually takes the easiest form to do the job. Like a small person. 

## Esten

To maintain Esten, to even think of a name them, would be too much effort. They are only ever temporary. She creates temporary servants when the process of creating them takes less effort than the thing she then has them do. 

You can call them the Pennau Cysglyd if you want. I'm too lazy to diverge the name to being unrecognisable from the literal Welsh.
