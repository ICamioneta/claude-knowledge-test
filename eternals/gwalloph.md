**Domain:** Madness

**Identity:** Old man (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Babbling old man or woman, whatever suits the need.

## Introduction

Gwalloph is the God of Madness. He spends his time making selected souls mad, and watching what they end up doing. He does this for amusement, but also because that’s what interests him: the antics of madness.

## Social

Gwalloph isn't usually an erratic communicator. They are verbally inspired by rambling, but they are perfectly capable of fine, structured speech. In more mortal contexts, he will mutter madly in a way that only those truly listening can understand. He's usually perfectly legible to other Eternals and Esten. 

## Domain

Madness is when people and creatures loose their mind. They break from their normal behaviour and logic and do something usually self-destructive. It's a disruption of physical neural pathways, connecting and stimulating the brain in irregular ways. 

Gwalloph makes people mad, in a usually very unlikeable way. Post-enlghtenment, he's still viewed negatively by mortals who accept that they cannot pressure the other gods to keep Gwalloph in check. Plus, his threat is miniscule. 

His domain is empty, apart from when he or his Esten enter it, which is usually in attempts to drive some mortal creature mad, or to push the boundaries of their cognition. In which case, they'll custom tailour their environment. 

## Manifestation

Gwalloph manifests as a blabbering old man, true to character. There’s not much else to say about that, it’s not as if every blabbering old man is him, and it’s not like asking is going to get a proper answer.

## Esten

Gwalloph's Esten are few and far between, especially post enlightenment. They are called the Gwalloph Tymer. They just do what Gwalloph does, and Gwalloph doesn't see much value in that unless he thinks of too many things to do in a certain time-frame.
