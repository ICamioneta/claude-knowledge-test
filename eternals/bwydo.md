**Domain:** Food and Taste

**Identity:** Masculine (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** His greatest friend, a mortal chef

## Introduction

Bwydo, the God of Food, the best chef and the most experimental. He is one of the reasons Muz's tastes are so refined. Bwydo spends his time creating new, interesting, and not always tasty, foods for the people to eat. He’s often the reason why succulent plants pop up out of nowhere.

## Social

Bwydo's one of the most social Eternals of all time, working directly with the mortal world's most distinguished and experimental chefs. He's really cheery and very good at commanding the kitchen when it comes to it. 

## Domain

Food or eating are poor descriptions of Bywdo's expressive taste profile. He not only enjoys food and eating, but every possible relation to the terms. He's an expert at nutrition, flavour, and culinary hospitality. He's into all foods: the good, the bad, and the conventionally inedible. 

His realm is a continental garden in which he grows and harbours everything that has a taste, which is everything he can find. Within this farm of everything, he will often host meals, banquets, and feasts that he invites whoever he likes to. From the highest of kings to random peasants he picks up while on the *Mortal Trot*. He'll also host "diplomatic banquets" between world leaders from time to time, hoping to ferment peace inspired by his best friend. 

The realm is also a hub for Ymdig's Esten, to recuperate or to acquire ingredients. Food isn't always the same after being transported through realms, but there are ways around this and often this is just added flavour. 

## Manifestation

Bwydo manifests himself as his greatest Mortal friend, a Lemuz chef from an ancient empire, known to settle the grand war between the Terailean Empire and the Conpotne Union with nothing other than a good dish, charisma, and hungry emperors.

## Esten

The Cogin Anik are Bwydo's Esten. They are agents of his domain, free to follow whatever cooks they desire so long as it generally contributes to the ideas of food. Most master cooks are in direct contact with a Cogin Anik. Many Cogin Anik decide to fill more grounded roles, like the heads of a communal kitchen.
