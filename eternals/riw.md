title: Riw

**Domain:** Partnership

**Identity:** Feminine (She, Her)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Anything that suits her jobs

## Introduction

Originally the Eternal of Procreation, Riw has come a long way in discovering herself and her domain. This somewhat reflects the people aspect of her domain, and how people discover themselves in their relationship. She is now fascinated by mortal relationships and love as a concept, although she's still into the procreation of creatures to some extent.

## Social

She loves talking to people in the most genuine way she can. She's often overblown with romance and can come off as a bit theatrical at times. Other times, she is more realistic and normal in her dialogue. 

## Domain

Her domain is relationships in all forms. Eternals have relationships, but they are as complex and above mortals as the Eternals themselves. Riw does like these relationships to some extent, and is both fascinated by them from a personal and academic standpoint. The bulk of her domain is the relationships of mortals, in all forms. They are just a lot more understandable and workable. 

Mortal relationships move from familial, platonic, romantic, and sexual. Riw is indulgent in all of these. She will spend her time watching them, intervening, prodding and stressing. She'll also often take part in mortal relationships, manifesting her own mortal body and living out an entire lifetime just to experience what she so admirably watches. 

She's never malicious, or so she says. The "worst thing" she does is intentionally break people apart, but she'd insist that they were never meant to be together in the first place. She is a very wellbeing-first god, especially post-enlightenment. 

Her domain is very small and empty. It consists of a few hand-crafted romantic or vibey landscapes, and a couple houses interspersed in-between.

## Manifestation

Riw primarily manifests as people. Even when she watches relationships, she will often infiltrate as a friend. She will manifest as a colourful moth when involving herself isn't appropriate. 

## Esten

Riw’s Esten are the Caradis. They are her agents, spending much of their time doing exactly what she does. Usually, it's not even at smaller scales or importance since Riw's domain can't really quantify these things. They'll reconvene with her as normal, sharing their experiences of the mortal world. 

Caradis don't have much power in Riw's realm, but she has a special Esten to handle requests when she's out of house. Her Esten are usually encouraged to hide themselves, but sometimes people just find out or they get revealed anyway. 

To recap, Riw isn't just about the romantic. She's also about familial. Riw herself couldn't possibly maintain a family, so the Caradis are just about the only way she can explore this front. Another example of what a Caradis whose exposed might do, if they're exploring platonic relationships and friendships, is transport their friend group to Riw's realm to hang out. This, and mortals knowingly living lives with a Caradis, are the main traffic for Riw's realm.
