**Domain:** Antisocialism

**Identity:** Masculine (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** a floating white ball

## Introduction

Even amongst the regulars of society, a social situation seems like a dangerous minefield. Avoiding saying the wrong thing, trying not to make assumptions. Wanting to be liked, trying to be good. Social situations are difficult to understand. Some people do their best to understand social situations not to better existence overall, but because they realise that it can cause personal gain. People are social creatures, and are vulnerable to social manipulation. Good people are coerced into doing horrible acts, regular folk live miserably in the wake of someone who asserts that they are better and more important. Bad actors who cannot conceive of guilt, shame, concern, regret, or remorse.

## Social

Gwrphcim is a scholar of the social situation, but much like those he names his domain after, he feels no remorse or any of the other things. Amongst the Eternals, this makes him unpopular for the sole reason that he harms mortals, which puts him in a similar situation to Maleisus. Unlike Maleisus, he is generally bearable and is capable of using his studies of the social situation for more than coercion. Although it seems impossible, Gwrphcim and Cyfapheb are not not friends, as far as their official relationship among the Eternals goes. Ultimately, their domains are the same, hence why Cyfapheb herself has dabbled in social exploitation, and why Gwrphcim has dabbled in social empowerment.

## Domain

I’ve almost already covered everything there is to say about Gwrphcim’s domain. Although outwardly, his domain is that of social exploitation, inwardly it is indistinguishable from the ideas of studying social situations, the same as is found in Cyfapheb’s domain. The primary difference, however, is the use of the study. Gwrphcim actively uses himself and his Esten in situations of social imbalance to encourage, help, and display those who are antisocial and perform these manipulative feats.

He'll occasionally promote and create antisocialism too. 

## Manifestation

Despite his domain being in the social, Gwrphcim’s favourite manifestation is a floating white orb. Some say it represents wholeheartedly the ideas and manipulations that his domain offers, others just say it’s a cool ball to be. Since those that he sees often think big of themselves, he doesn’t even need to mask that he’s a god. Those he shows himself to will often lie and cheat.

## Esten

Gwrphcim's Esten are the Diere Ketin. They do basically what he does, promoting antisociality and social isolation.
