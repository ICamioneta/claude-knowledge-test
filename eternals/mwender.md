## Introduction
Mwender is an Eternal with a lot of mortal history. A Ce'gan born on an island in Nusant, he would come to bring the entirety of Annwvyn under his banner in the year 800. It was just before he could sit on his throne that he was about to die, but instead he was ascended to eternity. He lorded over Annwvyn for a few decades, but ultimately abandoned his mortal connection.

It is little surprise that he chose ambition as his domain, as his ambitions consumed his mortal life.
