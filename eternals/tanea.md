**Domain:** The Forge
**Identity:** Masculine (He, Him)
**Preferred Manifestation:** Himself before ascension (Domuz smith)

## Introduction

By the time Tanea ascended, mortal ascension was an accepted but still not understood phenomenon. His ascension was the direct fault of Gwalloph, who as part of his routine made some Domuz smith mad. That domuz smith then dedicated their life to the forge, eventually ascending to godhood.

## Social

Tanea is a somewhat sociable eternal. He infrequently talks to other eternals, mostly doing so for some purpose such as expertise, knowledge, or their involvement. When it comes to mortals, Tanea finds himself and his esten frequently convening with mortals in discussion of his scattered artefacts. He knows his artefacts are like games to them, and he's more than happy to involve himself.

## Domain

One of Tanea's most recognised achievements in his mortal life was the creation of his 75 artefacts. Although then he didn't have the power to finish them as a mortal, upon ascending to eternity he then enchanted with the enchantments they were supposed to have, and scattered them across the mortal realm. He likes to see mortals scour the mortal realm for them, but dislikes collectors who don't use them. He still makes things from time to time, and often supports the expert mortal smiths in some of their projects.

Tanea's most recent endeavours are in collaboration with [Hell](/wastelife/eternals/hell) and [Cnthon Dan](/wastelife/characters/creatures/cnthon-dan). It is a set of hyper-advanced materials.

His realm is an island like West Lale with a huge iron structure in the middle. This structure is Tanea's forge, where he makes new things. Sometimes, he moves mortals into this structure, and its scale is quite amazing.

## Manifestation

Tanea manifests as himself before ascension, a common choice among ascended gods. He was a Domuz of pretty average build from Dondros

## Esten

Tanea and his Esten maintain a number of exclusive forge-based societies across the realms. Some of Tanea's Esten are bound to the functionality of his artefacts. They are collectively called the Gorg-Bound, as they often manifest as creatures of Gorg armour bound by light. The Gorg-Bound Mesther are specifically Tanea's regular Esten.
