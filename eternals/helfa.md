**Domain:** Hunting

**Identity:** Masculine (he, his)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Bipedal wolf-like

## Introduction

The Hunt is a time-honoured tradition in all communions. Early people hunted, and to this day many animals hunt for food. Modern hunting is a sport; thanks to agriculture, hunting for food is no longer a necessity. As people were massively hunting, and viewing it as a socially glorious action, the God of the Hunt appeared in Eternity. 

## Social

Helfa is an avid socialite, and they prefer large gatherings over small functions or one-to-one meets. They are a character of a hunter, boastful and loud in conversation. They are also always ready to praise and help others. One of their favourite activities, unsurprisingly, is a hunt. They will invite as many gods and even mortals as they can to hunt a beast in their realm or in the mortal realm. Often, this hunt is followed by a feast.

## Domain

The Hunt is an easily understood domain. You get a creature of some description, and the goal is to kill it! The issue is, as a God in your own realm, you're highly capable. The hunt then is seen as a game to Helfa. They will establish rules to make the hunt "fun" or "realistic" to the mortal setting. When the hunt is in the Mortal Realm, these restrictions are usually on the manifestations participants are allowed to take. When the hunt is in their own realm, they'll essentially set up a small game, with one or a few hunted creatures who are either copies of mortal creatures, changed mortal creatures, or a fun new creature of Helfa's own creation. 

Helfa's hunts often involve mortals. He's invited all types of mortals, from kings and emperors to Mike from down the street whose greatest accomplishment is a freshwater trout. Usually, in inviting a mortal, Helfa will offer them a reward if they are successful. Often, it is related to the killed creature. Sometimes, it's something else special. Always, it's to participate in the following feast. But mortal involvement isn't always fun. Unlike Eternals, mortals have a serious risk of death during these hunts. This risk increases infinitely if they become the subject of the hunts. Mortals do not always have a choice with Helfa, but he likes to keep things fair.

## Manifestation

Helfa's preferred manifestation is a wolf-like bipedal, a species of person present on Ouranxo and northern Annwvyn. Helfa designed this species of person, wanting to "boon" the wolves of Ouranxo for their wildly optimised hunting strategies. This manifestation is often depicted holding a spear or a bow, the chosen weapons of Helfa.

## Esten

Helfa has few Esten. Those that exist copy his manifestation and accompany him on his hunts. Some of Helfa's Esten are creatures to be hunted, but this is a little torturous for the Esten so he generally saves Esten hunts for special occasions, or if an Esten has upset him.
