**Domain:** The Crawl
**Identity:** Masculine (he/him)
**Preferred Manifestation:** A creepy humanoid but crawls everywhere with pale skin and long icky black hair.
## Introduction
The Crawl as a noun refers to the act of dungeon delving. The imagery is that of long gauntlets that adventurers must endure and at the end of their trials they are rewarded with some _loot_. Dungeons like this aren't very common in Wastelife, before Lusg most of them were of Tanea's design, or randomly designed by some rich person in attempts to protect their wealth through generations. As land control becomes absolute, dungeons like this disappear. 
## Social
Lusg is a very antisocial eternal. They rarely talk with other eternals unless they want something. They also talk to eternals (and people) for arbitration and rules making. Otherwise, they're just busy managing their massive dungeon empire. 

## Domain
Lusg's domain is his dungeons. They are massive, purpose-built often underground gauntlets with lethal challenges and rewards in every room and corridor. Lusg often designs them himself, and often he has his Esten work on and improve the designs, or diverge them to keep them fresh. 

It is a highly bureaucratic operation, each Esten fills a very specific and useful role. It's highly transformative on the Mortal World too: Lusg can't will the dungeons into existence so he has his Esten construct them out of local material. Similarly, his Esten procure and transport the dungeon rewards. 

His own realm is a vast landscape that contains the blueprint designs of his mortal dungeons. Also here are his most ambitious projects: ones that he could not hope to build in the Mortal Realm. It is hard or impossible for enchanted objects to work both in the mortal realm and another realm, so he often stashes them in an undisclosed mortal location or offers material riches he can manifest. 

## Manifestation
Lusg manifests only for a few reasons. The first is to talk to his Esten. The second is to watch people in his dungeons. The third is to be a boss battle in his dungeons. Generally, he likes to be his little pale gremlin thing, but often while watching people he'll just be a bat or something else small that goes unnoticed. The boss thing he often has to be big or powerful for. 

## Esten
Lusg's Esten operation come in a few discrete forms. The most common are the builders who create and maintain the dungeons. Their bodies are often highly specialised for digging or brick-laying or stone-cutting, but they do often just manifest as the local humanoids and engage with the local economy to procure tools and stuff. These builders also maintain hte dungeon once it's built; feeding animals and resetting traps. 

Another form of Esten are those who take the role of the dungeon monsters themselves: since maintaining an ecosystem or feeding Fido is challenging, it's often easier to just create an Esten to fill that role. Esten can also be a lot more powerful than mortal creatures. 

The final form are the ones that get Lusg's treasures. Lusg's Vault is somewhere in Hebas Deryd (but you didn't hear that from me because it's a secret). He has another one on the south pole, but that's only for the stuff he holds that's really powerful and not in use. These Esten both transport things from the vaults to the destination, as well as find, track, and obtain "artefacts" and potential loot. 

# Dungeons

Lusg's dungeons are basically the whole point.

In the modern era, centralised nations have had to codify Lusg into law. He wants to build dungeons, dungeons require land, and his dungeons often kill people. In a way, they're sort of theme-parks. 

Governrments, aside from granting plots, will often put some constraints on Lusg's dungeon building. They'll ask for no casualties or otherwise certain lethality goals. If Lusg still wants a dungeon, he'll just adjust the reward to better reflect how easy the dungeon is. These rewards are also to some extent why governments wants dungeons, but monopolised dungeon tackling (i.e. government ran dungeon-delving institutions) are against Lusg's ideals. 

To Lusg, dungeons must have a genuine danger and be open to every challenger.
