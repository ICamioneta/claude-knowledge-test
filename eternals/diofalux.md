**Domain:** Causality

**Identity:** Formless (It, Its)

**Preferred Manifestation:** A rock on the ground

## Introduction

Diofalux used to be branded the God of Accidents and Disaster. After much rebranding, it's managed to get people to understand that it's domain is really just causality. It just so happens that many accidents and disasters are good exercises in causality, and how small things can cause lots of other things. 

## Social

Diofalux isn’t that great at conversation, and much prefers to stand out and watch another conversation unfold. When it is found speaking, its almost guaranteed to be talking about the long-winded series of events that lead up to and influenced some random event its paying attention to.

## Domain

Diofalux's domain in causality explores every preceding event to a certain significant event, and the extrapolation of events from a certain context. Diofalux's obsession with it is scientific. It'll watch causal chains with great interest. This is on wildly varying scales, from someone stubbing their toe on a table to a great destructive storm.

Their realm is an abstract playground in which it is constantly exploring causal chains inspired by those found in the mortal realm. 

## Manifestation

Diofalux prefers not to manifest, but when it does its almost always a rock. The size can vary, but its usually about half a pace long and a little shorter than that tall. Not the most interesting of manifestations, but it allows Diofalux to observe and perhaps cause accidents itself.

## Esten

Diofalux's Esten are the Axosi. They help Diofalux with the study of causal chains, making their own observations and setting up their own events all in the name of testing infinity.
