## Introduction

Dim is the Eternal of the Void. Dim represents the concept of nothing, the concept of the void, although Dim would disagree with those statements because concepts are in fact something, and something is not nothing. Dim even refuses to define nothing, as nothing is just a word and is in fact not nothing, as nothing is a word. I’ve put “void” as the answer to the three criteria above but in reality those fields aren’t applicable, Dim doesn’t claim the void as a domain because that would be something. Similar in identity, and obviously Dim does not have a manifestation.

## Social

Dim is a bit of a difficult entity in social situations. Especially for mortals, there are no pronouns that refer to nothing, so you have to settle for just saying the name Dim. Despite this, Dim can be a very good listener, and despite Dim’s domain,  Dim can talk back too. Dim is really understanding, non-judgemental, and will try to help you understand things by yourself.

Although Dim makes no claims, or doesn't make no claims either, it is well known that Dim is in an Eternal relationship with [Prydferth](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/prydferth). The best mortal understanding of this relationship is the beauty of emptiness and minimalism. Their relationship even proceeded to a point where they had a child together, [Cov](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/cov). 

## Domain

This is usually the space where I talk about the Eternal’s domain, but in absence of that, I’ll skip ahead to what Dim actually does. Dim basically acts as the Eternal of Existentialism, Dim is a bit of a councillor and does actually have esten to help in that task. The only reason Dim has a realm is to properly organise the esten that exist to help in this strange counselling situation.

## Manifestation

This is where I’d usually talk about the Eternal’s manifestation, but seriously, Dim doesn’t have one. He is still capable of speaking to people, people who literally find themselves talking into the void only for it to talk back and help that person go through some struggle in their life.
