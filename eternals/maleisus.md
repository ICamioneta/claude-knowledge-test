**Domain:** Schadenfreude, finding joy in other's suffering

**Identity:** Masculine (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** ~~big red guy~~ like, a big bug, or a boar, both often bipedal.

## Introduction

Across the history of people, laws and social norms fluctuate and change. The social implications of certain styles of clothing have changed drastically over the course of mere decades, for example. A few laws predate society as social norms. Those that condone harm, in any form, is one such law. Maleisus opposes this law. He finds joy in other people’s suffering. Murder, mutilation, and torture all fall under his domain, and while such things exist without him, he and his esten actively encourage it.

## Social

For quite obvious reasons, Maleisus is a generally disagreeable person. Pain as seen from outside of the mortal realm is a trivial thing, a feeling used by active things to detect when they are in trouble. It is general consensus amongst the Eternals, even the traditionally evil ones, that mortals are pretty cool. Respecting them, their autonomy, and their want to not be in pain is common, if oft violated even by kinder eternals. The only argument in his favour is that he’s “testing the mortals” which doesn't hold post-truth and even he knows is not true. 

Maleisus doesn’t get invited to parties. When he’s caught in them, he’s not the type of person you just accept and let stand alone in the corner, you actively expel him.

## Domain

Shadenfreude is an active conflict between society and those who harm. It is a conflict that Maleisus actively participates in, sometimes making his own manifestations in the mortal realm for the sole purpose of causing harm. Such a situation is rare, as it is usually his Esten that do this for him by coaxing mortals and also doing harm themselves. If he does manifest, the rest of the Eternals often take notice and try their best to kill his manifestation. 

## Manifestation

Maleisus manifests in several different ways. His most powerful form, which also takes a while to make, is his Charging Boar manifestation. This manifestation is a giant white boar clad in metal armour, breathing fire and smoke, whose mouth and weapons drip with blood. A more modest manifestation is his knight, clad in deafeningly bright white but still dirtied with blood armour, Wielding a flaming sword, and never too far from their horse, an Esten in the same armour. When committing much smaller acts of badness in the mortal realm, he will often settle for a mostly unnoticeable cloaked figure.

## Esten

Maleisus's esten come in many different forms, all of which carry out essentially the same thing that Maleisus does. They enjoy harm and suffering, and are very autonomous compared to other Esten. They go and do their own thing, encouraging torture, murder, and all manner of previouly discussed ills that fall under Maleisus' domain. Because of their common actions, they have gained a notorious status among mortals as "demons of suffering". Most mortals are pretty good at avoiding them. There are certain "rituals" mortals can do to get rid of them. They're not magical rituals, but instead an invocation of an agreement made a long time ago pretending to be a ritual. Honest Esten and Maleisus himself promised to leave mortals alone if they do this ritual. To him, its kind of a game.
