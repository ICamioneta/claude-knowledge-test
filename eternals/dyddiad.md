**Domain:** Calendar

**Identity:** Object, semi-masculine (it, he)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Clockface

## Introduction

In early civilisation, people started datekeeping and watching the sun and moon to keep track of the passage of time. Dyddiad's domain, calendar, is concerned with date-keeping and knowing when things are. They were the god that suggested the adoption of the Annual, working with Adnabyd to find out exactly which year they first found the mortal world. 

## Social

Dyddiad is a very precise and particular socialite. He choose their words quickly and carefully, making sure every point comes across clearly. They aren't particularly social, viewing communication solely for its practical uses and not for its recreational ones. Dyddiad's friendships are more like business relationships. 

## Domain

Calendar and datekeeping are Dyddiad's domain, and he obsess over it an amount understandable for an Eternal with a domian. He's primarily concerned with maintaining his "Timeline" which is a comprehensive list of all notable events throughout all of history. It is preserved in a format only Eternals can understand in his realm. His Esten work tirelessly with Diofalux to figure out when exactly important events will occur, to make sure that they are there to witness and catalogue those events.

## Esten

Dyddiad's Esten are the Clockfaces. They have peculiarly thin and black bipedal Muz-like bodies, almost like stick figures. Their heads are clockfaces of various description. Their faces have evolved with the ages, initially just being rocks, but evolving alongside civilisation's time-keeping methods. Whenever visiting the mortal realm, they often adopt more canny visages to fit in with the background for whatever event they are witnessing.
