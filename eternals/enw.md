## Introduction

Throughout early civilisation, people socialising came to find themselves in talking to others. It is with this self-identification that Enw arose, a god of names, identity, and definition. Enw has a close relationship with Cyfapheb whose domain encapsulates many of these things, although Enw claims that the topics of identity only partially overlaps communication. 

## Social

Enw is a very social eternal. Enw often likes to talk about other people and their feelings, obsessing over uniqueness and being eager to group people. 

## Domain

Enw's domain is one of identification and identity. Enw enjoys names, self-expression, proper nouns and their language use. Enw catalogues these and encourages mortal expression. They are obsessed with style and subculture. 

Enw spends Enw's time taking to mortals and exploring their identity, as well as exploring identity culture.
