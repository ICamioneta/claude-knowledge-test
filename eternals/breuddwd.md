**Domain:** Dreams

**Identity:** Semi-Woman (She, They)

**Preferred Manifestation:** a colourful cloud or a shapeshifting creature

## Introduction

Breuddwd is part of the three Eternals that appeared after the 'Beautiful Event' that occurred in -4451. This event happened worldwide and was viewed by mortals and eternals, all in awe. It is said that the event spurred dreams across all that slept, and even those who stayed awake. Breuddwd's domain is these dreams, the literal brain phenomena that occurs in the minds of those who sleep.

## Social

Breuddwd is a very social eternal, speaking to both other eternals and with mortals face-to-face. Her communication is rarely direct. Her speech patterns are rifled with strange metaphors, similes, and implications all of which take a while to interpret (if you're not an Eternal used to her speech). She has also mastered the ability to alter dreams as they are had, and she manages to communicate with mortals in this manner too. When she talks to people, her demeanour changes depending on the situation. Sometimes, she's warm and soothing. Other times, she's elusive and distant. She is also capable of being deeply frightening. She consistently leaves lasting impressions on mortals, who rarely forget her words.

## Domain

Breuddwd's realm is an ever-changing and surreal place where no common physics apply. While Eternals have always been able to alter certain aspects of physical reality in their curved realms (see Eternity) such as gravity, Breuddwd took it a step further by creating a whole new dimensionality of realm. She created a fractal realm, which is somehow both infinite and finite. Mortals who find themselves here will find their dreams manifest into reality, as that's just something Breuddwd does. 

As for what she actually does with the domain of "dreams", she's managed to be able to alter the dreams of mortals to her liking as well as read them in a strange way by placing her consciousness in the same place as the mortals  She does this by transporting the sleeping to her realm, or by making a manifestation capable of surrounding the brain (like becoming some plasma that joins the blood of the creature, so she can reach the brain). She crafts mortal's dreams, plants ideas, and takes inspiration of them directly, to do whatever she wants pretty much.

She's not *explicitly* evil, but the moral implications of literally being able to read minds, as well as how these dreams she crafts can sometimes be purposefully torturous, makes her morally questionable. Mortals tend to like her though.

## Manifestation

Breuddwd's manifestations are a work of skilful art. Not only was she capable of inventing a new way to make realms, but she has also invented new ways to manifest that she almost exclusively uses. This was discussed previously, as she has managed to create manifestations that let her consciousness 'enter' the brain of a mortal, directly involving herself in her dreams. Otherwise, she likes to take on the form of a colourful and ethereal cloud, or some sort of mystical shape shifting creature where she takes forms relevant to the person she's speaking with.

## Esten

Breuddwd's Esten are the Cysga, amorphous creatures that share the shapeshift form. They dream through their realm, crafting it especially as Mortals or other eternals visit. They also travel around the Mortal Realm, influencing the dreams of some mortals. They are also directly connected to Breuddwd, her being able to communicate directly with them when in the mortal realm. This is how she's able to take forms relevant to the people she's talking to, as she'll have a Cysga literally in their head. When they speak, their voices are whispers that somehow silence everything else.
