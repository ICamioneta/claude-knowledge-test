**Domain:** Harmony

**Identity:** Agender (He, They)

**Preferred Manifestation:** a ghostly figure possibly made of light.

## Introduction

Distaw is among the three eternals that came from the Beautiful event of -4451. This worldwide event happened over the course of several days, and it is said that all conflicts between people were paused. Unfortunately, they were resumed almost immediately after, but from the event came Distaw, and their devotion to the vague idea of harmony. Distaw likes the quiet, but more generally their domain extends to the ideas of harmony *and* peace. They are a bit illusioned with the mortal realm, very annoyed at all the comeuppance and commotion that happens.

## Social

Distaw is essentially perpetually annoyed, but you would never tell just by looking at them. He always appeared quiet and contemplative, listening more than they speak. People who listen to them are often calmed by their voice. Distaw is not a passive socialiser, they speak with meaning when they want, often taking action against others when they feel the *balance* is threatened. Distaw somehow finds a way to get their annoyances listened to while not being very loud or abrasive in their speech.

## Domain

Distaw's domain of "harmony" is commonly concerned with maintaining some idea of balance and avoiding chaos usually expressed as unpredictability. Its a bit of a complex topic, but I can explain what Distaw likes and dislikes in the modern and ancient climates of the Mortal Realm. They dislike the Empire of Ralean for having a chaotic and unpredictable government (clearly on show with the southern revolts) but they like the Western Neble Imperia because its a highly stable society with strict rules where classes work in harmony to provide for society. This poses Harmony as a morally neutral concept, unconcerned with the suffering of WNI's lowborn or how Councilism provides for its people.

Distaw also likes art, but they are a bit particular about it liking the balance in the art. They come into conflict with Prydferth a bit, since Prydferth sees beauty in the way art is unbalanced whereas Distaw believes balance is the essence of art. Its a bit of a complex social relationship, since they are *the eternal equivalent of* siblings.

Their realm is a bit boring to be honest. Its very quiet, with little interesting juxtaposition in the art of it. 

## Manifestation

Distaw manifests themselves as some sort of ghostly figure of light, which is apparently balanced in some way, but I wouldn't really know.
