**Domain:** Skill

**Identity:** Feminine (She, Her)

**Preferred Manifestation:** A Female Nebdos, but will take any form to aid teaching and learning skills.

## Introduction

Medr is the Eternal of Skill. She's generally considerate and cares what others think of her, but only to a certain point. All she wants is to aquire as many skills as she can, and get as good at them as is eternally possible. She is always collaborative, and since most skills are harmless or productive she's generally seen in a good light. She has had her societal mishaps. Medr is the type of eternal to look at a murderer and go ‘Hey, I can help you get better at that’.

## Social

She knows how to sit alone and hone her expertise for every skill known ever, and she also knows how to show them off at parties. She enjoys helping others develop their skills. She's approachable, confident, and generally likeable. She is enthusiastic to a fault.

## Domain

Medr is the greatest pool of raw skill and talent in eternity. She's mastered every skill invented before a few thousand years ago, and is really struggling to stay on top of the modern skill explosion. She is also not always the best at a thing ever. She might be a master, but most of her skills were taught to her by mortals. Sometimes, she manages to surpass her master's abilities, but often she would prefer spend her time mastering other skills than pushing those pre-existing boundaries.

Her realm is a perfectly sculpted world with well planned cities, littered with workplaces and dojos that serve the single purpose of having the most perfect place to practice. Sometimes, she lets willing mortals into this realm with an Esten if they show promise in a skill. She is not very lenient when it comes to mortals slacking, but she's really good at picking hyper-focussed and obsessed people. 

## Manifestation

Medr likes to manifest herself as healthy, young people, often women. She prefers to manifest as a Nidos, which is her diverged subspecies of Dos, but will take whatever shape is best to perform a skill. She doesn’t like to be big or small. 

## Esten

Medr’s Esten are known as the Medres Anaml. Some are masters on several basic skills, but most Medres Anaml are really good at a particular skill. Medr’s Smith Esten, called the Medres Gof, were given a right arm that can change shape depending on the tool needed during smithing. They also have special eyes and other sensory organs that allow them to perfectly probe their smithed object.

One of the most powerful Medres Anaml is the one who manages Medr's realm, its ingoing and outgoing mortals. This (currently unnamed) Esten has several other Esten working underneath them, the Medres Gorchuil. The Medres Gorchuil are responsible for mortals in Medr's realm; their wellbeing and progression.
