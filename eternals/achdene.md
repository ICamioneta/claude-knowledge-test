## Introduction
Achdene is the god of lightning, spontaneity, and the immediate. They are essentially an adoptive child of Caesi, who created a huge lightning bolt from which Achdene appeared. They are somewhat erratic and spontaneous, and maintain some sort of inexplainable consistency in their actions.
