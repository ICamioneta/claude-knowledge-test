**Domain:** Strength

**Identity:** Masculine (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Himself before ascension (Lemuz)

## Introduction

Having been the first mortal ascension, Cryver's appearance in Eternity caused a lot of confusion among the other Eternals. They had accepted that new eternals just popped up from time to time, but the idea of one carrying the consciousness and memories of a mortal seemed fictional. Still, Cryver proved that if a mortal dedicates themselves to some unclaimed domain, they *might* ascend to Eternity.

## Social

Cryver is very sociable with mortals, but Eternals rarely convene with him unless they make an express effort to do so. Cryver spends his days on the Mortal Realm helping people attain the strength he sook in his mortal life, 
sometimes aiding trapped people himself. He is happy to help Eternals when they do eventually find him, but he always prioritises his mortal endeavours above what the eternals require.

## Domain

Cryfer's domain is Strength, but not just in physical but in mental too. A dedication to physical strength is meaningless for a formless god who can manifest as anything they want, so this branching into mental fortitude helps bridge that gap. It also helps that to attain high levels of physical strength, you need that mental fortitude to work through the pain. As for Cryver's realm, its basically a giant gym where he sometimes takes mortals looking for strength to train.

## Manifestation

Cryver became a very buff Lemuz before ascension, but many mortals after him have achieved much greater strength through specialised means. Cryver usually manifests himself as himself before ascension, with varying amounts of strength. He keeps ahold of a "buff body" which is some manifested vessel that's a copy of the Lemuz body that he continually trains to be the strongest possible person.
