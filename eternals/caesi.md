**Domain:** The Ocean

**Identity:** Agender (They, Them)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Usually some water hidden among a larger body of it

## Introduction

Caesi is the Eternal of the Ocean. They exist for the ocean and all the creatures in it. Caesi is well known among mortals for creating a whole bunch of species of sealife. Caesi creates more than sea life, and often creates many freshwater fishes as well. Their species are always named "Caesi's Fish" where Fish is usually a more descriptive name and not solely confined to fishes. 

## Social

Caesi is at least a little sociable, although they're often in their little marine life workshop, whenever they come out they can't stop talking about their creations. Caesi is a very proud god, and generally quite pleasant. They often talk with mortals at sea especially when they run into one of his more interesting creations.

## Domain

Caesi's realm is a huge ocean which is kinda inhospitable for people. Its here where they create their creatures before transporting them to the mortal realm. Caesi isn't just the Eternal of sealife, they also often observe or create oceanic phenomena such as large storms, whirlpools, tsunamis, and the likes. This has caused many mortals to worship them to some extent, hoping to them that the seas are nice to them. Sometimes, Caesi actually likes to help navigators and ships.

## Manifestation

Caesi almost always manifests as some water part of a larger body. This is useful when he's doing some stuff in the ocean, but for weather phenomenon they often manifest as clouds with a better overview of the whole situation. Caesi has also, on occasion, manifested as a sort of half-fish half-person, which is usually very confusing to whoever perceives them.

## Esten

Caesi's Esten, the Or Edych, do much of the cataloguing, upkeep, and fine tuning of their creatures.
