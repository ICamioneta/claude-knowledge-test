**Domain:** Knowledge

**Identity:** Masculine (He, Him)

**Preferred Manifestation:** A fancy looking male Nidos

## Introduction

Adnabyd is the God of Knowledge, and as it happens, he knows more about almost everything than anyone else. He spends his time learning, reading the books that Mortals make, and gathering knowledge from them. Sometimes, he likes to make his own experiments, to expand his own knowledge. He had a period of time where he harmed mortals because he wanted to learn more about them.

## Social

Adnabyd doesn’t care much about people. He cares about knowledge. When people seek knowledge, he likes that. When people ask about knowledge, he appreciates it, but it happens too much and annoys him sometimes. He’s a bit insular, and you would think so considering how he stays alone most of the time. He’s not obnoxiously all-knowing; he’s not going to correct you when he thinks that in the situation it doesn’t really matter. He’s helpful when he does correct, instead of going “um, actually”, he often provides the truthful information upfront and then provides an explanation to why that is the truth, and sometimes sprinkles why something untrue is commonly known.

## Domain

Adnabyd and his realm are the largest library of facts, history, and knowledge that has ever existed. He is not omniscient; he must learn all of his knowledge himself, meaning that some of the world’s greatest mysteries are unknown to him. Some of the mortal realm’s greatest mysteries are known to him, but often he likes to keep them hidden to see if the Mortals can uncover their own past. The spire in Ereso is one of these. He was there when it happened, and he was impressed, but he’s not going to tell anyone now what happened. If anything, he’s a little angry that everyone forgot such an epic. He has this pact with Medr, as he claims that gaining knowledge is a skill, and that if he told everyone what they wanted to know this skill would be lost, as the world relied on a God to inform them of everything.

## Manifestation

Adnabyd likes to manifest as a really fancy Nidos, with a bit of all the cultures they’ve developed across their past in his clothing and attire. He does often like to be big, but sometimes has to manifesr a bit smaller to fit in libraries. That’s not to say that he actually manifests often. He’d much rather sit (as a God does) in his realm getting others to find new books for him.

## Esten

Adnabyd’s Esten are a various bunch, but generally they resemble tall bipedal bird-like creatures that wear long and spooky cloaks. They undertake a number of tasks for Adnabyd, such as keeping admin in his library, copying mortal texts, and cataloguing the Mortal Realm’s infinite series of events. On occasion, they'll work with mortal universities on Adnabyd's behalf. They are called the Poud Gibod.
