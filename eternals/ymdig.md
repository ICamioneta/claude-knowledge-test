**Domain:** Behaviour

**Identity:** Genderless (They, Them)

**Preferred Manifestation:** various insects, usually butterfly.

## Introduction

Ymdig is the Eternal of Behaviour. They are an indulgent and exploitative Eternal, they spend most of their time watching mortal creatures behave and they like to push creatures to behave in new and unique ways. Through this exploration, Ymdig has become a master of understanding behaviour of all types, a bit of a psych doctor if they'd existed for 15000 years.

## Social

Ymdig is introspective and just enjoys being in situations. They don't talk much, but are strongly opinionated. When they do talk, most Eternals are weary because they are accustom to Ymdig intentionally poking social situations to get entertaining or new behaviours from mortals and Eternals. 

## Domain

Behaviour is a hot pot of rituals, instincts, personality, decision and impulse. It's a reptile's uncontrollable impulse to chase something if it runs away. It's a fly zipping away from something moving closer. It's a particular spider's way of making a web. It's curiosity, discovery, and much more. 

The behaviour of people is so much more complex with their cognition and thinking abilities. Ymdig is naturally fascinated by this, and enjoys watching people behave when put in unique situations. They also sometimes intentionally engineer situations for people to experience. Sometimes, this can be morally disagreeable situations in their realm, but post-enlightenment Ymdig makes sure mortals make it out alive.

Ymdig's realm is just a normal continental world where they can create their own creatures and bring mortals in to test behaviour. 

## Manifestation

Ymdig likes to manifest themselves as various insects, they did create most of them after all. Their favourite and most frequent is a butterfly. These creatures are familiar to them, and they act of raw behaviour, sometimes having a personality. They don’t like to be particularly big, but still likes to be bigger than a normal butterfly, maybe one with a wingspan as long as a full arm.

## Esten

Ymdig's Esten are silent cataloguers, mirroring Ymdig's testing and prodding and bringing back "test results" for Ymdig's enjoyment and recreation.
