**Preferred Manifestation:** Indescribable shape that Cyfun claims is an embodiment of Eternal existence and bureaucracy. Cyfun has manifested into the mortal realm maybe a few times.

## Social

## Domain

Cyfun's domain is not of cities in general, rather it is the Eternal City. Their existence in Eternity is a bit of an enigma, seemingly appearing out of eternal necessity rather than the development of some domain-related thing in the Mortal Realm. Cyfun appeared once there was around 40 Eternals in eternity, and collaboration between them became unwieldy. It is then incredibly convenient that Cyfun appeared with the explicit purpose of wanting to facilitate a hub in which every Eternal could socially mix.

## Esten

Cyfun has a few different types of Esten that help them with the duties of the City. Cyfun's esten are few and far between, and usually incredibly competent and capable. 

The Cyfun Wereg are the Esten that manage mortals, Esten, and Eternals alike all visiting the Eternal City. They mostly act as assistants, granting Hidol interfaces to visiting eternals on request and making sure mortals are well fed and enriched.

The Regregic Lof are master assassins and warriors unique in their field. They were specially created with the purpose of enforcing the 4th C.A 98. This topic of discussion was on rogue Esten who the Eternals decided to concern over after some egregiously negative actions of a particularly bad lot of rogue Esten.

Rogue Esten are those in the Mortal Realm who are not acting on behalf of their Eternal. The original Eternal can destroy them with some effort when in proximity, but it's considered a skill to destroy them. 

With some effort, Esten are capable of making destroying them quite difficult. Rather than spend the effort to seek out and destroy their rogue Esten, Eternals delegated this task to Cyfun. As the ultimate manager of Eternals and Eternal affairs, this was right in the purview of Cyfun.

Rogue Esten are fairly uncommon but consistent. Every century will see maybe 2. There are about a dozen Regregic Lof who find and execute rogue Esten with not only glorious efficiency but great care for mortal impact. As an example, some rogue Esten entrench themselves as god-kings or autocrats, or otherwise within mortal communities. The Regregic Lof make sure that no power vaccum is left, or otherwise that the ultimate impact of the rogue Esten is minimal.
