## Introduction

The God of the Moon's appearance in Eternity is difficult to attribute. Some say that worship of the Moon was popularised, others suggest that moon calendars were popularised. Regardless, LLeh exists, and their domain is the moon and the many things that mortals attribute to the moon. I should add, the name being the inverse of the God of the Sun is coincidence, LLeh is based on a Welsh word like any other god.
## Social

The moon's socialisation depends highly on the mortal world's moon's phase. It is generally understood that this is a conscious effort by Lleh, and that normally they are a quiet and reserved socialite. When the moon is in full, Lleh tries her best to become as outgoing as possible, much to their own fatigue. As the other eternals don't track the mortal moon's phases, an esten of Lleh accompanies her to forwarn other eternals on her current phase.

Her phases are:

- Missing - she is quiet and reserved, generally understood to be her natural social state.
- Coming Crescent - 
- Half-Alive
- Coming Gibbous
- Full - She is happy, sociable, and hopeful
- Leaving Gibbous - She is loud, boistrous, and dramatic
- Half-Dead
- Leaving Crescent

It is also well known that Lleh is in an Eternal relationship with [Hell](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/hell). LLeh often struggles in engaging with Hell's domain, but perseveres in their relationship regardless. They have a child, [Arhos](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/arhos).

## Domain

Lleh's domain is, primarily, the physical object in the Mortal Realm that is the Mortal World's moon. They like sitting on it and looking at it. Their domain also covers periodic phases, romance to some degree, and probably a few other things. Lleh likes to spend most of their time on the Mortal World or on its moon, existing and observing things. Sometimes, Lleh will watch or intervene in romances, playing a subtle role. 

## Manifestation

Lleh's preferred manifestation is usually a moon rock. Sometimes it's round like the actual moon, sometimes it's jagged like an actual rock. When manifesting as people, they prefer to manifest femininely.
