**Domain:** The Sun

**Identity:** Object (it, its)

**Preferred Manifestation:** a mini sun, heat and all.

## Introduction

Hell is the god of the sun, representing heat, life, and everything usually attributed to the really hot ball of gas that heats the mortal world. It is loud, confrontational, and not particularly kind. Hell is often grandiose in their behaviour. They are a sibling to [Enw](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/enw), having appeared around the same time. I should also say that it is a coincidence that their name also refers to an underworld often considered fiery. It's an adaption of a Welsh word, like always.

## Social

Hell occupies this strange social space where they are both incredibly social and incredibly introspective and silent. When it wants to make itself known, the room will know as it bellows its words and makes sure that any physical manifestation it uses actively lights up the room. Otherwise, Hell enjoys its own time. 

It's well known that Hell and [Lleh](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/lleh) are in an Eternal relationship, in the sense that they spend a lot of time together. The exact interactions and nature of this relationship are hard to grasp for a mortal, but what is rather simple is that they spend a lot of time interacting with each other's domains, a lot of time in each other's presence, and have both gone on holidays together. They also have a child, [Arhos](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/arhos).

## Domain

Hell's actual domain has always been quite vague. It claims an object in the Mortal world as their domain, but in general it enjoys all large astral bodies present not only in the mortal realm but in other realms. Their domain also involves electromagnetic radiation, atomic fusion, and photosynthesis. The vast majority of Hell's time is spent as its preferred manifestation floating around the cosmos of the Mortal Realm, sometimes actively inside the sun, sometimes exploring the Mortal Realm's other astral bodies. 

Hell's realm is a spatial realm that contains a bunch of solar systems. Some of the planets even harbour life, some of it being natural. These planets are inhabited by some of its Esten.

## Manifestation

Hell's preferred manifestation is a mini sun. The actual size often varies, but due to the complications of making energy-consuming manifestations their mini sun is usually quite small, from around 0.2m to 1m in diameter. This manifestation is often an active fusion reaction upheld by Hell's power. When visiting the Mortal World, Hell will usually stop the fusion reaction and settle for some other less dangerous phenomenon that won't set everything on fire to produce their sun-like visage.

## Esten

Hell's Esten are the Goleiaw. They are astral travellers that also visit the mortal world from time to time. They're pretty much completely independent, not holding any responsibilities or ties to Hell. They are sometimes a bit randomly seen doing things for fun in the mortal realm.
