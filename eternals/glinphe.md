**Domain:** Gluttony and Excess

**Identity:** Feminine (She, Her)

**Preferred Manifestation:** A Boar or a Goat

## Introduction

Glinphe is a promoter of excess, in all forms. Originally, this was best expressed by the excess of food, which was somewhat hard to come by early on. Later, as agriculture developed, Glinphe found her true awakening in those who would hoard the food, and consume it all when others also needed it. 

## Social

Glinphe is not a kind person. She is very explicit about what she wants and what she enjoys. 

## Domain

Gluttony extends to goods other than food. Glinphe encourages people to hoard materials and to indulge on excesses, especially those kings and lords who have the wealthiest of lives. She'll spend her time encouraging these people and sometimes even giving them the means to achieve greater excess. 

With the rise of capitalism and economic analysis, wealth inequality has become part of Glinphe's interests. She's not on anyone's side per se, but she might try to widen the gap for experiment's sake. 

## Manifestation

Glinphe, when she does, manifests as a boar or as a goat. She does this because they are both animals known to eat almost anything, so she respects them for that symbolism. Like any eternal, she'll manifest as a person to blend in and be more approachable. 

## Esten

Glinphe's agents of excess are known as the Durxi, they do much the same as what she does. The long-standing conclusion of the 1024th C.A. point 2 also means that she needs to acquire pre-existing excess rather than synthesising it herself, so her Druxi help her plenty with that.
