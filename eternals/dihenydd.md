**Domain:** Destruction

**Identity:** Agender (They, Them)

**Preferred Manifestation:** Anything that causes destruction, often a wolf-like person wielding an explosive mace.

## Introduction

Destruction as a concept involves increasing the entropy of some system, like a building, by breaking down its structured nature and collapsing it. Dihenydd really likes it when this happens. He is a purveyor of explosions, deconstruction, and things alike.

## Social

He used to be badly liked as he enjoyed the deaths of mortals, but he has changed since. He's actually a good friend of Cynnag, realising the contradictions in his own domain and how things need to first be created for them to be destroyed. Since his realisation that people create about as much as they destroy, he's become a lot more liked by other gods, if still generally being in negative opinions. This realisation came about after people destroyed the White Tyrant. While he originally liked the White Tyrant, seeing it fall was exhilarating. 

## Domain

Dihenydd is focussed almost entirely on things getting destroyed. He doesn't care for things staying destroyed, in fact often he likes for them to be rebuilt so that they can see its eventual destruction again. He spends most of his time watching things blow up and collapse, which is in abundance in modern society.

## Manifestation

Dihenydd manifests themselves as all types of things, but quite likes being an anthropomorphic wolf wielding a mace that causes explosions on hits. Given how they often just like to watch things blow up, their wolf form is usually perked on a high point with a good view.
