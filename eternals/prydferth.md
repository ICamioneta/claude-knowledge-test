**Domain:** Beauty
**Identity:** Androgynous (They, Them)
**Preferred Manifestation:** Usually a person wearing the height of their culture's fashion

## Introduction
Prydferth is part of the three eternals that came from the 'Beautiful Event' in -4451. This worldwide event is commonly falsely attributed to them, who actually was found in Eternity *after* the event. They are an artist, and a patron of artists (or at least specific types of artists), fascinated by the concept of beauty as a whole and all that espouses it. Patterns, nature, mathematics, and passion are all places they finds beauty, but they make a point in noting that beauty isn't possible without flaw.
## Social
Prydferth is magnetic and intimidating. Mortals find themselves drawn to their manifestations, entranced by their brilliant appearance and charismatic presence. Prydferth appreciates compliments, but their passion is artistry in all forms. She frequently visits mortal artists to talk to them, discuss inspirations and artistry, improving both their own sense of artistry and the senses of mortals. Although they recognises that flaws are an important part of art, they are also capable of scathing criticism when things aren't quite there.
## Domain
Prydferth's realm is essentially a massive gallery and a huge garden. Both are essentially showcases of copies they and their Esten have made. This is basically what their domain means to them, the seeking, appreciation, and perfection of beauty as some amorphous and hard-to-define idea. In Prydferth's own definitions of their own domain include: "Framed, carved, danced, sounded, grown, seen, experienced. It all can be beautiful. Even the ideas of mathematics, the understandings we have of the mortal world, it all has potential for beauty."
## Manifestation
Prydferth manifests when they want to study beauty with some mortal artist. For this, they almost always pick the image of a local species of person with a body in the height of modern beauty standards, garnering frankly angelic and wonderful clothing. It's all very culturally dependent, but they do their research to make sure that their manifestations are, truly, beautiful.
