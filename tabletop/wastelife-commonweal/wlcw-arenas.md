In WLCW, the game is split into three arenas - three different types of scenarios that are generally aligned to an attribute that offer unique and specialised sub systems. A particular instance of an arena occurring is generally called a conflict. These arenas are:

- **Combat**, the Arena of the Body
- **Dilemma**, the Arena of the Mind
- **Discourse**, the Arena of the Spirit

The arenas all have:

- **Sway** which indicate in whose favour the conflict is leaning.
- **Actions** which are things that can be done to attempt to sway the conflict in one's favour. Every skill has some type of action they can take for every arena, however some skill sets are certainly more useful than others.
- **Rolls** which, when given the series of actions, will influence the sway by randomly modifying the rate of progress, degree of impact of actions, and simulate risks and unintended consequences.

All arenas work in turns and rounds. When a conflict begins, the order in which participants make their actions is determined through some means. Once this order has been fully traversed, a round has occurred, and the order begins again. During a participant's turn, they will select one action.

On a regular basis, you might be managing several arenas at once. You might be in a duel while simultaneously trying to persuade your opponent, or need to work your way around a conversation while solving some puzzle. During situations like this, turns will line up, and while the GM manages two arenas you as a player must interact with two arenas. Or, you could focus on one ongoing arena while other members of your party engage in the other.
