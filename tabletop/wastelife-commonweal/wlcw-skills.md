Skills are fundamental advancements that are made as your character progresses. While they can be learnt like any other advancement, they are often more restrictively handled as gaining a new skill is akin to levelling up, compared to usual advancements that are more similar to mid level gains. 

There are 12 skill trees, which each have 5 skills in progression. There are three sets, each tied to one of the three attributes. 

# Character Attributes

There are three attributes: body, mind, and spirit. Attributes have a score that starts at 0 and increases whenever you gain a skill. These scores indicate a general development in the field, and provide some vague benefit because of that:

- **Body** score increases your stamina. You can use your body for longer without getting as tired
	- Athletics
	- Motorics
	- Stealth
	- Endurance 
- **Mind** score increases your focus. You can pay attention for longer periods of time, and can keep focus on more complex things.
	- Academics
	- Awareness
	- Logic
	- Memory
- **Spirit** score increases social resolve. You can talk for longer without loosing track, and can follow other's speech with less drag.
	- Charm
	- Leadership
	- Willpower
	- Empathy

Gaining new skills follows a particular schema:

- Whenever you gain a skill, it's related attribute increases its score by 1.
- Skill progression is linear; you cannot get level 3 of a skill without first having gotten level 1 and level 2 of that skill. 
- First-level skills do not require any attribute scores. 
- Second-level skills require 2 points in an attribute.
- third-level skills require 5 points in an attribute.
- fourth-level skills require 6 points in an attribute
- fifth-level skills require 8 points in an attribute.

Each skill provides some generic description of the capabilities of a character with this skill. These capabilities often overwrite the previous level's description, and the descriptions are more so general guidelines on what that level of a skill means for that character's capabilities.

# Body skills

The body is your character's control over their worldly vessel. 

## Athletics

Athletics is your body's ability to perform athletic feats, like running or climbing. Without any athletic skills, your character will not be able to run for very long, and will struggle to pick up heavy objects for prolonged periods of time.

In **Combat**, athletics will help you fight for longer without getting tired and do things like vault over obstacles on the battlefield. 

In **Dilemma**, athletics will help you lift or move heavy objects, or perform feats of strength.

In **Discourse**, a good athletics skill will show on your body, helping you physically intimidate or impress.

### Athletics 1 - Jogger

With basic athletics, you can jog mid distances without tiring too quickly. You can also climb, swim, and perform similar mediocre athletic tasks although not very well.

### Athletics 2 - Sprinter

You're capable of short bursts of speed, and can perform other athletic abilities without much strain.

### Athletics 3 - Athlete

Running long distances, climbing walls, swimming, and other similar feats become quite manageable. Your stamina is steady even under stress.

### Athletics 4 - Powerhouse

Your fears of strength, endurance, and agility impress others. Carrying heavy gear, leaping obstacles, or swimming for hours has become routine.

### Athletics 5 - Olympian

Your body is a honed machine. Incredible bursts of speed, marathon endurance, and heroic feats are second nature. Being the best of the best, however, is just out of reach.

## Endurance

Endurance is your body's preparedness pertaining to physical damage, not only from direct physical attacks but from diseases and poisons. Without endurance skills, your character topples easily from pushes and frequently finds themselves with a bad stomach.

In **Combat**, endurance will lead to advancements that will increase your save points, as well as help you resist poisoned weapons and at later levels you will take less damage overall.

In **Dilemma**, endurance will let you take more risks in puzzles as your body will be able to resist the physical consequences of risks.

In **Discourse**, endurance will make you appear more steady and calm, able to control your physical composure.

### Endurance 1 - Hardy

You can withstand a light shove and can survive minor illnesses.

### Endurance 2 - Stalwart

Poison, sickness, and fatigue hit you less hard. You can shrug off moderate injuries and stay on your feet.

### Endurance 3 - Iron Body

Physical blows, toxins, and harsh conditions are shrugged off with grit. You can endure exertion without faltering.

### Endurance 4 - Unbreakable

Diseases fear your immune system, many poisons linger without much effect. Your liver is a machine, and you can persist despite grevious injuries. 

### Endurance 5 - Cruvan's Shell

Pain is dulled. Exhaustion is rare. Wounds heal with alarming speed. You can resist that which would kill others.

## Stealth

Stealth is your ability to move silently and with deliberation. Without a stealth skill, your character moves very conspicuously.

In **Combat**, you will be able to hide directly from enemies, and will let you conceal your combative intentions.

In **Dilemma**, stealth will help you find solutions silently, alleviating certain time pressures. It may also help you just walk past the problem.

In **Discourse**, stealth will keep you stone-faced. You will be able to hide your social intentions.

### Stealth 1 - Sneaker

You can move quietly if you're careful, and can blend in with crowds. 

### Stealth 2  - Shadow

You slip by in dim lights and can hide effectively from a casual glance.

### Stealth 3 - Ghost

Your presence vanishes when you concentrate. Even Sharp eyes will miss you unless they're actively searching. You are as quiet as a cat.

### Stealth 4 - Phantom

You blend into bustling crowds. You escape even persistent eyes. You can disappear without a trace. 

### Stealth 5 - Invisible

To the untrained eye, you simply do not exist. Silence and concealment are your second nature.

## Motorics

Motorics is your ability to control your muscular movements, especially when it comes to handling things. Without a Motorics skill, your character's hand-eye coordination is way off and you tremble.

In **Combat**, motorics will help you hit things, either by the finesse of your sword or through your ability to line up bow-shots. Also, it helps you perform claps.

In **Dilemma**, motorics improves your ability to manipulate objects, letting you solve small complex puzzles with ease. 

In **Discourse**, motorics will improve your ability to gesture clearly, it will help you control your minute body movements.

### Motorics 1 - Steady Hands

You can reliably hold, aim, and balance small objects without stumbling. 

### Motorics 2 - Sure Shot

Your precision is impressive; you can throw, catch, and juggle with accuracy.

### Motorics 3 - Dextrous

Locks, mechanisms, and delicate tasks are manipulated easily by your steady grip and articulate fingers.

### Motorics 4 - Masterful

Fiddly devices, rapid aiming, and intricate crafting are easy tasks for your hands.

### Motorics 5 - Surgical

Your hand-eye coordination borders on unnatural. Your limbs and fingers move like precisely grafted gears. Repetitive hand tasks become second nature.

# Mind Skills

Your mind is that part of your brain that remembers and thinks. 

## Academics

Academic skills are those that help you study and become more rigorous in your thinking. Without academics skills, you're gullible. 

In **Combat**, academics will help you learn combat skills as well as useful spells and enchantments.

In **Dilemma**, academics will help you understand the situation faster, using your study methods to gain insight faster. 

In **Discourse**, academics will help you study modern psychological understandings and let you approach conversation in a more well researched way.

### Academics 1 - Student

You can follow structured lessons and grasp the basics of critical study.

### Academics 2 - Scholar

You adopt sound methods of inquiry. Cross referencing, note taking, and systematic problem solving come naturally to you. 

### Academics 3 - Specialist

You can break down arguments, studies, and data into their logical components. You can critique methods and identify strengths and weaknesses in reasoning. 

### Academics 4 - Polymath

You've developed frameworks of thought and can synthesise diverse ideas into coherent systems. Rigorous analysis is your second nature.

### Academics 5 - Sage

You think with masterful clarity. Your approach to study and critical reasoning is flawless. You can devise methods of inquiry others will be inspired by for generations.

## Awareness

Awareness is how easily you understand your surroundings. Without an awareness skill, you easily miss things in the room and struggle to spot things.

In **Combat**, awareness will get you to notice things about your opponent's movements like them preparing to feint.

In **Dilemma**, awareness will let you spot the clues of the puzzle, you'll see both the little picture and the big picture.

In **Discourse**, you can pick up on subtle physical cues in dialogue which may help inform you of your approach.

### Awareness 1 - Observant

You gain a basic awareness of your surroundings, and can spot things obviously apparent. 

### Awareness 2 - Perceptive

You pick up on subtleties. Footsteps, expressions, and small details that would be easy to gloss over.

### Awareness 3 - Insightful

You pick up the smallest of details and features up-close. 

### Awareness 4 - Hawk-Eyed

You notice distant figures, hidden compartments, coded gestures, faint sounds amidst silence. Little escapes your senses.

### Awareness 5 - Oracle

Your perception borders on precognition. You see beyond the viewable, grasping patterns and insights so quickly others suspect a second sight.

## Logic

Logic is your ability to reason and make informed conclusions. Without a logic skill, you fail to make simple conclusions.

In **Combat**, you will be able to apply and form your own strategies, and use your logic to make conclusions about your opponent's fighting style.

In **Dilemma**, your logic will help you take the information you know and connect it to solutions. 

In **Discourse**, you will be able to use rationing and reasoning in a persuasive manner, following good rhetoric in your discourse.

### Logic 1 - Reasoner

You are capable of simple logic and reasoning. You can follow simple arguments, make basic conclusions, and can avoid obvious mistakes in thinking.

### Logic 2 - Analyst

You can break problems down methodically and uncover hidden assumptions. Patterns begin to emerge where others see confusion.

### Logic 3 - Strategist

You construct plans and multi-step deductions with clarity. Your reasoning holds up under pressure and adapts to new data.

### Logic 4 - Rationalist

You resolve contradictions, untangle webs of misinformation, and produce conclusions that are difficult to dispute.

### Logic 5 - Logician

Your reasoning is flawless. You can unravel mysteries, devise elegant solutions, and think with the precision of a perfect machine.

## Memory

Memory is, simply, your capacity to remember things, especially when it comes to aspects of the world, it's history, or other memory-heavy fields of study. Without a memory skill, you struggle to keep information inside your head.

In **Combat**, you will be able to recall important details about weapons, strategies, and martial arts that can inform you on your opponents.

In **Dilemma**, you will be able to recall crucial information throughout the puzzle.

In **Discourse**, you will recall details about your past with a person which may help you decide on your conversational approach.

### Memory 1 - Recollector

You can recall simple facts, places, and faces with ease. 

### Memory 2 - Archivist

You store some details with accuracy: documents, conversations, even passages of text can be remembered.

### Memory 3 - Historian

Your memory becomes a library. Complex information like laws, maps, lore, and entire systems of knowledge can be recalled.

### Memory 4 - Mnemonist 

You can effortlessly retrieve memories others would forget. Exact times, smells, gestures. Your memory is pictographic, you rarely need to write things down.

### Memory 5 - Eidetic

Your memory is practically perfect. You relive details vividly and you recognise connections across your vast amounts of stored knowledge.

# Spirit Skills

Your spirit is the part of your brain responsible for social communication.******

## Charm

Charm is your ability to speak in a way that makes people like you. Without a charm skill, your speech is a little annoying.

In **Combat**, you can use your charm to catch your opponents off guard, or to get them to lower their guards.

In **Dilemma**, your charm will help you with the people involved in the puzzle.

In **Discourse**, you will find it a lot easier to gain people's trust, and people will like you with ease. 

### Charm 1 - Pleasant

You can hold a conversation without offending and your manners are acceptable.

### Charm 2 - Appealing

People warm to you naturally, compliments and persuasion come when you try.

### Charm 3 - Persuasive

You speak with great charisma. Jokes land well, negotiations bend your way, and you can disarm tension with ease. 

### Charm 4 - Enchanting

Your presence is captivating. You inspire trust, attraction, or admiration in nearly everyone you speak to.

### **Charm** 5 - Captivating

Your charms are irresistible. Crowds hang on your words. Enemies soften. Your influence seems supernatural.

## Leadership

Leadership is how easily you can let your presence be heard. Without any leadership skills, your voice is quiet and unheard.

In **Combat**, you can command others and coordinate your fellow party members. 

In **Dilemma**, you will find it easier to take control of a situation, coordinating your party's efforts in solving the puzzle.

In **Discourse**, you will find it easier to talk to groups, you will know how to appeal to the masses and rally others behind your cause. 

### Leadership 1 - Guide

You can give clear instructions to others and they will listen if they want to.

### Leadership 2 - Captain

You inspire confidence in small groups. People look to you in uncertain times.

### Leadership 3 - Commander

You issue orders that rally and organise. In tense situations, you voice cuts through fear and inspires.

### Leadership 4 - General

You command loyaltty and obedience. Large loyal groups heed your call without question, even in the face of danger.

### Leadership 5 - Monarch

Your authority feels absolute. Your presence alone unites others into a single will as if destiny itself favours your command.

## Willpower

Your willpower is how well you take mental stressors; your ability to resist fears. Without any willpower skills, you are frightened at even benign things.

In **Combat**, the fear of death will slowly subside as you gain willpower. You will flinch less, and be ready for aggressive but flawed manoeuvres.

In **Dilemma**, you will get frustrated less easily, and dilemmas that have fear as a primary element will be easier to conquer.

In **Discourse**, your confidence will show itself to others. You can resist personal attacks and stay stalwart in the face of the stressors of conversation.

### Willpower 1 - Steady

You resist basic fears and anxieties. Sudden shocks are no lover overwhelming.

### Willpower 2 - Resolute

You steel your nerves. Intimidation, stress, and pain are diminished.

### Willpower 3 - Unyielding

Your mind is an iron wall. You endure prolonged terror, despair, or manipulation without faltering.

### Willpower 4 - Indomitable

Few things can break you. Your courage inspires others; your determination carries you through hopeless odds.

### Willpower 5 - Iron Soul

Your will is unbreakable. Your fear, compulsion, and despair is tightly controlled. Your mind is a fortress.

## Empathy

Empathy is how well you understand other's thoughts and feelings. Without an empathy skill, you are often confused by other's reasoning and you find it hard to understand people.

In **Combat**, your ability to read people's emotions will help inform you about their intentions, which you can capitalise on.

In **Dilemma**, you can control your emotions in face of difficulties, and in being able to read others you might gain important cues.

In **Discourse**, your empathy helps you relate to others, which will help them trust you. Knowing other's emotions will help you decide on the best way to approach.

### Empathy 1 - Sympathetic

You grasp obvious emotions and can respond with basic kindness.

### Empathy 2 - Understanding

You intuit unspoken feelings and motivations. You read tone and body language with clarity.

### Empathy 3 - Compassionate

You can connect deeply with others, anticipating needs and soothing distress. Your insight makes others trust you.

### Empathy 4 - Intuitive

You sense hidden pain, subtle dishonesty, and concealed intentions. Others open up to you instinctively.

### Empathy 5 - Heartseer

You percieve others as though their emotions were written on their faces. Other's inner worlds are laid bare before you, granting you an almost supernatural insight into the nature of peoples.
