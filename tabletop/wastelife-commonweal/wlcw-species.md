WLCW is a very slill-based game attempting to be realistic and immersive. For that reason, species should be minimal and descriptive, and separate from upbringing. The thing here is that it doesn't really allow for species to do much, which is probably a good thing. 

So, here are one or more bonuses and detriments per Wastelife species-of-person and subspecies.

You're not allowed to pick one for the bonuses. I will be very annoyed if you try.

If I want to balance I can always give some unique damage resistances to the ones who don't have much going for them.

Resistant to a damage type probably just does 1 less damage, and weakness 1 extra.
