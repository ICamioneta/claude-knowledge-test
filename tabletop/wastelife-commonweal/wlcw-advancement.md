In Wastelife Commonweal, advancements are the core unit of progression. They provide abilities and actions. They must be learned in a game session. 

# Classes

Classes in Wastelife provide a clear progression of advancements and focus on certain roles. They aren't exclusive paths: Wastelife Commonweal explicitly promotes mixing paths to create your own "class", something that would be regarded as multiclassing in other systems. 

Wastelife has six classes. 

## Caedyll

Primary Skills: Athletics, Stealth, Awareness, Empathy. 

Weak Arena: Combat

Caedyll are forces against adversity, devoting their variety of skills to defend the innocent. Throughout history, they have fended off the foes of society that threaten settlements. The caedyll prioritises cunning over strength, outmanoeuvring a stronger foe and carefully waiting for openings. 

They are the scout of the group, excelling at reconnaissance and first contact. 

## Fighter

Primary Skills: Athletics, Endurance, Motorics, Leadership. 

Weak Arena: Discourse 

Veteran soldiers, military officers, trained bodyguards, and chivalrous knights. Fighters are masters of weaponry and the body, training since childhood to excel at the martial arts. Their strength and endurance comes in handy in a number of scenarios. 

Fighters are the front line damage dealers, excelling at controlling the battlefield. 

## Jack

Primary Skills: Stealth, Logic, Charm, Empathy. 

Weak Arena: Combat

Missing flavour text. 

Jacks are the utility, steering conversations and being a little bit good at everything the party otherwise lacks entirely.

## Mage

Primary Skills: Motorics, Academics, Logic, Charm. 

Weak Arena: Dilemma 

The mage is the ultimate spellcaster. They are a conglomeration of skill and knowledge, feeling the very essence of the hidol to enhance their powerful claps. They maintain a myriad of powerful and rare ingredients in their satchels. 

They are the burst and artillery of the group, capable of producing high energy reactions if provided with the limited resource of ingredient. 

## Velych

Primary Skills: Endurance, Willpower, Memory, Leadership. 

Weak arena: Dilemma

The Velych seek to tap into the same near-inexhaustible power that makes the Luminaries so powerful. Aligning themselves to domains of freak but gradual change, they seek to bridge the gap between people and nature's power. 

They are the anchor of the group, enduring damage and leading through adversity when they're the last one standing. 

## Wizard

Primary Skills: Awareness, Academics, Memory, Willpower. 

Weak arena: Discourse

Wizards define themselves by their enchantments. Enchanting is power within yourself, and with this power it’s unlikely that a wizard will ever live a life close to normal. They seek knowledge, and wherever they are they can be found improving and expanding their knowledge. 

Wizards are versatile in their enchantments, and can fit a number of party roles from healers, support, or their own hybrid damage dealers. 

# Character levels

Leveling in Wastelife Commonweal is dynamic rather than discrete, but if it were to be compared to D&D levels specifically, every 4 skills acquired represents one level. 

When creating an entry character, you should select four level-1 skills to have, and some extra advancements. 

# Requirements

Advancements have two different types of requirements: prerequisites and training. Prerequisites are other advancements required before this one may be taken to training, and training is a number of study, practice, and trial marks required to finally earn the skill.

Each advancement will describe how many of each training marks it needs, and what those marks look like. For example, studying manuscripts, practising by sparring, then fighting a real opponent.

### Weapon Class: Polearm

Weapon class Polearm applies to Spear, Halberd, Glaive, Poleaxe, and Bardiche

#### Polearm 1

- Prerequisites: none
- Study marks: 1 with manuscript or another Polearm 1 user
- Practice marks: 2 solo training / sparring
- Trial marks: 1 any fight with a real opponent 

Grants proficiency 1 in relevant weapons. 

#### Polearm 2: Microspacer

- Prerequisites: Polearm 1
- Study marks: 2 with manuscript or another Polearm 2 user
- practice marks: 5 solo training / sparring
- Trial marks: 2, each gained by maintaining a real opponent at a polearm's longest reach for two consecutive turns. 

Grants proficiency 2 in relevant weapons. 

Add 2 to your reposition rolls.
