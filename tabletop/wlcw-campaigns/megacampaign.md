What is the goal in Wastelife Commonweal? What is -the campaign- ?

I plan to think of at least 1 large campaign per region, sometimes regional campaigns, and some locational campaigns that at least every city should have. 

I'll list them and my initial ideas, as well as how advanced in the game player might have to be.

# Ereso

The Eresonian campaign is the Vevran Sepratists. Players must pick a side and promote or prevent the further splitting up of Eresonia.

There's also Vania whose campaign involves taking sides in the Estateer vs Labour conflict. It should probably end at Karsir. 

I think Narsyrdel should be the home of a Iavan Thell focussed campaign, maybe just some lake pirates or something. Also to be included is the southern bit of central Ereso.

A Reipo campaign would probably be very cushy and without combat. The Key Collector lives here so maybe there's that.

The Artefact Collector in Eosyrdel is definately a big anchor of Eresonian adventure. 

I want something for Oharaset, Caroset, and Kerosir specifically. Maybe some mystery surrounding Kerosir's big thingy. 

Ursan's thing can be Rhedzin related. Exactly what i'll think of later.

Raikeor could also have a thing. Maybe a border dispute with Bistcran.
