Bracai are mollusk-like in body form, and their body looks as if it is made out of choral. They are very diverse in shape, but often have a thick core and around ten tentacle limbs. They come in many different sizes, from two metres tall to ten. 

They are the luminaries of the rivers. They get their power from the diversity of life from the movement of the water. They are highly predatory and forage a lot. They collect magery ingredients, which they use on mass with unique and complex spells. This is the source of their power. 

There are many in Aerlada, but they are also present in Terlaye.
