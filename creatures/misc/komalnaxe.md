The Komalnaxe are a flying squid-like species that fly high in the air. Their tentacles point downwards, and on their upper bodies sits four tentacles that can be used as arms.

# Origin

These creatures are somewhat original by Daklyn, although their appearance bears a resemblance to an ancient Kithral myth that tells of the ocean creatures that stole clouds from the sky. The name Komalnaxe literally means “Cloud eaters”, and is an original name by Daklyn.

The original myth talked of the Komalkifryd or “cloud takers”. The similarity in name is there, but the myth describes them exactly as they appear in Wastelife. The Komalkifryd are the servants of the sea, and they serve it as their purpose in life. The Komalkifryd know that the sea couldn’t exist without the sky’s clouds, but the sky had been kind and was giving the cloud’s rain to the sea, keeping it existent. The Komalkifryd thought of a scheme to end their reliance on the sky, and so they flew up to the sky and stole the clouds so that the sea didn’t need to rely on the sky for rain.

It is believed that this myth was an explanation of the sky’s reflection in the sea. Kithral would have known that the sea was reflecting the sky, but it still makes for a nice myth.
