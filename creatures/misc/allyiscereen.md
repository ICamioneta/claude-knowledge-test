The Alliyscereen are a species of gargantuan oceanic titans. They are the largest creatures in Wastelife. Their main body is somewhat conical in shape and varies between 40m and 100m in diameter during adulthood. From it, eight groups of tentacles spin away often reaching up to a kilometre away from the body. 

They are the luminary of the ocean, evolving alongside the immense tides of the deep and the forces of oceanic erosion. Their power comes not only from their size, but by their seeming complete control of the ocean surrounding them. 

They primarily feed from hyperactive algae colonies and undersea chemical events. They can also photosynthesise to some extent although they do not appear green due to chromatophores. Allyiscereen are elusive and rarely seen. They are not usually aggressive.

Although there are hundreds of Allyiscereen in the open ocean, the most notable ones live in shallower waters. [Jia, Kraken of the Strait](/wastelife/characters/creatures/jia-kraken-of-the-strait) is an Allyiscereen notable for aggressive behaviour towards large convoys crossing between Barogate and Anula. [Jetty of Lihle Isle](/wastelife/characters/creatures/jetty-of-lihle-isle) is the name of an old and wise Allyiscereen in Aerlada who reportedly acts as a sort of "tree of wisdom" to the people there. On Annwvyn, there are two Allyiscereen who live in Nusant and Colamdezhin, who act in a rivalry towards one another. 

Allyiscereen are known to be born only in the deep ocean. They reportedly live for around 1600 years, fully maturing after 200.
