The Arcan Ddida is a giant crow with a wingspan of around 3m. They are wanderers, and sighting one is rarer than encountering a Lovyn. The Arcan Ddida have their own language and are an intelligent species. When one is sighted, cultures react in different ways. Sometimes, a huge festival is held where the Arcan Ddida individual is invited, and a translator is brought for the Arcan to relay their stories. Other cultures, however, view the Arcan Ddida as a threat, for their own reasons.

The Arcan Ddida are well-known for having incredibly potent alchemical ingredients within them, from their organs to their stone. In most cultures, they are classified as people (hence killing them is murder rather than a hunt or slaughter) however in others, notably most Terlayan cultures, the Arcan are actively hunted for their ingredients.

Their status of non-personhood is often attributed to generally not involving themselves in the people's society.
