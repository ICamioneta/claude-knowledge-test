The Yulaesin is a sister-creature to the [Yulachea](/wastelife/creatures/misc/yulachea), both being almost identical other than the Yulaesin having additional limbs protruding from their back.

Yulaesin are large chitinous creatures with 4 legs. Their bodies are flat and crab-like, with their thick and bulky legs extending out in each direction bludgeoning the ground. On the front of their body sits a large head with a shark-like jaw. Just behind the head, two long and thin limbs protrude with sword-like spikes at their end. These limbs are used for hunting.

Yulaesin are incapable of surviving upside down. This is because their circulatory and digestive system can't handle the orientation. Unlike the Yulachea, some individuals have strong enough back limbs to flip themselves, but this happening is rare.

There is a particular breed of Yulachea present in [Acvidaras](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/acvidaras) with thinner green shells.

For the origin of Yulaesin, see Yulachea.
