The Amdonio is getting a modern re-visit as the luminaries of the desert. They are native to Hebas Deryd and migrated to the rest of Mothnial, Barogate, Annwvyn, and the world. They are secretly social but live so distant from society that they cannot be called people. They are like 5 metres long maybe.

The Amdonio is a stand-out creature among those in Llyhai as it is almost never directly interacted with. These creatures often soar high in the sky. They somehow share an appearance with an elongated manta ray and a leaf. They catch the ash-filled wind drifting purposefully across the sky. Noticeable in their appearance is their bioluminescent vains. This glow often creates intricate patterns of light that shift and change as they move, creating a mesmerising effect. They travel in large packs, making an overhead sight of amdonio a spectacular event.

These creatures are mysterious as they fly too high to reach using any conventional method of flight. Rarely, they fall out of the sky. This is sometimes due to a rock hitting them, another creature killing them, or some unknown cause likely to be disease.
