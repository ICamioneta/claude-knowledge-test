Also called Llofyn, Lovyn, Löfyn, etc.

The Löyn are Wastelife's stand-in for dragons. They are a rare and disastrous species. They are usually territorial with each other, but willing to co-habit with civilisation. 

Their wingspan reaches up to 50m Their wings are like sheets of metal strewn between a frame of pipes. Their antennae like ram horns. Their eyes are shrunken as they did not keep up with the size changes. Their legs are thick, with unique insectoid graspers at the ends. Their mouths they often hide inside, but when feeding it's like a curling spear piercing victims all over. 

Lovyn are mythic beasts, perfectly intelligent and capable of negotiation. Their larvae, however, are on a more animal spectrum. Long, with many legs, these are the "wyrms"of Wastelife.

# Life Cycle

Löyn lay eggs in areas they believe has enough food for their larva. They lay 2-5 eggs sometimes in different places, and often try to guard them. The larvae emerge a month after the eggs are laid.

Löyn larvae are Siani. They are usually large, long, powerful, and hungry. They will eat any and all biomass that they want until they have enough to pupate. Since digestion is a factor, this is at minimum 10 years and at most 60. 

Once a Siani pupates, its pupa is usually picked up by the parent Löyn and taken to a more secure location, such as the Löyn's own den. It will pupate for 50-60 years before an adult Löfyn emerges. 

Adult Löfyn usually find a partner after their first 20 years and before their first 80. They can continue to live for up to 400 years. They need food to survive, and continuously grow.

# Types


## Skippers 

based on Hesperiidae. Small bodies, thick thoraxes. Mottled brown-orange wings. Skippers are solitary, terse, and unbothered by hierarchy. They move in quick bursts favouring hit-and-run tactics.

## Hairstreaks

Based on Lycaenidae. Smaller Löyn, commands swarms of a species of giant ants. They're dipllomatic, transactional. Usually the most willing to broker with civilisation either living within societies or with their own sovereignty. 

## Nymphs

Based on Nymphalidae. The most common Lövyn, and quite varied within. It itself is divided further:

- Monarch
- Leafwing
- Mapper
- Danaid
- Lybith
- Admiral
- Morpho
- True nymph

Some types have the poison breath Lövyn are known for.

## Swallowtails

Based on Papilionidae. The largest Lövyn, they also have the poison breath. They are visually grand, deliberate, and powerful in several ways.


## Allies

Based on Pieridae. They are nomadic and without a solid territory, only staying in the same area to watch their young. They coordinate in numbers and show up in force when they show up at all. 

## Metalmarks 

Based on Riodinidae. They're smaller and jewel toned with spots on their wings looking like genuine metal. They're recluse, like to set up camp by acutal ore deposits. Their bodies are quite valuable for gemstones that form in their bodies.
