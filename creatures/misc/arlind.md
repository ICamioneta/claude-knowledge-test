Arlinds are one of the more well-known creatures in Wastelife. They are large caterpillar-like creatures who are about a storey tall but only ever about 7 metres long. They are short and stubby. Many believe the Arlind are the larval forms of [llofyn](wastelife/creatures/misc/llofyn.md), but this is not true.
