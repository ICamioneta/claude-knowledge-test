The Flutsxa is a weird predator. It has six ant-like legs that come from an otherwise mammalian body, its skin is like sand in the sense that it’s course and leaves damaging marks on the mushroom trees it brushes by. The monster is roughly 3 meters tall when standing regularly. Its mouth is large and takes up a great portion of its face, and its teeth are the size of fingers. There are holes in the centre of its face that it uses to mimic the call of the Nidxa. Above those, there are its eyes, which are buried a fingernail into its head.

# Origin
The Flutsxa as it appears in Wastelife is mostly an original creation, however its likeness is similar to a way of interpreting a myth about a creature that dwells in the rainforest of Chi. The myth describes the creature, missing the several holes in the Flutsxa’s face. In the myth, the creature uses an attractive voice to lure people into its trap. In the myth, the creature was a warning to children not to blindly enter relationships. It is clear to see that part of the myth is not represented by the Flutsxa in Wastelife.
