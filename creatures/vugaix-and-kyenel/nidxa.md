The Nidxa are a dog-like species that are pale white and with skin the texture of fungi. They are dog-like in many ways, except from that they don’t have tails, they have three frog-like toes and their heads are completely different. Instead of the typical canine head, their heads continue from their bodies. They have 3 tentacle-like portrusions where the mouth should be, and like an ant-eater’s mouth they have holes at the end.

# Origin
The Nidxa don’t have much origin in actual Kithral myth other than a myth relating to furless hounds, but that myth shares no other similarities to the Nidxa. In Wastelife lore, Cynnag created the Nidxa to spread mushroom spores to aid in the Everlasting War.
