Genoyg are the large lizards that are very rare, and only really exist to fight the [Kruvan](/wastelife/creatures/vugaix-and-kyenel/kruvan). They are native to the border of [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix) and [Kyenel](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kyenel)
