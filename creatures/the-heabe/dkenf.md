The Dkenf are a group of large bat-like species that are present mostly in the Heabe. The Dkenf are about the size of a person, and are known to prey on Komalnaxe and animal herds. They are mostly present in the Heabe, but they seep into other regions and have a substantial presence in Darie and Dithas. They are known to live in cave systems or dips in the ground, where they build nests out of fabric and carcases.

There’s a related species called the Dkenfaur, which are very large, about half the size of Lovyn.

# Origin
Their inclusion in Wastelife is attributed to a Kithral Chi legend that claims to originate from people who have adventured past the mountains. One of these aural stories likely mentioned large bats, which a story was made from. As a creature, the Dkenf represent a fear of the night.
