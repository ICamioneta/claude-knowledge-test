The Taper Goat is a species of [Caprinae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caprinae) highly diverged from the normal goat population. It's native to the Taper mountain range in western Terlaye and southern Gordol Monre. It is medium-small compared to common goats with a lean, muscular build with elongated limbs and sharpened hooves. Their eyes are at a 45 degree angle to the forward, allowing for some simple binocular vision while not abandoning the common goat appearance. They have sharper canines and premolars playing into their heightened sense of carnivory. While still omnivores, the Taper goat hunts small mammals and birds, and commonly tackles larger pray using pack-like tactics.

In [Terlaye](terlaye.md) myth, the legendary character Nater is believed to have been a Taper goat. The Taper goat is the character species of Terlaye, its unique intelligence giving it a more dog-like role in human companionship. Its unassuming goat-like exterior plays into themes of hidden danger, but the main significance with Terlayan culture is in the Nater legend.
