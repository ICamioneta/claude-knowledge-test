The Dytwels Cat is a type of [cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felis) found in the valleys of [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao). It can also be found in the wild anywhere across Kusao and Cronar in direct competition with the [Usgrous Fox](/wastelife/creatures/cronar-and-kusao/usgrous-fox). It is a fairly standard cat with thin and soft furs. 

The Dytwels Cat is the character animal of Kusao. In Kusaoan culture, the Dytwels cat is kept as any other house cat. Wild cats also enjoy the attention of people in cities and the many rodents they attract.
