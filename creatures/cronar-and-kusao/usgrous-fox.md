The Usgrous Fox is a species of [Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulpes) closely resembling the [Fennec Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fennec_fox). It is native to the deserts of [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar) but has spread to the wetter regions, into Kyenel, and is also found in [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao)'s dry valleys and plateaus. They have a diverse range of breeds for different regions.

The Usgrous Fox is the character animal of Cronar and is a revered species. In Cronar culture, the Usgrous Fox represents the lighter side of its craftmanship and strength focussed culture. It represents playfulness and careful instinctually. It's position as a notable middle predator, praying on smaller creatures while being prayed by birds and larger creatures, serves as a thematic reminder to the people of the culture that the hunters are also always hunted.
