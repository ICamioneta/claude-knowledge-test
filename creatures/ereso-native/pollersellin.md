The Pollersellin is a watery toad that is dangerous, and rare. It is known for sucking water out of things it touches. This might sound especially dangerous, but it usually can’t suck up occupied waters like rivers or lakes. Water it takes cannot feasibly be stored within it, bringing about the belief that the water is stored in some other realm. When killed, all the water it stores leaves its body at a decently high pressure. As this creature can often store way more water than its body volume, it can sometimes expel several metres cubed of water, causing small floods or waves of water.
