The Gunaxeth is a hive-mind of slug-like creatures. These slugs travel together, coming together to form what looks like a mega-slug. When they aren’t formed, the tiny critters coordinate their consumption of leaves and other slug-food. When one is attacked, the others put all their efforts into killing its attacker, even if some slugs perish the lesson taught goes down through generations of slug-eating birds. These slugs are uncommon in developed areas, but can show up from time to time.
