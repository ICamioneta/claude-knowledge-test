The Eresonian Panther is a species of the genus Panthera that is visually similar to a black jaguar, only just below a metre tall. It is a nocturnal hunter.

In [Ereso](ereso.md)'s modern climate of intensive development, the Panther species is not a threat in the majority of Eresonian regions. While a few groups are present in developed areas such as the great plains or Vania, these groups have learnt to co-exist with the local Muz population and are extremely hesitant to attack people. The Eresonian Panther is most common in Daraerula, the Northern Forest, and the Kiu Strip. The stand-out is in Daraerula, which is generally a developed region but due to local culture farmers often intentionally allow the panther to feed on their livestock.

The Eresonain Panther is the character animal of Ereso, symbolising terror in the subtropics. A particularly striking image in Eresonain culture is the Eresonain panther in front of a white Dolomite brick wall. The dolomite has historically represented Eresonain resilience and perseverance. The panther adds symbolism for strength and power.

# Iavan Cat
The Iavan Cat is a domesticated breed of the Eresonian panther which is generally smaller. They are village cats, and primarily hunt rodents around farms and houses, while enjoying companionship with people.
