The Vinguirusod is a bird with a large wingspan. It resembles one from the [Ardei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardei) suborder, and is characterised by the high-frequency small dots that cover its feathers, often dark greyish brown in contrast with its white base. Normally, the Vynguirusod is a normal bird, but when attacked it has a unique defence mechanism. If its skin is split, a bunch of tiny moth-like creatures emerge from its body and swarm the attacker. These moth-like beings are often smaller than 5mm in wingspan, and have sharp wings. The tiny [boids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boids) emerge from a single spot somewhere near the bird’s keel, and not at the scene of the cut.
