The Haerkidokin is a worm-like creature with about a quarter metre diameter. It has lots of small thin legs that it often uses to propel itself. Its skin is a almost transparent offputting yellow and black. It sports a mouth that’s essentially just a slit on its front end. This creatures goes around biting plants, not chewing them but sort of absorbing their nutrients, leaving them with a withered, yellowish part. It isn’t known for attacking creatures, but because it’s a pest people often kill it.
