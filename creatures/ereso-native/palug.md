The Palug is a species of [puma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puma_(genus)) that lives in northern [Ereso](ereso.md), being native to the [[ereso#Ursan Coast|Ursan mountains]]. It is visually similar to a juvenile cougar when mature, and is only slightly larger than the average house cat. They have thin grey fur.

Ursan myth refers to the 'king' of the Palug, a physically larger individual perhaps more visually similar to an adult of the cougar species. This individual is said to live on the top of Ereso's tallest mountain, Rouval.
