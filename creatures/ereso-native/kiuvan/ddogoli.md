The ddogoli is a sort of snake-dog, sharing many physical features of both. It's a scaled creature, often coloured brown, with a snake-like head held low, but a body structure similar to that of a dogs. The mouth still dons the sharp inward-facing teeth that are as long as hands when extended, but also contains several other sharp teeth made for ripping apart flesh and swallowing it without much chewing. Its body is not quite a tube but similar, a ribcage persisting past the chest and down the entire body. The legs are somewhat dog-like in shape, but allow for the body to sit close to the ground while moving, by moving the thigh joint outwards at a horizontal angle to the body. The ddogoli cannot be seen by those it looks at.
