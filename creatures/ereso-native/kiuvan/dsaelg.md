The Dsaelg is a giant centipede that often raises the upper part of its body to a towering 3 metres long. Although in reality it only has the number of body segments regular for a centipede, its illusion makes it appear as if it never ends, wrapping around the landscape. Because of this, it’s quite difficult to tell if a body segment is real, as the exact position of its head is sometimes shifted along its endless illusion.
