The Xzeddrye is a pale humanoid figure with skin. Its arms are slightly longer than a muz’s in proportions, while its legs resemble those of a tall muz. Regardless, the creature sports long and sharp claws at the end of its fingers. Atop its body sits a somewhat ape-like head with a huge gaping and almost always open jaw. The creature is said to uncontrollably blink in and out of existence. When it’s out of existence, it cannot move (partially because it doesn’t exist) so its movement appears jumpy. The blinking is random, and it is said to be in constant pain because of it. But, like, if it was born this way and breeds this way then surely it’d just like a survival mechanism?
