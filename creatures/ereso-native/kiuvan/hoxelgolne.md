The Hoxelgolne is a six-legged deer-like mammal, but its mouth is more similar to that of a spider’s. The Hoxelgolne has the ability to spit a nasty toxin that, if it touches any part of the body, blinds the creature hit. The blindness is painful at first, but the pain eases after a few minutes. The sight stays gone for weeks, and even after it recovers it’s blurry for an extra few weeks before returning to normal. With health potions, the blindness will only last a day or night, and stronger or specialised ones can be rid of it within minutes.
