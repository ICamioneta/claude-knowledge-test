The saxym is an elusive creature. It’s a coyote-like animal with a beak instead of a snout. The creature disappears the moment its seen, only really visible in the corner of your eyes. When its seen, it probably just goes invisible and runs away, although there is no actual observation behind that. It might teleport when seen, or just cease to be in that moment. Saxym are holders of very powerful clap ingredients, but they must be fought blind.
