The Sefare Kite is a species of [Kite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite_(bird) resembling the [Brahminy Kite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahminy_kite) with minimal divergence. Although native to the [[ralean#Sefare Eysenre]] region, the kite is found across Ralean and Terlaye. 

In northern Ralean cultures, particularly Sefars and Eysen, the Sefare Kite is a common animal companion and pet, often forming life-long bonds with their owners. 

The Sefare Kite is the character animal of Ralean. This is in the disappointment of the Ithun culture and other people of [[ralean#Desheg]], where the bird doesn't nest. Despite the potentially striking iconography of a kite, it is taboo to include the bird in any coats of arms or other similar heraldic designs. Instead, designs wishing to use the bird must instead take inspiration from parts of its design such as its colouring. The Wide Diamond is a commonly accepted abstraction of the Sefare Kite, but the abstract shape has outgrown this as a direct thematic connection.
