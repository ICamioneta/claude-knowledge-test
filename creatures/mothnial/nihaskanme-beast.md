A Nihaskanme beast is a giant humanoid with long disproportionate arms. On Barogate, they are present in [Llyhai](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/llyhai) as one of the many beasts making it the most dangerous place to live. They originate from [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd), though, and are quite prevalent in [Asenhal](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/asenhal) too. They say that the beasts walked across the bottom of the ocean to get to Llyhai. The Nihaskanme Beast uses natural ice spells in offence against its prey.
