The Muddaha are a larger mammal-like species of creature that roughly resembles fluffy bison. Usually, they have a fur-like coat draped over a thin inner body. These creatures have adapted to graze on ash, and do so in the protection of small packs. They have a good sense of when they are threatened, so do not often attack others approaching peacefully when they are in packs. When outside of packs, they become extremely aggressive. Their pack sizes are around 7 muddaha strong.
