The Nenzhoh is a small, rodent-like creature that feed on the little plant life that can exist in Llyhai, who are also privy to a munch on flesh. Most of their diet, however, is the ash which they eat and somehow get nutrients from. These little critters are pack monsters, often living in groups of over 20 individuals. Some groups can reach hundreds large, and its every Llyhai colonist’s worst nightmare to be run over by a horde of a thousand of these. They have mouths with two strong teeth capable of crushing rock and also somehow capable of scooping up heaps of ash (which they filter in their mouth)
