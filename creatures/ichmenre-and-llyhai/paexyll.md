The Paexyll is a peculiar creature usually just shy of 2 metres tall that feeds of the heat itself. They have a flexible exoskeleton that’s highly conductive in material, and also contains a number of heat-absorbing structures such as ridged fin-like protrusions that it can extend when feeding, concave plates, hexagonal patterns, and four sword-like arms that double as actual swords. It has a large torso which stand upright, and two thick and sturdy legs. It has a small head that can pivot extremely quickly, and also has a mouth which it can use to ‘eat’ hot things. It moves using all six of its limbs, and although it is a very capable combatant it usually runs away from predators as it is basically a herbivore.

This creature usually rotates between a few heat sources in Llyhai, being extremely present around sources of magma and hot springs. Although they usually flee encounters, they will contest sources of heat ferociously if another creature attempts to claim it. They are not social with others of their species, often performing a sort of ritualistic combat whenever they come face-to-face. Although usually ruthless and ferocious, when up against combatants with four arms wielding four swords they show the same honour as they do when fighting members of their own species.
