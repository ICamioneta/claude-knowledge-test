The Ashforged Behemoth is a massive creature over 10 metres long that somewhat resembles the shape of the ancient megatherium sloths. Its body is encased in a segmented armour plating of pure black, similar to the ankylosaurids. This armour plating is present all around. On the outside it is made of bone, but in areas that need to be compressed such as the belly or parts of the leg it is made of a more flexible chitin-like substance. This armour is covered in structures that act as cooling vents, keeping this behemoth at a reasonable internal temperature. It also has horns on its head, giving it an insane frontal combat ability.

It is by far the most dangerous creature in Llyhai, but it is typically a solitary creature. It feeds on whatever it can, and often slumbers at a slow pace through Llyhai. When in combat or enraged, it is capable of moving at incredible speeds. They are feared and respected by everything including the Lords of Ich Mena Re.
