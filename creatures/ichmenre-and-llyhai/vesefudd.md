The Vesefudd is a three-metre tall and thin creature that somewhat resembles a grasshopper standing upright. Their exoskeleton strongly resembles the black volcanic rocks that are scattered around Llyhai, and when standing still in their idle pose they are almost indistinguishable from those rocks. Their diet consists almost entirely of these rocks, although like most creatures in Llyhai they are perfectly capable of eating other creatures.
