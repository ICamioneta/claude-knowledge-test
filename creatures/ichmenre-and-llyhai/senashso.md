The Senashso is a peculiar slow-moving stream of ash that moves fluidly around the land, carving intricate patterns. These streams are fully sentient and react to external presence. Anything caught in the stream is slowly turned into ash. It is possible to move through the stream and survive, but usually these streams are too deep for people to walk through wit their head above the ash, and the ash breathed once or twice guarantees death.

They can, in some capacity, be commanded by the Lords of Ich Mena Re. They are often used to block or hinder the movement of people as they traverse Llyhai. These streams also make denser the hidol that surround them, like most other streams do.
