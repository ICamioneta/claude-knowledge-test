The Kaddenf is a mostly rock beast bound by a magical fire in a similar way to the Lords of Ich Mena Re. They are bat-shaped, but without any wing-like structures. They have more monstrous heads than bats too. The Kaddenf, when attacking, use the ends of their front legs to pommel and smash things. The tips of their front legs are mace-like, and often have spikes.
