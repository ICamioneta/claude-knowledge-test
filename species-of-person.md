The category of "person" is widely encompassing in Wastelife, It is, essentially, my stand-in for "human", except because this is a fantasy world it becomes very interesting to have a great diversity in the people. 

Many species were created and diverged by the gods. Many naturally evolved and were present in the mortal world before the gods discovered it.

## [Muz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/)

The Muz were created by the original six gods in -10000. They were the first god-made species of person, and have since experienced a unique rapid divergence into a diverse range of people.

- [Lemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/lemuz), native to [Ralean](/wastelife/regions/barogate/ralean), generally better brawn and social ability.
- [Kaemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/kaemuz), native to [Ereso](/wastelife/regions/barogate/ereso), known for their adaptability and intelligence.
- [Danmuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/danmuz), native to [Terlaye](/wastelife/regions/barogate/terlaye), generally better at Hidosis.
- [Ximuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/ximuz), native to [Kyeron](/wastelife/regions/barogate/kyeron), very hairy with articulate tails.
- [Domuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/domuz), native to [Gazi Lale Darie](/wastelife/regions/barogate/gazi-lale-darie), skin like fish-scales and naturally adapted to oceanic swimming.
- [Difmuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/difmuz), native to [the Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate/the-heabe) but have spread all around the Wastes. Known for their adaptability and regional culture.

## [Dos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/)

The Dos were created by [Ymdig](/wastelife/eternals/ymdig), [Medr](/wastelife/eternals/medr), [Cyfapheb](/wastelife/eternals/cyfapheb), and [Adnabyd](/wastelife/eternals/adnabyd) in -10000, around a century after the creation of the Muz. They were originally based on falcons, with an extinct prototype Dos not lasting more than a few millennia. The current selection of Dos were customised from the proto-Dos by Medr, Cyfapheb, and Adnabyd. The Dos were distributed among Anula by the Gods after they populated Barogate with the Muz. The proto-dos were native to [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene).

- [Nebdos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nebdos), native to [Neble](/wastelife/regions/anula/neble), the remaining falcon-like Dos, are known for their combat aptitude.
- [Nidos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nidos), native to [Vezoen](/wastelife/regions/anula/vezoen), made to look more like corvids, and are known for their intelligence.
- [Nudos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nudos), native to Thagryene, made to look more like swallows, and are known for their social ability.

## [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/)

The Gan were created by Ymdig, [Bwydo](/wastelife/eternals/bwydo), [Riw](/wastelife/eternals/riw), Cyfapheb, and [Hell](/wastelife/eternals/hell) in -8400 as a part of Hell's "Eternal orientation". They are amphibian-based, and have received some divergence over the course of history. The first Gan created were bipedal [anaschisma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaschisma), crocodile-like amphibians. It is said that Hell dried their skin in the sun so that they may walk on land. They are native to [Annwvyn](/wastelife/regions/annwvyn)

Modern Gan subspecies were Eternal-altered versions of the original Gan. Worth noting is that the only subspecies of Gan common on Barogate and Anula are the [Coesgan](/wastelife/species-of-peroson/coesgan). They are known as "[Akiiogan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/akiiogan)" in Anula and "[Korongan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/korongan)" in Barogate, but they are the same species.

- [Ce'gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/cegan), the original Gan placed in northern Annwvyn in -8400
- [Ellegan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/ellegan), similar to Ce'gan but diverged by [Maleisus](/wastelife/eternals/maleisus) and [Glinphe](/wastelife/eternals/glinphe) in -7400
- [Deimgan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/deimgan), Nocturnal Gan resembling axolotls, diverged from the Ce'gan by [Lleh](/wastelife/eternals/lleh)
- [Dregan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/dregan), snake-like Gan based on [Caecillans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caecilian). Diverged by [Sirphni](/wastelife/eternals/sirphni) in -7900 and later further diverged by Maleisus
- [Sangan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/sangan), gecko-like Gan diverged by Enw in -7800
- [Coesgan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/coesgan), A frog-like divergence of Gan by Hell, Lleh, [Caesi](/wastelife/eternals/caesi) to diversify the Gan in -5800. They populated much of southern. They were altered by [Cryver](/wastelife/eternals/cryver) in -5400 who increased their natural musculature. There is a lot of genetic diversity within Coesgan.

## [Ens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens)

The Ens are an evolved goat-like species of person native to [Gordolmonre](/wastelife/regions/barogate/gordolmonre) and the Heabe. The Ens population is quite well distributed across Barogate, but there aren't many individuals off the continent. 

- [Colsens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/colsens), generally more intelligent than their other Ens peers, and often seen as on-par with the average person. They are more ready to integrate within societal structures.
- [Nadens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/nadens), the most populous subspecies with most members living stereotypical nomadic Heabe lifestyles.
- [Reyens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/reyens), shorter and lankier mountain-Ens. Closest to the first Ens species. 
- [Popens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/popens), beefier Ens who sprint using their knuckles. Incredibly rare outside of the Heabe.

## [Cut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut)

The Cut are a guinea-pig like species of person native to [Calaeddodd](/wastelife/regions/anula/calaeddodd). They are creations of Riw, Adnabyd, Medr, Cyfapheb, and Bwydo in -8200. They are short and hairless, and usually have shorter stockier limbs than humans.

- [Chōcut](wastelife/species-of-person/cut/chocut), the original Cut, Known for their strength and hardiness.
- [Lescut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/lescut), an incredibly sociable, populous, and widespread Cut. Diverged by Cyfapheb.
- [Cafcut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/cafcut), known for their sociable, comedic, and heroic cultures. Diverged by Cyfapheb.
- [Dancut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/dancut), generally more magically inclined, diverged by Medr.
- [Diacut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/diacut), known for their great engineering works in Brwrd. Diverged by Medr.

## [Ews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews)

The Ews are a whole different thing. The [Ysgews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/ysgews) are the original "Ews", and are an evolved species. The other Ews however are creations of the Ysgews, variations upon themselves created a long time ago using Hidosis. They are all "tree-like" in the sense that their skin is like bark and they often come with tree-like features like random branch-like offshoots to the ends of their limbs. They are not trees, however. They have their own internal structures.

- [Ysgews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/ysgews), large oak-like trees. Incredibly magically and physically powerful.
- [Codews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/codews), humanoid tree people, by far the most populous.
- [Caddews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/caddews), large warrior Ews
- [Calews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/calews), a"revived" Ews warrior unique to [Silpt](/wastelife/factions/anula/silpt)
- [Fruchews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/fruchews), an aggressive and thirsty Ews found in [Asenhal](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/asenhal) and [Varan Del](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/varan-del)

## [Mad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad)

The Mad are an incredibly diverse species of mushroom-like person. The original Mad is an evolved species, but some gods created subspecies. Generally, their skin has a similar texture to mushroom skin. Their mushroom caps might be part of their bodies, or they might just be hats.

- [Buemad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/buemad), the most populous Mad who primarily live in forest clearings in [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate/vugaix). They are the closest to the original Mad.
- [Dimad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/dimad), a distinct subspecies native to [Dithas](/wastelife/regions/barogate/dimad) with slimmer limbs and rounder bellies.
- [Cuemad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/cuemad), a miniature Mad subspecies common in southern Vugaix but found all over. Diverged by Lleh in -6600.
- [Pucmad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/pucmad), grey-skinned "goblin" like Mad, sharing visual similarities to Dimad. Diverged by Maleisus in -3970.
- [Guarmad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/guarmad), 4 meter tall Mad with trunk-like limbs. Diverged by Cynnag in -6500.

## [Fall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall)

The Fall are an evolved species of reptilian person. Usually, different subspecies resemble wildly different reptiles, but they are all the same species. In general, Fall have thick skin. They have no collective native region.

- [Matafall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/matafall), the most populous Fall resembling geckos, native to [Duela](/wastelife/regions/barogate/duela).
- [Rutefall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/rutefall), lizard-like fall most closely resembling komodo dragons, native to Duela.
- [Laurafall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/laurafall), an iguanid-like fall species common in and native to Kyeron.
- [Tudofall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/tudofall), a tortoise-like species in low population, native to both Duela and Dithas.

There are more subspecies of Fall in other continents. I might add another few to Barogate. This is all there is for now. 

## [Phax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax)

The Phax are my beloved scorpion people. They have a mixture of chitinous hard spots and soft spots, and are native to [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate/kusao). They are an evolved people.

- [Sosophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/sosophax), the most populous and regular phax whose population far exceeds the other Phax combined.
- [Gofophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/gofophax), a subterranean subspecies known for colourful colouration, their affinity for climbing. They are generally more slender and their tails prehensile.
- [Tolusphax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/tolusphax), a larger and more physically capable Phax known to live a semi-nomadic lifestyle on Kusao's canyon plateaus.
- [Allophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/allophax), a large and monstrous Phax variation, still people.

## [Volcanic](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic)

Volcanics is a group of species of person who are confined to volcanic communities. There are the [todd](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/todd), [llysag](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/llysag), and the [Fridnyrm](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/fridnyrm).

## [Çin](/wastelife/species-of-person/cin)

The Çin, or Kuçin, are a species of cat person present across Annwvyn and Mothdao. They originated in Kuçin in Annwvyn, and certain subspecies were transported to Mothdao after divergence. 

- [Telikuçin](/wastelife/species-of-person/telikucin), inhabitants of [Kuçin](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/kucin).
- [Shinkuçin](/wastelife/species-of-person/cin/shinkucin), inhabitants of [Asenhal](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/asenhal) and [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd).
- [Kahrçin](/wastelife/species-of-person/cin/kahrcin), inhabitants of [Kahklin](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/kahklin).

# Species without subspecies
Many species of person do not have any subspecies distinction.

## [Lindi](/wastelife/species-of-person/other/lindi)
Caterpillar like species of person living in little holes inspired by the Skara Brae site. They are short and known for their honour. Lindi were created by members of the Techia institute in an experiment that I haven't detailed, but likely involves the gods.

## [Calapeth](/wastelife/species-of-person/other/calapeth)
Shapeshifters with a shadowy natural form. They are often violently excluded from society in fear of their capabilities. The Calapeth were created by Lleh and Arhos in -4480 and later altered by Maleisus and Gwrphcim.
