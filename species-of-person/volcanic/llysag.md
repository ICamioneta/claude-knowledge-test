The Llysag are a species of [Volcanic](volcanic.md) person who exclusively live in volcanic communities. Generally, they are all made of fires of varying degrees of heat. This ranges from 
- [Perllysag](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/perllysag), the lowest temperature Llysag most commonly doing jobs outside the communities
- [Colcethllysag](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/colcethllysag), medium-temperature beings that are most commonly artisans and hard labourers.
- [Nuillysag](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/nuillysag), high-temperature beings that perform all manners of jobs.
- [Llethllysag](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/llethllysag), the highest temperature of Llysag performing mostly artisan and mantle diving jobs.
