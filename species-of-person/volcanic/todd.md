The Todd are a species of magma and lava-like creatures.

- [Machtodd](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/machtodd), medium-temperature creatures who do all types of jobs
- [Volltodd](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/volltodd), high temperature creatures that do all types of jobs
- [Bartodd](/wastelife/species-of-person/volcanic/bartodd), the highest temperature creature whose member individuals frequently work in mantle-diving.
