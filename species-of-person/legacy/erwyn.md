The Erwyn are a species of person that are based upon rats. They are an outdated species of person who are no longer really included in Wastelife due to their copy-paste nature from standard fantasy tropes. The role that they fill, being underground dwellers, hasn't really been fulfilled by another Wastelife species, but I wonder if such a role needs to be filled.
