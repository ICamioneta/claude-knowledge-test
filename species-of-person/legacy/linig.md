Linig are a legacy species and are not often considered in the world, as their role as silly lil guys living outside of society has largely been usurped by the much more original [Lindi](/wastelife/species-of-person/other/lindi)
# Gorlinig
The Gorlinig are a small humanoid species of person who are often grey skinned, have sharp facial features, and are quite human-like. The term "Gorling" can refer to any Linig subspecies.

Gorlinig are a potentially outdated species of person. They were originally a direct port of a friendly goblin species from common fantasy, but any role this species had was replaced by the much more unique Lindi.

# Oflinig
A small humanoid species known to be quite hostile, with large stomachs and twig-like limbs, and large mouths. They are another legacy creature that were stand-ins for a more aggressive fantasy goblin stereotype. Much like the Gorlinig, I have since created species of person who are infinitely more unique and are perfectly capable of filing this role. The Pucmad, specifically, fulfil the aesthetic role of the Oflinig and I see no reason why the Pucmad wouldn't get around and be a nuisance everywhere.
