The Ews are a set of tree-peope species most of which originate from the Great Forest of [Codia](/wastelife/factions/anula/codia). The Ews are also present in many other large forests across the world. On Barogate, they are present in [Ereso](ereso.md)'s northern forest under [Raikeor](/wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/raikeor), and in [Ralean](ralean.md)'s southern forest under [Eswin](/wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eswin). They are also present in smaller forests in [Vezoen](/wastelife/regions/anula/vezoen) and [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene).

- [Ysgews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/ysgews), the elders and centres of Ews society
- [Codews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/codews), the regular muz-sized and muz-shaped Ews
- [Caddews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/caddews), the large warriors and sworn protectors and vanguard of the Ysgews.
- [Calews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/calews), a revived Codews present in [[Codia#Silpt|Silpt]]
- [Fruchews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/fruchews), a non-person and highly aggressive type of Ews on Mothdao.

Ews societies are usually founded around a subspecies called the [Ysgews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/ysgews). These Ews often form the centre for villages of other Ews, usually [Codews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/codews) and [Caddews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/caddews). There are some variants of Ews society, namely in Codia, that operate on different social hierarchies. The Silpt sub faction of Codia has [Calews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/calews) and a bunch more in addition to the typical Ews in a society, althogh the regular three species of Ews when part of Silpt have a blue tint and have their own thing going on. 

For an overview of Silpt's specific system of reviving and incarnating Ews and the species or archetypes that come from them, look in the [Calews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/calews) document for a proper disection, as I'm not going to create six different new documents for each of the incarnated Ews types.

[[Codia#Shurb|Shurb]] societies are very differently structured and often don't have Codews or Caddews. Instead, all the Ysgews of Shurb operate on a direct hivemind (compared to the regular message linkage that Ysgews have when rooted).

# History

## Creation Era
Like the majority of well established species of person, the Ews came about during the Era of Creation, first being found by an Esten of Adnabyd in the forest now known as Codia. This Ews was different and its exact type is said to have diversified into the modern Ews. It was alleged to be a large bipedal tree like person.

During the creation era, the vast majority of the liveable land was trees. Although Codia has always had the connection to the torfol of trees, this wasn't thoroughly understood until more of the land became not trees. During this era, the Ews were like any other species of person.

## Community Era
In the second era, the Era of Community, people in general started banding together. This includes in some part the Ews, however their larger stature made them fit in less with Muz and Dos society. 

During the Community Era, large sections of land were cut down to make way for agriculture. The Ews' connection to the forest only really began once the liveable land started not containing trees. Throughout the community era, Ews were slowly banding together and forming their own forest-friendly village communities.

## Civilisation Era
With the birth of civilisation, organised farming, animal husbandry, and iron, the Ews found their communities threatened by organised bodies of armed men partaking in civilisations fuelled by farmland. Ews civilisations were formed around these in order to mediate the relationships between the forest and the civilisation. In general, Ews communities accepted forestry and promoted the growth of civilisation alongside the forest. This lacklustre relationship continued throughout the civilisation era.

## Empire Era
The fourth era, characterised by the unification of Shildo under Techia, saw entirely new challenges face the Ews. By now, the idea of an Ews civilisation of interconnected Ysgews villages with Codews workers was established. Ysgews had figured out how to establish subterranean root-based communication between them: [[Ysgews#The Wire]]. It was at the start of this era that the wire network was highly established across the two continents, leading to the well-assumed height of the Ews.

But right at its height, Iron began to popularise and a second wave of deforestation went across the two continents, shattering wire connections. Many civilisations broke their preestablished forestry agreements with native Ews communities, however all of the cool empires were disrespecting this and establishing full control of many forests. 

During this era, the continental wire network was being shattered and Ews started to clump together in certain areas.

Losing the continental wire network, the separated Ews communities had to make decisions on how they would face their new situation.

Many Ews communities would face genocide: a targeted removal of their power. The most notable genocides were in Darie, Plaen, Vezoen, and Terlaye. In Darie's case, the completely disconnected Ews community didn't stand a chance and now only a few Ysgews live inland. In Plaen, the forest was entirely demolished and regrown, exterminating the Ews there and establishing the position of the modern northern borders with Codia. In Vezoen, the march of industry and township drove a need for more wood, and the civilisations there deemed the Yws a direct threat, leaving only a few remaining in eastern communities. In Terlaye, the land became fully developed leaving no wild woods for the Ews to inhabit, and the few that remain struggle with the forestry industry to base their power.

However in other places the Ews persist under two main forms of relationship: respected inhabitants of another faction or as their own national power.
- **Raikeor** is a community of Ews in northern Ereso who had been relegated there by major deforestation at the hands of the Dolomite Empire. After having their own 'kingdom' formed during the Eresonain Kingdom era, they pledged allegiance to the Eresonian Empire to join in a war and gained significant autonomy, and now act within Eresonain borders as their own organisation having close ties to some of the towns in their forest.
- **Eswin** is a community in the southern forest Desheg. Following the deforestation of Senolado, most Ralean Ews fled to this forest and protected it vehemently against the dynasties, eventually culminating in the common local agreements it survives under to this day.
- **Disconnected Eysenre** Having never really been heavily forested, the Eysenre valley Ews exist in small population only having experienced a short period of wire connectivity during the Ews height.
- **Codia** remains the most vast and powerful Ews faction, commanding and defending its own borders against expansion, involving several emergent factions like Silpt and Shurb.
- **Eastern Vezoen community** exists

## Future Threats
To the Ews around the world, it feels like there is another major deforestation. Already in a weakened state, the relationship between the Ews and local powers is likely to change just as much as it did during the iron deforestations. Many places are getting super involved in capitalism and industrialisation, the consolidation of the human population into towns and an obsession over the efficient use of land. Ews' mastery of Hidosis meaning their mastery over war is already beign challenged by the SPCC and other industrial inventions. The Ews are in a "make it or break it" cusp of change.
