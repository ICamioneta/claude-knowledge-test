The Reyens are a subspecies of [Ens](ens.md) that are native to the mountainous areas of western Barogate. They are significantly thinner than the other Ens, and their horns do not protrude but rather serve as some sort of hard hat. Their fur is grey, and their hands are very capable in terms of grabbing. Their arms have amazing strength.
