The Popens are an [Ens](ens.md) subspecies usually seen nomadically scouring the upper parts of the Heabe, hunting and gathering and tenting. They have brown and thick fur. Compared to Nadens, their legs are often beefier, and they sprint using all four limbs. Their hands are closer to that of hooves, but they are still perfectly capable of tool using and holding things.
