Colsens are a subspecies of [Ens](ens.md) that are often integrated into societies outside of [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/the-heabe). They are similar to the Nadens, but are often seen as more intelligent and with smaller horns. The Colsens fit well within Muz societies and have became a common sight across Barogate everywhere but the Heabe.
