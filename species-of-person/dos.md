The Dos are the bird-like people of Anula. The Dos were designed by four of the original gods, with three of the four gods creating their own subspecies of Dos. These gods were Ymdig, who did not create their own subspecies, Medr, Adnabyd, and Cyfapheb. 

Medr made the [Nebdos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nebdos)
Adnabyd made the [Nidos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nidos)
Cyfapheb made the [Nudos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nudos)
