The Phax is a species of person native to the [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao) region. They infrequently leave it, so a sight of Phax outside of Kusao is very rare. The Phax often form social and egalitarian societies. 
The Phax are a scorpion-like species of person with armour-like exoskeletons, claw-like thumbs, and large tails. They communicate primarily using hormonal signals, but are capable of verbal speech.

# Subspecies
- [Sosophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/sosophax), the regular baseline for all Phax
- [Tolusphax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/tolusphax), a more naturally dangerous Phax
- [Allophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/allophax), a large and monster-like Phax
- [Gofophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/gofophax), a thin and colourful Phax with a prehensile tail
