The Deimgan are a [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/index) diverged from the[[ Ce'gan]] by [Lleh](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/lleh) in 1100. They are nocturnal and resemble axolotls. They are native to Taggrun, but are present in low population across [Annwvyn](wastelife/regions/Annwvyn.md). Having only existed for a little over two thousand years, they are in very low population. Many of their communities are well protected by location, they are well suited for subterranean waters.
