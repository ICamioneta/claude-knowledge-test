The Ellegan are a subspecies of [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/index) diverged from the [Ce'gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/cegan) in -7400 by [Maleisus](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/maleisus) and [Glinphe](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/glinphe). They are very similar to their Ce'gan relatives, resembling Anaschisma. Their features are much more similar to crocodiles, with hardened scales and large teeth. 

They are present across all of [Annwvyn](wastelife/regions/Annwvyn.md) and are not a majority in any region. However, they are easier to find in Daerah and Kerré.
