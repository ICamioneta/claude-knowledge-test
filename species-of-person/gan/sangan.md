Gecko-like [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/index) peoples who diverged from the [Ce'gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/cegan) by [Enw](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/enw) in -7800.

They are common across [Annwvyn](wastelife/regions/Annwvyn.md)'s Taggrun, the Tar desert, and Kuçin. They are also very present in Nusant and Kerré.
