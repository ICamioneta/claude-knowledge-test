A subspecies of [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/index) known for being the original Gan. They are based on Anaschisma, crocodile-like amphibians

The Ce'gan are an average of 2.4 meters tall. They appear somewhat slouched, and are well balanced by large tails. 

They are most common in [Annwvyn](wastelife/regions/Annwvyn.md)'s regions of Utaire, Colamdezhin, and Kerre. They are in high population across all of Annwvyn.
