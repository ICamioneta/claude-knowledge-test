Coesgan are a subspecies of [Gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/index) diverged from the [Ce'gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/cegan) by [Cryver](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/cryver) in -5400. Adnabyd was intrigued to see if an ascended god was equal in power to a natural god, and encouraged Cryver to make the divergence. 

![Coesgan.png](/wastelife/images/drawings/coesgan.png)

The Coesgan are similar to toads or large frogs. They are short and squat in build, and are extrodinarily strong thanks to a high muscle capacity. The muscles are not all natural, Coesgan must train to reach muscle masses considered normal. 

They are common across Daerah.

# Continental Divergence
In 100, there was a massive migration of Coesgan from southern [Annwvyn](wastelife/regions/Annwvyn.md) to [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene) and [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar), both wildly different climates to the rainforest they had previously adapted to. Coesgan are notoriously stubborn, so they did not move further to find the more suitable climates in these continents. 

Over time, these Coesgan experienced a massive cultural divergence to each other, as well as limited local adaptions. More pertinently, they received local stigmas related to their circumstance.

## Akiiogan
The Akiiogan are the Coesgan who landed in Thagryene in Anula. They integrated peacefully into the pre-existing Dos and Cut communities. They are also somewhat notorious for having some members utilise their short stature for stealth. For these reasons, the Akiiogan are considered diplomatic, cunning, and sneaky.

## Korongan
The Korongan are the Coesgan who landed in Cronar on Barogate. Due to their migratory leadership, the Korongan drove the sparse natives of Cronar out and claimed the land well for themselves. Since, Cronar has been the birthplace of large and capable empires, breaking the natural boundaries of its expansion with sheer will of force. 

For this reason, the Korongan are known for their resilience, strength, and autocracy. They represent a more authoritarian side to strength that Cryver often denies is part of his domain.
