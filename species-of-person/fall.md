The Fall are the reptile-like inhabitants of [Duela](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/duela). They are territorial, and do not take kindly to people in their lands. They are known to be naturally impulsive, consistently responding to certain stimuli with predictable behaviours. These behaviours can be resisted by resolute individuals. 

- [Rutefall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/rutefall), a lizard-like Fall that resemble komodo dragons and other similar lizards. They have sharp claws, large mouths.
- [Tudofall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/tudofall), a tortoise-like Fall with long lifespans and chitin armour on their fronts and backs.
- [Matafall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/matafall), a gecko-like Fall who are agile and stride with a strange posture.
- [Laurafall](/wastelife/species-of-person/fall/laurafall), an iguanid-like Fall who are native to Kyeron
