The Cuemad are a small species of [Mad](mad.md) native to [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix) and [Dithas](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/dithas). They often live on the edges of these regions, such as Vugaix's border with [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/the-heabe) and [Ralean](ralean.md). They have a slender body figure. They are usually about the height of a human forearm. They are very visually diverse: they are all colours of the rainbow and have wild variations of their cap, although the cap is usually spherical and large. They live in small communities of 10-20 individuals, but often also live alone.

They are well known for their affiliation with [Hidosis](/wastelife/hidol/hidosis). Their skin has a rare property where it reacts with itself, meaning the Cuemad can produce their own one-material clap. Their cultures often revere the procurement of ingredients for use in many ways, including claps.
