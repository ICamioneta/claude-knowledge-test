The Guarmad are a gargantuan subspecies of [Mad](mad.md) native to inner [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix). They fit in with the mushroom forests, and when standing still they are easy to mistake for just another mushroom tree. They are about four metres tall. They have tube-like bodies with large graspers for hands. They are not very social, but found miniature nomad communities of about three to six individuals.
