The Dimad are a displaced species of [Mad](mad.md) who once mostly lived on the coasts of [Dithas](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/dithas), but since heavy [[Domuz Republic#Colonies|Domuz colonisation]], they have been displaced from their original homes and moved to dedicated areas, or integrated into the colonies, or further inland. They are a small species of Mad, only being about the height of a dinnerplate. They stand upright, have skinny arms and legs, tall but round bellies, and thin chests. They often build their houses in the ground.
