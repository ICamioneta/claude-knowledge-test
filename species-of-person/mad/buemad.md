The Buemad are a subspecies of [Mad](mad.md) that are generally known as the most regular Mad, despite sharing little similarities to any other Mad apart from the body plan of Dimad and the height of the Pucmad. They are native to [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix) and live in small town communities carved out of the forest. They have very tube-like limbs and normal bodily proportions.
