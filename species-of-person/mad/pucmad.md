The Pucmad are a subspecies of [Mad](mad.md). Their faces are hard to look at, their front lip extends out and their eyes and noses have sunken into their face. They do not have much of a forehead, and are generally seen as less intelligent. They are about 1.5 metres tall. They live in straw huts in small communities across the western Wastes, including parts of [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/the-heabe) and [Gazi Lale Darie](gazi-lale-darie.md). They are also known to form small communities essentially anywhere.
