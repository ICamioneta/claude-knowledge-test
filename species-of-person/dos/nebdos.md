The Nebdos are a [Dos](dos.md) subspecies from [Neble](/wastelife/regions/anula/neble) that resemble falcons. Being designed by Medr, they are seen as a skilfully gifted species. This presumed gift of skill is used in artisanry and war, which are both popular in cultures cultivated by the Nebdos. The Nebdos war culture is reflected in the two main factions that are on Neble and of a Nebdos majority. The [Empire of Plaen](/wastelife/factions/anula/empire-of-plaen) are well known for their aggressiveness and warmonger culture, and the [Western Neble Imperia](/wastelife/factions/anula/western-neble-imperia) are known for their aggressive defence military model in opposition to [Codia](/wastelife/factions/anula/codia).
