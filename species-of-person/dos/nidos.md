The Nidos are a subspecies of [Dos](dos.md) designed by [Adnabyd](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/adnabyd). They are seen to be gifted in their intelligence and wise. Their culture highly regards education and scholarly research. The Nidos are often skilled craftsmen and pioneers in culture and technology. Literacy in [Edris](/wastelife/factions/anula/edris), the native home of the Nidos, is higher than anywhere else by far.
