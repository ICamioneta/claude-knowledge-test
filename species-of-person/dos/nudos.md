The Nudos are a subspecies of [Dos](dos.md) designed by the god Cyfapheb. Being the god of communication, the Nudos are often seen as great communicators. This is reflected in their societies, which strongly favour cooperation, diplomacy, and negotiation. The largest faction on the two continents is on the Nudos native homeland of [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene), the Union of Thagryene Countries. While the other Dos don't really have wing structures, the Nudos have enough of a structure to reliably glide while falling.
