The Calapeth are an incredibly rare species of person living in Wastelife. In their natural form, they are shadowy beings with human-like proportions. The number of limbs they have is important to their shapeshifting abilities, but almost always they have four limbs and a head. 
# Shapeshifting
The Calapeth are shapeshifters. Shapeshifting for a calapeth is a skill, most Calapeth are capable of imitating another species' appearance when looking at them, this appearance would then be a random person, or a person who does not specifically resemble any other. Calapeth who have trained in this skill become capable of shapeshifting without a reference, and potentially able to mimic others later down the line. More skilled Calapeth become able to shapeshift into non-people.

The singular caveat of the Calapeth shapeshift is that when shapeshifted, their skin or fur when in direct contact to sunlight slowly purples. For this reason, Calapeth often wear heavy clothing while shapeshifted.

# Formless
The Calapeth's presence in the mortal realm is limited. While calapeth in their normal form and while shapeshifted are capable of grasping things, heavier or more dense objects tend to pass straight through them. Older calapeth who have accumulated skills are capable of grasping anything without issue.

It is also an ability of the Calapeth to turn into a completely formless mist. When they are this mist, they do not have any sensory capabilities apart from a senseless perception of that which happens in or around the mist that they are. Calapeth are completely unable to interact with anything in this form
