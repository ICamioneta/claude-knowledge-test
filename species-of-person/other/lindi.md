The Lindi are the humanoid caterpillar-like species. Although it’s unbelievable, it seems that the Lindi only appeared recently on the planet. There are no accounts of the Lindi before 1500. The Lindi tend to live in windy areas, which are usually hilly or near-mountainous.

Their culture and dwellings are based on the real-world neolithic site [Skara Brae](https://cy.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skara_Brae)
