The Gofophax are a subterranean subspecies of [Phax](phax.md). They are equally as sociable as the [Sosophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/sosophax), but their species has adapted to terrain traversal in [[Kusao#Underground Caverns|Kusao's large underground caverns]]. 

They are great climbers, they have hands better suited for the occasion, being more claw-like. Their legs are quite similar to the hands for the extra climb ability. Their tails also end in a claw appendage that helps them climb, and can be used as if it were an arm. Instead of the usual drab colour of the Phax, the Gofophax come in a wide range of colours, even sometimes being bioluminescent.
