The Tolusphax are a subspecies of [Phax](phax.md). They are visually similar to Sosophax, but their claws more closely resemble claws than hands, and their tail poison is significantly more potent. They are often easily distinguished from Sosophax by their usually bulkier stature. Their colour is often tan, compared to the standard Phax brown.

The Tolusphax are frequent inhabitants of the river valleys of [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao), but they more frequently inhabit the plateaus and they travel much more often.
