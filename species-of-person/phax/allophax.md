The Allophax are a subspecies of [Phax](phax.md) that are more monstrous in appearance than Sosophax. They are significantly larger and have claws instead of hands. Their posture is somewhat similar to a gorilla. They can live in river valley communities in [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao), but they more frequently live a nomadic hunting lifestyle in Kusao and [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar).
