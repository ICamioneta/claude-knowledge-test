![Ysgews.png](/wastelife/images/drawings/ysgews.png)The Ysgews are a species of [Ews](ews.md) and are generally regarded to be one of the most powerful creatures in the mortal realm. They have the bark-like skin typical of Ews, but their form looks incredibly similar to regular trees in that they have a trunk, roots, and branches. They have two massive branches like horns from their heads, which then split off in a distinctly tree-like fassion. They are no larger than a typical oak tree or other tree type found in areas they inhabit. 

The Ysgews are incredibly intelligent, capable of knowing tens of thousands of people and processing vast amounts of information in depth and well.

The Ysgews have two main states: rooted and uprooted. When rooted, they are able to use the torfol of plant life in the forest. They use this potential to perform powerful [Hidosis](/wastelife/hidol/hidosis). When in this state, if placed in a sufficiently large forest such as [Raikeor](/wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/raikeor) or [Eswin](/wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eswin), they are amongst the most powerful hidists in the mortal realm. When rooted in a massive forest such as [Codia](/wastelife/factions/anula/codia), they are the most powerful hidists in reality. 

When uprooted, they loose their ability to use the torfol of the forest but still have their own incredibly powerful torfol, which they are capable of bolstering before uprooting. They also gain incredible speed and strength, using their root-like apendages to scuttle rapidly across difficult terrain. In combat they use their root-like appendages as whips, used to bludgeon, slash, and rip their way through any opponent. Ysgews will spend the vast majority of their lives rooted, only uprooting when moving is absolutely necessary or if they are required to join an offense. 

They often form the centres of Ews societies, having [Codews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/codews) living spaces built around them in a circular fashion. They act the role of a wise elder. They also communicate with other Ysgews, therefore other villages, using [[#The Wire]].

An Ysgews is rarely without [Caddews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/caddews) nearby. the Caddews are the sworn protectors of the Ysgews, and are grown solely for this purpose in life. Within villages, they act as a sort of royal guard.

An Ysgews in [[Codia#Silpt|Silpt]] is a Letarch. They are capable of incarnating and reviving Ews using some spirit of a fallen person.

## Life Cycle
Ysgews are born like any other Ews, from a seed-like egg implanted in the ground. This seed-like egg comes from other Ysgews. This is often an asexual reproduction, however Ysgews can transfer gametes between eggs. These eggs need to be planted by old fauna with developed Torfol. Ysgews will grow completely rooted until near-maturity, where the last few stages of maturity necessitate moving while uprooted. 

After a few months, a child Ysgews growth must be connected through The Wire to a fully mature Ysgews. There is a link between the speed of maturity and the size of the Wire network an Ysgews is connected to, attributed to the child receiving vast amounts of information through the wire to fast-track the wise stages of life. 

Ysgews take 50 years to fully mature. During their childhood they are near completely dormant, communicating with other Ysgews through The Wire. Outwardly, they will blend very well with surrounding trees. Ysgews will "wake up" from this dormancy if they are threatened, using Hidosis to defend themselves until a threat is no longer felt. Their ability to defend themselves relies on the torfol strength of their surrounding trees and their connection to The Wire network.

Around 50 years of age, the Ysgews will uproot from the location of their growth and go on a mission to find a place to found a new Ews village. Once they have found a location they are happy with, they will re-root and plant some new citizens, often borrowing mature citizens from other villages to help with the raising of their own.

Ysgews will continue to grow in size slowly, sometimes peaking in size and sometimes getting smaller. Ysgews have a gargantuanly long lifespan, commonly living until 600 years of age but frequently surpassing onwards to 1000. This is often attributed to a highly developed and capable immune system. 

Ysgews memories are perfectly capable of remembering every one of these years from maturity. The oldest Ysgews around will remember events as old as the Dictatorship of the Dead and the Emperor of Terlaye, or the first Eresonian Empire. Ews societies throughout history have scarcely written anything down, instead relaying important history through stories to younger Ysgews, which they must remember amongst the vastness of their own history.

Ysgews draw energy from the torfol of flora around them, uniquely requiring the more powerful torfol of trees to sustain themselves properly. They are incapable of surviving rooted without at least a few trees around, and can survive for about 8 days uprooted without using their own torfol for hidosis.

There is a disparity between the lifespan of an Ysgews and the other Ews who live in their village. Assuming their village grows to the near-maximum size of around 10000, they will live through 60000-1000000 lives of their villagers. The social capacity of a typical Ysgews is around 20000 people. They are often required to be familiar with everyone in their village and every other Ysgews on their Wire network, and this capacity is plenty on most wire networks.

# The Wire
The Wire describes a crucial piece of infrastructure to Ews society. Essentially, lanes of information are established between unbroken lines of trees. Electrical signals are sent through these routes and relayed to other Ysgews connected. Ews have to prepare trees as wire lanes, and wire lanes deteriorate slowly so require some maintenance. The Wire is near instant and universal, the vast majority of what the Ysgews do is relay information on The Wire passing it on to the Ysgews of intended destination. 

Wire networks are essentially limitless in size. There is a limit in the ability for an individual Ysgews to process and relay information from and to The Wire, this only limits traffic through certain points of The Wire, especially choke points. Wire networks are often intelligently designed so that information sent across is reasonably distributed to each Ysgews, often with bypass wires if there is too much information heading to a certain Ysgews.

The ability for the Ews people to form nations entirely hinges on the connectivity of wire networks. Different Ews societies are defined by their severance from wires.

## Wire Networks
There are three major wire networks on the two continents, each making up the three Ews factions. Eswin has around 600 Ysgews. Raikeor has around 400. Codia, including Silpt and Shurb, has 1000 Ysgews. The first ever Wire network, the largest by far established before the first major deforestations, connected 3000 Ysgews across Barogate and Anula.
