The Caddews are a large, 2.5 metre tall Ews species often regarded as the protectors of [Ysgews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/ysgews).  They are often grown alongside specially cultivated plants that will form the weaponry the Caddews will adorn during adulthood. The Caddews have incredible physical prowess, as well as pronounced horns.

In Ews society, they take a solely protective role respecting the authority of an Ysgews. They are not inherently tied to an Ysgews like any species of person, but their societal role defines them more so than other subspecies of Ews

Like most Ews, Caddews create seed-like eggs that are planted and eventually grow into Caddews. Ysgews are also capable of creating these eggs.
