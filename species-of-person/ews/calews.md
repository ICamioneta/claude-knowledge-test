This document not only covers Calews, but also incarnated Ews under the [[Codia#Silpt|Silpt]] model.

In Silpt, [Ysgews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/ysgews) are different in that they have a blue glow to them. These Ysgews are known as Letarches. Letarches have the unexplained ability to revive dead [Codews](/wastelife/species-of-person/ews/codews) and create new [Ews](ews.md) species. This ability occurs shortly after a non-Ews creature dies near the Letarch. Theories speculate that Letarches are capable of "capturing" and using the [[Hidosis#Torfol]] of recently deceased individuals and placing them into the corpses of deceased Ews and into special nearby tree species to create Calews and Lethews.

A Calews is a revived Codews. It is uniquely different to a regular Codews in that it doesn't retain any of the memories, and instead assumes a new identity. They are also no longer capable of reproduction, as no Ews created by Letarches are.

# Lethews

A Lethews is an incarnated Ews created by a Lethews out of a special species of tree common in Silpt land. There are several different types of Lethews. The conditions which cause certain Lethews to be created are not understood, but those who have asked the Silpt Lethews have received the answer that it depends on the recently deceased's 'emotional state'. These variants come with a species name and a common, ghostly, name.

## Lethews (Wraith)

This incarnated Ews is said to be made if the recently deceased had a particularly powerful Torfol, or if they died in an angry emotional state. Lethews are both physically powerful and adept in [Hidosis](/wastelife/hidol/hidosis).

## Fonisews (Banshee)

A Fonisews is said to be made when the recently deceased died in fear. Fonisews are known for being powerful Hidosis users, and for having horrific screams.

## Lefews (Phantom)

A Lefews is said to be made when the recently deceased died in sadness. They are known for the ability to pass through objects and people, and appear semi-transparent whilst doing so. They are also fast, which is bolstered by their ability to remain unhindered by terrain.

## Syndews (Aster)

A Syndews is said to be made when the recently deceased died in surprise. Aster Ews are characterised by the star-shaped objects they have as eyes. They are adept at Hidosis, primarily causing explosions or bright flashes. They are also capable of a fast, etherial, movement, which is easily mistaken for teleportation. Asters have a blue ghostly apparition following them. Observers have stated that they could see the faces of the recently deceased in these apparitions.

## Llonews (Poltern)

A Llonews is said to be created if the recently deceased died in joy. In combat, Llonews fulfill a support role, singing warsongs that seem to inspire Ews but demoralise enemy combatants.

## Carews (Moroi)

A Carews is said to be created if the recently deceased died in love. Morois take the role of specialist assassins. They tend to 'lock on' to a target. Targets locked on slowly lose Torfol mass. When this mass reaches near-zero, some people are known to immediately die. This death only occurs in those with creature stones. 

In large battles, they usually attempt to operate on the sidelines and break through front lines in order to kill their target. They are also highly competent outside of battles, being incredibly stealthy.

# Ghouls

Although the vast majority of Lethews are made after a person has died, it is known that this process can be done after any creature has died. However, non-person creatures that become Lethews are not the same as when people become Lethews - they become Ghouls. Ghouls are Lethews created using animals as the deceased, and these Lethews usually inherit the likeness of the recently deceased creature that caused their creation. 

Ghouls are usually created as mounts such as horses for Silpt-themed mounts. Sometimes, more powerful creatures are hunted down to create a powerful and monstrous Lethews.
