The Fruchews are a unique type of [Ews](ews.md) found in [Asenhal](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/asenhal) and [[Varan Del]]. They are highly aggressive and really thirsty. They are not too powerful while dry, but if hydrated they become incredibly fast and strong. 

Due to their unilateral aggression, they are not considered a species of people.
