The Gan are an Amphibian-based species of person introduced to Annwvyn around -9000. 

**[Ce'gan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/cegan)**
The first Gan was the Ce'gan, resembling anthopomorphic[Anaschisma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaschisma). They were created in -8400 by [Ymdig](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/ymdig), [Bwydo](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/bwydo), [Riw](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/riw), [Cyfapheb](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/cyfapheb), and [Hell](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/hell)

**[Dregan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/dregan)**
The next Gan to be diverged were the Dregan in -7900. [Sirphni](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/sirphni) first diverged them after being challenged to make a creature. She claims to have "made Ce'gan that had long and slender bodies perfect for lying down with, and made their legs vestigial so that they could not move". However, she had put enough thought into the Dregan as to make them perfectly mobile like snakes. They are more accurately based on [Caecillans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caecilian)

Shortly after their initial creation, [Maleisus](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/maleisus) would morph the species into their more modern form. He made them larger and stronger, and gave additional function to their arms so that they could interface with the world once more. 

**[Sangan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/sangan)**
The Sangan were diverged from the Ce'gan in -7800 by [Enw](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/enw). Enw had performed this divergence in hopes of helping Enw find Enw's domain. The Sangan are gecko-like, but still strictly amphibian.

**[ellegan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/ellegan)**
The Ellegan are a larger and harder-skinned subspecies of Gan compared to the Ce'gan, diverged by Maleisus and [Glinphe](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/glinphe) in -7400. They much more closely resemble anthropomorphic crocodiles with their stature, scales, and teeth, but remain ultimately amphibian-based.

**[Coesgan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/coesgan)**
A frog-like Gan created from the Ce'gan in -5800 by Hell, [Lleh](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/lleh), and [Caesi](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/caesi). The motivation is somewhat unclear, but they were probably just hanging out and decided that the Gan needed more diverse subspecies. The Coesgan are short in stature and often a bit plumper, which makes them very visually different from the Ce'gan from which they were diverged. 

In -5400, Cryver modified the Coesgan to give them naturally stronger muscles. 

Around the year 100, there was a mass migration of Coesgan from Annwvyn to [Barogate](barogate.md) and [Anula](wastelife/regions/Anula.md). Although these gan are identical in subspecies to the Coesgan, they are often referred to as the [Akiiogan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/akiiogan) (from [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene) in Anula) and the [Korongan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/korongan) (from [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar) in Barogate).

**[Deimgan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/deimgan)**
The Deimgan resemble Axolotls. They were diverged by Lleh recently in 1100 after she took a random interest to the natural Axolotls found on Annwvyn.
