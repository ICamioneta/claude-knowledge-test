The Difmuz are a subspecies of [Muz](muz.md) who adapted to the Wastes. The Difmuz particularly are known for their adaptability and survivability, an extension of the original Muz's traits before they finished diverging. The Difmuz maintain a number of region-specific cultures around the Wastes unique to each region. These separate cultures often cultivate their own traits, which are slowly emerging as inherent features of the Difmuz born in these families (when compared to non-Difmuz in these cultures). 

# Regional Cultures
## Ashlander
Ashlander is the culture of Difmuz in [Llyhai](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/llyhai). The land is harsh and hostile, but the Ashlanders who live there persist regardless. Their success in Llyhai is attributed to generational familiarity and persistence.

## Mudscraper
Mudscraper is the culture of Difmuz in [Duela](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/duela). They have cultivated a generational knowledge of utilising the dirt of Duela to create huts.

## Sandcamper
Sandcampers is the culture of Difmuz in [Kyenel](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kyenel). The harsh and unforgiving desert. They have cultivated the ability to survive without food and water for longer than usual, and are good at tracking and finding their way.

## Madmuz
The Madmuz is the culture built by Muz living in [Dithas](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/dithas) and [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix). They have cultivated a knowledge of the local fauna and flora. The culture emphasises the importance of always being on guard.

## Steppechild
The Steppechild is the culture of Difmuz living in [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/the-heabe), and the primary culture of the Aredvics. They have cultivated a culture of ranged hunting and camouflage. 

## Mountaineer
Mountaineer is a Difmuz culture unique to [Gordolmonre](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/gordolmonre). This culture has a strong tradition of harsh mountain climbing.
