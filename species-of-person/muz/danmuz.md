The Danmuz are a subspecies of [Muz](muz.md) adapted to [Terlaye](terlaye.md). This land is full of rivers and flowing water, areas known for their dense [[Hidology|hidol]]. The Danmuz as a diverged Muz are naturally adept at [Hidosis](/wastelife/hidol/hidosis).

The danmuz-majority cultures are known for their focus on peace and calm, contrasted by strong and tactical armies driven by the goal to maintain the peace.
