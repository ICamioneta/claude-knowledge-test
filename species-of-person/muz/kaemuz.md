The Kaemuz are a [Muz](muz.md) subspecies and the native inhabitants of [Ereso](ereso.md). Ereso is a small and climate diverse region, for this reason the Kaemuz are seen as often prepared for difficult climates and hard terrain. The cultures of Ereso have gained a reputation for being architecturally astute and good engineers.
