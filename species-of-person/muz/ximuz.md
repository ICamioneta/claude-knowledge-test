The Ximuz are a subspecies of Muz native to [Kyeron](kyeron.md). Kyeron is a wind and unpredictable rainforest, and the Ximuz have adapted to this by the evolution of a prehensile tail, as well as fur that generally protects them from the foliage of Kyeron. Their cultures have emphasised the importance of knowing how to traverse nature.
