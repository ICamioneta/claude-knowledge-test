The Chōcut are a subspecies of [Cut](cut.md) present across [Calaeddodd](/wastelife/regions/anula/calaeddodd). This mountain range is home to the Lovyn and other dangers. The Chōcut , the Chōcut are a hardy and capable bunch, they are agile and have excellent metabolisms. 

The Nebcholes family of the [Nebcholes-Remen](/wastelife/factions/anula/nebcholes-remen) are Chōcut.
