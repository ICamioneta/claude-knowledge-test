The Cafcut are a subspecies of [Cut](cut.md). They are known to be comedic and performative. Compared to other cut, they have small family sizes of about 5-8 siblings. They have cultivated a culture of heroic deeds such as self-sacrifice. They are, however, not as hardy as their Diacut relatives, and are capable of far less impressive deeds. 

The Remen monarchy of [Nebcholes-Remen](/wastelife/factions/anula/nebcholes-remen) are Cafcut.
