The Çin are a cat-like species of person. They were created by [Prydferth](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/prydferth) and distributed across [Kuçin](/wastelife/regions/annwvyn/kucin) around the year 400. In the centuries leading up and following their creation, Prydferth had a certain fascination with cats and their role in society. She lived as a "cat lady" mortal in [Kerre](/wastelife/regions/annwvyn/kerre) for an entire lifespan, and at the end remembering her fondness for the creatures, she was inspired to create a new species of person based on cats. 

The Çin placed in Kuçin are the original species. They came to be known as the [Telikuçin](telikucin.md) once the Kuçin diversified. 

The [Kahrçin](/wastelife/species-of-person/cin/kahrcin) are a çin subspecies diverged from the Telikuçin diverged by [Fflam](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/fflam) for yet unspecified reasons. They were placed in Kahklin in 860 and later modified by a young [Mwender](/wastelife/secret/eternity/eternals/mwender). The Kahrçin were placed in Kahklin and eventually would become more populous than the pre-existing species. Kahklin remains incredibly diverse. 

The [Shinkuçin](wastelife/species-of-person/cin/shinkucin.md) were diverged by [Medr](/wastelife/species-of-person/cin/shinkucin) in the early 1000s. The circumstances around their creation is rather complex. They were created specifically to be mortals that interact with the silver ruins present across [Hebas Deryd](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/hebas-deryd) and [Asenhal](/wastelife/regions/mothdao/asenhal). Medr's original plan was to purposefully establish a cult for this nature, but she is more comfortable with her living relationship with these people post-5005th.
