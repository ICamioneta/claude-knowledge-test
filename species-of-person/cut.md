The Cut are a species of guinea-pig like people. They have little body hair. They have large torsos and heads, but smaller limbs. Their body plan can be described as 'chonky'. 
The Cut are generally characterised by their large families, although some species of Cut have significantly more offspring.

- [Diacut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/diacut), the industrial engineers of Brwrd
- [Chōcut](wastelife/species-of-person/cut/chocut.md), the hardy fighters of Calaeddod
- [Lescut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/lescut), the social Cut with massive families
- [Cafcut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/cafcut), the social, comedic, and heroic Cut
- [Dancut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/dancut), the magically inclined Cut
