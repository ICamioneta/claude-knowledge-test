The Ens are a goat-like people native to [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate/the-heabe) and [Gordolmonre](/wastelife/regions/barogate/gordolmonre). They have two-sectioned hooves that are like feet that help them stand, as well as hoof-like hand constructions that aren't as dextrous as other species of person. Their cultures are traditionally nomadic but many Ens have settled. 

- [Nadens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/nadens), the most common Ens
- [Popens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/popens), the brutish and purely nomadic Ens
- [Reyens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/reyens), the mountain climbing Ens of Gordolmonre
- [Colsens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/colsens), the most common Ens living outside of the Wastes
