The Mad are a species of person native to the [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix) and [Dithas](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/dithas) regions of Barogate's wastes. These regions are well known for their mushroom forests, and going with the theme of these places the Mad are a mushroom-like people. They are an internally diverse bunch, coming in a spectrum of colours, shapes, and sizes even within subspecies. The only physical similarity between subspecies is a mushroom cap and their mycelium-like skin. 

- [Dimad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/dimad), the displaced small Mad of the Dithas coast
- [Cuemad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/cuemad), the tiny, magical, and objectively adorable pals
- [Pucmad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/pucmad), the goblin-like grey skinned Mad
- [Guarmad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/guarmad), the giant protectors of Vugaix
- [Buemad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/buemad), the mad with muz-like proportions that live in small towns
