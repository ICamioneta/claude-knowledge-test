The Tudofall are a tortoise-like subspecies of [Fall](fall.md). Even though they are described as tortoise-like, the only real similarity is in the head, and that they have hardened chitin-like parts on their front and backs. They live mostly in Duela, but have also found some good homes in Dithas and Darie.
