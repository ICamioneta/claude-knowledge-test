The Rutefall are a subspecies of [Fall](fall.md) who dominate the Duela. It is the only region they are commonly found in. They have heads like Komodo dragons, but other than that they are fairly humanoid just like the Ens or the Phax. They have sharp claws, large mouths, and like all Fall are very impulsive.
