The southern revolts were initially catalysed by the Republic of Ralean and [The Guerrilla](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-guerrilla.md), the former of whom were seeking to form a democratic republic for [Ralean](ralean.md) to better represent its landed oligarchy and competent ministers. A huge number of highly competent officials in the Empire were unsatisfied with the ruling of Ruion Reimig, so they decided to lead in rebellion alongside many other factions. These officials established a ruling republic where the competent officials of the Empire would make collective and informed decisions in the place of a singular autocratic Emperor.

The Reimig Empire’s best tradesmen, generals, and politicians fled to the Republic of Ralean leaving the Reimig empire with a few competent loyalists and many incompetent people who wouldn’t benefit so much from joining the Republic.

The Republic is ultimately positioned well. It is on the southern parts of Ralean and although it has a long border, this border is mostly with the Kingdom of Ithun where defence isn’t much of an issue. As a revolutionary organisation, it falls into direct conflict with the Exiled Empire and the other revolutionary organisation, the [New Councilist Province](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/new-councilist-province.md). Most of its military action is on this border, where its up against the well defended Exiled Empire and the well-read councilist province.
