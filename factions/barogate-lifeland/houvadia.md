Houvadia is a separatist nation that formed from [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md)'s southern revolts. The revolts happened in 3484, and the formation of Houvadia was an early phenomenon happening in early 3485. It formed from the province of Detkaindinum.

![Houvadia.png](/wastelife/images/flags/houvadia.png)

During the revolt, a number of guildsmen, mayors, and other people who controlled stuff decided that they owned that stuff and that the Emperor didn’t. Houvadia prides itself on its allegedly meritocratic system, where the head-of-state of Houvadia was selected after a “Rulership Examination” was conducted. During the initial four years of the nation, there was lots of social mobility. Not only were the guildsmen and mayors getting wealthy, but the well-read scholars and thrifty peoples of the land were also experiencing their fair share of mobility. The head-of-state ended up being an economics professor at a university in [Detfygh Samon](/wastelife/settlements/ralean/detfygh-samon).

After three years, the system has calmed down a bit. Although the people are generally living significantly better lives, the head-of-state has been in power for an uninterrupted three years. They say that more examinations will be held in the year 3490, starting a five-year cycle for heads-of-states. The government and head-of-state make compelling arguments as to why a 5-year term is sufficiently regular to review and assess the qualifications and performance of its leaders, however Houvadian critics suggest that a frequent opportunity to change the head-of-state would lead to a more prosperous nation.

The exams conducted during the initial period have been reviewed and studied, and generally agreed to be of a high quality. The body created to manage these exams is acclaimed. People have faith that these exams are fair and well-made.

Houvadia’s goals as a nation participating in the Southern Revolts against the Reimig Empire are to merely exist. Consolidate their lands and legitimise themselves to other states. The government is friendly with the Setia-Debid Corporation commending the CEO of the Setia company for "working his way to the top on fair grounds".
