People everywhere talk about the current politics in [Ralean](ralean.md). The [Lemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/lemuz) trait of unity is being questioned. [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md), the greatest modern bastion of power on the continent, invaded and annexed the lands of [Terlaye](terlaye.md) 80 years ago. Only the eldest: the druids, and bards alike remember the wars. Since then, the Empire of Ralean has done all it can to keep these lands, destabilising regional economies, forcing cities to rely on each other and Ralean.

In these 80 years the cities of Terlaye remain subjugate all except from [Council of Llofilot](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/council-of-llofilot.md), which escaped the autocratic grasp of the Empire and landed in the councilist circle of [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md). Llofilot’s independence was 10 years ago, and 70 years of subjugation has left a stain on Terlaye, a stain that Llofilot Council wishes to bleach. The Empire at the time could not enforce its control over Llofilot because this would mean war with Ereso. A mixture of Eresonia's uncharacteristically large military and the Empire's own internal struggle made this a highly unfavourable predicament. These internal struggles have since exploded in the south, causing the Empire of Ralean's modern crisis.

In 3484, what had been provinces of free serfs and workers serving the Emperor suddenly rebelled, becoming an angry mob. The guards and militia of the regions only helped the revolts, as they clearly took sides with their local rulers and the people of their revolting land. There was, and still is, a great hatred towards the Emperor and his position in those parts. 

In 3484 the provinces in central and southern Ralean exiled any remaining Empire authority. These rebellions were a mixture of the people, the guards, the local militia, and unhappy aristocrats. Weapons supplied to everyone fuelled a season of anarchy. A boiling pot ready for the ingredients for new ideologies. As chaos unravelled, different administrations attempted to form. Some succeeded, creating new countries and outlooks in the world. The Empire had no hopes of quickly regaining control, as every warring ideology in Southern Ralean was united against the Emperor. The most authoritarian governments would unite with egalitarian regimes in warfare against the Empire.

The first 2 years of the rebellion were frenzied, and there is no clear idea nor map that could convey what was happening. Now, 2 years after events settled somewhat, we are left with 6 primary players in Central and Southern Ralean, and the ever-present Empire.

The Empire quickly conquered the rebelling lands around their capital, Cania, but this is all of central Ralean they could regain. Ralean is split into a culturally and geographically distinct south, centre, and north. These revolts hapenned all around central and southern ralean, but the name of this conflict is the Southern Revolts because the revolts are generally beneath Cania, which is believed to be in the vertical centre of Ralean.

Each of the Southern Ralean factions have their own document. They were originally all listed here, but due to a reorganisation of the documents of Wastelfife, this part of this document now serves as an overview of all the factions, and a link hub.

- [Houvadia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/houvadia.md), a "meritocratic" society with a strong oligarchy and a base in the working and peasant classes
- [The Guerrilla](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-guerrilla.md), an anarchic society wit the capability of spontaneously forming militaries around invading armies
- [Setia-Debid](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/setia-debid.md), a company turned oligarchic regime
- The [Kingdom of Ithun](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/kingdom-of-ithun.md), a feudal monarchy
- The [New Councilist Province](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/new-councilist-province.md), a councilist movement in Ralean
- [The Republic of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-republic-of-ralean.md), a movement of the oligarchy, aristocracy, and old Empire officials to remove the emperor and install an executive republic in Ralean.

# The Exiled Empire

The Exiled Empire is a small sector of land still held by the Empire and the Emperor’s regime. The land has no direct land supply to the rest of the Empire, but the bordering nation, the New Councilist Provinces, has allowed limited transport from one side to the other. The only real way for the Empire of Ralean to get supply to the Exiled Empire is through the port city Nater’s End. Due to the territory lost in these riots, Nater’s End, and the surrounding area, is the only connection the Empire of Ralean has to the sea.
