Eswin is a faction of [Ews](ews.md) in southern [Ralean](/wastelife/regions/barogate/ralean). It is regionally powerful given its militant history and collaboration with the lowborn and average citizens of southern Ralean. It was not formally part of [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) prior to the southern revolts. It maintains no fomal sovereignty over its borders instead opting to safeguard its Ews communities and collaborate with local governments. 

Currently, there are three factions claiming parts of Eswin as their land. Eswin has managed to form informal agreements of mutual benefit between each of these powers. These agreements are often challenged by the more ambitious leaders of the separatist factions. 

These are the factions that Eswin lives inside:

- [The Kingdom of Ithun](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/kingdom-of-ithun.md)
- [The New Councilist Province](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/new-councilist-province.md)
- [The Republic of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-republic-of-ralean.md)
