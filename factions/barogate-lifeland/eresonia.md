Eresonia, the grand and populous egalitarian nation. Perhaps not as large as the [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) but just as powerful. Eresonia’s power lies in its social order, the agreement of all peoples on how to run the nation. Following Councilism’s tenets, [[ereso|Ereso's]] capacity for production is quickly increasing.

# Government

![Ereso City Names.jpg](/wastelife/region-maps/ereso/ereso-city-names.jpg)

Eresonia follows the government system described in Rosefie Cetyevliefen and the Formist’s doctrine of Councilism. Given how these ideas are largely outdated and not entirely applicable to the modern culture in Ereso, a few adjustments have been made by the New Councilists and the celebrated Karsol Maxnen. The ideas the Formists wrote about in Councilism formed the basis for a functional democratic society and is used in the organisation of democracies around the two continents.

While the Formists were focused on forming a government from where there is none, the New Councilists focus on overthrowing the current system and establishing Councilism over the course of a few years. The Formists still exist in The Guerrilla, but these formists have little history tying them to the original movement. The New Councilists were successful in Ereso, but they still encourage movements elsewhere, especially in the political turmoil present in Southern Ralean.

It has been 188 years since Councilist Eresonia has been formed, and it has achieved all the ideals it set out to get. However, there is still a sector of Councilism, Post-Modern Councilism, that argues that the socialist systems of councilism will be significantly more effective if the resources of the world were abundant, and the standard of living for all would be increased beyond belief.

Modern Councilism in Eresonia exists alongside many ideas continued from the regime of capital. Guilds are still present, even if they are significantly socialised. Although there are no real laws to solidify it, these guilds generically control the methods of production. In addition to this, many of the cultural conflicts festered by the ruling class of the Second Empire leave stains on the generations.

## Local Councils

Under Councilism, the locality rule themselves. For every town, village, and city, there is a council of the elected among the populace. For smaller bodies of population, such as towns and villages, many people know each other. For these councils, there is no need in adjustment from direct democracy. The people of these communities organise the size of their own councils and are also educated on efficient council sizes by the education system. Then, the members on the council must have a certain amount of support, and if their role on their council is challenged, there may be an election held to remove them from the council, and possibly replace them if it is wanted. Usually, the size of councils remains about the same, and changes to the size of councils are extraordinary.

For cities, local councils are often organised based upon regions within the city. City councils strictly follow a formula for assigning efficiently sized councils. Each district of a city is given a number of positions on the council that they should fill by electing from among themselves.

Areas which a certain council are responsible for are called regions, or for that particular council, the local region. For example, the local council of [Galresir](/wastelife/settlements/ereso/galresir) controls Galresir’s local region.

The local councils are primarily in charge of local organisation including development of the local, assigning public council policies, settling public disputes.

## Public Councils

The public sector in Eresonia is managed by the Public Councils. These sectors include but are not limited to services such as policing, healthcare, education, and the distribution of resources. Each of these services have their own National Public Council. A National Public Council acts similarly to the local Public Councils but its members are usually members of local Public Councils. The National Public Council’s meetings usually discuss their service on a national level, and their meetings’ agenda usually include policies for their service to adopt, such as new methods and ways of doing things.

Public Councils have tri-weekly meetings on the distribution of the services resources, allocation of labour resources, and generic organisation. These meetings are not always tri-weekly, and are held at the council and the public’s discretion. The tri-weekly meetings are always public events, any member of the public whether they’re on the council or not may attend and speak with equal privilege to those on the council.

### The Public Guard

As an example of a public council, there is the Public Guard. Every civilian in Eresonia is armed and therefore capable of defending themselves from minor threats that would otherwise threaten their safety. As part of education, there are several educational organisations primarily focused on self-defence in all forms. The exact organisations are told of below under the Educational Organisations section.

The Public Guard was established to protect the people from threats that the public generically cannot handle such as dangerous wildlife, common misdemeanour, and public threats, the last of which often manifests as hostile factions such as banditry. The Public Guard are given a standardised set of arms and armours in order to conduct their role efficiently and to set them apart from the armaments of the public.

The public guard are not the only people capable of responding to these threats. They are usually the only consistent resource the public has to respond to these threats, so their relevance is heavily justified. In towns, there is usually a few people who are more capable than the public guard at responding to particular threats who are not members. These people might be associated with the self-defence education guilds or may otherwise have more experience than the public guard in a particular threat.

Local Public Guard council members often consist of acting public guard, generic public with an interest in local defence, or veteran of the public guard aiming to share their wisdom. Attendees may include anyone as per the usual, but often includes the smiths or heads of smithing guilds that supply the Public Guard. Meeting’s agendas are often the discussion of patrols, the acquisition of resources from smiths, labour resources in the form of recruitment and allocation, and generic discussions on the organisation’s efficiency.

## Surplus Growth

In Eresonia’s particular implementation of Councilism, there is a focus on growth away from the cities and into the smaller towns. Resources from cities are generally taken as they are required for growth, and any surplus resource is traded often at a slight loss to the lesser towns and smaller communities. The idea of this extraction of surplus from the cities is to develop the farms and fields with the ultimate goal of reducing the amount of labour required to grow food.

## Currency

The general exchange token of Eresonia and several regions following its economic ideas is the Mark of Labour, or just Marks, or as they are most frequently called, Mar. Marks of Labour are supposed to represent the labour value that went into an item for it to be exchanged, for example a smith will sell their weapon for a certain amount of marks to represent how much labour they believe they put into it, and the general selling price in the exchange value of the weapon. Marks of Labour are not a good measurement of the labour value in an item, because the exchange value effects its price and hence its mar cost, like any other item in any other currency.

# Conflict

Eresonia is undergoing an internal political conflict. It has only been councilist for 188 years, and the Second Eresonian Empire before it had 257 years to solidify its economic and ideological rule. This, and Eresonia is implementing some form of socialist state, and the productive forces aren’t developed enough for that. Capitalism is still present in Eresonia, especially in the further reaches of its democracy.

## Geopolitical assessment

The Eresonian core, the majority Sapyat culture areas are well integrated into Councilist society. [Mouvasir](/wastelife/settlements/ereso/mouvasir), Redel, and their towns are very ideologically and economically sound. Narsyrdel, despite having a strong ideological base, has a somewhat large corporate faction. Towns tied to Narsyrdel are the largest targets to corporate factions.

Namoset is a strong corporate stronghold, with places like Reipoten being infamously rich. Reipoten’s only other town, Caroset, has a strong and growing councilist core. Synset is very into the labour thing, whereas Lesten has a core of capitalists strongly against the councilist system.

## Vania

Vania seceded from Eresonia in the early days when a necromage crisis couldn’t be handled by the unreformed military organisations of Ereso. It was due to the lack of cohesion between the government and the military that intervention was delayed, and the capitalists left in Vania used this as the utmost stronghold against councilist ideas. Vania has its own struggle going on, but the government is on the side of their capitalist faction, the Estateers.

## Guilds vs Companies

One of the main conflicts is between these two bodies, guilds and companies. Guilds are government-backed organisations that exercise worker’s control over their workplaces. Companies, however, are the strongholds against Councilism too deeply rooted by the Pri-Conners and not focused upon by the revolutionary Eresonians in 3277.

# Guilds

A guild in Eresonia is an organisation founded by the people. This is in opposition to the Public Councils which are founded by the National Council or the Local Council. Guilds, like any other organisation, offer a particular service. In Eresonia, guilds are almost always publicly owned and follow similar organisational structures to the Public Councils.

## The Popular Adventuring Guilds

The popular Guilds is a set of guilds generally present across all of Ereso and some bordering regions All of them have places in cities, but not always in towns. Adventuring guilds come from the people’s want to experience the world. They cultivate the knowledge of survival and organise periodic trips to places close, far, and everywhere inbetween.

### Explorer’s Guild

Ereso is a very learnt land, there isn’t much more to discover. But what is there is untouched. The Ereso Explorer’s Guild is a popular guild with about 80000 members that is dedicated to helping people explore Ereso. It is a democratic guild.

### Planar Guild

The Ereso Planar Guild is a guild dedicated to the cultivation and learning of planar theory. This isn’t actually the guild’s primary focus, because most people are more interested in learning enchantments.

### Enchanter’s Guild

The Ereso Enchanter’s Guild is a guild similar to the Planar Guild, and which has a bitter-sweet rivalry with it. The Enchanter’s Guild is dedicated to teaching people how to enchant things. None of that elitist theory knowledge required.

### Mage Guild

Simply the largest guild in Ereso dedicated to mages and the (rather aggressive) practice of clapping. It’s the largest because it’s popular, and its popular because it does what it says.

### Botanical Guild

The botanical guild is a guild for plant lovers everywhere, it just so happens that most “plant lovers” are mages looking for ingredient help. The Botanical Guild is seen as a rival to the Mage Guild, but both respect the mage guild’s dominance and popularity. The Botanical Guild and the mage guild work in tandem, where the botanical guild provides more knowledge about ingredients.

### Reipan Practical Self Defence

RPSD is basically a club for learning self-defence. They have specific courses for scenarios, such as travelling on the road or surviving in the city. Whether you’re up against a wild animal or an armed combatant. Reipan Practical Self Defence has it all.

### The Spire Fencing Academy

With a logo of Ereso wielding the spire to fence the Sun, the Spire Fencing Academy is a friendly and competitive environment where people of most ages can learn the art of fencing combat. The Spire FA focuses on combat as a martial art, rather than as self-defence. Hence, it does not usually teach group combat or creature fighting.

### Arlind Assassinators

With a name that alliterates in any language it uses, the Arlind Assassinators is a guild not focused on eradicating the pesky Arlinds but in beast combat in general. The Arlind Assassinators are all about defeating creatures, usually as a group. They sometimes bring in live animals to test skills on, but it’s usually only ever with a ballista pointed at them in case anything goes wrong. The Arlind Arenas are in most major cities. Loads of people criticize the Arlind Assassinators for inhumane handling of creatures.

# History

![3200 Ralean.jpeg](/wastelife/region-maps/history/3200-ralean.jpeg)

## Foundation of Eresonia

The history of Eresonia starts in 3200, at the height of the Second Eresonian Empire. The map to the left is of this time. This empire would collapse 50 years later, in a manner that is described through history books as “aggressive”. The end of this empire, like many nations before it, came about because of a mix of external and internal affairs. As this is not the history of the Second Eresonian Empire, the only one of these affairs that we are interested in is the one that lead to the rise of Eresonia. The culprit is the smallest blob on the map.

The Commune of the Two Peoples (CTP) is an egalitarian society in the border mountains in Ralean. It was true to its name; most people within were of similar wealth in all measures. Much like the modern-day Guerrilla, this stable society is only possible because of the culture that inhabits the lands. The CTP’s existance was in direct opposition to that of the Second Eresonian Empire. The Second Eresonian Empire promoted private property and private growth, while the CTP was a land shared by all its inhabitants. In 3212, a small group of people from the CTP travelled to Ereso to assess the situation and attempt to bring it towards a fairer society.

The collapse of the Second Eresonian Empire began in 3253, with a wave of homeland strikes by the workers and peasantry of the country. These were in opposition of the system the Pri-Coner armies had fostered, where influential guildspeople held control over land, money, and labour. The Eresonain State was forced to grant the people of Ereso better living conditions. The Eresonian occupied lands would be inspired by this action, aware of how they could change their own situation. Lauvednolandian and [[terlaye|Terlayan]] rulers cooperated with figureheads in Southern Ralean and southern Terlaye to sow the seeds of 3257, where the legacy of the Eresonian strikes carried into these regions. The purpose for these strikes was brought to its ultimate conclusion, and southern ralean and southern Terlaye were made to let self govern. Terlaye returned to its monarchy routes, with many of the exiled monarchs whose families held these lands returning.

The Second Eresonian state deteriorated for the following two decades. It could no longer afford many of the concessions it offered the people of Ereso due to the loss of its conquered regions. This all came to the forefront of the people of Ereso in 3273, when a series of strikes and revolts occurred. With nothing left to offer, the Second Eresonian Empire was forced to fight for its existence,

The next four years saw the conflict between the Eresonian people and the remainder of the Pri-Coner armies. This is where the Commune of the Two Peoples comes into play. Those people who came to Ereso who wanted it to become a better society played a massive hand in encouraging the Eresonian peoples to strike and push away the Pri-Coner armies. They adopted Formist ideals of democracy and federative planning and founded the ideals of Eresonian Councilism, taking into context the material position of Ereso at the time, as well as lessons learnt from the revolts of 3253 and 3257.

These Eresonain Councilists included the likes of Karsol Maxnen, Ealen Hevrad, and Dorsoj Lehnon. They faced internal opponents, namely the Guildsmen and Artisan coalition originating from Lesten, and the ideas of Presidential Democracy coming from Vania. Throughout the four years, they convinced the population of Ereso that they could organise themselves under the principles of councilism. The civil war ended in 3281 with the Pri-Coner armies being strangled from all sides, trapped in the city of Namoset.

From then, the country called itself Eresonia, and took the next 20 years teaching the people about organisation and commissioning the complete rebuilding of cities and towns to include the infrastructure necessary for Councilist democracy. Many of these new council chambers were build upon old wealthy areas. Even after this time frame, Eresonia would continue to develop, but the final foundations of Eresonia are said to be laid in 3300.

The military wouldn’t receive proper reforms until 3330. The military wasn’t properly organised and had elements of older army structures that were incompatible with councilist ideas. Luckily, this military never saw conflict until the necromancer Byrnt attempted to conquer Ereso starting with the town of Aone. The army was completely unprepared. Its leaders decided to hold off at the Vanian mountains as Byrnt conquered his way through Vania. There was a clear push to help from the soldiery of Ereso as many defected and rushed into Vania to help.

Luckily, Vania was saved by a hero. With this, the people of Vania felt betrayed, and the group advocating for presidential democracy in 3280 came back and used this feeling to bring forth a self-governing Vanian state, using the image of the hero as its icon. This was ultimately for the good though, as Eresonia realised the inefficiency of its army and reorganised it to include councilist ideals. Vania kept its close ties to Eresonia, and maintains healthy economic cooperations and defensive pacts with the nation.

# Military

Before the turn of the 34th century, the Councilists were working hard to figure the details of their government. Military was one of their main concerns, as a military is vital for the continuation of a nation, but it’s often top-down nature was directly opposed to the ideology of Councilism. To adhere to the concepts of Councilism, the Military was made a single National Public Council, with the inner organisation heavily specified.

## Branches

The Eresonian Military has branches. These branches each handle a separate aspect of the military. Although there are not many branches as of yet, the branch system was designed to allow for the expansion of the Eresonian military in the future, when controlling the seas and the sky might become more important. The branches are as follows:

###  Generic Guard (EGG)

The Generic Guard are the Eresonain national guards. They are essentially a well-armed and trained police force. They hold a separate role to the Public Guard in that they usually protect the roads and places outside of towns and cities, which is where the Public Guard operate. The Generic Guards and their commanders are trained to defend in Ereso’s lands.

### Boot Military (EBM)

The Boot Military are the ground forces that the Eresonain Military holds in reserve should their country come under attack. They are highly trained in comparison to the Generic Guard, as they have nothing to do but exercises. The Boot Military and Generic Guard often exchange soldiers to keep people useful.

### Grand Fleet (EGF)

The Eresonian Grand Fleet is the navy of Ereso. It is small, as are all branches apart from the EGG and EBM, but it is getting more money pumped into it as time goes on. The Grand Fleet currently performs anti-piracy operations on the Eresonian, Kyeronese, and in the continental strait for the benefit of all nations. After the loss of Ralean’s South, the EGF is the second largest navy across the two continents.

### Airborne Force (EAF)

The Eresonain Airborne Force is an experimental branch of the military that currently consists of hopefuls who attempt to ride the many large flying creatures of Wastelife such as Lovyn and Dkenf. The more productive members of the EAF are wizards that specialise in enchantments that make people fly.

## Rank System

There are nine ranks in the Eresonian Military. Because of voting, there is a very complex method of demotion that means that even if you’re qualified to be a martial, you may be in a squadron. A person in a squadron is called a unit. This is not a rank.

- People joining the military fresh are **Recruit**.
- Those who command squads are **Corporal**.
- Those who command groups are **Commander**.
- Those who command companies are **Captain**.
- Those who command battalions are **Colonel**.
- Those who command brigades are **Colonel Commander**.
- Those who command divisions are **General**.
- Those who command corps are **General Commander**.
- Those who command armies are **Marshal**.

### Seniority

Each rank can be senior, in which case they are \[RANK]-senior up until Colonel, when they become \[RANK] senior.

- Recruits must be for 8 years before they are **Recruit-Senior**
- Corporals must be for 4 years before they are **Corporal-Senior**
- Commanders must be for 4 years before they are **Commander-Senior**
- Captains must be for 2 years before they are **Captain-Senior**

From then, seniority is determined by those above you and those among you. Generally, the gap is 2 years.

### Candidate-Seniority

To become a candidate for the voting of the next rank up, you must go and educate for that rank. Recruit-Seniors must train for 4 years, Corporals up to captains train for 2, and ranks further on require a minimum of 2 years with exams at the end, tailored by those who already have that rank.

### Demotion

If you have not achieved candidate-seniority and you are not voted back into position, you are demoted and lose your rank. You become a candidate-senior for the rank below. If you did achieve candidate seniority, you become the lower rank acting rank candidate senior, for example Corporal-Acting Commander-Candidate-Senior. When you are promoted again, you are a senior of that rank if you were before (which you probably were because of the time between election.)

Note that it is entirely possible for a unit acting general commander candidate senior to exist, and be acting under a recently promoted commander. In this situation, the unit acting general commander candidate would be underneath their own corporal, but they would be above all other commanders. They would also be below their corporal’s commander, but above all other commanders. This works up the chain until they are above all other general candidate seniors.

### Voting

When a tie occurs in voting, the most senior member gets promoted. If both members are equally senior (unlikely), they pull straws.

- For the position of corporal, units vote, and their votes are counted independently.
- For the position of commander, corporals votes are counted independently and units votes are counted as one for a squad.
- For the position of captain, corporal votes are counted in pairs, commander votes are counted independently, and unit votes are counted as one for a squad.
- For the position of colonels, corporal and captain votes are counted independently.
- For the position of colonel commander, captain and colonel votes are counted independently
- For the position of general, colonel and colonel commander votes are counted independently
- For the position of general commander, colonel, colonel commander, and general votes are counted independently.
- For the position of martial, unit votes are counted as one for a company, captain and colonel votes are counted in pairs, colonel commanders, generals, and general commanders’ votes are counted independently.
