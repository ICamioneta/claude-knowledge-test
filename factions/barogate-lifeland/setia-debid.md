The Setia-Debid company is a nation that came out of Ralean's southern revolts, being officially formed as a nation-state in early 3486. The roots of its power go further back.

![Setia-Debid.png](/wastelife/images/flags/setia-debid.png)

Setia-Debid came about in a similar way to [Houvadia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/houvadia.md). However, its roots are different to Houvadia's strong landed capitalists. Instead, the history of Houvadia follows a corporation that was founded during the Empire years and used the revolts to accumulate ownership of more of its area.

At the time of the revolt, the Mayor of [Kovedlajo](/wastelife/settlements/ralean/kovedlajo) was given ownership of all state machinery that existed during the Empire's time. This meant he was in control of city development and its civil services (except for the guard). The mayor founded a corporation called Setia, the name of which is supposed to have connotations of settling down and living a comfortable life.

Using the inherent monopoly of owning a town, the mayor used surplus from exploiting the region's peasantry to merge with many of the other companies and guilds that formed. The Setia company slowly began to own more and more of the developing society to the point where the capitalist company Setia eventually owned the entire country, and had its own state methods.

The Debid corporation had done the exact same thing north of Setia, so in the interest of profit the two companies merged and formed one Setia-Debid state.

The Setia-Debid corporation is blatantly a tyrannical oligarchic regime, the chief executive officer of the company-government is essentially a feudal lord. The corporation publishes a legal document that all citizens of its lands are assumed to agree with, stating that any disagreeing must move out of Setia-Debid owned lands.

Much like Houvadia, Setia-Debid prioritises its existence over the collapse of the Empire.
