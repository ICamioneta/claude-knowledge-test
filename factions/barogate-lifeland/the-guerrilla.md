The Guerrilla is the vague concept of a military organisation build for the exclusive purposes of fighting against sovereign forces. As a anarchic nation, it takes after its most similar relative in history, the Commune of the Two Peoples, and has created a military organisation similar to those many Formists proposed as a solution that avoided economic planning while still forming a force to fight back against outside powers. 

![Guerilla.png](/wastelife/images/flags/guerilla.png)

Although the conflict is often called to be occurring in Southern Ralean, the heart of the fighting rebellion sits as central as it could possibly get. During the initial uprise, the Lauvedno culture that resides in central Ralean tool the arms of the Empire and declared each individual self-governing. Benefiting from the assortment of cultural ideals influenced by other central ralean communities such as the Two Peoples, the people here set up a declaration of what lands exist under everyone's own rule.

Every person in the territories of the guerrilla are armed and know how to fight. The people of the land are beyond ready to follow acclaimed military leaders into battle against the despotic empire. Hence, the guerrilla’s lands are viewed by the Emperor’s armies to be a black hole. If you set foot in there, an army ten times larger will materialise surrounding you, lead by generals ten times more competent.

The name of the region, the guerrilla, is a hint towards their military organisation. Unforgiving guerrilla warfare tactics where the locals of the land know their area perfectly, and use this knowledge to trap and pick off any invading armies. Often, the citizens of the Guerrilla form organised militia around some highly influential commanders of the old empire, who realise their sympathies to the people of Lauvedno and promise to protect it by guiding the unguided masses with ingenious military strategy. 

The people who actually live there recognise their Guerrilla, but if you asked them what country they were in they would respond with the name of their local area, or simply say "Lauvedno land".
