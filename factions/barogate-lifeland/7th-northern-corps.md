The 7th northern corps are a group of 40 [Lindi](/wastelife/species-of-person/other/lindi) living in the typical Lindi-style settlement west of [[terlaye|Serven]] and north-east of Siluenot. They are notable for having a strange arsenal in their possession. With this arsenal, they organise themselves on a horizontal military hierarchy. They are split into squads.

# Settlement

Their settlement is typical of the Lindi: an array of underground tunnels and rooms. The 7th Northern Corps’s particular equipment needs certain unique facilities. They have 3 armoury rooms that arm different squads and teams. They have a stable in which they keep their horses and their tank. They also have a HQ room with a thinly constructed roof from which the HQ carry out their operations. This HQ is right next to the stable and their horse-pulled cart and horse in case they rapidly need to move.

# History of Conflict

Sometimes, a large group of ill-intentioned individuals looks on Lindi settlements and realises that they can just take whatever they want, so long as they can get past the Lindis. Lindis are formidable fighters, but they are fighters nonetheless and will fall to a large and determined bandit group. The 7th Northern Corps are the foil to these bandit groups. The 7th Northern Corps aren’t well known, and are quite often seen as ‘just another lindi settlement’. This is a mistake for a group of bandits hoping to attack it.

Literally every member of this lindi settlement has a gun. A modern, 21st century Earth gun. Literally no members of a large bandit group can protect themselves from that. Maybe, if they have enchantments on armour or a shield they can stop the Glocks from penetrating, but I still wouldn’t want to be on the receiving end of that force even if I knew it wasn’t going to penetrate.

The Lindis are capable of mounting an effective defence, but they usually spot any opposing forces before they approach the settlement. This is because the lindi people just like going for walks or being outside, it often isn’t a coordinated guarding operation. When a force is spotted, usually the recon team is sent out to keep a watch on them. The recon team are essentially invisible. With their advanced optics, they can keep watch so far away that those being watched have no chance of seeing them.

If there is any suspicion of hostile intent, the 7th Northern Corps are mobilised. Usually, before opening fire, the HQ regiment will go out and try to communicate with potential aggressors, kindly asking them to leave and that they will interpret any action within their territory as aggression and will act accordingly. Sometimes, this doesn’t go well and the aggressors aggress immediately attacking HQ. This is a bad decision, as the entirety of the 7th Northern Corps are hiding behind the next hill.

The Recon Team, who always watch these encounters, will open fire immediately on anyone close to harming HQ as HQ quickly extracts. The Mortar Squad provides covering fire, as the Infantry Squad and MG teams pop out from their hiding places and open fire on any aggressors chasing after the HQ. This is an ambush, and most aggressors in this case either retreat or die.

If aggressors continue to aggress, its on to more conventional warfare. Prioritising the usage of the surrounding hills for concealment and range benefits, the 7th northern core will fire upon the aggressors from ranges they can’t even hope to fire back from. Often these lower level confrontations require no help from any of the specialist teams, but if attacks are mounted from multiple directions the specialist teams are used to increase defensive capacity.

In the case of an all-out war, or any confrontation where the infantry squad might need help, this is where the tank crew are mobilised. Everything is useless against it apart from the most powerful and untraditional of magics. Use of the main turret is rare, as the tank’s machine gun is significantly more effective at culling the forces of a medieval infantry assault.

[The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) even had a go at the 7th Northern Corps. They brought a whole army. Lost 800 men to 15 Lindis, and then decided it wasn’t worth the death. They learnt that this was the right course of action as they slowly learnt of the 7th Northern corps’s true capabilities.
