The Kingdom of Ithun is a feudal kingdom formed in 3486 during [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md)'s southern revolts.

![Kingdom of Lemuz.png](/wastelife/images/flags/kingdom-of-lemuz.png)

Upon the initial uprising, many powerful people in these lands looked to the most influential figures for assistance. In exchange for money, the influential figures were capable of rallying the populace to defend a cause, the cause of feudality. These influential figures eventually set up an electoral monarchy, where the monarch is elected from among the nobles of the land.

Now it’s a traditional monarchy as laid out by the [Kingdoms of Terlaye]. Everything is split into titles, which are hereditary.

The title of monarch is the “King of Ithun”. Ithun is a culture dominant in the lands which the kingdom controls. The nobles claim that [Ralean](ralean.md) is divided into several de jure kingdoms, each belonging to a specific culture that is dominant in its area. It then claims the Empire of Ralean as a concept – not ruled by a dynastic Emperor but by an elected emperor from among the kings.

The Kingdom of Ithun’s goals are left ambiguous. It is mostly attempting to defend itself from the New Councilists and the Republic of Ralean. If it succeeds, there is a significant chance that these annexed lands would form the Kingdom of Meison, in which case the nobles would split into two chains of feudal power, and likely attempt to continue this until they can form the feudal Empire of Ralean.
