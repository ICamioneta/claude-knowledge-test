The New Councilist Province is the latest instillation of the ideas of Councilism into a land. Many revolutionary Councilists from across the world are moving here to help their state flourish against [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) as part of the Southern Revolts, in hopes to achieve a Shildo-wide success of the system seen in [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md).

The New Councilist Province makes its goals clear. It will work to establish Councilism and Social Democracy across Shildo until it is done. This fervent and ambitious goal brings it lots of attention even from nations that otherwise would not have conflict with it such as [Houvadia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/houvadia.md). It is placed in an awkward position inland, having a large border with Reimig’s Empire, the [Kingdom of Ithun](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/kingdom-of-ithun.md), the [The Republic of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-republic-of-ralean.md), and the Exiled Empire.

Despite its disadvantageous position, the New Councilist Province has two powerful allies. Its first and most obvious ally is Eresonia. Eresonia is already in direct ideological and economic competition with Reimig’s Empire especially after helping the province of Llofilot out of the Empire’s grasp and into its own economic ideals. It is true that Eresonia is sending weaponry, soldiers, and ideologs to Ralean, but these aren’t a direct result of the social governing body of Eresonia. These are the individuals of Eresonia recognising a cause that they support and deciding to support it.

Its second ally is the people of [The Guerrilla](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-guerrilla.md). Councilism advocates for the people to decide how they are governed, and the Guerrilla is an exact example of how that isn’t always councilism. Sometimes, it may be an agrarian commune. Many ideologs are going to the Guerrilla and educating its people of the history of the Two Peoples, and how it fell. The movement is extremely similar to that of the Formists, which initially lead to Councilism as an idea.
