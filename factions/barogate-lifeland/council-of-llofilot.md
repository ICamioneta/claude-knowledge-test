The Council of Llofilot is an independent state in [Terlaye](terlaye.md) that separates from [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) in 3470. It collaborates heavily with neighbouring [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md) to solve trade issues arising from the destabilisation of Terlaye's economy. Its city is [Llofilot](/wastelife/settlements/terlaye/llofilot) and the nation heavily relies on the port of Maseiras to function.
