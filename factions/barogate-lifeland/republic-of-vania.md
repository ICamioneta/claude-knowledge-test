# Government

Since the 3456 Constitution, the Vanian National Council has been transformed into the Republic of Vania. This was a large shift in Vania's legislative branch, however executive and judicial branches remain familiarly Councilist in nature. The government of Vania is this transformed Councilist structure trying to reform itself into a capitalist state from the councilist one that it inhereted from Eresonia. So, here's the run-down.

## The Republic

The Republic, formerly the Vanian National Council, is a recently transformed legislative branch. Formerly, the national council would consist of delegates from local councils. Now, members of the Republic's parliament are elected directly from electoral regions. Local councils still exist and perform all duties of local council, except they no longer send delegates to the national council. 

It is the responsibility of the members of parliament to propose legislation and laws, as well as elect from amongst themselves Vania's chief officers. Most officer positions are jointly held due to a lack of any majority in government. 

There are a few political parties with a significant number of representatives in parliament.

### Estateer party

The Estateer Party is the party of the Estateers, a political-economic class who own certain usually small portions of the country for them to privately develop. A common part of being an estateer is believing that this way of socio-economic organisation, being around private property, should be the primary way Vania is organised.

### Labour Party

The Labour Party is a party that formed around a coalition of workers and working councils to oppose the progress of the Estateers. While many do not objectively oppose to the Estateer's existance, they are all united in defending their own political-economic organisations which are those of a more traditionally councilist value. People who work organise that work around working councils, who organise labour in a distributively planned manner. Labour-organised land isn't private in any sense, it is public so that anyone may use it as they are able, provided they work with their community and their working councils to organise this development. 

### Free Trade Party

The Free Trade party is the party of many independents who gathered in hopes to secure a fair trading environment for themselves and others. They arose due to hostile economic action taken between Estateer and Labour working organisations where they would insularly limit trade from one another in attempts to hurt the other's economic potential. The free trade party, above all else, wants to enforce free trade between organisations regardless of political affiliation. The politicians of this party have an ear out for any and all independent's concerns while they are caught up in the war between the Estateers and Labour.

### The Guard Party

In an attempts to remain politically neutral, the Guards and the Vanian Military formed their own political party to represent their interests. This was to keep the guards politically aligned with one another, to stop the army from civil disputes and breaking apart. While most members of the military still have their ties to other political parties through their family, they at least recognise the importance of maintaining their own political front to keep the peace between the Estateers and Labour.

The Guard Party have very few actual seats in parliament, instead serving as a politically unified front for keeping the peace between the two main warring parties.

# Economics

Vania can be split up into roughly eight economic regions, each with their own specialised economies completing Vania as a fully self-sufficient economy.

For a brief overview of Economic Regions (ER),

- Karsir ER, administrative with new financial institutions and national orgnaisations. Luxury goods and high-end craftsmanship catering to new Estateer populations, and a trade hub on Karel.
- Kardel ER, massive port-city with practically all industry revolving around the huge trade moving through the mouth of Karel.
- Aseshty ER, bread-basket with huge grain and farm produce production supporting Vania and other places.
- Iboa ER, primarily woodworking and freshwater fishing.
- Cyaleta ER, mining towns and massive metalwork industries.
- Ebysarsir ER, huge textile industries of several flavours with a large rural population doing a lot of textile work outside of the main city.
- Aone ER, isolated and necessarily diverse industry that focusses on specific and hard-to-grow crops like dyes and magery ingredients, as well as luxury culture-themed produce.
- Fallen White ER, practically part of Naternegorna's ER providing the foreign city with food and metals. Also, because of this, the best trade route Vania has with Terlaye when trying to avoid the overpopulated port.

## Karsir ER

Karsir's primary specialisations are the government and administration, finance, national organisations, luxury goods and high-end craftsmanship, and a river trade hub. Politically, it's mixed between wealthy Estateers and motivated Labour workers, with independents and the guards equally mixed up in the brewing pot.

As the capital city, Karsir hosts the Vanian parliament. The administrative staff, scribes, national lawyers and notaries, and printing such as cartography and secure document storage all produce a certain demand that's fulfilled by other Karsir institutions and its surrounding towns in the region. 

Karsir is also full of finance and commerce, although most Estateer institutions are largely migrating over from Ebysarsir. Major merchant bankers and Estateer trading houses have set up shop, creating demand for well educated workers and even more paper and documents. The national treasury is also in Karsir, facilitating large-scale transactions and bills of exchange. The mint is also here to give Vania its currency.

With an influx of wealthy Estateer elite living and working here, luxury and artisan production caters to them. Karsir has numerous workshops for bespoke luxury goods such as fine furniture, elaborate textiles, jewellery, watches, silversmithing, and other artisanry. Not only do these institutions have their own unique demand, but there is demand for skill and apprentices. 

It's location and importance also sits in its location along Vania's river system. Karsir is located after all other major rivers have joined Karel, thus all trade moving from upriver to Kardel moves through it. Extensive wharves, docs, and warehouses line the river. Alongside this, a large workforce of porters, dockworkers, boatmen, and bargemen are engaged in dockyard activity. Karsir's small shipyards construct and repair river barges and lighter vessels.

With such a large concentrated population, Karsir is a major consumer of market goods from all the other regions. The service industry is huge. Inns, taverns, and livery stables tend to the travellers and other temporaries. A extensive resturant network caters to the workers and the elite. General stores work tirelessly to provide convenience to the population in their consumption.

## Kardel ER

Kardel's primary specialisations are maritime trade, national import/export, and ship building and repair. Politically, it's majority Estateer with a strong Labour workforce underlining Estateer's efforts of privatisation. 

Kardel's vital location at the mouth of the river Karel makes it the most convenient route for handling Vania's national and global trade routes. It must manage this trade across its deep water port. It does so with massive networks of warehouses. Freight forwarders and customs officials are important managers for this kind of work. Alongside bulk imports are some refinement industries, providing basic processing of import goods when they might enter the port raw.

Backing this port industry are huge shipyards, working on shipbuilding and maintenance to upkeep trade fleets. This industry alone demands from the surrounding area enough to justify its specialisation, with carpenters, sailmakers, rope makers, blacksmiths, and other ancillary trades needing to be local to back up this industry. Kardel's maritime services also upkeep trade fleets, such as chandeleries, pilot services, cartographers, and a large population of idle sailors, dockworkers, and navigators waiting for work. 

## Aseshty ER

Aseshty's primary specialsiation is large-scale grain production and food processing. Politically, it's the only majority independent region. These independents struggle to organise themselves against Estateer encroachment, and there is little Labour activity to aid them. 

The fertile plains are littered with staple grains and other produce from smart crop rotations. With no cities nearby, this grain is exported and forms a good supplement to local food production. Animal husbandry is also common to support the arable farming, providing dairy and textiles for local consumption and manure and draft for aid in farming.

This arable farming is often processed locally by stream water powered mills. Large salt and drying industries support food preservation, and fermentation for cheeses is common for this purpose too. Local blacksmiths are entirely concerned with the production and repair of local farm tools, and markets help move goods from the farms to the other regions.

## Iboa ER

Iboa's primary specialistation is timber and woodworking, with a good freshwater fishing industry backing it up. Politically, Iboa is a microcosm of Vania's larger conflict, almost a front line between Estateer, Labour, and the Independents providing the perfect setting for a video game. 

The extensive forests surrounding Lake Iboa and along its rivers are managed thoroughly for its timber production. This involves logging, transportation of logs via rafts on the rivers, water-powered sawmills, shipbuilding, and a large carpentry industry in the town of Iboan Lake itself. As a supportive industry, charcoal kilns produce fuel for domestic heating and metalworking industries.

Alongside a large woodworking industry is a well sized freshwater fishing industry focussed in the town of Iboan Lake . Fish are consumed fresh locally and preserved through smoking and salting for a wider distribution. To facilitate this, local boat building is a fairly large industry.

## Cyaleta ER

Cyaleta's primary specialsiation is mining, heavy metalworking, and tool and weapon production. Politically, it is almost entirely Labour dominated.

With rich mineral deposits across the ER, this region is dominated by mining operations for iron ores and other metals depending on local geology. Mine shafts, adits, ventilation systems, and steam pumps are common across the region. These raw ores are then transported to towns that alongside coals process iron in blast furnaces and other metals in kilns. 

The city itself, Cyaleta, is the heart of Vania's metal manufacturing. Foundries cast metal goods, forges powered by water hammers produce a wide array of wrought iron and steel goods. Smaller productions like hand tools and nails are common, and naval ironworks such as chainworks and structural ironwork occurs closer to the coast in Cyaleta's ER. Alongside common tools and ironworks, Cyaleta also produces most military equipment in Vania. 

Moving these heavy goods calls for specialised transport networks, including reinforced roads and strategically located loading points for river barges.

## Ebysarsir ER

Ebysarsir's specialisation is textile manufacturing and a diverse light industry. Politically, it's strongly held by Estateers. 

The most dominant textile industry is the wool industry, locally harvested from sheep across the Ebysarsir plains, woven on handlooms, and processed with water-powered fulling mills. Cottons are the second largest textile, with its ginning and cleaning happening nearby rivers, yarn formation, spinning, and winding. Dyeing and finishing requires special workshops that are frequent in the larger towns and the city of Ebysarsir. Bleaching and other finishing processes prepare these fabrics for the market.

Many Estateers use a putting out system, where merchants distribute the raw materials to rural households for spinning and weaving, then collect the finished cloth for central processing and sale. This is common in more rural areas of the Ebysarsir ER. While most fabrics are sold as bolts, an emerging industry for ready-made goods provides promising prospects for Estateers. 

Other than its specialised industry, Ebysarsir's ER has quite a diverse local economy with various small-scale industries making Ebysarsir rightfully Vania's second largest city. Mineral mines and processers closer to the mountains, good managed forestries in more remote parts of the ER, and local shipwrights for shipment across the Ebyjevin, Baldesrei, and Nidioi rivers.

## Aone ER

Aone's primary specialisation is specialised high-value agriculture. Politically, it's firmly Estateer controlled with no Labour and few independents. 

Aone maintains several cash crops, using its reputation as an area of premier culture to bolster the sales of its fine wools, quality dairies, and rich alcohols. Other than these industries, Aone's ER farms many other unique crops for dyes, medicinal herbs, and magery ingredients. 

Due to its isolation, Aone is required to be somewhat self-sufficient with its primary national trade routes being with Ebysarsir and by sea to Kardel and other ports. This need for a broader range of basic crafts has produced a highly diverse set of Estateer-owned industries holding up Aone's society. 

## Fallen White ER

Fallen White's ER is unique in that it barely serves the Vanian economy. Instead, it's more focussed on providing for the city of Nater's End. Nater's End, or Naternegorna, has historically been Shildo's largest city and was only recently overtaken by Cania a century or two ago. Given how Naternegorna sits at a hairpin point in Terlaye, much of its surrounding area is actually in other regions. The region of Fallen White has also historically been a part of Naternegorna's government, and only joined Vania following the centuries of Eresonian dominance over southern Terlaye. 

Naternegorna's population nears a million, alone it rivals any individual Vanian ER. Only about half of its intake is from Terlaye itself, with the other half coming jointly from Fallen White, the bordering Ralean region of Meishioladinum, and various nautical trade routes from Neble, Ralean, and Ereso. 

Fallen White's ER then is almost entirely agricultural near the border, catering to Cyaleta's economy near that border. Notably, away from the border, Fallen White is a large supplier of metals to Naternegorna with its access to the Vanian mountains.

Otherwise, the town of Fallen White itself is the place where trade with Terlaye and Naternegorna is organised. Trade deals established here are incredibly lucrative, and as Fallen White is a majority Labour-controlled area it's the people who get the high demand returns of Naternegorna.

# History

## The Hero of Vania

Just wanted to outline the backstory of the creation of Vania as a country. It occurred because of the Hero of Vania.

The Hero of Vania, and the creation of the country, occurred in 3330. It is now 3488. It has been 158 years since the foundation of the country.

The Hero of Vania stopped a necromancer from taking over Vania. Before this, the region of Vania was part of [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md), part of a provincial council based in Karsir. The region was incapable of being neglected by the rest of Eresonia because of the way the economy was set up, but it did fall into a bit of army neglect. A Necromancer by the name of Byrnt rose to power in the seclusion of the mountain range and saw this neglect of garrison from the Eresonian Army. Eventually, Byrnt gathered enough soldiers to begin his takeover, and he chose Karsir’s region for strategic reasons.

Byrnt began by taking a silent hold on a large span of area not populated by any large town, the land region between Aone and Port Kardel. And eventually, by praying on unsuspecting travellers, the region was avoided by all. This completely separated Aone from land travel, and since it had an undeveloped port it was then starved by supplies. Karsir’s council did request the Eresonian Military inspect the region, but it was put on a low priority so Karsir had to make do with its own garrison.

Byrnt needed to make himself known to the world, and he did this by attacking Aone. Aone was garrisoned up due to certain suspicions among the Kiyvan population, but it was ultimately unprepared for an invasion of thousands of undead. With a large town under his control, Byrnt cemented his power on Ereso, and even Barogate.

During the days of Empire, the three predominant Eresonian cultures were Vevran, Sapyat, and Kiyvan. These cultures were distinct and shared the Eresonian heritage. During the Second Eresonian Empire, much of the ruling classes were of Sapyat culture. The region known as the Iavan Coast used to be inhabited by a distinct Iavan culture which was a branch of the Vevran culture. As part of the Second Eresonian Empire’s legacy, this culture was suppressed and pushed out by migrating Sapyat ruling classes. A similar thing happened around Noraten and Port Higaro against the Kiyvan culture.

The town of Aone is home to a secret order created during this phase. The Versidillen exists to protect the Kiyvan culture. Nowadays, they promote the Kiyvan culture in areas traditionally Kiyvan. And among this order was a person who would come to be known as the Hero of Vania. They were living an ordinary life, or at least as ordinary as you get when you are part of an elusive order. But then, with Byrnt’s attack, they were called to action. The lair of the Versidillen remained untouched, and the only entrance and exit was through a long and winding tunnel that lead outside of the town.

The Hero of Vania’s journey through the exit was a long and arduous task. The challenges he faced, the dnial of his call to adventure. His mentor throughout, which was a close friend he saved from the attack. The end of the journey, the crossing of a threshold. The Hero of Vania learns of her innate talent in the world of magic, which his friend and mentor helps him find. He realises that the tunnels were still untainted by the Necromancer, and now they are in a hostile world.

The Hero makes their way to Ebysarsir to get an item that will help them to take out the Necromancer. As she arrives, she realises that Ebysarsir is going to be attacked by Byrnt, and takes arms agains the aggressors. This is his first encounter with the power of Byrnt, and he is overwhelmed. Although his magical powers are impeccable, they don’t stand up to the challenge that the undead army provide. During the battle for Ebysarsir, the Hero comes face to face with the villain, and almost dies in the process. She is saved by her friend and mentor, who lead him through the caves.

Having lost Ebysarsir and the majority of Kiyvan land to the necromancer, the Karsir Council tries to call on the rest of Eresonia’s military, only to learn that it is prioritising constructing barricades on feasible crossing points across the Vanian mountains. Karsir realises that Eresonia is not going to help it now. A few deserters from the Eresonian Military arrive, realising the importance of stopping the advance of death. Karsir holds the undead back at the river, because past it sits most of the Kiyvan’s settlements, and Karsir itself.

Port Kardel is exposed and difficult to defend, and a loss of Port Kardel potentially means Byrnt gains access to boats. If Port Kardel falls, Karsir itself will be next, then Iboan Lake, Cyaleta, Fallen White, then Byrnt will have the region.

The Hero of Vania doesn’t go to protect Port Kardel. He knows that he will lose again there. He must go back to the Versidillen and advance his power to the next stage. He takes the risk, journeying to Aone. When she gets there, and sneaks through the town, she comes to know that the lair of the Versidillen has been found out. Walking through the ruins, she finds the remnants of a valiant battle, and the bodies of her comrades. He cannot fight them, so his troupe must instead.

Then, in the order’s lair, behind a locked door, she finds a survivor. The leader of the order, who explains it all to her. Her power, and how it will defeat Byrnt. She exits Aone once more and goes into seclusion, where she trains with this knowledge. Her comradery continue the fight with one another, primarily they work to find the habits of Byrnt, and they help in the siege of castles protecting Port Kardel and the river.

The Hero of Vania returns, fully realised in their power. During a battle for a castle, they rush in and conquer the undead army without sweat. Then, the group and them go to Aone, the centre of Byrnt’s operations and where he is likely to be. The Hero of Vania must face that final challenge, and defeat Byrnt.

Then, the final showdown occurs. The Hero of Vania has an epic magic and close quarters battle with Byrnt. The image of the Hero of Vania floating with four arms appears. Two physical ones and two magical ones. The Seven-point star. The battle ends, and the Hero of Vania is victorious. But still, the zombies exist.  Byrnt controls superconduits, then superconduits control conduits, then conduits control the regular footmen. Kiling one above only stops coordination, so there are still armies under the control of superconduits, and they wander the land even if they don’t have a goal.

Refugees move back into the towns of Aone and Ebysarsir. Their population barely represents what they were before. The Eresonian military finally moved in to secure these towns and provide population to rebuild them, but the Hero of Vania, the last of the Versidillen, realises this is the Sapyats erasing Kiyvan culture in these regions. The Hero of Vania declares that the Sapyats will not have anything to do with the region. The country, Vania, is created. Eresonia forces judicial collaboration, which is fine by the Hero.

The Hero re-establishes the order of the Versidillen to protect the Kiyvan culture that still exists in the bogs. The flag of Vania is created by one of the Hero’s followers, and they invoke the image of the seven-point star.

## Vanian Independence

Following the defeat of the Necromancer, and the clear inability of the Eresonian military to help Vania, the Versidillen were able to kick up an independence movement that was massively popular and swiftly successful. 

In terms of government, not much was changed on the running of Vania compared to Eresonia. They adopted a more centralised army structure and implemented a new delegation-based officer selection, something the Eresonian military would mirror a few years later after re-assessing their own military which at the time of the Necromancer still had structures that reflected the Pri-Coner armies.

The independence of Vania, and the Necromancer crysis, would influence the early rise of capitalism in Western Vania. With the relocation of much of Western Vania's population, and the general devastation at the hands of the necromancer, western Vanian society was destabilised and the economies of the returning populace was in disarray. Most importantly, worker councils were almost completely disrupted, while the return of private entities and capital investment was almost welcomed by a post-devastation western Vania. 

This movement was somewhat backed by the government, which passed motions to support the re-establishment of Western Vanian economies. These included mutual funds, which were generally more accessible by private entities than freshly returning working councils due to the general ability of the private entities to quickly establish themselves.

## Early Capitalist rise in Western Vania.

Vania was formed at the beginning of Eresonian class conflict, and over 100 years from the foundation of an independent Vania, the Capitalist class of Vania made their work in consolidating power across the country. Although not explicitly a coordinated effort, slow gains were made across Vania by the Capitalists in bringing the Artisanry into Capitalist control. Slowly, private entities had been competing with independent worker councils - who traditionally ran society - and independent peasant farmers to grow their own profits and control over society. This would eventually culminate in the "New Constitution of Vania" or the 3450 Constitution of Vania, which is discussed later. 

Capitalism swept across Vania in a wave originating from Aone. Being the birthplace of independent Vania and the home of the now public Versidillen, the Capitalists of Aone were able to take the cause of independence and use the Versidillen's culturalist outlook to more or less enforce privatisation after the 30 year's removal of it during and after the Eresonian revolution. Ebysarsir was its second target, and Labour and the Peasantry barely put up a fight, leading to both Aone and Ebysarsir becoming Capitalist strongholds. 

It's at this time - roughly around 3410, that the Capitalists of Vania cooperated. The first mentions of a "political party" were discussed, and the term "Estateer" was used for the first time to refer to the private-property based ideology of the Capitalists in Vania.

The Estateer movement on Karsir was an explicitly coordinated economic attack on the workers and peasantry of the city. Companies would team up and form "mutual cooperation funds" that would support the establishment of new private corporations in the city and aid outside corporations in gaining a foothold in the city itself. With the idea of "supporting competition" in Karsir, the Capitalists very effectively bought out workers councils and outperformed them. This move was a crucial turning point in Vanian capitalism, representing the moment which the majority of Vania's productive capacity fell under Estateer control.

After the move on Karsir, once the Estateers were in the majority, the "new constitution" would be proposed, backed to the populace by a strong Estateer press active in all towns of the country.

## The 3456 Constitution

In 3456, the "new" constitution would finally be ratified by the Vanian central council with a majority vote of 65%. The motion passed easily because central council representatives were by majority the owners of private companies who had plenty of time on their hands for political work. Worker delegates, by comparison, were often balancing their political work with the management of their workplace. This Council majority was supported by a vast public approval by the Vanian population in regions where the media was almost exclusively controlled by Estateer companies.

The 3456 Constitution of Vania completely reorganised the highest stage of government. Previously, Vania had been a Councilist-adjacent system with minor reform suggested on its foundation by the independence movement lead by the culturalist Versidillen. On a surface level, the 3456 constitution remained a council democracy. However, the fine details of its national council, or the "parliament", was changed to support Estateers.

The 3456 constitution defined a reorganisation of the highest body of government. Instead of a national council operated identically to the regional councils and local councils but on the national scale, Vania would adopt a parliament where members of parliament, instead of being delegates of the people of a region, would become representatives regularly elected by the people of a region. An election cycle would occur every 4 years, allowing for frequent renewal while keeping people in position long enough to make plans and change.

The key difference in these systems is that a council delegate would often communicate with the people of their region in regional council meetings, whereas a representative would be elected during an election cycle and "represent" their elective's interests without much backwards communication. The promise is that elected representatives would put forwards a plan and then run their elective campaign based on that plan, then during an election cycle people would vote for their candidates based on how closely that plan adhered to their interests.

The 3456 constitution also outlined the establishment of a head of government. The marketing surrounding the 3456 constitution were heavily focussed on practicality, and the legislature around the head of government would be extremely careful to show them as a "first among equals" rather than the global regular autocrat or oligarch in head-of-state positions. The constitution would emphasise the role of the head-of-state as one of practical administrative necessity, a facilitator and coordinator of the proper functioning of government.

The head-of-state would be directly selected by parliament, which according to the constitution would mean they were effectively selected by the electorate who elect the members of parliament. An important part of bringing a head-of-state to Vania was ensuring constitutional accountability to the parliament, ensuring that any head-of-state would only elect with the vast majority confidence of the parliament. The person in the position of the head-of-state would effectively be selected in a similar system to the Councilist delegation, if a little more legislated and officially controlled. 

The exact vast majority set in the constitution was 75% - three fourths. If any candidate managed to get the support of this many parliament members, they would be elected and fulfil this role. Otherwise, the Parliament of Vania would run without a head of state, which would technically make it still a council. Any elected head-of-state would have a normal term length of 4 years - which would align with the election of members of parliament, after which the election would be held again. However, at any time, a vote of no confidence supported by 50% of parliament could be pushed to immediately remove the head-of-state and start a new election cycle for a head-of-state.

The head-of-state of Vania was named by the constitution as the "chief executive parliamentarian".

The first few election cycles of this system saw the same sort of people enter government. However, as time went on, a certain political shift occurred. Delegates of the workers now turned parliamentary representatives under the "labour" party were incapable of maintaining popular support during election cycles. Their specific campaigns focussed highly on their connection with communities and their continued attendance and reporting in local councils. This branding was effective, but did not stand out as better than the branding of a dedicated and focussed official who proposed their plan and stuck to it. While many labour MPs retained this initial branding, some workplaces and their councils realised the need of a dedicated member devout to the working cause that represented working interests in parliament.

Other parties would be established to represent other class interests, such as the Open Market party, an agrarian and rural party whose candidate MPs were often family members of peasant families supported by their families to have such a political career that vouched for their interests. The Guards is a relatively recent party formation established by members of the Vanian military (a highly democratic and social organisation with some government control), and the Artisan political party which is highly fringe and struggles to elect members of parliament, supposed to represent the skilled labourer.

What follows the 3456 Vanian constitution would be an all-out economic war waged by the capitalist class against the remaining working councils. Tactics would include acquisitions, out-competition, government intervention, new regulation, and out-marketing specifically focussed on diminishing the economic power of the working councils. 

Electorally speaking, Aone and Ebysarsir are still Estateer strongholds. Karsir is also strongly Estateer with some regions gaining Labour MPs. Iboan Lake is a point of contention and an ongoing economic conflict, and struggles to consistently elect MPs belonging to a certain party. Cyaleta is a strong Labour stronghold, while Fallen White often elects both Estateer and independent (or Artisan) MPs.

Parliament is yet to elect a chief parliamentarian, although many candidates have come close to the required popular support. The Estateers initially struggled to agree on a candidate, but currently still only have 60% control over parliament and would have to propose a candidate that at least 15% of non-Estateer MPs would also support.
