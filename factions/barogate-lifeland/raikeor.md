Raikeor is a faction of [Ews](ews.md) in the northern [Eresonian](/wastelife/regions/barogate/ereso) forest. It is formally part of [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md). It enjoys a great deal of autonomy and a mutually beneficial agreement of collaboration.
