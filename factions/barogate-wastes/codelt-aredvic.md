The Codelt Aredvic faction have land in the northern Heabe. A part of their land includes a coastline in high contention with the [Domuz Republic](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/domuz-republic.md). They are moderate with their tax collection from independents and benefit from trade and tithe. They are an egalitarian faction, they have no one ruler but instead the entire lead tribe makes decisions about how the Codelt should behave. The considerations of other tribes is always taken into consideration as the lead tribe has to act as a mediator between individual tribal interests.

One of the Codelt's most controversial activities is in their direct opposition to Deksie interests by offering escort to travellers and merchants wanting passage through Deksie and Hezan land. This comes in the form of additional payment to a Codelt tribe that would have the tribe escort them savely through Deksie and sometimes Hezan land. This comes with an additional fee, but it is often lower than paying Deksie tax. The protection also includes general defence against the Heabe's many other hostile factions and fauna. 

This protection service is a direct conflict against the Deksie, who claim it as an insult to their territory and Aredvic tradition. Active skirmishes occur between tribes where tensions rise. One one hand, the Codelt are taking advantage of the poor Deksie position and the Aredvic identity to stop the Deksie collecting passage tolls. On the other hand, the Deskie are an openly aggressive faction who charge extortionate amounts for travellers and independents and they need some recourse and Aredvic disapproval. 

Often, the protection fee afforded by travellers isn't worth the loss of life that the open conflict often causes. The Codelt view this as a matter of principle.
