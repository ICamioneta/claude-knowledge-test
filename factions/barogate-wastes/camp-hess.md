Camp Hess is a city state in [The Heabe](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/the-heabe) and a [Hezan Aredvic](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/aredvic.md) protectorate.

It was originally founded by [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) in 3085, but was conquered by the Hezan Aredvics in 3090. Since, it has served as a supply hub for people moving across The Heabe and Vugaix. It also serves the Hezan as a permanent settlement, paying taxes as part of a protectorate relationship with the Hezan.

# Politics

Camp Hess is officially a _protectorate_, dependant on the Hezan for both protection, supply, and land rights. The state also recognises the Hezan as the sovereign protectors. 

It maintains a miniscule civil service providing almost exclusively public guard and infrastructure management. These jobs are treated as any other occupation in the system. The Mayor of Camp Hess is in charge of all the organising and governing of the town. This president is directly appointed by Hezan leaders.

Although the Hessian-Hezan relationhip is almost exclusively subservient, there are small Hessian independence movements. These are discarded as unrealistic, as Camp Hess is not self-sufficient and could not survive a siege. Even minor economic action by the Hezan would cripple Camp Hess.

Although perfectly capable of it, further submission of Camp Hess isn't possible under modern Hezan and Aredvic structures.
