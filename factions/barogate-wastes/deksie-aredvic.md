The Deksie Aredvic faction are the only Heabe-locked Aredvic faction having no non-Aredvic borders apart from with the Basinal Highland which is unclaimed but Acvidaras de jure. Their borders are defined by sets of rivers, and they make an aggressive point to control the bridges of those rivers as a primary source of wealth. In addition to bridge tolls, they are the most aggressive when it comes to taxing independents often offering no leeway and demanding heavy loads of goods. 

They are a very traditionalist faction of Aredvics believing strongly in a joint Aredvic identity. They are also the most controversial and hostile of the Aredvic factions relying on direct conflict with their neighbours, often over the bridges, to enforce any semblance of power, control, and wealth. This is because their controlled territory is out of the way for any caravans moving through the Heabe and detours are always taken to avoid their hostility and heavy tolls.

Their internal political structure is autocratic. The leader of the leading tribe holds all power and all familial tribes are subservient. While autocratic, this structure isn't entirely centralised, but any Aredvic in Deksie land has the same set of issues and will likely follow the same ideas of hostility. 

The other Aredvics basically have to put up with Deksie hostility, affording them control over their river's bridges and not making too much a point of their poor treatment of the independents and other Aredvics. There is a limit to the affordability of the other Aredvics, but they realise that it is too much effort and death to take a bridge and that the Deksie need them a lot more than they do.
