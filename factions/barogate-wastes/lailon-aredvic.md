The Lailon Aredvic faction actually mostly operate in Darie. Upper Darie is sparsely populated but is in de jure and de facto Domuz Republic territory. While the Lailon control sections of land not claimed by the Domuz Republic, the vast majority of their activities is held within it.

The Lailon hold a unique relationship with the Domuz Republic, acting more like a gang than an honourable and self-governing tribal structure. They perform different activities when compared to other tribes. By controlling much of inland Darie they have access to its economy, which includes the trade of goods. They actively and legally participate in this trade, often dealing with other Aredvics.

The Lailon also have a bit of a criminal underbelly, facilitating illegal trades and illegally extorting their own taxes from travellers and sometimes even towns. These activities are highly discouraged by the Domuz Republic, and they are often combatted by the guard of the local province, Dariede. Although their actions are illegal, the land they control is quite far away from the real economic hubs of Dariede (Heuwei Afphftha and Hapheme). In the grand scheme of things, the value of the Lailon's legal activities outweigh their illegal ones as there just isn't enough money in the places the Lailon control to make a difference.
