The Natin Aredvic faction are present in a small south-western corner of The Heabe. The [Cosbon Complex](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/cosbon-complex.md) sits on its lands, but the institutions within it are independent from the Natin. The Natin does maintain a small landed presence in the town for their own purposes and to influence the city. The main source of Natin revenue is trade tariffs from the city, as well as other tariffs collected from travellers moving between northern [Terlaye](terlaye.md) to further into the Heabe, especially to the Basinal Highlands and Acvidaras. Because of tolls, many traders will take a detour to Ralean before entering the Heabe as they are likely to pay less tolls taking routes trough Hezan land. 

As an organisational structure, the Natin Aredvic are quite progressive, supporting the growth of Cosbon and its towns in any way it can. It still, by necessity, takes tax from other Heabe settlements and independent nomads. 

As for its organisational structure, the Natin Aredvic faction holds a council for important decisions in which the familial tribes get a direct say in the decisions of the lead tribe. The lead tribe is still an autocratic figure, its leader position being hereditary, but there is an understanding that to go against the will of the tribes would be a bad decision by the autocrat. Like this, the Natin aredvics are a form of parliamentary monarchy. While there is no formal constitution document, the "laws" on the tribe's council are more culturally based and formed on common agreement between all Natin tribes. 

# Natin Progressivism

As for their progressive nature, the Natin Aredvics champion a policy of development. There are very large movements to try and move away from a loose nomadic organisation and more into a defined centralised territory, officially controlling its lands with a soldiery that would be the tribe's new role, and watching over an official citizenry, the independent settlements and nomads, and institute Cosbon as its capital. 

Proponents of this movement flaunt that should the centralised government be formed, the Natin faction would be significantly wealthier and have much more control over their land. They could collect taxes more consistently, and by incorporating Cosbon as the capital of this new nation they would already have a unique and prosperous source of consistent income. This increased control would have the Natin Aredvics become a recognised faction to its neighbours in Terlaye and Ralean.

Opponents of this movement include Aredvic traditionalists and the free people of Cosbon. Traditionalists suggest that to become a recognised faction in the eyes of common civilisation would make them a target and pull them away from Aredvic identity. They suggest that their current guerrilla tactical approach to invaders is incredibly effective and forming more organised armies would necessitate difficult logistical issues and lesser the efficiently of each tribe's warriors as a whole. They also suggest that the lands Natin control do not have the resources necessary to build the infrastructure of an organised nation.

The Free People of Cosbon suggest that Natin control of Cosbon is not possible. The armed militia men of Cosbon would be easily overpowered by the Natin Aredvic on the open steppe, but within the confines of Cosbon and its supplying towns the Cosbon people say that they could easily hold off against a forced occupation. Such a siege would be bad for both parties: the Free People of Cosbon would fight for their freedom, and even if the Natin were to succeed, success would necessitate the destruction of large parts of the City of Cosbon and its supplying towns. Once occupied, Cosbon would be a weakened capital for decades until it can rebuild.

There is a small but rising movement that suggests that the City of Cosbon and the Natin Aredvic Tribes should form coalition for this new government. This way, the Natin get all the benefits of an organised nation and the City of Cosbon gets to keep its freedom from Natin taxes while also playing host to this new nation's economic and administrational activities. Many Natin Progressivists reject this claiming it would empower Cosbon in the new nation, demoting the Natin to nothing but a military force and a tax collector for the independents.

The independents don't get a say in this. They are literally powerless when it comes to the arguments of the economic behemoth of Cosbon and the military might of the Natin Aredvic.
