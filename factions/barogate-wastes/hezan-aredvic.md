The Hezan Aredvic faction is by far the largest and most influential. It is a traditional tribe with its organisation and culture. The familial tribes are organised themselves under parent tribes, then it is the father tribe that reports to the lead tribe and is expected to carry the messages down to its child tribes.

The Hezan Aredvic control the entirety of the border between the Heabe, Vugaix, and Dithas. This gives them a monopoly over fungal wood as a resource. Fungal wood is in competition with traditional wood types, often being traded in from Darie, Terlaye, or Ralean. Still, this complete control over Heabe-based transportation from Vugaix or Dithas makes the parent tribes and leading tribe very wealthy and powerful.

Despite their potential for complete trade monopoly, they often allow other Aredvics passage through their land and resource harvesting rights to moderate degrees. This decision is one of respect to the Aredvic name. Traders and non-Aredvics are almost always tariffed for travel over Hezan land. 

They collect taxes from settlements and nomads on a lenient basis, waving taxes if times are hard. They are only really capable of this because their sources of income through trade tariff and resource trading themselves is gargantuan.

The Hezan Aredvic maintain dominion over [Camp Hess](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/camp-hess.md). Camp Hess has their president assigned from among the Hessian populace by the Hezan leader, and relies on Hezan trade to supply it as a town. The Hezan collect tax from Camp Hess, but as they operate on a fixed-rate model system tuned to the general size of a traditional Heabe settlement, Camp Hess pays minimal dues.
