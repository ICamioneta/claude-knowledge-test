Nasarch is the only city in the [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix), and the only faction as the several [Mad](mad.md) towns generally do not associate with one another. 

The city state of Nasarch was built upon a large source of incredibly pure iron, a rare sight anywhere let alone in the middle of the Wastes. This iron is surrounded by Hematite, Silicon, and Quartzite. The City mines create all manner of tools and weapons for all Madmuz and adventurers in the city. The houses of Nasarch are primarily fungal wood, but some of the wealthier houses are made out of cut Quartzite brick. The palace features steel accents in its architecture.

Nasarch is the centre of Madmuz Difmuz culture in the Vugaix. The Vugaix is incredibly hostile, so all but a few of Madmuz living in the Vugaix live here. Nasarch’s permanent population is supplemented by a huge temporary housing market that covers a quarter of the city. The city holds renouned festivals celebrating Madmuz culture.

Nasarch and the [[wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/aredvic#Hezan|Hezan Aredvics]] have a sort of exclusive trade agreement that allows the Hezan to facilitate for Nasarch's trade, where goods are transported from the edge of the mushroom forest to Nasarch by its own employees.

Nasarch has a group of elite hunters called the Duporea (Dullporehajo, Darkrunners). The Duporea act as a sort of military for Nasarch, defending it from the dangerous creatures that give Vugaix the second-place title for most dangerous region.
