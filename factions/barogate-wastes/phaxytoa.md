Phaxytoa is the nation uniting the [Phax](phax.md) and peoples in [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate/kusao). Kusao is a great land covered in huge canyon valleys. Although most living occurs in the habitable and civilised valleys, there is a good volume of life above the canyons, on the hot and assaulting plateaus. Perhaps the most important possession of Phaxytoa is the Valley of Life, which sits and defines Kusao’s border with the region of Cronar.

# Settlements

Kusao is already a small region, just over half the size of Terlaye and Ereso, and sharing last place with Duela for Barogate’s smallest region. The liveable area for the Phax is made so much smaller as they are packed into the few livable areas the canyons provide. They built out all they could almost immediately, and since have focused on living tall rather than across.

## Cities

Kusao is home to five cities.

- Daerai is a sprawling city in the large open area that is the Valley of Live.
- Bamitho is in the same situation and sits further east.
- Kosfail is the westmost city.
- Caryx is Phaxytoa’s largest city, and its eastmost. It is not the administrational centre.
- Darcenyd is the administrative centre of Phaxytoa.

## The Valleys

Given their limited area to work with, the list of towns is quite short. There are still smaller settlements in the thinner regions of the canyons. These settlements are often grouped in which valley they are in. Valleys are defined by their rivers.

- Callyr is a town that sits at the first source of the River of Life, which flows through the Valley of Life. Callyr sits on a fork of rivers, with both leading to dead-ends.
- The Elae Valley and River is the westmost river.

	- Twynir lies at the end of this valley.

	- Daerai is at the mouth of Daf
	- Selleiaf is further up
	- Koraridd is at the same latitude as Kosfail and Daercenyd
	- The root of the Daf river is Fynaf.

	- Koraven is where Efil joins the Rhywnoi river

## Hidden Settlements

All of the settlements above are populated by majority Sosophax, but hidden under the surface are the famed Gofophax, who inhabit the caves of Kusao and are part of the Phaxytoa government.

## Plateu Settlements

The Plateus are mostly uninhabited thanks to their contrast with the valleys and the rivers, but some people do live up there, noticeably the Tolusphax and Allophax. These smaller settlements often don’t join the Phaxytoan government in its managings.

# Government

Phaxytoa is a pure democracy, exercising a planned, democratic centralism. The Phax have always organised their society in this way. Historically, it was the only way they could oppose [Kan-Koronar](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/kan-koronar.md) in the earlier days. It was effective, despite the harsh control of the monstrous empire Kusao was able to regain autonomy. This organisation became powerful after the end of Kan-Koronar, but instead of invading and taking over the weak, split Cronar, Kusao instead opted for peace.  Thanks to this display of peace, even after the Gan nations united once more they have shown limited hostility towards the Phax.

The Phax system of centrally managed economy works to an extent where goods are traded efficiently across the valleys and scarcity is scarce.
