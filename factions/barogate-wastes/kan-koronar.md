# History of the Old Empire

One of the most influential milestones of civilisation is the uniting of [Shildo](shildo.md) by Techia. But, one thing led to another, and this monolith Techia turned out to be a bunch of internal warring factions. At this time, [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar) and the Korongan had their own internal faction conflicts, until eventually the conflicts would dwindle, and a new power would emerge. Perhaps the first example of an absolute autocracy and empire in the world, Kan-Koronar emerged in 1700. This autocracy exercised its deadly efficiency. At its height in 2000, where it controlled Cronar and Kusao, but reached far into [Ralean](ralean.md) and held possessions in Kyenel and the unconquerable Llyhai.

But, subject to its own internal contradictions, it lost its colonies by 2100. The citizens of Kusao revolted in 2170. While its colonies in Llyhai were lost, it warred with the citizens of Kusao for the remainder of its time, a trend that still exists in Korongan society to this day. The Eresonian Empire was slowly gaining power in Shildo until it eventually bordered and began striking the Kan-Koronar in decline. The Eresonian Empire would soon spell the end of Kan-Koronar by winning the Siege of Leuhb.

It wasn’t long until then when Kan-Koronar succumbed to a succession crisis and split into several smaller factions. To many, this spells the end of Kan-Koronar, as the next 800 years of history saw the steady development of the situation in Kronar into what it is now.

In 3210, Cronar united once more under a new system of government detached from the expansive empire of the past. This is the government that exists today. Although it has little connection to the original Kan-Kronar, Kan Kronar holds a special place in Korongan culture as most powerful empire in history. So, the new organisation is named after it.

# Government

The Kan Koronar government is a warlord republic. When it was being formed, the emperor was conquering warlords by offering them seats in his government or death. When warlords chose to go to war with the emperor, he had them killed and then appointed their next of kin into the position of power, holding the warlord’s old lands.

The emperor never managed to appoint a next of kin. When he died in 3294, the republic of warlords he had created decided to continue ruling the nation as a collected body. The emperor had made a brutal effort to settle the differences of the warlords, and those that still stood chose that the united Kan-Koronar would not fall to another succession crysis.
