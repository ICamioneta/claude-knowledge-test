The City of Hegrel is the de facto government of [Acvidaras](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/acvidaras) and, as the only government structure present in [Gordolmonre](/wastelife/regions/barogate/gordolmonre). It sits in the bowl, which is all often regarded as Hegrel de-jure.

The government of the City of Hegrel manages the city of its namesake as well as the five supplying towns: Mihusta, Kensta, and Acvista.

# Industry

Farming in Hegrel’s modern days does not serve the same purpose as it did at the city’s founding. Although originally built to feed adventurers, it now feeds the city (as well as its temporary inhabitants). The main industry of Hegrel in the modern day is to exploit a large source of iron, copper, and nickel that were prospected in the mountain right next to the city itself. These mines are a hundred years old by now, and are believed to be large enough to serve Hegrel's demands for centuries.

These mines fuel many crafts based in Hegrel, but the main one is tool smithing. Hegrel has found it extremely lucrative to create useful tools purely out of metal. Nadens nomads often stop at the city to trade for said tools with whatever it is they found. These tools are made entirely out of metal, handle, and all, because of the scarcity of wood that Hegrel faces.

For the same reason that all tools are fully metal, all of Hegrel’s buildings are almost entirely made out of stone. For the same reason, they are often only one storey tall, but some buildings do manage to get themselves an extra few stories with the limited growth available. This is the main use of Hegrel’s wood.

Hegrel maintains a small forestry to its west. The Heabe's soil and climate is incredibly unfit for trees, so this forestry requires constant attention from wizards who use certain Hidosis methods to accelerate and facilitate the growth of these trees. Because of this costly process, wood in Hegrel is in high demand, and the wood grown from these trees are used exclusively for necessary structural purposes, tool making, and as a luxury for those who can afford it.

# Economy

Hegrel to this day still primarily uses a barter economy, where goods are directly traded for other goods. Some in Hegrel do accept certain currencies, knowing that they can use these with other tradespeople and adventurers who will send this money back to the country of origin. The government of Hegrel is attempting to push its own coinage which comes in two different coins. The first and oldest are a nickel-chromium coinage called Owens (two Owens make a GS), where the chromium metal is imported from Cosbon. The second coinage was only introduced this year (3488) and is made of local copper and nickel, called a Tries ( twelve tries for a GS). 

# Government

The government of Hegrel is a hereditary republic full of people who claim to be descendants of Hegrel herself. Every five years, each of the members may propose a plan for the city’s next five years. Then, the people of the republic choose the proposals that they support, and then the proposal which has the most support is enacted upon. The most recent plan enacted by this system was the introduction of the Elas coinage.

More recently, the other towns wanted to have a say in what happens in the city, because the plans that occur in the city often affect the towns as well. So, the government invited a few of the Nadens elite into their republic, which was a monumental change. The Nadens in the republic, however, all have a claim to being direct descendants to the founders of each of their towns. So far, it’s been better for the people of each town to have some representation but in reality, the votes of the leaders from those towns hasn’t changed what types of plans are enacted.

# History

Much of the Mortal Realm’s history is a cloud. For example, the Ancient Ruins: they are present across all parts of the Wastes except for most of the Heabe (see [Cosbon Complex](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/cosbon-complex.md) for the main exception). They are, however, present in an extremely ruined form across Gordolmonre. As far as any mind knows, these ruins have always been here.

The first feature to be established in the region was the now city then camp, Hegrel. It is written in some old book stored in the library there that Hegrel was the name of a good farmer and a trader, who farmed the few farmable lands around and sold the produce to [Nadens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/nadens) passing through the area, or other muz seeking further into the mountains from the Heabe. Although it was a small farm, Hegrel expanded.

Hegrel began to build housing to house both new workers and farmers in the region and also as let-outs for travellers staying in Hegrel. These houses were initially built from dirt, but later on more advanced materials were used such as wood and stone.

A Nadens quarry worker by the name of Mihu was working in [Darie](/wastelife/regions/barogate/gazi-lale-darie) at the time of Hegrel's founding. Hearing of the low-quality dirt infrastructure, he and many of his fellow workers abandoned their quarry in Darie and travel to Hegrel in order to establish the region's own masonry company. Their quarry and masonry buisness was successful immediately on its foundation as many of Hegrel's housing owners desperately wanted their lets to be made out of better materials. Mihu's legacy survives to this day in the form of a town named after them.

Hearing of Mihu’s success, many Nadens also settled in the area seeking to fulfil other holes in Hegrel’s trade. The second of these adventures was lead by a Nadens nomad called Ken. She, using a nomadic technique passed on by her family, started up a water purification plant to the north of Hegrel. With this she too grew powerful in the region, providing a much-needed and somewhat unique consistent supply of clean and useful drinking water.

The next Nadens-lead adventure for filling gaps in Hegrel's markets was a group of Nadens seeking to spread art and culture to the region. This adventure was a lot less profitable, but the town that they founded eventually became Hegrel's town for the richer citizenry and travellers. 

Hegrel as a town exists to facilitate further expeditions and adventures into Gordolmonre for adventurers and tradesmen alike, but it has far outgrown this purpose and has become the staple city for the entire region. A new town further into Gordolmonre was formed as an expansion of Hegrel's original purpose, but specifically focussing on preparing for expeditions and adventures into the area's silver ruins which are locally known as Gatesweep.
