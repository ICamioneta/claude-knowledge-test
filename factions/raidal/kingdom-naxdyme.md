Kingdom Naxdyme, or otherwise known as "Red Storm" is a feudal monarchy operating in central and western islands of Raidal. Each island is seen as a fiefdom held and managed by a lord, who is a vassal of the King of Raidal. Kingdom Naxdyme is one of the more powerful factions in Raidal, coming into direct conflict with the [Domuz Republic](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/domuz-republic.md). Adding to this conflict is how the majority species for the kingdom, the Caran species, are capable of breathing underwater for a long period of time, making them capable of opposing the Domuz's aquatic prowess. Their navy is also good to boot.
