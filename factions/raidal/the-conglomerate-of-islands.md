The Conglomerate of Islands is an island conglomerate situated in the eastern Raidal Islands. It has a councilism government that was actually formed before [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md), drawing on egalitarian ideals between the islands and using ideas of the Formists of the Two Peoples.
