The Independent Raidal Islands is a descriptor used to refer to the numerous [Islands of Raidal](wastelife/regions/islands-of-raidal) that are self-governing in their local archipelago or island. This topic also covers Island Conglomerates, which are small independent factions of islands where the islands under the government are spread further apart than a single local area.
