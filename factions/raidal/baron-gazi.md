Baron-Gazi is an autonomous colony state of the [Domuz Republic](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/domuz-republic.md) Baron-Gazi is a relatively small colonial project, only holding a couple of Raidal islands such as its capital territory, Llynllanes. Its connection to the Domuz Republic makes the faction's military significantly stronger, as it can call upon Gazi Lale Darie resources at any time they need them.
