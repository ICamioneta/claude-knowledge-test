Nomyn Dop Council, or The Council at Nomyn Dop (the CND), is an authoritative city council of Teclin that acts as a centralised government for the Shinkuçin people. 

Although it does have sovereign territory, the CND acts more like a trade guild. Its specific trade is the exploration of the silver ruins. 

The silver ruins in Mothdao occupy much of its highland, notably Hebas Deryd, Asenhal, and the continent's many mountainous areas. They are the tip of a vast subterranean network of these ancient ruins. Vast undercities connected by uniform tunnels, stretching for thousands of kilometers beneath the desert. 

The Teclin are a class of silver-wizards who can interact with the ruin's systems. It is a learned position, but no more important to Shinkuçin society than the adventurer or humble trader.

The Shinkuçin have been exploring silver ruins since their creation by Medr 2500 years ago. They acted as independent exploring groups and camps until the Teclin pacified Mekẃier in 2430. Mekẃier formed the first unified hub of Shinkuçin, a known place they could share their knowledge and skills. A few more ruins were pacified until Nomyn Dop, deep in Hebas Deryd, was pacified in 2610 without a Shinkuçin stepping foot anywhere near the surface. 

Knowledge of the Sílath is common amongst Mothdaoans. Less known is knowledge if the Unvahn, a much slower and more dangerous version of the Sílath that connects many more silver ruins than the Silath. With this underground network of tube-tunnels spanning all of Mothnial, Nomyn Dop sat as a natural centre-platform where many Unvahn routes converged. 

With the resurrection of the Sílath, Nomyn Dop became an even more crucial hub of trade and activity. Not just for the Shinkuçin, but to the many other people utilising the commercial Sílath routes.


Teclin are organised into conclaves. Each conclave either roams or is dedicated to a specific ruin. Conclaves each have a mandate, approved by the CND, limiting their actions with the outside world. Mandates essentially limit the extraction of artefacts and the over-extension of Shinkuçin conclaves. 

Surface sovereign territory is always around a ruin, usually negotiated with other nearby claimants and purchased with the establishment of trade. The largest single bit of surface territory owned by the CND is around Nahiva, which takes advantage of Aerlada's fertile soils to extensively farm food for the under-colonies
