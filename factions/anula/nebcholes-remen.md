---
title: Nebcholes-Remen
description: The Triality of Cutkind
---

Nebcholes-Remen is a dual monarchy of two royal houses, Remen and Nebcho. Its lands span across Calaeddodd into both Neble and Thagryene. These royal houses are of the [Chōcut](wastelife/species-of-person/cut/chocut) and [Cafcut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/cafcut) species respectively, and these species are generally seen as a more noble and royal species (Nebcholes-Remen being one of a few places practicing speciesism in 3488). The other majority species, the [Lescut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/lescut), are often designated to peasantry.
