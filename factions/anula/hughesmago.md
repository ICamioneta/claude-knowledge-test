Hughesmago is a nation of primarily [Dancut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/dancut) in [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene). While it has close economic ties to [Uthec](wastelife/factions/anula/uthec.md), it is an independent land that often displays opposing interests to Uthec. Its government, policies, and diplomacy are openly attacking Uthec, and the people of Hughesmago often attempt to sieze land from [Homesteads](wastelife/factions/anula/homesteads.md) to the east. In the past, the country has had irredentist wars against Uthec nations.
