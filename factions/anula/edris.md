---
title: Edris
description: Technocratic nation of science.
---

![Edris Nation.png](/wastelife/images/flags/edris-nation.png)

Edris is what most people think of when they imagine a government of Vezoen. It sets all of the stereotypes for the [Nudos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nudos), in that it is a nation full of scholarly research. The country prides itself as a pure meritocracy, where the executive leader of the nation is also the head of an acclaimed scientific university. They have little in terms of military as they have no aggressive neighbours, but they upkeep a small military to aid its neighbouring [Roughwalk Imperium](wastelife/factions/anula/roughwalk-imperium.md) in its defence against the [Empire of Plaen](wastelife/factions/anula/empire-of-plaen.md).
