Brwrd is a city and faction of [Diacut](/wastelife/species-of-person/cut/diacut) in the centre-east of [Neble](/wastelife/regions/anula/neble). Its existence is in direct opposition to [Codia](wastelife/factions/anula/codia.md), and they are in a constant war where both sides are fighting for their existence. Brwrd in its modern capacity is a recent development, and it has essentially already become the first nation to industrialise as the industry of Brwrd drives its [Ews](ews.md)-fighting war machine.
