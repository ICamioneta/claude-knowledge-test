The Union of Thagryene Countries is the largest nation on Anula. Despite this, it is heavily federalised and the local governments (often called “countries”) that comprise it are highly independent from one another. This high federalisation leads to a divided military. Another political issue within Uthec is the lack of a land connection with the country of Eynon, which is fought with against [Nebcholes-Remen](wastelife/factions/anula/nebcholes-remen.md). The government of [Hughesmago](wastelife/factions/anula/hughesmago.md) is also an antagonistic faction complexifying the political landscape of Uthec. 

There are 6 countries of Uthec. First, you have Merida, which is often seen as the primary and most important of the countries. The government of Uthec is stationed in Merida's capitol, Merida. There is the navally powerful Cyfrin, the also very naval but otherwise politics-focused Leision, the social and multicultural Ris, the capitalist Bach, and the industrial Eynon.
