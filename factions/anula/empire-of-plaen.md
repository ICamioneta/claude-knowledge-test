The Empire of Plaen is a highly militaristic empire on the northern Neble region Plaen, or the Plains. It is an aggressive nation, constantly attacking its neighbour, [Roughwalk Imperium](wastelife/factions/anula/roughwalk-imperium.md), as part of a cultural tradition to prove strength and boldness.
