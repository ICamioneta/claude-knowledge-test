---
title: Cutland
description: hardyy war-like tribes
---

Mountains are usually considered their own region, but here Cutland, which resides entirely within the mountains, is closer to Thagryene than its other neighbor, Codia. Cutland is not a central government, rather just a nation that informally exists between the several Cut communities that live in Calaeddodd. Given how Calaeddodd are also the home of the Löfyn, Cutland is _quite_ dangerous.
