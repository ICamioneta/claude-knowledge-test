Carehope is a cult the size of a provincial government in Neble on the southmost eastern border with [Thagryene](/wastelife/regions/anula/thagryene). They share a border with [Western Neble Imperia](wastelife/factions/anula/western-neble-imperia.md), the [Uthec](wastelife/factions/anula/uthec.md) oligarchy of Cyfrin, and the [Shurb](wastelife/factions/anula/codia.md#shurb).

# History

The history of Carehope begins in Kyenel, in Vaiskeron specifically. The head-of-religion at the time, Vais, began proselytizing in south-eastern Neble. Unfortunately for him, this did not sit well with the nobility of [[wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/shaikeron|Kyeron]]. Alongside a number of other scandals and conflicts with the nobility, Vais was eventually exiled. He decided to go to the place where his missionaries were proselytizing and decided along with his missionaries to set up a cult, believing him to be the Immortal that the religion talked about.

But, as a very mortal man, Vais did eventually die. But, instead of end the cult there, the missionaries apologized that Vais truly was immortal, and lived on in the spirits of the cult members.

# Members

The members of Carehope believe themselves to be the spirit of the Immortal, who in this case is Vais. They are a collective entity that transcends individuality and thus their individual mortality. The members have a strong sense of devotion to the organisation, to each other, and to the cause of Carehope. Because of this, they actively scorn personal autonomy, diversity, and identity. They refer to themselves as “Continuates”, “Continuate”, and such.

Members of Carehope, in belief that they are the spirit of the Immortal, believe themselves righteous and morally correct. Their morals are often handed down to them by the main body of the organisation, who state that any diverging moral from a Continuate is an idea supplanted by a Demon and should be righted immediately. Although they have no tolerance within the cult, there is some tolerance to diverging morals of the members outside of the cult as they do not yet carry the spirit of the Immortal and are still under the influence of the demonic Gods.

The Organisation, in addition to their mandated morals, have other codes of conduct that are enforced by local spiritual leaders. These codes regulates the behaviour of the members. Firstly, there is the uniform of Carehope. Restrictions on wear are tightly enforced. Clothing must be cloth-based, and only dyed on the grayscale and in shades of blue and orange. In members with facial hair, it is only allowed in five types, and only by those higher in the organisation. Facial hair or not, grooming is required almost every day to keep up the good appearance of the Immortal. Any deviance and letdown is a member disconnecting themselves from the spirit.

As the spirit of the Immortal, it is a member’s purpose to prepare for the revelation of the Immortal, an upcoming war the Gods will wage against peoplekind. They, the immortal, will participate in the final battle against the Gods and their Esten. They trust the Organisation in being the greater functions of the Immortal’s spirit, who guide them in their actions. Constructing buildings such as temples and houses, participating in agriculture and gathering resources, Extending the spirit of the Immortal by bringing more people into Carehope, and fighting that which threatens the Immortal’s continued existence in the form of external bodies.

Members of Carehope are assigned two syllable names at random, which is usually enough variety to avoid doubly-naming people.

# Rites and Practices

- A twice-a-day meditation where they commune with their fellow community members remotely and strengthen the spirit of the Immortal, their own spirit.
- An on-demand ceremony where a local leader reconnects their body with the spirit of the Immortal following a perceived disconnect.
- A weekly ceremony where everyone sings songs of their spirit, community, and their pledge to their goal.
- A seasonal celebration of the tasks completed during the season, and a special song for the occasion.
- Yearly visits to the beach upon which Vais first came to Carehope.
- A harmful initiation ceremony. A Spiritual severance from one’s own spirit, and the subsequent filling of the body with the Spirit of the Immortal. This is usually delivered as a painful remedy that supposedly severs the individual spirit, followed by another remedy that stops the pain from the last and supposedly connects the body with the spirit of the Immortal.

# Prayer

Short prayer to be said before morning meditation and after evening meditation: “Join together, continuate, the eternal Spirit of the Immortal.”

Morning prayer after meditation: “The Immortal awakens for another day.”

Evening prayer before meditation: “The day ends and The Immortal rests.”

# The Echebuth Protocol

In 3452, House Cyfrin and the Imperium of Wyn signed the Echebuth protocol. By all accounts, it was a plan of war to "liberate" the people of Carehope by establishing it as a Wyn protectorate. While Wyn wouldn't spare the expense on their own, the protocol established that if war were to occur, Cyfrin would join as an ally of Wyn in exchange for a number of ports and trade rights. 

Although Wyn were once confident in a future war, rising complications with Shurb have halted any invasion plans.
