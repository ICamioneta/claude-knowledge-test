The gods, or more properly the eternals, are a typr of being that exist in Eternity. The gods are often categorised by age.

The Gods keep time relative to the Annual. They counting years in terms of the Mortal Planet to revolve around its sun, beginning with the first observed annual in -16225. The C.E. (Corben Erum, the Shildoan calendar) year 0 is the 16225th Annual, denoted A16226. The Cyfun Annual refers to the annual meeting of as many Eternals as can attend starting from the first time Cyfun hosted such an event in -4000 (A12225).

The Eternals are split into different categories, usually corresponding with the circumstances of their existence (found, ascended, or born) and the era of their creation. Also, in the list below, unless otherwise specified they came about due to a natural event of their domain i.e. the God's domains predate them.

I'll also provide a short description of every Eternal just to help get me started. 

## Domains

Eternals generally promise themselves to a certain domain: an aspect of reality or society that they just like a lot. Their activities revolve around their domain, and their realms are seen as a reflection of their domain.

## Categorisation

There are a few extra ways to categorise eternals based on non-concrete things. Group, for example, is a loose category describing what their domain generally is:

- Information
- Society
- Art
- Vice
- Fate
- Death
- Life
- Elements

There is also they category of what they do that pertains to their domain:

- Cataloguer, those who primarily catalogue what there is of their domain as it expands naturally
- Indulgent, those who seek to interact with and experience their domain every moment.
- Disseminator, those who actively coax mortals into their domain.
- Autonomous, those that generally agent on their own, doing things as they please, often in relation to their domain.

These two categories are called _group_ and _type_. 

## Original Eternals

The Original Eternals were the first six eternals to enter Eternity. They are the ones who figured out how to create their own realms, manifest, and they found the Mortal Realm and Sanded Realms before any other Eternal entered Eternity.

### [Adnabyd](/wastelife/eternals/adnabyd) E0

_The Eternal of Knowledge, found E0_

Knowledge has meant a lot of things to Adnabyd throughout known history. Like many others, he often didn't care for mortals in the pursuit of knowledge. However, in the modern day, he's viewed quite favourably. He and his Esten mostly just cooperate with Mortals to catalogue things. Adnabyd works with mortal universities. 

### [Medr](/wastelife/eternals/medr) E0

_The Eternal of Skill, found E0_

Medr is by far the most skillful in everything without a dedicated god. She spends most of her time working directly with the most skillful mortals, checking up on them. Her Esten do most of the mentoring and training of developing skills. She's had some controversy and still does. She's associated with figures cultivating more harmful skills in fairly recent memory, but she tries her best to keep herself in good moral light. 

Despite being one of the oldest gods, she somehow maintains a youthful vigour. Perhaps Eternal youth is a skill?

### [Ymdig](/wastelife/eternals/ymdig) E0

_The Eternal of Behaviour, found E0_

Ymdig is an experimental socialite, and likes to poke all types of creatures to get fun and unique behaviours out of them. They are more of an introvert socially, preferring to watch other people interact over interacting themselves. Ymdig has somewhat therapeutic interests when it comes to the behaviour of people, but chaos is also a behaviour that Ymdig enjoys.

### [Bwydo](/wastelife/eternals/bwydo) E0

_The Eternal of Feeding, found E0_

Bwydo has come a long way from his origins. First he was a god of just eating to sustain ones self, before slowly pivoting into the modern ultimate chef of flavour he is today. Manifesting mortal tongues and experiencing the unique pleasures it can afford. He's not pretentious but genuinely experimental. Not everything he makes is a five-star taste either, he's privy to just slapping things together for the unique novelty.

### [Riw](/wastelife/eternals/riw) E0

_The Eternal of Relationship, found E0_

Perhaps the greatest testament to how Eternal's personalities are anything but that. Riw went from the god of reproduction to a god of sex and pleasure to now a god of relationships in the modern era. Riw's an observer and a cataloguer, but whatever her obsession might be at any time, she also likes to experiment with it and push its boundaries. 

### [Cyfapheb](/wastelife/eternals/cyfapheb) E0

_The Eternal of Communication, found E0_

Cyfapheb is one of the Eternals who is pretty good at sticking to the same meaning of their domain throughout history. They experiment with language, in all forms. Both from people and not from people. They are both an avid participant, for the hands-on experience, and a scholar who likes to catalogue and imagine. Cyfapheb knows all of the languages, and their Esten help other Eternals learn popular languages for when they manifest. 

## The Second Eternals

The Second Eternals, or the Antonyms are generally understood by mortals to be "bad" eternals, and while they are kind of polar opposites to the original eternals, mortal morality breaks down when you're a hyperconscious god.
These gods "came into Eternity" sometime after people started doing their thing. The general understanding is that gods just sort of start to exist when something notable of a certain domain occurs.

### [Gwalloph](/wastelife/eternals/gwalloph) -9000

_The Eternal of Madness, found -9000_

Madness has changed forms significantly over the millennia, much to the enjoyment of Gwalloph. They aren't super mad themselves, but whenever they communicate or when they manifest for mortals they often like to take on the persona of a muttering fool. He likes driving mortals mad, which is a big part of why mortals don't really like him.

### [Sirphni](/wastelife/eternals/sirphni) -9000

_The Eternal of Lethargy, found -9000_

No-one really knows what Sirphni does daily. It's so irregular for an Eternal to genuinely spend their time doing basically nothing. She does have hobbies, but they're very mortal like crochet or painting. She's an Eternal of leisure, and will avoid any effort at all if she can. Apparently, she barely even has a realm. She likes hanging out with mortals. In history she was seen as a temptress, but modern understandings see her as the chill dude she really is.

### [Diofalux](/wastelife/eternals/diofalux) -9000

_The Eternal of Causality, found -9000_

Diofalux was seen as the God of Accidents, but now most people understand it as the Eternal of Causality that it is. Diofalux is still plenty capable of causing "accidents", and often does, even sometimes not as a study in causality. Causality isn't just little things, but larger systems and even the causes of the Mortal Realm itself. As for conversation, it doesn't much like it. 

### [Glinphe](/wastelife/eternals/glinphe) -9000

_The Eternal of Greed, found -9000_

She used to be the God of Gluttony until civilisations invented money and wealth-hoarding. She likes the hoarding and openly advocates for it, but she remembers her gluttonous roots and also likes people eating lots of food at their own leisure. She's a fairly simple character, she likes some people having lots of things. For this reason, she's not usually seen as morally good by mortals.

### [Maleisus](/wastelife/eternals/maleisus) -9000

_The Eternal of Schadenfreude, found -9000_

Maleisus is one of those gods whose domain and day-to-day actions have so little compatibility with the morality of people that they have no concern for a good mortal image. They are malicious. They enjoy people's suffering, in a very literal sense. They follow and watch mortals who do the same. They glee at torturous acts. They've been doing this consistently for 12000 years, and they're not going to stop. The only real evolution of their domain is in the general advancement of torture and pain that mortals conjured up themselves.

### [Gwrphcim](/wastelife/eternals/gwrphcim) -9000

_The Eternal of the Antisocial, found -9000_

Another eternal whose domain is so morally objectionable that they revel in their poor public image. Gwrphcim specifically likes the manipulation of the social in harmful ways. People using words to influence others and make them bend under their own will. Gwrphcim is also proactive. He helps and encourages antisocial behaviour, in a sort of cynical overview of the purpose of the social for ones own gain. Unsurprisingly, he's partners in crime with Maleisus, taking on the more social side of suffering. 

## Gods of Creation
These are gods that were found during the Era of Creation, -15000 to -6000. Specifically also not including the second Eternals.

### [Hell](/wastelife/eternals/hell) -8500

_The Eternal of the Sun, found -8500_

Like many similar Eternals, Hell's domain is tied to a mortal phenomena, specifically the sun of the mortal world. There are other astral bodies similar to the mortal world's sun, but it's not really about physical object but more about what it represents. Life and creation. Happiness, the dawn. Hell takes on this radiant personality, and even enjoys Mortal worship to some degree. 

### [Enw](/wastelife/eternals/enw) -8500

_The Eternal of Names, found -8500_

Enw is more properly the eternal of proper nouns and identity. Enw enjoys exploring the full implications of identity and self definition, like all god's this extends beyond just people. As for the God of Proper Nouns, Enw asks one simple question: is a hot dog a sandwich? Identity is to be found in all things, says Enw, and Enw takes a very involved approach to stochastically understand this element of social existence. Enw is very argumentative.

### [Dim](/wastelife/eternals/dim) -8400

_The Eternal of Void, Found -8400_

Dim is a very peculiar character. Although generally accepted as the eternal of existentialism and those types of themes, Dim themselves insists that they are not an eternal of anything. Not even an eternal of nothing, as they would argue that nothing is in fact something. They are merely the eternal of "". They do not have a realm, rather they did until some other Eternal stated that a realm was something. Sometimes, they even insist that they are not a thing. Dim has kept this act up for 12000 years.

Perhaps refreshingly, they are in an eternal relationship with Prydferth, who offers an acceptable social pathway into Dim, or more accurately a spokesperson. 

### [Lleh](/wastelife/eternals/lleh) -7030
_The Eternal of the Moon, found -7030_
Best buddies with Hell (and intentionally the reverse text of Hell), Lleh's domain isn't necessarily tied to the physical rock orbiting the mortal realm, but the ideas that it represents. Timidity, dusk, reflection, and _the menstrual cycle_. Because, for some reason, Wastelife's moon has a similar cyclic period to our own. Socially, she's a bit of a mixed bag. She's internal and quiet when the mortal moon is waning, and outgoing and loud when it's waxing. For an Eternal, this is extremely chaotic and rapid behaviour. She often has an Esten forewarn other Eternals she meets as to what the current stage of the mortal moon is.

### [Dyddiad](/wastelife/eternals/dyddiad) -7000
_The Eternal of Calendar, found -7000_
The most punctual Eternal. Obsessed with time and events, they have this single eternal project called "The Timeline" which is in essence a chronological reference table of everything that has ever happened. It's a branching project, with area focussed timelines and the overarching "most important events" timeline. Socially, Dyddiad is very precise. He only says what needs to be said, when it must be said. He chooses his words carefully, but does so fast. He also likes fitting into the old eternal stereotypes that other older Eternals viciously defy.

### [Cynnag](/wastelife/eternals/cynnag) -6800
_The Eternal of Growth, found -6800_
Growth! Growth at all costs! Cynnag is a bit of a fanatic, nothing like how the pe-medieval civilisations imagined him, although they had the God of Agriculture to pray to more specifically for the wellbeing of their crops. Cynnag can be difficult at times because of this obsession of things getting bigger. His mortal project is also no secret - "The Everlasting War" was an ancient written lore documenting the endeavours of Cynnag and Pydred regarding the growth and destruction of the mushroom forest on Barogate known as Vugaix. This forest didn't exist before Cynnag, and if left unchecked he'd probably find a way to make all of Barogate's desert a growing forest. 

### [Pydred](/wastelife/eternals/pydred) -6800
_The Eternal of Decay, found -6800_
Cynnag's evil sister, Pydred is actually often seen as the more reasonable of the two. She at least has the maturity (they are the same age) to recognise that growth and decay are a natural cycle that needs it's balance maintained. Regardless, these reasonable pleas fall on deaf ears. To be clear, she's not much better. She's engaged in "The Everlasting War"as much as Pydred, sworn to decay and destroy the mushroom forest back into the sandy desert it once was. She also likes decay, as a concept. It's more of a fascination that a fanatic approval. 

### [Helfa](/wastelife/eternals/helfa) -6800
_The Eternal of Hunting, found -6800_
The hunt to Helfa is a thrill and a sport. He is a man of leisure and social activity, often taking mortal creatures into his realm to be the target of a fair hunt, where him and the events participants hunt the creature in mortal conditions. He holds hunts all the time, sometimes even in the mortal realm. Mortal people sometimes join the hunt, like kings and emperors, though not always as the hunter. He also comes up with his own little beasties from time to time. Socially, he's a bit grand and pomp, but ultimately just. 

### [Caesi](/wastelife/eternals/caesi) -6400
_The Eternal of the Oceans, found -6400_
The best word that describes Caesi's domain is a happy obsession. They like the seas, they enjoy being in the dead. They like making really big stormy, and really big waves. They like making little and big creatures to live in the seas. It's a simple obsession, but it's as deep as the oceans. Socially, this makes Caesi really pleasant to talk to, if you're willing to engage them on the topics of deep sea life. 

Notoriously, Caesi is also the Eternal that makes Rahlamor such a dangerous ocean. Caesi is directly responsible for the conjuring of storms over and across this ocean, beyond what normal hurricanes/typhoons would do. Luckily for Caesi, they are an old god, so these constant rough storms have become a fact of nature rather than a God meddling in mortal trade. 

### [Achdene](/wastelife/eternals/achdene) -6220
_The Eternal of Lightning, found -6220_
Achdene's appearance in Eternity is often attributed to Caesi conjuring a storm that let off a nuclear blast of lightning. For this reason, in retrospect, they are often seen as an adopted son of Caesi. Achdene may better be called the eternal of spontaneity.  Spontaneity is not to be confused with random. Achdene is an avid socialite, enjoying conversations and parties more than a normal eternal. They really enjoy mortal parties of the folk variety and not of the regal mind. In general, they think society is a bit of a drag and that mortals should just have fun. They do also understand that mortals are individual people who don't have a collective consciousness that can abolish society. Very much of the lowborn over the highborn.

## Gods of Community
The Era of Community was defined by a rise of people civilisation, and the birth of the White Tyrant. It lasted from -6000 to -3500

### [Ysbiti](/wastelife/eternals/ysbiti) -5700
_The Eternal of Hospitality, found -5700_
Hospitality means hosting people and being friendly. Ysbiti's domain has barely changed. Ysbiti is a very happy and friendly eternal. They enjoy hosting things, and often work with mortals for events of hosting. They're a bit arbitrary in who they cooperate with, helping both kings and your neighbour down the street. They promote hospitality, and despise hostility. Very social.


### [Cryver](/wastelife/eternals/cryver) -5510
_The Eternal of Strength, ascended -5510_
The first mortal ascension. Cryver is, however, an old god. His mortal memories are a small part of his existence, so socially he's practically just a normal god. His domain, strength, is not only about physical strength. In fact, it's more about mental strength than the physical. This is not to say that Cryver does not enjoy a good show of physical power. He's a bit of a gentle power. 

### [Fflam](/wastelife/eternals/fflam) -5400
_The Eternal of Fire, born -5400_
Around this time, Cynnag was experimenting with spontaneous growth, and hanging out with Achdene a lot. Cynnag was beginning to get into mushrooms, especially bursting mushrooms and fungi that can grow massive mushrooms overnight. Achdene was in on it too, learning about the spontaneity of nature and even some mycelium electron communication stuff. 

At some point, they were hanging out a little too closely, and did whatever the Eternal equivalent of reproduction is, and the product of this was Fflam, the first born Eternal. Both Cynnag and Achdene were very confused. After letting the word out, and after Drex and Anifey continued the pattern, Eternity just accepted that this was a thing that can happen.

The domain of Fire is both in literal fire, and in life and physical warmth. Fflam themselves are a very sociable person, maybe a bit too enthusiastic about doing things. Fflam's daily activities are joining in on mortal bonfires, dancing parties, and also pestering other gods to join them. 

### [Thaler](/wastelife/eternals/thaler) -5300
_The Eternal of Stories, found -5300_
Stories are the small tales we tell our friends of our days. Stories are grand epics and theatre plays. Thaler enjoys it all, recognising the bridges between all takes ever said. They enjoy hearing and telling stories, and as quite an old Eternal they have plenty to tell.

### [Drex](/wastelife/eternals/drex) -5270
_The Eternal of Glass, born -5270_
Pydred and Cynnag were locked in the height of the Eternal War, and Pydred was not happy with Achdene being on Cynnag's side and helping him overgrown the desert. However, once Fflam was born Achdene did a radical pivot and spent the next few decades with Pydred instead. Weary about the change, Pydred warmed up to Achdene when they made appeals to understand the graduality of decay, and together they formed an interest in spontaneous decay, like explosions, and just how destructive high velocity sand is.

Drex, being one of many old gods, is now very departed from their origins. They have come to understand their domain as one of fragility, transparency, and they are also slightly frantic.

### [Anifey](/wastelife/eternals/anifey) -5240
_The Eternal of Beasts, born -5240_
Hunting and hospitality are hardly naturally complementing domains, but as Helfa came into their own their hunts were surprisingly hospitable environments. Back then, Helfa only hunted creatures he created, so his later expeditions in killing mortal creatures weren't a burden between his and Ysbiti's relationship. 

Anifey is a somewhat strange and chaotic creature, usually characterised by whatever creature they're fascinated by in the moment. They fluctuate their fascinations every hundred years or so, which is long to a human but fairly frequent to all of their peers. Anifey does have some consistent character ideals, like they are fair and a bit of an actor. Underneath their animal peerage they're generally quite eager and excited. 

### [Dihenydd](/wastelife/eternals/dihenydd) -4897
_The Eternal of Destruction, found -4897_
Originally, Dihenydd was a very antagonistic god who enjoyed the death of mortals, as it was often a by-product of destruction. He was an avid observer of the White Tyrant, but since it's demolition he's reconsidered his domain a bit. He's still somewhat raging and very much enjoys watching things get destroyed, but he's humble enough to realise that things must first be built. His domain comes off more like a childish fascination than a malevolent demolitionist.

### [Senne](/wastelife/eternals/senne) -4800
_The Eternal of Sound, found -4800_
Senne is for any sound, but instead of just enjoying sounds she's a bit of a snob about them. Not like, musician snob, but more like "ooh this drill is making perfect moshing waves" type thing. She is capable of being a bit more understandable, but like any eternal they are obsessed with their domain. She often loves hearing audio speech, and attends random mortal events where people just talk like parliaments and councils. 

### [Arhos](/wastelife/eternals/arhos) -4800
_The Eternal of Eclipse, born -4800_
In a completely unsurprising move, Hell and Lleh, eternal partners, did the thing and made Arhos. They're both eternals obsessed with mortal-realm astral bodies and their mortal implications. They're compatible opposites: one radiant and enthusiastic and the other quiet and reflective. 

Arhos is patient, after all the eclipse only happens a few times each decade. Arhos is also often seen as the eternal of omens, enjoying random predictions on the future. They will make these predictions as often as they like, and Arhos has a way of often being correct. According to Adnabyd or Medr, Arhos finds some way of informing their omens rather than tapping into some unknown way to determine the future.

### [Bedd](/wastelife/eternals/bedd) -4775
_The Eternal of Graves, found -4775_
Ritual and mourning are both Bedd's domain, as well as the physical practices of grave in burial, cremation, open graves, and others. Bedd is often described as a bit melancholic and a mood killer in the eternal social sphere. Their daily actions are usually related to helping or observing mourning in the mortal realm, so they certainly don't have topics that excite other Eternals.

### [Rhif](/wastelife/eternals/rhif) -4700
_The Eternal of Numbers, found -4700_
As counting became common place in the mortal realm through early book-keeping, Rhif entered eternity to embody this practice. They embodied the practice, cataloguing and mastering every counting system and bringing cultures to the forefront of numerics. They love accounting. Usually, they aren't very excitable, but with modern mathematical developments Rhif is one of the most engaged and excited eternals. 

### [Tanea](/wastelife/eternals/tanea) -4650
_The Eternal of the Forge, ascended -4650_
The second ascended mortal. They were a paleo-Domuz artificially driven mad by Gwalloph, but in their madness they became the most active and skilled smith in Eternity, even out mastering the volcanics who taught him. Tanea's most famous for his powerful enchanted "artefacts", the first of which are Tanea's 75 which he started while mortal and finished as an Eternal. He has released many more since. He's a very professional god and likes forge metaphors. Easily annoyed.

### [Breuddwd](/wastelife/eternals/breuddwd) -4451
_The Eternal of Dreams, found -4451_
The dreams to which Breuddwd finds her domain are those of mortal minds, but she does at least symbolically subscribe to hope-kind dreams. Regardless, her personality's more on the needlessly abstract side, never saying things directly but instead implying them in a socially hazy way. Her daily activities involve invading mortal's dreams through a process which makes no sense to any other Eternal including Medr, who doesn't understand this process as a "skill". 

She, Prydferth, and Distaw all make up the "Aroura siblings" who are all attributed to this one mortal world sky phenomenon that happened because of the sun's general hostility.

### [Prydferth](/wastelife/eternals/prydferth) -4451
_The Eternal of Beauty, found -4451_
Beauty is both magnetic and intimidating. Mortals are often drawn to Prydferth's manifestation entranced by their brilliance and charismatic presence. They are passion and artistry in all forms, enjoying discussions on inspiration and art. When socialising, they speak with a certain declarative elegance that's hard to describe. 

They're also in an eternal relationship with Dim. Prydferth is entranced by the beauty of the void, both mental and literal. Meanwhile, Dim rejects the relationship publicly (despite obviously reciprocating in private) out of the principles of Dim's domain.

### [Distaw](/wastelife/eternals/distaw) -4451
_The Eternal of Harmony, found -4451_
Despite the common interpretations of harmony, Distaw is outwardly quiet and contemplative and internally perpetually annoyed. Their domain is what it is; they enjoy natural cooperation and everything lining up and being nice. Their annoyance comes from this never really being the case. They struggle themselves to maintain their own harmony.

### [Sbri](/wastelife/eternals/sbri) -4200
_The Eternal of Revelry, found -4200_
The merriest of the gods, Sbri takes the concept of party and makes it their eternal personality. They enjoy the loud and bodacious, and hence are loud and bodatious themselves. They are also an enjoyer of alcoholic beverages and other similar mortal intoxicants like beer, lager, vodka, and the such. 

### [Cyfun](/wastelife/eternals/cyfun) -4000
_The Eternal of the City, found -4000_
Every Eternal accepts that their domain is inherently connected to the mortal realm. Of course, their own realms are the places where their domains exist in the purest, but their domain is but a reflection of the mortal realm itself and it's ongoings. 

Cyfun is the exception to this. They are not generically an eternal of cities, gatherings, and organisation. They are the eternal of _the City_, their realm serving as the greatest cultural hub in all of Eternity. It hosts Eternals, their Esten, and even many mortals most notably as a neutral meeting place and exchange. Importantly, it's also where the Cyfun Annuals are held, which are organisational discussions between Eternals usually on the matters of mortal conduct. 

Cyfun themselves is incredibly organised and professional, speaking very clearly and purposefully.

### [Creed](/wastelife/eternals/creed) -3920
_The Eternal of Invention, born -3920_
Breuddwd and Tanea found a fascination in each other, one inspired by the creativity of enchanted objects and their possibilities, the other by the limits of the wants of mortal wishing. Together, they did the thing that even Eternals don't really understand and from it came Creed.

Creed is a bit of a failed inventor. They really enjoy mortal inventions and definitely have some important things in their name, but it is ultimately mortals who make the best things in the mortal realm. Creed is hopeful and doesn't let this get him down, and he finds comfort in participating in inventing and in cataloguing and coordinating things. In the modern world, his Esten are certainly doing work collaborating with mortals, although it's still disputed how effective they are.

### [Gwaed](/wastelife/eternals/gwaed) -3800
_The Eternal of Blood, -3800_
Only seven gods know what brought Gwaed to Eternity, and they have kept it to themselves. Gwaed may well have been found, or perhaps they are a mortal, or even a born god from some folly. Regardless, the end product is the same. 

Gwaed's domain is sacrifice, pain, and gore. They are not a fun god to talk to, often mildly disturbing not in an edgy way but in a way that's offputtingly sincere. They are usually quiet, finding a silent enjoyment in the topics of their domain. But they are not cruel; they do not wish harm on others but merely observe it. 

### [Hapsus](/wastelife/eternals/hapsus) -3566
_The Eternal of Randomness, found -3566_
Hapsus is a very difficult god. They do nothing coherently. Even when they are coherent, they do it in a way that isn't overall coherent and that in hindsight was in fact not very coherent. Their speech is not real talk, although when speaking the eternal language they are apparently somewhat decipherable.

## Gods of Civilisation
The Era of Civilisation was characterised by a rise in organised person civilisation, where distant documents mention a huge "Union of Lifeland" as Shildo. This era lasted from -3500 to the year 0, when the empire Techia would unite the Shildo region under an actual organised empire.

### [Raifel](/wastelife/eternals/raifel) -3400
_The Eternal of War, found -3400_
War to Raifel is organised, brutal, and not glorious. It is the final form of conflict, when diplomacy is over only bodies suffice. It's achieving your goal above all costs. They are incredibly professional and like a lot of other eternals they really like war metaphors.

### [Xyldo](/wastelife/eternals/xyldo) -3400
_The Eternal of Revolution, found -3400_
Xyldo's domain is the overturning of the status quo. Like when fire was invented, or when agriculture began. Xyldo is often overjoyed by the overturning of the status quo in any context, although they usually prefer progressive overturning. When revolutions aren't concerned, they're often professional and organisational. 

### [Marol](/wastelife/eternals/marol) -3400
_The Eternal of Death, born -3400_
The Eternal of Destruction was still in their death-loving phase, and perhaps because they caused a lot of them the Eternal of Graves, Bedd, reciprocated some connection. Bedd was a less agreeable eternal at this time through some fluctuation of their personality.

Death is terrifying, but it's also inevitable and not malevolent. Marol takes a similar role to Bedd: while Bedd's domain is the mortal experience post-partum, Marol's domain is during the process itself. It's pain and inevitability. Like Bedd, they are often a mood killer, taking on a very serious and unfunny socialisation. 

### [Ammehdytha](/wastelife/eternals/ammehdytha) -3300
_The Eternal of Agriculture, found -3300_
Ammehdytha really likes agricultural practice. They're a grower of things and like watching plant life mature and be productive. This also applies to their outlook on others. 
### [Ahergefmot](/wastelife/eternals/ahergefmot) -3300
_The Eternal of Coincidence, found -3300_
Ahergefmot is a very simple eternal who just enjoys coincidence. It's a very specialised domain, when two completely unrelated events occur in tandem. They are often annoyed by mortals attributing incidence to coincidence and vice versa. 

### [Mwedre](/wastelife/eternals/mwedre) -3240
_The Eternal of Insects, born -3240_
Born to Achdene and Anifey.

### [Lusg](/wastelife/eternals/lusg) -3120
_The Eternal of the Crawl, ascended -3120_
The Crawl represents the hard and taxing process of dungeon delving and completion. ## Gods of Empire
The Era of Empire is the most modern era, if you do not believe in the transition into the Era of Diplomacy. It is from 0 until now, and is characterised by the organisation of mortals into large empires. The nature of empires was constantly changing: from the slave-states of Mothdao and Annwvyn to the massive bureaucracy of the Empire of Ralean. This era lasted from the year 0 until the modern day, 3488, but is also said to have ended in 3300.

In the centuries around this point, they had a sort of energetic renewal and began exploring modern topics of name and identity with all of the Eternals they hadn't really interacted with.
