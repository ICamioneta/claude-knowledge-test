# Introduction
I am Harvey, a Lemuz scholar studying in the College of Lore in the University of Jonati. It has been my recent fancy to study the gods, ask around about them, and learn what they are. We all know the gods, the eternal beings with expanded consciousnesses and power over existence greater than any mortal could achieve alone. Many people participate in worship of the gods without truly knowing who they are, or even what they do. 

I have spent the last few years dedicating myself to the full research of the Gods. This book is the result of that report: a concatenation stories passed down through the ages, curious scholars convening with the gods themselves, and analyses of the God's direct meddling in our mortal affairs, collected throughout millennia of the history of people. If I were to point out organisations that consistently appear in my reference lists to thank: the Nao Institute and the Universities of Kuddheja have made greatly successful efforts to ask the gods themselves in matters of inquiry.

# Gods, plane and simple
The Gods are conscious beings that exist within Eternity. I, for example, am a conscious being that exists within the Mortal Realm. It’s the same concept, but the consciousness of the Gods have a greater capacity than those of Mortals in a way that we simply couldn’t understand or interpret. With some confidence we know six of the Gods were conscious at the start of Eternity. All other Gods came after the initial six created the Muz and the Dos.

Every God has their own Realm, their own Plane of Eternity. Within this realm, they have complete control over everything. They can move mountains, erect cities, create and destroy at their whim. The Mortal Realm is the Plane of Eternity we people live in. People usually think that Gods have complete control of the Mortal Realm. In reality, the God's control over the mortal realm is limited.

The Gods are well known for being the creators of several beings in the Mortal Realm - many species of person included. But, not all living things in the Mortal Realm were created by gods. On the contrary, most of the living diversity naturally came about through the natural process of evolution, descendants of creatures that were on the Mortal Realm when the Gods found it. It is these natural creatures that the gods base their own creatures from. 

Many creatures, specifically species of person, seemingly come about regardless of the processes of evolution. It is generally understood that the Muz were the first peoples, and at that time the only peoples were Muz. This creation occurred around 10000 years before the unification of Shildo, which is the year 0 in the common era. However, the Ews, the first peoples to be discovered to exist without the creation of a god, were discovered around -8000 C.E. 

All people who were not created by the Gods were discovered before -6000 C.E. These species of person are often labelled "found people", as opposed to "created people" or the elusive title "evolved people". The prevailing theory is that the Gods simply hadn't noticed the found people, which is relatively plausible given the sparse demographies of prehistory. Alternative theories are Ews mysticism, covert creation, or rapid evolution.

The Gods swear themselves to a domain, which is often a concept or collection of concepts that involves certain activities. Later, I will get into each of the gods, their domain, and what activities they perform with regards to that domain. The gods create other conscious creatures - the Esten - to help them in the affairs of their domain. These Esten are usually tied to a physical body and occupy the God's realms. They are fully independent and often act on their own whim in the name of their God. While some Esten are capable of transporting themselves between their Gods realm and the Mortal realm, most Esten require their god create a portal or move the Esten themselves.

Although their capacity of consciousness and power is far beyond that of people, the gods are still limited in their ability. The Esten are often much more like people with the same capacities of thought and socialisation. For many gods with particularly involved domains, the Esten perform the vast majority of "leg-work", especially when it comes with interaction in the Mortal Realm. In myths and legends, the gods themselves have many appearances, but we are now aware that many of these appearances were likely of Esten rather than the God.

The gods have omnipotent control over their own realm. What is often not understood is the control they have over the Mortal Realm. Most verifiably non-fictitious cases show the gods only being able to "transport" things between their realm and the Mortal realm, potentially by using the Hidol. This has generally been backed up by interviews with the Gods and their Esten. However, the existence of certain phenomena attributed to the Gods suggest that there is more to this. 

When Gods give themselves bodies, these are called "manifestations". Although God consciousnesses can "float" freely in the mortal realm, creating physical bodies allow them to actually interact with the world. The bodies are mortal, as are all living things in the Mortal Realm, but when the bodies die the consciousness of the God simply slips out. This is true for Esten also, according to themselves. When in pure-consciousness form, the Gods are capable of just "knowing" everything going on in the same space their consciousness occupies. This is not true for Esten.

# Adnabyan Knowledge
Frequently, throughout our developmental history, we have taken the words of the Gods as implicit truth. The several reasons often given for this are generally understandable. Some cultures regarded the gods as the ultimate masters of their domain, so therefore their word on their domain must be the greatest analysis of their domain. Some cultures regarded the gods like the Elders of Eternity, just as they were taught of their surroundings by their elders, their society was informed by the Gods who were older than everything. 

With the modern advent of the scientific principle, which in truth has been around for a century or two, the idea of taking anyone at their word was discarded. The scientific method demands knowledge be observable and repeatable, and the gods are often proven contradictory. They have also been unbelievably useful. Adnabyd, the God of Knowledge, claims that his Esten follow principles similar to the scientific method to verify information, but we also know that his priorities have been in collecting information rather than verifying it. 

In the modern age of science, we cannot take the god's word as truth. But, we accept that as they generally are the best in their domain any consciousness could be, and we recognise their efforts in the advancement of their domain, we classify the knowledge we get from them as _Adnabyan_.

Adnabyan knowledge refers to information obtained by directly convening with the Gods. Adnabyan-Esten knowledge is a subset reserved for information gained from the Esten of the Gods that have not yet been confirmed as the consensus of that God. Adnabyan-Eternal is the information gained directly from the God of the domain. Most Adnabyan knowledge is Adnabyan-Esten, given how more frequently the Esten interact with people compared to the Gods themselves. 

All Adnabyan knowledge falls into three categories. Confirmed Adnabyan knowledge are claims from an Adnabyan source that the scientific community has independently verified. Because we've been able to verify it independently, the Adnabyan source becomes part of the scientific literature. Unconfirmed Adnabyan knowledge is knowledge from an Adnabyan source that we are aware we can confirm, but has not yet been subjected to scrutiny. We do not accept unconfirmed Adnabyan knowledge as scientific consensus, subject to nuance. Contingent Adnabyan knowledge is knowledge that could in principle be tested if we had developed some scientific technology that would allow for the claim to be observed. If we ever do develop said technology, contingent Adnabyan knowledge would turn into unconfirmed Adnabyan knowledge. Anecdotal Adnabyan knowledge are claims from Adnabyan sources that are inaccessible to verification, usually due to the passage of time, or they are philosophical in nature, grounded in personal reasoning rather than empirical evidence. 

Acceptance of Adnabyan knowledge has been a highly contentious topic within the scientific community since the formulation of the scientific method. In general, it is subject to the relationship the scientific community has with the Adnabyan source. For example, Adnabyd himself has consistently been a reliable repository for scientifically provable truths. All unconfirmed claims that Adnabyd has made about the natural sciences has been verified. So, we generally accept Adnabyd's Adnabyan knowledge. 

Adnabyan knowledge from Sirphni - the God of Lethargy - and her Esten have consistently proven to be incoherent or demonstrably false, demoted from Anecdotal Adnabyan to verified false information. It is widely accepted that most utterances from Sirphni are unconsidered or deliberately misleading. For another extreme example, Adnabyan knowledge gained from Gwalloph, the God of Madness, has been unpredictable. Contingent Adnabyan claims made by Gwalloph and his Esten have both turned out to be verified and verifiably false. The issue is that the difference between false and true information coming from Gwalloph has historically been indistinguishable, so we cannot use Adnabyan Gwalloph knowledge.

Most other gods are somewhere in-between the self-disciplined rigour of Adnabyd and the lies from ignorance of Sirphni. Every piece of Adnabyan knowledge has to be carefully considered by every individual, giving harsh consideration to the source, before they choose to believe in it. Adnabyan knowledge is always a belief, even when it is most likely the truth. 

The relevance of the topic to the subject of the title is that most knowledge about the nature of Eternity, the Gods, their realms, and their activities is by nature Adnabyan knowledge. Knowledge about what happened in the past, with the certainty that Adnabyd and the other gods give it, can only be corroborated if at all through archeological or indirect material evidence. Knowledge about that which is unobservable to us, knowledge about the nature of Eternity and the Hidol, is contingent Adnabyan. Knowledge about the history of Eternity, the histories of the Gods between themselves and their realms, is all anecdotal Adnabyan. Knowledge about the beginning of history can only ever be anecdotal Adnabyan too. 

For this text I will adopt appropriately uncertain terminology. I will refer to events and histories as the Gods claim them to have occurred. It is my personal opinion that historical claims made by Adnabyd can be accepted as reliable information, but in recognition of the rigour of the scientific principle, and in recognition of differing opinions, I will present Adnabyan information with all the nuance that is necessary for the reader to make their own informed judgements.

# Natural Eternity
The natural sciences seek to understand how the world and reality work. Its methods are easily applied to the Mortal Realm, as it is the location in which we exist. We know for certain that the Mortal World is not the only place that exists; it does not encompass everything. For one, the God's realms are known to exist. Mortals are very frequently transported to their realms, we have several reliable and consistent accounts. 

There are areas of reality that seem both unobservable and hard to conceptualise. We know the Hidol exists from the phenomena it produces, and researchers of the Hidol Sciences are working diligently to understand it to its core. All we know for certain is that there is another medium that co-exists with space at every point in the mortal realm, and that it is full of energy of varying densities. This energy is often transformed into the phenomena of Hidosis. 

We have convened with the Gods several times in order to ask about the nature of reality. Their accounts have been overall consistent with each other, but for now the following information remains contingent Adnabyan knowledge, provided by several Adnabyan sources. The claim is that the Hidol is multi-dimensional, and permeates all of reality, connecting the Realms of the gods through its extra-dimensionality.

The topic of what multiple dimensions refers to is one difficult for a mortal to grasp. Imagine a piece of paper. It is two-dimensional: there is the dimension that stretches from the bottom of the paper to the top and the dimension that extends from one side to the other. The third dimension, then, is the depth of that paper, accentuated by stacking pieces of paper together. Three-dimensional space is that: like a cube from top to bottom, side to side, and front to back. The fourth dimension is then like stacking these dimensions on top of each other, in another dimension. Like how stacks of two-dimensional paper make a three-dimensional area, stacks of three-dimensional space will make four-dimensional space.

To our knowledge, three-dimensional space is the only space that is possible; the Hidol's extra-dimensionality is not spatial, but in some form that we have not yet come to understand. The extents of three-dimensional space is its own topic: Astronomy. It is an evolving field, but we are partially aware that the Mortal Realm is finite. It in a way loops back on itself, i.e. if you go in one direction for long enough you will eventually reach back where you started. We also know that the mortal realm is massive, and that the planet upon which we live, the Mortal Planet or the World, is but a tiny speck in the mortal realm. 

We are also aware, through Astronomy, that the bright objects in the night sky are either other sun-like objects called stars that are just really far away, or strange phenomena of the Hidol manifesting light in the sky. We do not know why the Mortal Realm exists. According to the original six gods (Adnabyd included), the Mortal Realm and the Mortal World was already present and flourishing when they found it.

There is more in reality than the Mortal Realm. This is where the topic of Eternity comes up. It seems to predominantly be a terminology used by the Gods to encompass everything, but they state that it is different from the term of "reality". According to the consistent accounts of the Gods, Eternity is indimensional, that is it has no dimensions in space, so it only has time. Eternity encompasses everything, so potentially it is just the way the Gods describe time? Nothing is certain about Eternity as the Gods describe it, and it seems untestable. 

Within Eternity, where the Hidol permeates everything, the Gods have their own realms. The Gods created their realms, but it is also claimed that the Gods and their realms are inherently tied to another, and although new Gods do not automatically have a realm they seek to create one even if they do not know how. The realms of the Gods are often subtly different to the Mortal Realm, but it is inconsistent. We have not been able to study the God's realms in person, so the following information is purely contingent Adnabyan knowledge. 

The Gods realms can be finite and looping like the Mortal Realm, where the Gods create their own planets and populate them similarly. The realms can also be infinite, but only in one dimension, where the ground is all that there is, and the edges of the ground loop back on themselves but the sky extends upwards infinitely and the ground infinitely down. There are also realms that are infinite in all directions, but there is an extent to which there is an edge to the Realm's space but it is not a hard edge but a soft edge. 

The idea that we will ever be able to test and explore the God's realms in the same capacity that we will explore our own seems unreasonable. We have no reason to distrust the gods on the nature of their own realm, and therefore of Eternity and Reality. We also have no reason to believe that the Gods do not have omnipotent control over their own realms. Ultimately, applying the scientific method to analyse natural eternity feels like a futile task when there are beings with so much more power and domain over these aspects of Eternity. For now, it is enough to understand what we can, the Mortal Realm and its natural sciences.

# The History of Eternity
We have not yet been able to trace the history of the mortal realm and its world to its origin. The origin of the mortal realm is something that even eludes the God of Knowledge. We may never find ways to date matter and trace the history of our world. But, in matters of history, we have books, reports, and charters dating back to the beginning of civilisation. And before that still, we have Adnabyan records of the history of the Mortal Realm. In this section, I will provide a chronological overview of the history of Eternity, according to the records of Adnabyd.

## The Origins of Eternity
At some point in Eternity, the original six gods came to self-realisation. They, at some point, understood their own existence, and thus came into being. The gods do not know how long it took, so they call these moments the events of realisation. This self-realisation of the gods is known as the Zeroth event, or Event Zero. 

Event One is then when the Gods found their domain. Adnabyd was the first to learn how to create a realm, by some method utilising the Hidol. The gods at this time decided on their domains, although they were unthought and unbased. 

Event Two is the final timeless event, and it was the discovery of the Mortal Realm. Its existence confused the Gods who had believed that only what they created was that which existed. The Mortal Realm was full of life that they had not created, and lands that they could not control. With its discovery came an almost full realisation of their domain. And, more importantly, a way to categorise time, and the concept of a year as a revolution of the world around the sun. 

These six Eternals were Adnabyd, the God of Knowledge, Medr, the God of Skill, Ymdig, the God of Behaviour, Bwydo, the God of Food, Riw, the God of Pleasure, and Cyfapheb, the God of Communication. The domains of the Gods predate our concepts of them, thus they are generally more expansive and descriptive than a simple word could achieve. 

The gods spent a lot of time experimenting with the Mortal Realm, creating their own creatures, often based on pre-existing creatures, and taking inspiration from the Mortal Realm into their own realms. These creatures were created by individual gods.

Then, much later, the Gods managed to collaborate on creating a new mortal creature, one with a capacity of conscious thought. They created the Muz, the first people, basing them from apes and spreading them across Shildo. 

## The Antonyms
It is said, then, that the people created by the gods exhibited traits that they did not expect of them. Alongside the recognition of these traits came the next six gods, the antonyms of the original. 

Gwalloph, the God of Madness, Sirphni, the God of Lethargy, Diofalux, the Eternal of Accidents, Glinphe, the Eternal of Gluttony, Maleisus, the Eternal of Suffering, and Gwrphcim, the Eternal of Antisocialism.

Traditional theology and understandings of the Gods pits these new gods in direct opposition to the original six. However, under closer inspection, their domains seem in no way directly opposed. While the history of the gods and their relations is long, convoluted, and complex, it is currently known that the Gods do not all hate their antonyms. Adnabyd, for example, is quite fond of Gwalloph, the motivations of this fondness we may not really ever understand.

## The creation of other gods
It is said that new gods appearing come under three different circumstances. The first circumstance is around some notable moment of the mortal realm. Like the actions of the "original" people creating the antonyms, the Event of Beauty creating the Arorua triplets. Or, a particularly bad storm creating the Eternal of Storm. 

The other event that leads to a new god is the "ascension" of a mortal. The process of ascension isn't well understood even by the gods. In instances of mortal ascension, the mortal is said to have dedicated themselves to a domain as if they were a god. Then, consistently, an existing god convenes with the mortal and realises some unspeakable element of their person, and then has some ability to "ascend" them to godhood. 

The third event is referred to as a "domain overlap" and is a result of a certain type of action or relationship that is indescribable in mortal languages. Whatever it is, it is said to result in a sort of metaphysical overlap between two gods domains, catalysing in some manner to produce another consciousness in Eternity. This "Born God" then selects their own domain that could be logically inferred from the two parent domains.

Generally Medr ascends mortals to godhood. Other gods are capable of it, but Medr has stated that the act of ascension is a skill that she is the best at. When a person ascends, their body dies and a new god appears in Eternity who is then taught how to be a God by Adnabyd. The god retains the memories of their mortal life, but with their new expanded consciousness their relationship with their memories is different. Their short mortal lives eventually become a small speck of their history as they become accustomed to Godhood.

Generally, we categorise the gods with a mixture of what era they appeared in and whether they were mortal ascensions or naturally appearing Gods. Every god is wildly different, but in general their age conveys a certain maturity that cannot be described, and is very clear to the gods themselves. While all gods are connected to the mortal realm in the way that it is the only place where their domain occurs naturally, mortally ascended gods generally have more of a connection due to their mortal histories.
