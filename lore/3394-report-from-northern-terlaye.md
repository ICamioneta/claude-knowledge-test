# Research Report: 4 Chodin Dekja 3394
Sources: Serven Library and Serven University

Research Objectives:
Search for existing reports on the Lindi settlement discovered on 3 Edinbethna Dekja 3394
Research onto the handheld equipment of the observed Lindi
Further inquiry onto strange kart shape and Lindi horsekeeping practices.

Findings:
The scouting team spent the prior week at the Serven library and university. Findings show no other reports of this Lindi settlement. Research onto Lindi equipment show similar long barrelled weapons manufactured in a city located in Neble named Brwrd. These weapons are called 'Springburst Pneumaflux Confluence Catalyst', 'SPCC', only 'Confluence Catalyst', or only 'Springburst Weapon'. The SPCC mechanically creates torfol reactions, where a spell is generated in a chamber and directed outwards by the barrels of the weapons. 

Operation of an SPCC is as follows: ammunition is loaded into the top and closed into the weapon by a metal panel. A spring is pulled back and locked into a place, preparing the mechanism. A trigger is pulled that releases the tensions in the springs. The mechanism produces the necessary pressure difference to generate torfol reaction and then pushes the resulting spell through the barrel using the main trigger action. Ammunition is specially prepared ingredient blends placed into easily destructible and non-alive material, usually paper. Discussion of the weapon from the source shows that it is useful for a non-mage, but mages often perform its function with greater speed, at greater strengths, and without the need for complex maintenance. 

Further discussion from the source mentions similar weapons being found in a Domuz colony in Llyhai. These weapons are often called Archtech Repeater. The design is not fully understood, but in general it functions in the same way. Core differences are ammunition is often stored in a sort of clip that holds ammunition and pushes it into the mechanism. The mechanism also uses the energy of the reaction to reset itself. It doesn't feature any springs, potentially using metal in tension. The ammunition is found alongside the Archtech Repeaters in their place of origin, Fanthel, and the ammunition has not been successfully replicated.

Other notable findings on the weapons include that they are generally effective at close to mid ranges, depending on the spell cast by the ammunition. The furthest effective range of the SPCC is said to be 100m, and the furthest effective range for the Archtech Repeater was not mentioned and is believed to be further.

The bi-scope seen in use by the second Lindi seems to be a rare design of scope intended for binocular vision, often discarded for its absurd length. The bi-scope in possession by the Lindi was significantly shorter than the design we were able to research.

No meaningful research for the black box held in the second lindi's hand.
No meaningful research for the strangely shaped cart.
Conversations with library folk confirmed that Lindi, as a rule, do not keep horses.

Reccomendation:
Copies of sketches from source materials to be compared to Lindi equipment.
Further observation of Lindi settlement

Reporting: Sebrel Bretha, Captain
