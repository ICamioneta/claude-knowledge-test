# Present Situation

A diplomatic meeting between Dxledrit Nvxin and cohort and Addreoug Chalsie and cohort, in the Domuz-Kyeric border-town of Rexit Blange. 

Dxledrit and cohort are present on behalf of Prophet Mana. Addreoug Chalsie, Empress of the Domuz, is present on behalf of herself and represents the Domuz people. The aim is to bring an end to the sixth Kyeric-Daeried War, and an end to any future wars between the powers that be in Kyeron and the powers that be in Darie. 

# Settlements

The following articles are to be followed to ensure the continued peace between the Domuz and the Ximuz.

## Article 1

The exchange of diplomatic embassies. Embassies are to be sovereign territory of a foreign power within the country of the hosting nation. Embassadors are to be non-partisan. 

Manakeron, and all northern Kyeric powers following, are to establish and maintain embassies in the following cities:

- Mencell 
- All
- Eseia

The Domuz, and all powers in Darie that follow, are to establish and maintain embassies in the following cities:

- Dxrindu
- Shtiads
- Aroru

Embassies are to be subject only to the law of their nation. Safe passage between the embassy and it's nation is to be guaranteed by the host, in all cases. Diplomatic seals verify missions between an embassy and it's nation. 

# Article 2

The establishment of the Kyeron - Darie border in its current state, marked by the Dike of Nvxin-Chalsie. This border is immutable for the duration of the treaty. 

The demilitarisation of the border. No military operations are to be carried out within a march of the border. Domestic military actions must be approved by the leaders of both nations, otherwise the military actor is in violation of this treaty.

Border guard is not permitted. Free trade and travel across the dike is guaranteed by this treaty. Travel further inland to either nation is not, border control is allowed past the demilitarised zone of the treaty.

## Article 3

There shall be no conflict between the Domuz and the Ximuz on Barogate. Any provocations are violations of this treaty. To ensure peace, the Domuz and the Ximuz are to only engage cooperatively in their diplomacy. Any transgressions are in violation of this treaty.

# Penalties for violations

Both parties agree that any violation of this treaty is to be discussed and deliberated. Ithe case that the treaty still stands, the Nvxin-Chalsie dike is to move into the territory of the transgressor to grant the transgressed no more than a squared half-march.
