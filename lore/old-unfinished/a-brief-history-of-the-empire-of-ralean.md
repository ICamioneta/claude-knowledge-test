# Introduction
I am Harvey, a scholar at the University of Barogate Lore in Northern Ralean. As part of my studies, I have taken an interest onto the history behind the current state of the Ralean Empire. I wanted to know why the south is as it is. I wanted to know why Terlaye was part of the Empire’s lands. I wanted to understand the beginnings of the empire, why its capital is Cania. I spent the past few years lobbying historians, speaking to old Empire officials all to complete this brief history of the Ralean Empire.

# Modern Day (3488)
The year I have finalised this text is 3488, and in case you have no clue as to the current situation of the Ralean Empire, this section will fill you in. First, the basics. The Empire of Ralean is an autocratic government lead by an Emperor, a hereditary title. The Empire of Ralean’s de jure territory includes the entirety of the Barogate region of Ralean and the region of Terlaye. The de facto territorial control of the Empire only includes northern and west central Ralean, and the entirety of Terlaye. In southern and east central Ralean, the Empire has been subject to several separatist and rebel uprisings.

## The Northern Cores
The Empire’s north includes the cities of Jonati, Ilandus, Londus, Vezandus, and Detsemoced. The land is very hilly in these parts, the trident-shaped mountains leave large valleys, which themselves are full of hills. Next to Ilandus sits a shallow lake. The mountains taper off in the north, leaving a steep downhill onto River Calaerula. Due to its history as the birthplace for the modern empire, money from the centre often gets funnelled north. The landscape also harbours several castles and forts, constructed using local stone and the centre’s money and ensuring that the northern cores are unconquerable.

## Terlaye
The region of Terlaye is in an unfortunate spot, while it might have been able to become its own large empire, its positioning between Ereso and Ralean has made it a destination to be conquered. In the modern age, Terlaye is fully integrated into the Empire after 80 years of occupation. Terlaye’s geography is fairly consistent, being full of tributary rivers to Nater’s river and River Rullan. To the east of each river also sits some high ground. The conquest of Terlaye was a crucial territorial gain for the empire, although the city of Llofilot has managed to detach itself from the Empire and side with Eresonia. Due to the situation on mainland Ralean, Terlaye, and the city of Nater’s End, is the Empire’s only port.

## The Centre
Central Ralean is a plain terrain with many rivers flowing south-east towards Ralean’s coastal region Senolagor. The wealth in the centre isn’t well spread. Firstly, there’s the gargantuan city of Dococaniral that overtook Nater’s End as the largest city a few centuries ago, when the Empire declared its new capital, and the cities of Rodchan Heire. These two cities, the former often known as Cania, contain a disproportionate share of the centre’s wealth. That said, with the new factions appearing old cities are getting new love. The Empire only controls the aforementioned cities and the city of Leuhb in this region. The often-named southern revolts are occurring in southern Ralean and south-eastern central ralean, hogging the coast.

## The South
Southern Ralean is dominated by a forest on high ground. It isn’t populated much, and is seen as a dangerous area to travel in. Only a small pocket around the city of Milad is still controlled by the empire, as the rest is owned by the newer factions. This pocket is often referred to as the Exiled Empire, as its only direct supply can come from the city of Nater’s End into a small port town serving Milad, although any serious conquest of the exiled empire could easily blockade any one of these ports since the entire empire’s navy was lost during the revolts.

## New Factions in Southern and East-Central Ralean
After the initial revolts and revolutions beginning in 3484, southern ralean revolted. Although many sources will say that this revolt was a time for ideologies to flourish, but in reality many people revolting already had an idea of what type of regime would take power in their place. The revolts were more of a collaborative uprising of several separatist and independence factions. The exact history of the revolts will appear at the end of this document.

### Hovadia
Hovadia is a strict meritocracy. They have a leader selected from several candidates, whom all must compete in a test of intelligence to see who is worthy of ruling the country. Since its foundation, the country has only ever had a single leader. He is, according to the system, the best person in Ralean to rule a country. He claims to be better than Ruion Reimig at running a country. Houvadia is an independent faction, and will settle to exist alongside the Empire. They are directly opposed to the ideologies of the Kingdom of Lemuz, and claim that the king is unfit to rule.

### The Guerrilla
The Guerrilla isn’t a government. It is more of a declaration of the lands that exist under a functional anarchy system. The Guerilla, in terms of warfare, might as well be a deep void that armies can fall into. Every community is heavily armed and ready to take up arms against any Empire army that marches in. There is no collective goal for the guerrilla but for the factions lands to remain with its current regime. Revolutions have been attempted in neighbouring towns.

### Setia-Debid
Setia-Debid is a country owned by a guild, a corporation. The city there once had a simple ship constructing company that slowly paid its way into power, seizing many means of production and eventually owning almost all local production. It cooperated in the revolts, seeking absolute control over the regions it already owned. Their intentions are to stay independent, and the nation has no expansionist ambitions. The Setia Guild recognises that war is unprofitable in this situation. Setia-Debid acts as a buffer state between the ideologically opposed Houvadia and Kingdom of the Lemuz.

### The Kingdom of Ithun
The Kingdom of Ithun is a kingdom acting under a centuries old feudal system last seen three centuries ago. The land is divided into hereditary titles, and these titles are owned by a lord. The title “Kingdom of Ithun” is said to cover almost the entirety of southern ralean, or where the Ithun culture has spread. The Kingdom of Ithun claims this entire territory as its DeJure, and has fought wars against the NCP and the Second Empire to claim this territory. Its king has claimed that unification of southern Ralean under its banner is the primary objective of the kingdom, but there are talks among the nobles about a feudal Empire of Ralean that would rule over the kingdoms of Ithun, Meison, Lauvedno, Londa, Rodxan Sefars, and Eysen.

### New Councilist Province
The New Councilist Province (NCP) is an Eresonia-inspired government system in Southern Ralean that claims that it is only a single province of the United Ralean Provinces, not unlike the way that some in the Kingdom of Ithun say that it is a kingdom under a united Empire of Ralean. The NCP currently has a military truce with the Empire, and is currently disliked by most other governments in the area, especially the Second Empire, which shares the goal of completely reforming the Empire. The NCP aspires to call this new Ralean “Raleania”.

### The Second Empire
The Second Empire is a reformist movement that seeks to abolish the Emperor and replace his role with a republic that represents several aspects of the government. Most of the Empire’s best peoples joined the Second Empire. Their best generals, tradesmen, and politicians. It is for that reason they are powerful. The government has reasoned a truce with the Kingdom, and is actively at war with the Exiled Empire and the New Councillist Province.

## The Current Government
Right now, the Emperor is Ruion Reimig. The Reimig Dynasty have been in power as the Empire’s leaders since 3100, when their lands were only northern Ralean. Life is mostly good under the Reimig dynasty. Ruion’s government is fair and just. It’s a powerful government that keeps its citizens safe. The economy of the northern lands is flourishing at the expense of the integrity of the Terlayan economy. Almost all of Ruion’s armies are down south, either fortifying against the south’s attacks or preparing to take back population centres within.

# 2800s: The Appearance of Reimig and Heusi

## 2823: Partition of Eresonia
The Eresonain Empire had been a dominating power on the continent for 650 years, slowly centralising and expanding. The history of the First Eresonain Empire is a complex matter, but all we need know for Ralean is that the Eresonain Empire owned all of Terlaye apart from the cities and regions around Serven and Ateimot, and all but northern Ralean. This northern Ralean has been using an autocratic dynastic method of rule since its complete freedom from Kan-Koronar in 2530.

In 2823, the Eresonian Empire partitioned its lands between local lords, who kept on ruling it using a similar system to the one the Empire had set up for a few decades. A few decades passed, and with the governance of the Eresonian Empire being a distant memory, the local populaces transitioned to rules of governance that the people, and those in power, liked more. Much of Terlaye transitioned into its heroic, chivalric feudal system that its generations had been familiar with in stories passed down since the fall of Nao Techia. Central Ralean had taken the systems from the north and had become a dynastic autocracy, although the south would need longer to convert.

## 2876: Appearance of the Reimig Dynasty
It was around this time when the Reimig dynasty’s name first appeared in history, on a letter requesting a specific ingredient not for magic, but for cooking. Several additional mentions of the Reimig legacy sprung up throughout the decades, with the cooking being for local governors at first before their clientele became more important throughout the decades. There is some dispute as to if this was the same lineage as the modern Reimig dynasty, as it’s not the most glorious origin despite the clear reputation of their food. The Reimig dynasty has embraced this knowledge as its legacy, while many more nationalist folk think that it adds too much illegitimacy to the story, and that the Reimig dynasty must have been something more.

## 2889: Reimig Dynasty seizes control of Ilandus
In the year 2889, Heusi Reimig assumed control of Ilandus. Cook or not, Heusi had gained a large set of loyal followers. Among these ranks were commanders, soldiers, local figureheads. Around this time, the city of Ilandus was unstable. A drought in the lake had food supplies running low, and the local governor refused to address the issue. Heusi was vocal about how he would fix the issue if he were in power. He held many speeches with the support of the popular figureheads.

The country that Ilandus was part of was controlled by the Eisgal Dynasty, whose capital was the city of Vezandus. Usually, the dynasty’s two princes would be in Ilandus and Detsemoced but at this time the Eisgal dynasty was at war at its western border, the Farlan dynasty. While they only owned one city, their riches were more spread out and they controlled more land. The Eisgal dynasty had mobilised its rulers to lead armies in this war, leaving the city of Ilandus defenceless apart from its militia, which only half was loyal to the governor.

The governor at the time could recognise what type of movement Heusi was brewing, and on the third day of the second week of the eighth month, or as it is in the calendar, Entindina Dures Nelenia 749, the governor sent his local guards to stop a rally started by Heusi. With his own set of soldiers and commanders, and a devout following, Heusi and his followers rioted against the governor. The governor’s guards weren’t expecting a military battle and were slaughtered by the well-put-together commanders and their soldiers as they used tactics that aided the commoners in dealing damage. They later stormed the palace, exiling the governor and putting Heusi on his desk, to declare the independence of the people of Ilandus from their previous lacking regime.

Heusi began their declaration of independence by sending letters to both warring dynasties, the Eisgal and the Farlan. These letters, of which our university has copies of, states the following transcribed from the original Dynastic language to the modern Empiric. The letter to Eisgal follows, and the letter to Farlan afterwards.

“Iancol Eisgal, it is with a sorrow that I have to say, as a representative of the people of Ilandus, that the rule of the Eisgal dynasty has failed the city and left us without food during this time of drought. Worse yet, a needless war has stolen our youth and sentenced them to death. In light of this situation, the city of Ilandus will no longer associate itself with the Eisgal dynasty, and will find its own methods of feeding its thousands. Signed, Heusi Reimig.”

“Dear Tensar Farlan, I, and the people of Ilandus, have recently come across a drought leaving the city starved. We have disconnected from your current enemy, the Eisgal dynasty, in hopes that self-governance could bring us greater prosperity. We are aware that alone, as a city, we are unable to sustain ourselves, so we call upon you in our time of need to relieve us of your enemy when they inevitably target our independence. Upon arrival, you will have the support of the people of Ilandus, and its immediate area. Signed, Heusi Reimig.”

Both of these letters paint a clear picture, that Heusi expected one of the three Eisgal armies to turn back and besiege the city, hoping to starve it out so Heusi couldn’t fulfil his promises to the people. At the same time, Heusi hoped that the Farlan dynasty would respond positively, sending a relief army to the city to keep it out of Eisgal hands. As expected, mere weeks later the city was surrounded by a Eisgal army. Heusi and the people of Ilandus suffered hoping that an army would relieve them soon.

Within two days of the siege starting, the army of Tensar Farlan appeared on the horizon, and Heusi and his commanders mobilised the militia, and as the army of Tensar attacked, the militia sallied out as well. The Eisgal army was defeated within the day, routing and leaving. After the battle, Heusi and Tensar held a meeting in which they discussed the trajectory of the current war, and the position of Ilandus within it. Not much is known about the exact words said, but Tensar communicated his goals in this war, and how the city of Ilandus would help play that part. At the same time, Heusi negotiated the sending and receival of a caravan of food.

## 2891: Ilandus controls the Valley of Illan
Within two years, the city of Ilandus had a tight control on its entire valley, the Valley of Illan, and the Lake of Illan. Heusi designed and ordered the construction of a dike across the draining river of the lake, claiming that it would hold water in for longer should such a drought that caused him to get into power occur again. Around the same time, the head of the Eisgal dynasty was killed in battle and its lands were divided among the princes. During this divide, Ilandus was expected to have claimed control over more of the valley, and that of the lake.

# 2900s: Reimig Solidifies as a Dynasty

## 2914: End of the Farlan Dynasty
On one historic day in 2914, the entirety of the Farlan dynasty was assassinated. The tale still exists to this day, told as a piece of folklore to the youth of the modern world. I’ll recite the tale here, although it is known to deviate from the real story in parts.

The Farsan Family ruled Jonati

Lived their life in seven-fourty

The Eisgal rulers fought to expand their territory.

1.      With three cities and three princes, the armies of the Eisgal crossed the second fork of Sefare Eysenre:  
Gyda thri dinas a thri thywysog, croesodd byddinoedd yr Eisgal ail gangen Sefare Eysenre.

2.      The armies hoarded food during a time of drought, and held grain from the prosperous lands by Calaerula.  
Casglodd y byddinoedd fwyd yn ystod cyfnod o sychder, a chadwodd ŷd o’r tirion teg wrth Calaerula.

3.      The locals raided and stole the food, as they couldn’t grow their own. A city even revolted, outraged without food leaving Eisgal with two.  
Ymosododd y lleolion a dwyn y bwyd, gan nad oedden nhw’n gallu tyfu eu hunain. Gwrthryfelodd dinas, wedi digio heb fwyd, yn gadael Eisgal gyda dau dinas.

4.      The Farsan armies took advantage of the enemy’s starving soldiers, taking more land than they had expected, and more than the Eisgal had been willing to give.  
Manteisiodd byddinoedd y Farsan ar filwyr newynog y gelyn, gan gymryd mwy o dir nag yr oedden nhw wedi disgwyl, ac mwy o dir nag yr oedd yr Eisgal wedi bod yn barod i’w roi.

5.      The head of Eisgal died gloriously in battle, defending his city. The last prince held on to his land fearfully, while the exiled prince wanted revenge.  
Bu farw pennaeth Eisgal yn ogoneddus mewn brwydr, gan amddiffyn ei ddinas. Daliodd y tywysog olaf at ei dir yn ofnus, tra roedd y tywysog alltud eisiau dial.

6.      He found a cook, and the Farsan’s food taster. He fed the food taster small bits of poison over decades, until he was immune.  
Bwydodd y blaswr bwyd darnau bach o wenwyn dros ddegawdau, nes iddo fod yn gwrthsefyllu’r gwenwyn.

7.      Then, he laced the cook’s food with plenty of poison, and when the taster had it, it tasted just like normal, as he’d been tasting the poison his entire career.  
Yna, fe wnaeth roi ddigon o wenwyn yn fwyd y cogydd, a phan gafodd y blaswr ef, roedd yn flasu’n arferol, gan ei fod wedi bod yn blasu’r wenwyn trwy ei flynyddoedd dwethaf.

8.      The entire Farsan family gathered around a table, eager to try the cook’s renowned recipe.  
Casglodd yr holl deulu Farsan o amgylch bwrdd, yn awyddus i roi cynnig ar coginio enwog y cogydd

9.      They ate the whole lot, and thought the poison was an odd relish. That odd relish would keep them asleep that night.  
Bwyton nhw’r cyfan cwbl, a meddyliodd fod y wenwyn yn gwisg flasus. Byddai’r gwisg flasus hwnnw’n eu cadw’n cysgu’r noson honno.

The tale is mostly historic, although Iancol Eisgal didn’t die “defending his city”, rather he fell on the front line. And the other “prince”, who was his son, wasn’t left without land as he was given Detsemoced. The whole tale about poison is generally believed to have happened though, as other accounts recollecting the events state that the taster lived a long and bountiful life afterwards.

The primary oddity is that usually when the story is told, the Farlan dynasty is replaced with a “Farsan” dynasty. This error is believed to be a spelling mistake by a courier sharing the news across Farlan lands, that stuck since.

At this time, Heusi was still very much a populist leader, enacting policies that the public enjoyed. As some policies show, he was intending to move his nation closer to the more traditional way of ruling. Many of these more traditional policies he enacted without much news.

With the entirety of the Farlan dynasty dead, its controlled lands had no real successor. After a brief moment of light chaos, governors of towns and cities declared their independence. Some figures would force their way into power. These warlords came from all across Ralean, strong and cunning commanders and fighters that usually acted as mercenary armies for the dynasties saw an opportunity to take some unclaimed land. To the north, on the border with the Heabe, the neighbouring Natin Aredvic clan had answered some calls for help by towns and villages under threat of these warlords.

## 2938: Succession of Heusi
After fourty years under Heusi’s control, his government more or less resembled any other dynasty, with a few popular choices of policy still holding up from the initial rebellion. The people were mostly content, remembering Heusi for saving their starving lives many years ago, and most saw him as a legitimate ruler. Heusi had managed to solidify his family name as one of the many important dynasties, although his populism and good cooking stood out among the conquerors.

In 2938, Heusi died of old age, expected to have reached the age of 90. Heusi had six children at this point, four of which were of his blood and the other two were adoptees, and the youngest. His first child, successor, and son was named Tensar. Tensar Reimig was suspected to have aided in the ruling of his father’s lands as he was aging. When Heusi died, Tensar took a more complete control over the lands. Tensar at this time was aged about 40 years old, although he was aiding in the ruling of his fathers lands for decades prior. Despite its popular routes, the Illan valley became like any other land in northern Ralean, and the dynastic rule had taken place once more.

With the legacy of his father to look up to, Tensar would be ambitious, but these ambitions would manifest as usual business for a dynasty. He set out to control the entirety of Eysenre, primarily the city of Jonati, and its valley. His competition was a warlord who had taken Jonati at the fall of the Farlan dynasty. Defeating this experienced commander and his well-equiped troops would be a challenge enough, but Tensar also needed enough support from his people to justify a war. Tensar made a number of speeches to his people, and skewered news in an attempts to convince them that the people under the warlord’s rule were miserable.

## 2940: Tensar’s war with the Warlord of Jonati
After two years of news, propaganda, and speeches about the worse lives of the people of Jonati, Tensar managed to have the nation’s people support a war against the warlord. Tensar moved an army west from Illandus directly to Jonati. About halfway there, in the mountains, a scout relayed to him that the warlord’s army was approaching and battle was inevitable. In the following battle, Tensar would be wounded by a stray arrow in the shoulder, but ultimately come out victorious with the warlord’s army fleeing southwards.

With the corridors to Jonati open wide, Tensar marched his army and sieged the city. The warlord had taken much of the militia to fuel his own army, so Tensar had a few infiltrators spike up enough courage in the local population to fight the warlord’s militia from the inside. The siege of Jonati ended mere weeks after it started when some of the local population opened the gatehouses while fighting with the warlord’s militia.

Jonati would come under siege from the Warlord’s army, but with a barely depleted garrison from Tensar’s army, the siege was futile as Tensar sallied out onto the fields of Jonati to fend off the warlord one last time.

Tensar sent smaller army divisions across the lands under Jonati’s influence to drive out the rest of the Warlord’s men and garrison them, solidifying the Reimig dynasty’s control over the Jonati Valley. Tensar remained in Jonati making speeches about his policies to the local population and its governors, much to the joy of the people listening. Tensar would spend the rest of his years in power remaining mostly populist and convincing the governors of nearby towns that used to be in the Farlan dynasty to join his protection.
