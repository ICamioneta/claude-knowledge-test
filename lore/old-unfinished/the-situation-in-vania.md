A 3488 report on the Political and Economic Situation in Vania, a Precaution for Eresonian Councilism.
# Chapter 1: A Political History
In Councilist Ereso, we are complacent with our fairly mundane politics. We elect delegates from our fellow workers and townspeople, and attend our weekly reporting meetings with that delegate to get our news. This was largely how Vania's politics worked until 3456, with some important political caveats.

## The Rise of the Estateers
In 3410, Vania saw the emergence of the first organized political party in Ereso since the New Councilists dissolved in 3300. Following the disaster of the Vanian Necromancer in 3330, pre-established labour-based economic organisations had been dismantled. The following reconstruction was paid in large part by Eresonia even after the regional council of Vania voted for independence. This money was primarily given to independent entities who organised reconstruction efforts. As a consequence of how the reconstruction was organised, labour-based working organisations were small and few in the reconstructed areas.

Throughout the late 3300s, these private organisations lead independent efforts to undermine their local labour-based competition. These efforts used several different strategies such as market manipulation and buying out or cooperating with suppliers. At this time, these efforts were disconnected, tactics devised by individuals analysing their economic situations. This would radically change in the beginning of the 35th century.

The Estateers emerged as a genuine economic and political organisation formed from these small and medium business owners in Aone, Ebysarsir, and the various villages west of Karel. It's subtle goal was to create an organisation to aid private organisations in outcompeting labour organisations in hopes to remove labour influence west of Karel. The official goal of the organisation was to "promote competition" and paradoxically, "Facilitate the cooperation of corporations".

The first act of the Estateers was to declare an economic war that the opposing belligerents were unaware of. The Estateer's economic assault on Karsir used proven tactics bolstered by the national cooperation of the businesses. The Estateers could fund businesses artificially lowering prices to outcompete labour to a far greater degree. The Estateers as an organised economic party explicitly raised prices to Labour while lowering prices to fellow businesses. Within a year of the beginning of the economic assault, markets in Karsir were physically divided between the sections of labour, independents, and the Estateers.

The economic assault on Karsir was as successful as it could have been. At the time, Estateers controlled about 45% of Karsir's economy, with Labour making up 40% of the remaining economy (15% reserved for independents and government). This is compared to a 78% market share by Labour before the assault.

This success lead to the proposal of a reorganisation of the political body of Vania. It is common knowledge that the model of Councilism is predicated on democratic labour-based organisations, but by 3450 over half of the Vanian economy was company-based. The "New Constitution" was a proposal by the Estateers to re-organise Vania to better suit its economic reality. The motion passed with a 65% majority, which is usually attributed to the Estateer ownership of media outlets west of Karel and in Karsir.

## The 3456 Constitution
On a surface level, the 3456 constitution proposed a reform from a democracy to a democracy, however the fine details of the new political organisation of the "National Council" would subtly support the Estateers, an argument that will be explained later. 

The Constitution outlined the adoption of a Parliament of Vania. Instead of council delegates, defined constituencies would instead elect members of parliament (MPs), often separate from working organisations and local councils. The role of the MPs is nearly identical to council delegates, however their method of selection, election, provides the key difference over delegation.

In delegation, sections of labour choose from among their own councils who will go and represent them on local and national councils. These choices happen in councils, public meetings, to which anyone can attend. The delegates chosen from and in these meetings still attend, actively informing their council in the matters discussed in their higher council. In elections, however, potential "delegates" more properly called candidates campaign for the support of the constituency who, come some day of the year, will choose which of the candidates will most represent them. MPs elected this way have no home council that they must attend, nor any workers they talk to. They go to Karsir and live in parliament. The promise is that elected candidates are dependent on acting out their promises to have the continued support of their constituents. The promise is that they will act out the interests of their constituents.

Another key point in the constitution was the potentiality of a head-of-state. Touted as a "first-among-equals" and a "practical implementation" for executive decision, the policy was clearly unpopular even among Estateers. The constitution made important clarifications on the role, such as accountability to parliament and a unanimously selected candidate. For any one person proposed as head-of-state, they would need to gain the support of 75% of parliament, a near impossibility.

The first few election cycles of the new constitution saw the same people who were previously delegates become MPs. However, throughout the next decades, we would see a shift naturally supported by the system, a shift that was likely intentionally designed.

## Labour Party
Political parties formed as a consequence of the layout of Parliament. Parliament's affinity towards political parties was an effect well understood by all passing the bill, so many political parties were formed in preparation for the system. 

One such party had its roots well before the proposal of the constitution. The Labour Party started as an organisational council in Karsir whose goal was to oppose the efforts of the Estateers during the economic attack. It helped in halting the economic takeover, but was too weak to reverse it. During the proposal and acceptance of the 3456 constitution the Labour Party spread to include all labour-based organisations. 

Labour party MPs were initially the same people who were delegated to the national council. Initially, Labour MPs still attended their worker councils regualrly. The structure of the political party has evolved in large part to keep up with the Estateer party. Now, many Labour MPs are no longer workers but dedicated politicians making immense efforts to represent the case of the worker in parliament. 

## Free Market Party
Something often unmentioned in surface-level overviews of Vanian politics is the role of the minor party. There is too much focus on the primary conflict: Labour versus Estateer. After the establishment of these major parties many independents and other constituents felt severely underrepresented. These constituents, when not in support of one of the major two parties, have formed their own political organisations. 

One such party is the Free Market Party, founded on the belief that independent farmers are not benefitting from the new political organisation and the economic war, and seeks to keep markets free and open without any inflated "special prices" for members not of the seller's particular party. The MPs of the Free Market Party are often family members of independent families who are motivated and economically unburdened due to the support of their families. 

Target constituents are all independents who are affected badly by the economic war, but it primarily appeals to farmers who sell their goods in the market. It's membership also includes many independent artisans who sometimes have places in markets.

## The Guards
The role of the Guards and the organised military in Vania has historically been relaxed. Under early councilist military organisation, the guards operate through a unique bottom-up system much like Councilism itself. The common soldiery from among themselves choose a corporal, who are part of a section who delegates from among themselves a commander, and so on until the general commanders elect the Marshal of Ereso. At all stages, the military reports to the councils of Ereso.

Vania and its military split from Ereso before many minor reforms to this system. For example, early Eresonian delegation did not require candidates undergo military training. This was because of the youth of the army. Now, courses in military academies have to be completed to even have a chance at running for your section's upper rank delegation. Vania never adopted such a system. This is not to say its generals are not educated: Vania has military academies and many of its highest ranking officers studied in the established Eresonian institutions. 

The plan of Vania over the past hundred years has explicitly been economic revival. The military has largely been ignored by Vanian politics. This is not an issue, as Vania is under the military protection of Eresonia. The military and the closely connected guards have still remained an element of Vanian society, as always, representing some form of order.

The structure of the organisation is tied to locality. Squads, corporals, and commanders are all beholden of a certain local council, with higher ranking positions answering to the councils of towns or cities. The 3456 constitution made no mention of the military, so it was assumed that the leaders of the army, who answer to the national council, have a seat on parliament, as delegates of the military. 

The existence of a military political party was disputed among the soldiery, who often had established political leanings through their family. Regardless, once the case was made, a proper organisation was nigh inevitable. The guard party is explicitly limited in its nature, the guards don't dominate any constituency, but the presence of the military leaders in positions of power is significant. The party then acts more as a statement of political unification among the soldiery, so as not to have the military itself torn apart by a political-economical conflict that it could otherwise survive. 

The guards act as a mediating force between the two hostile political factions, using their position as the holders to the monopoly of violence to keep conflicts bloodless.

# Chapter 2: Economic Overview
Vania is a fully autonomous economic unit. It produces the fundamentals of its own economy. The Vanian Economy is split into several sub-units based on locality and primary production. In general, sub-units are based around the towns in a sort of hub-and-spoke model of town distribution. Most production is handled locally, although many hubs still need to import key goods from neighbouring hubs.
