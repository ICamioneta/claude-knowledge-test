The God of Knowledge has come to me to help distribute this truth. The gods do not care for worship, and they believe churches to be against their domains. This pamphlet is to help get the truth out there. I will be touring the country with Esten who are unprovable of the good gods. There will also be Esten touring independently. 

The gods do not care for our worship. They are not listening, and even when they are they will not act on our prayer. The gods and their Esten act on their own accords, according to their domain and nothing else. We do not understand their domains fully, and we should not worship our characters of their domains.

There is no moral good or evil determined by the gods. The morality of the gods is complicated and far beyond mortal understanding. The gods wish that we define morality on our own, without pretending it comes from them, for all of our moralities come from us anyway.

The heavens are not static. The gods change constantly. New gods enter and leave Eternity constantly. The latest god is the God of Trickery, Dwyll. We should not add this god to our pantheons. We should merely understand their domain, and choose to follow it if we wish.

Adnabyd is the God of Knowledge, and they would appreciate it if we focussed on expanding the knowledge present in the Mortal World rather than record what knowledge we do have. Being rid of the church will inform the people so that they may extend the limits of knowledge. 

Medr is the God of Skill, and she wants us to live our lives as we do. She will send herself and her Esten to help those of us she wants to gain skill, and she will train herself alongside the best of personhood to expand her domain of skills.

Ymdig is the God of Behaviour. They want to understand all behaviour, and is interested in the adaption of behaviour in all creatures. They cannot think of a way that mortals could expand her domain but act true to ourselves, and behave in our most authentic way.

Bwydo does not want us to feed everyone. He is concerned exclusively with the culinary arts. The creation of new tastes, the production of interesting foods. He and his Esten will continue to appear to the world's chefs and help them with the finest of tastes. He cares little for our hunger.

Riw wants us to live our most authentic lives. She does not care for how we proceed in our sexual and romantic activities. The churches and followers in her name are artificial, and following an idea that does not exist. She wants us to do what we want, and she and her Esten will observe and intervene whenever she deems suitable.

Cyfapheb only wants to observe and catalogue the communications of everything. She does not care how we communicate, and wants us to talk in authentic ways and evolve language as we will without her intervention. 


The traditionally evil gods, the Antonyms as they are often called, are said to not be inherently evil as we have come to believe them. 

Adnabyd likes Gwalloph because madness often leads to an expansion of his domain as the mad proceed in ways that the sane would never consider. 

Sirphni is not evil because she reminds us to take a rest, even if we should be aware not to do it in excess as Sirphni might want. 

Diofalux is not a god of accidents and random pains, it is a god of understanding all preceding to any event, and what the real root of accidents are. They are a god of causality.

Glinphe is a god of greed. They advocate for us to think only of ourselves. They are certainly more against our traditional moralities, but they want the removal of the church for their own bad-will reasons that we should be aware of as we remove the churches in the wishes of the other gods.

Maleisus and Gwrphcim enjoy our pain and suffering, and seek to challenge social norms. They are the most morally abhorrent to us people, and even the gods do not like their actions on principle. They do not want us to be educated because it is easier for them to manipulate those who worship blindly. 


Hell believes that we will continue to worship the sun regardless of our knowledge. The sun remains the ultimate life-giver and provider of worldly warmth. They would prefer we worship the sun over them if we worshipped at all. Lleh is much the same. 

Enw's domain is identities. The churches in their name do not understand this. Enw wishes for us to explore the concept of identities to its fullest, by being our authentic selves without the influence of a church.

Dim is the God of the Void and proclaims that their domain cannot even be nothing, for nothing is something in concept. Worship of Dim is something, so they wish for it to stop.

Dyddiad is the god of days and time, they do not care for the churches that carefully keep track of time. The churches are meaningless to Dyddiad's actual domain, which is his own date-keeping and calendar collection.

Cynnag will not grow your crops. Decay will not cause their downfall. There is no use in praising one and cursing the other. Cynnag's domain is in growth as a concept, in all forms, both natural and artificial. Decay is in the end of all things, the eventual end of life and the temporality of all living things. One cannot exist without the other.

Helfa, the God of Hunting, enjoyed her worship but realises the need to dissolve the churches. She does not want us to stop revering her at the beginning and end of every worship, but know that if she or her Esten are not on your hunt then praising her does nothing to the success of that hunt, and she will not hear you. 

Caesi will not stop your ship from sinking. They will not keep the skies clear for your voyage. They will do whatever they want, causing storms, creating sea life, for their domain is the ocean, and their favourite ocean is Rahoamor.

Achdene's domain is not only the bolts of lightning that we often attribute to her. No, her domain is spontaneity in essence. All sudden movements and decisions fall under her domain, including quick natural phenomena. She does not cause all lightning bolts, there is no reason we should scream her name whenever we hear one. 

Ysbiti wishes that the hospitals established in his name continue regardless of our reverence of him. We must know that these hospitals do us good, and that he does care about our hospitality to one another even if he cannot respond to prayer and if prayer means nothing. 

Cryver is annoyed that we pray to him wishing for strength rather than realising that the only way to gain our own strength is through our own training and will. He would also like for us to know that his domain extends beyond physical strength, but includes mental strength and resilience in opposition to difficult times.

Fflam wants us to know that it is we who make mortal fires. When a house burns, it is more likely a candle knocked over than his malicious wildfire. When a campfire is lit, it is because we are intelligent in our application of fire, not because he wills it. 

Thaler does not believe we need a church to tell good stories. We are to live life and use it to inspire our tales. 

We have misunderstood Drex's domain. They are not the God of Glass, the passage to truths. Their domain is more aligned with mirrors, in reflection and also in change. Fragility is also in their domain. Their domain is multifaceted and with many edges, like glass. 

Anifey does not respond to our calls to her to control our domesticated animals, and she is not the God of wild beasts. Her domain covers the animals we control. The cattle and the dogs. She wants us to focus on our relationship with animals without pretending that she will do anything to help.

Dihenydd wants us to destroy, but also understands the need to take respite to destruction to allow us to rebuild. Regardless, he does not want the churches to end for he can exploit faithless worship as a vessel for his destruction.

Senne, the God of Sound, does not care much for music. They care only for interesting noises and the energy of sound's pressure. They will do their own thing, and worship of them does nothing for us.

Arhos's domain is rarely understood. They are the God of the Eclipse, of thresholds, of unique events and of liminality. There is no worship of them we could provide that would be meaningful to their domain. They have no power over the phenomena of an eclipse, when the moon hides the sun.

Bedd wants us to continue our intricate burial rites for they provide us closure and spirituality, but they wish for us to understand that he is only a god of graves.

Rhif, the God of Numbers, does not see any reason why we should spend time worshipping them when we could be learning classical concepts of mathematics. They will continue to do their number stuff and they wish for mortals to innovate in the field. 

Tanea, God of the Forge, is forging their own things and helping others with their own forges. Prayer to them is meaningless, they cannot ensure your success unless he or his Esten are directly helping you, and you will know when this is happening.

Breuddwd does not control dreams. They are capable of entering dreams and altering them to some aspects, but there is only one Breuddwd and their Esten are not capable of anything but watching dreams. There are so many people and only one God of Dreams. Prayer to them will do nothing. Dream as you will, and know it is your own mind creating the dreams and not the God of Dreams.

Prydferth believes we have misunderstood her domain to the point of malice. She sees beauty in all things, and hope we'd do the same. She states no-one is more or less beautiful than anyone else, and that natural phenomena that are beautiful are not her products but the things she enjoys alongside us. 

Distaw does not support the churches of silence. They do not advocate for silence. They just enjoy the peace of the moment, and wishes for us to do the same. 

Sbri did not create beer. They do not start any parties. They enjoy them alongside us, they congratulate us in our revelry. Praying to them hoping for a fun time is needless.

Cyfun, the God of the City, is completely misunderstood. They are not the god of mortal cities and settlements. They are the god of their own city in Eternity, the place where all gods and their Esten mix.

Creed wishes that we would congratulate ourselves whenever we invent. It is we who create new things, and not them. Thanking them for inventions is cruel to the person who actually created the thing you are celebrating. 

Gwaed is disgusted by how many cultures make people bleed in their name. They are a god of life, and blood is the representation of that. They are also a god of strong emotion, which is in their domain. Know that any blood that spills should be done in passion, or naturally. They are a multifaced god.

Hapsus, the God of Randomness, curses the predictable randomness we pretend is quirky and odd. Goats are not random. Shouting potato is not random. True randomness is unpredictable, and quirkiness is predictable. 

Raifel as the God of War does not want us to start wars in his name: wars are to be natural and he will help neither side in a war. Xyldo, often misunderstood, has their domain in the overturning of systems. 

Marol, the God of Death, does not wish death. They do not care about the circumstances of death nor are they concerned by the aftermath. they only care about the moment, and they are there to observe it and understand mortality, for mortality fascinates the gods.

Ammehdytha does not care for our crops. Praying to them will not improve our harvests. We are to perform our agriculture to the best of our abilities, and Ammehdytha will observe and help those of us they want in the expansion of their domain. 

Ahergefmot is annoyed that we often attribute events of incidence to coincidence. That is all they wish for us to know, and that crying their name in moments of coincidence is pointless.

Mwedre sees all types of curses and praises. They want us to know that bugs are their own creatures. Mwedre merely makes their own bugs, and has bugs as their domain. They do not control bugs.

Lusg, God of the Crawl and Dungeons, has enjoyed the sportsmanship of his worship, but realises in the interests of the other gods that worship needs to be ended. They know that we will continue to test ourselves against his challenges.

Adberth is disgusted in the volume of mortal sacrifice in their name. Adberth's domain is not in ending life, they are disgusted by it and have been powerless to stop us from it. Their domain is in personal sacrifice for that which matters. Like when we work for the greater good of society, or take it upon ourselves to clean our house. That is the sacrifice covered by Adberth's domain.

Adferiad, much like Ysbiti, hopes that we continue the Church of Healing regardless of our worship of her. The church of healing does mortals good, and she wishes for this good to continue. She states that most churches of healing were already aware of her true nature, and it is only their clients who thanked her instead of those people actually working in the church. 

Awel, god of Air, has their domain in the intangible, in distance and direction, and in the breath of life. They are a multifaceted god, and worship of them is meaningless.

Dayer, God of Mud, is a god of fertile grounds and of the physical earth. Their domain is in the grounded conception. Worship of them provides nothing to their domain, and in fact furthers our tethers to the ground. We are to remain grounded in our beliefs, and not continue our meaningless worship of the gods.

Hirle, the God of Erosion, is rarely worshipped by us, and wants us to know that their domain is in natural processes that we have little control over.

Ofidea, god of Snakes, is not the god of deception, a characterisation of snakes. She is merely a mortal who dedicated themselves to snakes themselves, and their domain is now in the actual creature that is snakes and everything they are. They are not related to any characterisations we make of snakes. 

Cov does not hold every memory and they cannot help you remember. Their domain is in the concept of memory itself. 

Trefin does not want order everywhere in the Mortal Realm, they merely appreciate order where it exists and they try to apply their domain wherever they can to the world. They enjoy the expansion of their domain, which is occurring naturally as our governments evolve. 

Dyrgel does not hold your secret unless one of their Esten witnessed it or it was mentioned around them. And do not worry, your secrets are safe in their hands. Dirgel is not a malicious god. 

Darlin the God of Conspiracy, does not organise any conspiracies, they merely enjoy them and often partake in them if they are sufficiently exciting. As mortals, there is nothing we can do, but prayer to Darlin if we have our own conspiracies or if we want conspiracies against us to stop will do nothing. 

Resum, god of Logic, is who proposed to educate us in the first place. They view worship of the gods as illogical from a perspective of perfect information. They hope their field can be expanded as we act logically given this new information.

Gewis just wants a good play. You cannot pray to them to improve the quality of your performance, unless he or his Esten are directly participating in your play there is nothing Gewis can do for you. Use your own power to put on a good play.

Cerrig, God of Rocks, is a new god that we have not come to worship yet. They wonder what we would have worshipped but ultimately do not see how we could connect the concept of rocks to any meanignful worship. Their domain is in rocks, which are more interesting than you might think at first glance. They appreciate stonemasonry.

Mwender, God-King of Annwvyn, wants mortals to know that ambition is something we achieve for ourselves. If we put our lives into something we will likely do well in it. They no longer rule Annwvyn because their mortal ambitions are but a small part of their new godly consciousness.

Dwyll is the newest god, they are the god of trickery. They enjoy a good trick of all kinds, both harmless and malicious. Prayer to them is not something we should begin. We are to live with the gods as they observe and interact with us on their own accord. 

The gods are real but uninterested in worship. Churches distort their domains. Mortals must reclaim meaning, morality, and progress for ourselves.
