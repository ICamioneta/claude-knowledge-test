The Everlasting War is a rare book. The origins of the book are found in the northern Ralean University of Barogate lore, where several years ago the heads of the university communed with Adnabyd and asked them about the history of the mushroom forest of the Vugaix, and Adnabyd gave them a book. Then, the scribes made two copies of the book. One for the university’s personal collection and another to go north into Lale Darie. There is an alleged third copy. They say that it was copied again in Lale Darie. No-one knows where this third copy is.

# 1. The Beginning and Cynnag
In the beginning, when the Gods found the Mortal Realm, it was only geography, with the realms of Muz and Dos being only forests. Parts of the Waste were also recognizable, the Heabe was still the Heabe, and the northern parts were sparce forest and swamps. Notably, the Dithas and Vugaix were different. Firstly, the Dithas only had trees, none of the tall mushrooms we see today. Secondly, the Vugaix did not exist, it was part of Kyenel.

After the Gods created life, Bwydo spent his time creating new foods for the Muz. One of these he created was the Mushroom, something completely different for the Muz to indulge in. He made it grow all over, the small edible ones. Soon, the spores of the mushroom flew over the Dithas, and it flourished in the soil.

At the same time, the mushrooms were changing. Some became inedible, against Bwydo’s wish. Some grew taller in the Dithas, and some invaded trees sapping them of their hard-earned resources. This is when the Gods saw another Eternal appear. Cynnag, the Eternal of Growth. His appearance is attributed to the mushrooms sudden change, and his appearance was nothing new as the Second Gods had recently made their own appearance.

Cynnag seemed to wish well. The concept of growth is greatly appreciated by all, and he is credited to helping the Muz and Dos grow. But, his main focus on growth was the mushrooms he dearly stole from Bwydo. Bwydo did not like Cynnag for this reason. Cynnag would grow his mushrooms into the nice Dithas, and have his mushrooms steal the trees there before them. Soon, the trees of Dithas were co-inhabited with the Mushrooms of Cynnag. Cynnag did not want to stop there, and as he grew his mushrooms slowly onto the sands of Kyenel, the God of Decay, Pydred, was born.

# 2. The Eternal of Decay
Pydred’s appearance is attributed to the decay present in the Mortal Realm, but he rejects that. Pydred believes that he appeared for no reason in particular, and claims he chose Decay to suit him. He saw the decay of life after death in bodies and in food, and grew fond of it. This was no evil trait, as he believed without decay there would be no room for life. In his own famous words, ‘Sun does not hit a ground covered by intact corpses.’ Pydred, despite his horrific subject, is highly regarded and liked, especially by Bwydo. Bwydo claims that if food did not decay, people would become greedy and stockpile for no reason. He wishes for people to eat the food, and not keep the best for never.

Pydred soon saw the desert of Kyenel in its infinite glory, a bastion of decay, the sand. He took it and made the sands his own, and when he saw the eastern and southern parts being overrun with mushrooms he was not happy. He created great gusts to send sand over the mushrooms, expanding the desert hoping to return it to it’s former glory. It is for this reason Cynnag does not like Pydred, and soon the Everlasting War would begin, before any mortal decided to take arms.

# 3. The Everlasting War
Cynnag would grow his mushrooms in the sand, and have them turn the sand into dirt. Pydred would have great winds rip mushrooms from their roots, and cover them. At this point, Cynnag and his mushroom forest had created what is today the upper half of Heabe, and he also grew his mushrooms between Ralean and Cronar, and as we know there are no more mushrooms between Kyenel and Cronar.

Cynnag would make a special mushroom, one with harder shafts like bark on trees. He would place these all over his forest, which the mortals were calling ‘Vugaix’. These thicker trees would withstand the winds Pydred made, and with that the Vugaix grew twice as much on both fronts. Soon, Pydred would create his own creature to fight back against these harder fungi.

## 3.1 Weapons of Nature
Pydred would create the giant tortoise that roam Kyenel. He would not invent the shape, it was merely a shape he took off the northern shores. These Kruvan were illogically massive, and grew on all sizes. It was a great mystery to the mortals on how these creatures survived. In the end, Adnabyd was called in the University of Barogate Lore and simply said “The Kruvan exists because Pydred wills it.” These giant weapons were deployed en-masse to the south and trampled all of Cynnag’s mushrooms in that area. This is why the southern parts of Kyenel are in fact desert.

Cynnag’s effort in eastern Kyenel were pushed back too, but not before he created a mushroom with a massive and hard internal spike to impale the feet of any tortoise that would step on it. These grow on the boundary, the warzone. More recently, cosmologically speaking, Cynnag created what mortals call the Black Hearts, as a source of magical energy in the Vugaix to help fight against the desert.

To aid in his war efforts, Cynnag created a dog-like creature with white skin, three eyes, and three mouths that protruded like that of an anteater. These dogs would consume special mycelia and mushrooms that contained spores, and carry the spores around spreading them in their growth. With this, Cynnag could plant new seeds easily into Kyenel, so Pydred created his own beast to hunt down these dog-like creatures. He made an almost indescribable creature with leathery skin made like sandpaper. They have giant heads, with teeth the size of fingers, eyes a fingernail deep into their faces, and numerous asymmetric holes where the nose might be. Their bodies were odd, they have 6 legs that each have massive claws at the end. It uses sound to lure Cynnag’s creature and then consumes it. These are the Nidxa and the Flutsxa.

Quickly, Pydred was able to adapt his tortoise to have hard feet, so that they could still crush the spikes under the trees. Just doing this was not enough, but luckily for Pydred the people living in the Mortal Realm often picked and used the Black Hearts for magical claps. This softened the internal spikes within the trees, allowing them to be the trampled ones. Cynnag needed another way to deal with the Kruvan, and such he created giant lizards. The giant lizards, much like the Kruvan, were not an original shape, but one Cynnag used from the natural inhabitants of Kyenel, Duela, Dithas and his own Vugaix. Although not as large as the Kruvan, these Genoyg could take down the Kruvan in packs of three, by aiming for their neck and feet. The Genoyg do not wander the Vugaix, as they would be trampling the mushrooms, defeating the purpose of themselves. Instead, they are present across all of the open desert, hunting down Kruvan wherever they may be. They are more frequent on the border with the Vugaix, though.

# 4 Where we are now
Still, the war rages on, with giant tortoises trampling mushrooms and sandstorms getting heavier. By looking on a map, we can see that the Vugaix is succeeding on its southern front while it’s lacking on it’s northern. More black hearts have been growing south, which is most likely why such a thing is happening. Against his wishes, the Black Hearts are a useful ingredient in mortal claps, and they often come and harvest great amounts, weakening his front line. They also come trampling the undergrowth. As well as this battle of the plants, on the edges where the Vugaix turns into Kyenel a battle of ginormous proportions is happening, between the Genoyg and the Kruvan, both capable of defeating each other. The Genoyg attack people as well, given how the creature is on the Vugaix’s side and people destroy the Black Hearts.
