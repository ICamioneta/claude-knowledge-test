I should probably expand the Everlasting War into a full book as opposed to a short pamphlet.

I should start with the overview, which is just the original pamphlet.

Part 2 is delving into the origins of Cynnag and Pydred. What brought them about, what they did immediately after realising themselves, and the build up to their rivalry

part 3 is exploring the relationship of Cynnag and Pydred to the other gods of their time. The focus will be on their attitudes towards the war, and the war's consequences for them.

part 4 is the geographical evolution of Kyenel and the Vugaix, outlining every territorial gain highlighting the periods of significant front line shift.

part 5 is the weapons of nature. I'll have to detail each of Cynnag and Pydred's creatures and how they play a part in this war.

part 6 is the mysteries of the war. Just make some shit up, and the silver ruins.

part 7 should be the current state of the war, however short it should be. 

part 8 could be a conclusion, if there's room for it.
