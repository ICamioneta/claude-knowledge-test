Unknown author, highly distributed and sources well documented and sources verified to be true accounts of the eternals. Some sources are contradictory, with explanations as to reconciliations based in the reliability of eternal account. 

In our modern world, the phenomena of the Hidol is the target of much scientific inquiry. It promises the power of the gods, and mastery over the hidosis once thought as magic. 

As mortals, our ability to interact with the Hidol is limited, for reasons that we once didn't know. All we can interact with is our physical world, the masses and energies we can touch. The Hidol interacts with the physical world, and through this it indirectly interacts with us.

We are well familiar with wizards and mages, former magicians who through the diligent work of the Hidol institutes demystified themselves into Hidists, people who use the Hidol to produce some phenomena. Classical Hidology understands the Torfol as something that every living life-form. While groundbreaking, it struggled to find reasons why the Torfol exists, or describe the existence of Torfol in traditionally non-living things such as gem stones.

Now, with our modern scientific institutions, there is yet more inquiry on the Torfol and the Hidol. We know it exists, but not why. 

In our favour is the demystification of the gods. They, as a consequence of the well known 5005th Cyfun Anual, made themselves and their natures clear to us mortals. We were not satisfied, these revelations only gave us more questions. 

I have collected a number of accounts of discussions between mortals, eternals, and their esten. These accounts contain descriptions of eternal existence. They describe the Hidol and its inner workings, as the eternals understand them. 

Although undoubtedly useful, these descriptions are only the beginning of scientific inquiry. We must discover for ourselves how the hidol interacts with our mortal realm. We must try our best to test the limits of the torfol, and to quantify it. 

# The Account of Adnabyd

**Adnabyd on the origins of Eternals and Eternal life.**

It is difficult to talk about, your languages do not have easy words for the concepts. But I will try.

The Hidol is a multi dimensional substrate of energy with no space or time. It consists of atoms, which themselves have no space nor time. We Eternals materially consist of these atoms, it is the base of our consciousness similar to how mortal brains work. Without space nor time, it is hard to describe eternal existence. 

In the realms, there is space and time. For the realms of Eternals, we have constructed this space time ourselves. In realms, the hidol is conformed to space and time. To create things in realms, we turn the atoms of the Hidol into matter. We bring them from the Hidol into the realm, shaping and setting them as we will them to be.

_On the power of Eternals._

You are aware of atoms? Realms have atoms with space and time. The Hidol's atoms have no such quality normally. When the Hidol is bound to a realm they have a space and a time. They still possess the many more dimensions of the Hidol, something mortals have come to call the planar vector*. Manifesting requires certain planar vectors, and often a little push. 

(* planar vector is a common component of planar theory. Before these confirmations, around 1900, mortal hidol research described the extra-dimensional properties of a torfol using this term)

Eternals and our Esten can interface with the Hidol bound to a realm. We can arrange these planar vectors to our will, and bring them through into a realm using this interface. This is how we manifest, bound only by the width of the interface we may carve out for ourselves. 

In our own realms, we are the interface. That is what makes them our realms. When visiting another Eternal's realms, we may be granted an interface by the hosting eternal. In the mortal realm, we must always carve the interface. 

How do mortals manifest? Their torfol are the quality of the quantities of the motions of life. Natural alignment of the planar vector. Impressively, your mortal wizards can change their own torfol's planar vector, with the goal of aligning it to one that manifests. Eternals and our Esten too can do this when we manifest a mortal body.

Another way mortals manifest is through cleverly causing overlap between two or more torfol of smaller proportions, not tied to a thinking being. These torfol are the same, but due to their size they are more malleable. In a clap, their torfol overlap and together, if the dimensions line up, manifest into your spells. 

Such actions are constrained by the torfol and its potency. 

These are not the torfol creating an "interface". Interface is a crude word; we have real terms for it in our own languages. Torfol, through specific alignment, merely create the circumstance in which manifesting occurs.

**Adnabyd on the dimensionality of the Hidol**

How many dimensions a hidol atom possesses is a difficult question to answer when mortal methods of counting are not easily applicable to such concepts. In some cases, it is infinite. In others, less than infinite. Suffice to say, there are many, but countably many. 

**An Esten of Adnabyd on the existence of hidol consciousness**

If we want to see into the mortal realm without manifesting into it, we have to make that interface that you should be aware of. We can see the space that the interface is next to, but nothing more. If we actually want to see, we need to manifest ourselves some physical eyes and ears to sense the mortal realm like you do. 

In our own realm Adnabyd grants us interfaces as large as we need them. We can see whatever we want, to a certain limit. 

# The Account of Medr

**Medr on the skill of manifesting**

One impressive skill of mortals is their manipulation of the torfol to manifest. Neither wizardry, magery, nor gemcasting are easy to master. They are efficient in one way. They utilise the pre-alignment of the torfol to create easily reproducible manifestations. The way we and our esten do it is much more involved. We must align each atom we intend to use. Our advantage is we have access to every atom in the area, while mortals must make do with the atoms their torfol can access.

It is a skill, but it is one that all eternals can do at their greatest capacity. The real skill is tearing an interface between a realm and the hidol. I am not the best by far, I find it enough to manifest myself and learn the torfol way to manifest in a mortal body. I believe Pydred and Caesi are the most impressive on this front. Pydred has esten that constantly maintain interfaces to keep her giants alive. Caesi, on the other hand, tears huge interfaces with which he produces the most grand of forces to stir Rahoamor. 

When I was part of that roving bandit group, I asked of him to pause his storms so that the mortal Leider, I, and his bands could cross between Barogate and Annwvyn. He had only just finished preparing his next tempest, so I had to call in a favour I was owed. He urged me to see his preparations so that they would not go to complete waste. There, I was impressed at the sheer scales, potency, and precision Caesi was capable of. 

Mortals are capable of the same method of manifesting that we do. I taught the mortal Leider to create an interface, however small it was. He was not ever able to use this interface in his mortal life. 

An esten of mine, Scithe Dion Crant was his mortal name, scouted a potent wizard in 1223. A Naotec by the name of Ystezal Udrethei if you have heard of her. With the Esten's aid she was taught the creation of the interface. Scithe came to me for aid on how to teach the wizard to manifest through the interface. Excited by the possibility, I called for Adnabyd to join me. He was busy as he ever is, but made the room in his schedule for this hunch.

She was not the most motivated, her struggles and failures did not help. The challenge was that our minds as eternals are made of the hidol. We cannot emulate a mortal interfacing with it because we simply do not have mortal consciousnesses. 14 years in, she had managed to touch upon a manifestation that I had tried desperately to make it easy for her to reach to. 20 years in, she was capable of interacting loosely with the hidol on an accessible interface of my creation. After 40 years of arduous training, mostly by Scithe, Ystezal was able to manifest directly from the interface without our help. Her own interface was so small and her technique so unrefined that she could produce only a small spark of light. 

Despite my efforts to preserve her mortal life, she died before she could achieve anything more. She did not manage to teach anyone else what she had learned, but I have learnt how to teach mortals this ability more effectively. 

There have been many more wizards creating and using their interface since, but it is a skill I have delegated almost entirely to the purview of Scithe Dion Crant and his team.

**A Medres Gorchuil on movement of matter between realms**

(For those not in the know, a Medres Gorchuil is an Esten of Medr responsible for the mortals in her realm)

You mortals don't appreciate the lengths we go through to get you from your realm to ours. For you, it's just a silent transportation. One moment, you're in the fields of Kerré and the next you're in the most comfortably prepared bed in the resorts of Tanglin. In all that time, we've had to tear a hidol interface around your entire body and align every single atom with those around that cosy Tanglin bed. 

Do you know how many atoms that is? 20 octillion. I bet you don't even know what an octillion is. You've certainly never seen an octillion. 20 octillion on both sides. 

Once you're through, all that work is gone. Re-scattered back into the natural chaos of the Hidol. So be thankful. We put a lot of effort into it.

**From another Esten**

20 octillion? Sounds about right. There are more around you, but that's all you need, maybe a little too much especially if you're as small as yourself. Oh! I meant no offence! 

No, that number doesn't apply whenever we manifest anything. Usually, you only need as many atoms as you want in energy. The reason it's so much for transportation is because you need a sort of perfect matter portal. In a way, we make the the two sets of atoms at the source and destination indistinguishable, so that physical atoms can be mapped onto them in a way that they can just be pulled out from the other side, or pushed into from one. 

Anything less wouldn't actually transport you. Something we used to do, tens of millenia ago, we used to just copy physical atoms as they were and recreate them in the new realm. The copy was always imperfect in some way, like although it was a copy of the matter it wasn't actually the same matter, so it wasn't the same person. 

Then there were the ethics. I'm a pretty upstanding Esten, but Medr doesn't really care for ethics, she only started doing it the other way when the other eternals made her. Then, she got results, and acted like it was her idea.

# The Account of Dyddiad

**Dyddiad on Time**

You have a question for me? Well, that's more Adnabyd's domain. He's the one who set this up. I do know. Apparently, before he found the mortal realm, time in the six original eternal's realms were all different times. After he found the mortal realm, he saw it as an anchor. An unchanging event that our existences could finally be compared to. 

Well, truth be told, the Hidol does have time. Our consciousnesses are made out of material just like yours, just it's hidol atoms rather than realm atoms. Part of that consciousness is the hidol atom's time dimension. Our consciousness requires sequential coherence, which is part of why tying the time of our realms to this same dimension is so useful. To bind time to anything else is weird, and begs for caution. 

It fundamentally alters what time means for that realm, so we tend to avoid it. It also makes keeping a solid timeline a bit more challenging. 

I suppose, if you want another way of thinking about it, time isn't a backwards and forwards. It's not a past and a present. It's a now, and it's only a now. What you call memory is really just matter in the now making you remember things. 

Eternal memory is just the same; based on the dimension that is our now, we can infer the past and make predictions on the future. 

One thing, if it interests you, is that space dimensions don't need to be the same. They sometimes are, like Medr's realm. Generally speaking, this is how the different topologies of realms are defined. 

You are interested then. No, Medr's realm isn't reachable without transportation through the Hidol. It's other dimensions wildly differ; it is not in the same space as the mortal realm, it just uses the same dimensions for space. 

# The Account of Resum

*Adnabyd had rejected certain questions, but offered alternate sources. Resum was provided when Adnabyd "Why does the Hidol exist?".*

Have you not first asked why the mortal realm exists? Perhaps this is why Adnabyd was annoyed by your question. He believes mortals are occupied with the wrong mysteries. Ones he has the answers to, but refuses to share out of sport. He conceals himself in that way.

You have questions about the nature of existence. Well, here is my philosophy.

Consider a world where the Hidol did not exist. Then you would not be able to ask the question of why it does exist. The reason the Hidol exists is because it simply must exist. The question is itself a contradiction.

*The questioner concedes, and asks why the mortal realm exists*

It's well known in your own circles of religion and book-keeping that only six "gods" existed before the mortal realm was discovered by one of them. Then, the rest of us came out through some logic or reason. Adnabyd is one of these Eternals. 

I am little over 3000 years old. We do not know Adnabyd's true age as time was hard to track before the discovery of the mortal realm, but he estimates his own age at around 28500 years. 

The mortal realm has existed for much longer. We cannot reason for how long. There may be some residue pertaining to physical or hidol atoms around the Hidol that give away its age, but these have not been discovered. 

The mortal realm may have always existed. The logic for its existence are as existential for you as the reason for the Hidol's existence is for us. 

One thing that we do know. The mortal world may only be between 5 and 8 billion years old. Lleh and Hell (*Two Eternals concerned with the mortal heavens, or space, all that is the mortal realm but not the mortal world*). They claim to have observed new worlds being created from fire and stone, occurring distances so far from the mortal world as to be unreachable. While Rhif is a perfectly capable mathematician, I have verified the calculations myself, and to reach the temperatures of the mortal world these figures are roughly how long it would take. 

You want figures? Go to Hell and Lleh.

# The Account of Hell

**Hell on the size of the mortal realm**

Oh what a question! I did not expect to be in the presence of such inquiry. Surely Adnabyd, or his Esten are better suited to such formalities. 

If you insist on my participation. It is true. I have explored the mortal realm's limits. The mortal sun remains my one true fascination. Unlike the others, the heat it emits is life. It is fire! No other sun in the mortal realm does such a thing. They merely illuminate rocks, which can't be seen as stars from the mortal realm. I wonder why these exist, what is a sun without purpose? Without the nature of existence?

The question? Of course. 

In your favour, Rhif has requested that we familiarise ourselves with mortal measurements. I have not done this. The mortal realm is around 38 billion sun-diameters.

*This amounts to around 54 quadrilion kilometers*

**Hell on the luminance of the heavens**

Yes, you are right in saying that the mortal realm's topology has it loop on itself. You are right to question why there is a night sky at all. I have discovered that material travel across these great distances takes an additional unexpected energy. I have dubbed this myself: hidol drain. 

The mortal world's rotation? Unaffected. There is a threshold, and it is not enough to slow down planetary systems. 

It is not enough to see a few copies of stars in the skies, ever dimmer each time. It is plenty strong to stop the sun's light always reaching the mortal world, no matter which direction it takes.
