# The Situation in Vania
# A Brief introduction to Vania
Vania is a subregion of Ereso with a majority Kiyvan cultural population which is a distinctly Eresonian cultural group. Vania makes up one half of the Kiyvan peninsula, which is geographically separated from the rest of Ereso by the lake Iavan Thell and the Anlyrer river. While the peninsula is named after its culturally majority culture, the dry land close to Anlyrer is majority Sapyat. The peninsula is separated in half by the Vanian mountains, which stretch from the west coast to Terlaye, by Nater's End. 

On the north-west side of the Vanian mountains are the Kiu wetlands, a freshwater swamp bounded by a small hill range and these mountains. On the other side, Vania. 

The majority of Vania's development in the 28th century near a second height of the Eresonian Empire. Efforts to re-conquer Desheg put pressure on the local economy to support armies, so the developments of central Ereso largely made its way to Vania. Since, especially in modern times, Eresonia has become as developed as the centre. 

There are several ways to partition Vania, but what you will hear about several times in this report is the mention of Vania's several rivers. As major geographic barriers, they are great descriptors of economic development. Vania has 6 towns, 19 villages, and one city, according to the conventional town-hub classification system. Its major partitioning rivers are the River Tilhos, the River Eby, and the River Karel. 
# Historical Preamble
This is not a historical document. It does not seek to explain in detail how the modern situation in Vania arose, but a general historic overview is still important to provide some necessary context.
## Vanian Local Council, 3300-32
Councilism as a political system was a theory before 3300, the well-known official foundation of our modern-day Eresonain Councilist government. But councilism wasn't built in a day: our ancestors worked tirelessly from the March on Llofilot in 3281 building the physical and political foundations of Eresonia. Thousands of council buildings and other social and worker's hubs were founded in two decades during which the Wartime Council facilitated the transition from itself to the National Council. 

In 3330, a necromancer launched an invasion of the Vanian town of Aone. There are several theories on how the necromancer established themselves throughout 3320, many of which highlight the general oversight by the local council in handling local issues. This was, regardless, a failure of the Eresonian system. But this was the first prod of a branding iron that would leave a harsh mark on early Eresonian councilism.

The necromancer worked quickly, and the National Council had no time to respond. In weeks the Necromancer had made it to Ebysarsir and to the river Karel, at the doorstep of the local Vanian capital. The Eresonian military at the time had a frayed organisation. In the 30 years since the establishment of councilism, the military had never been the target of reform. The military begrudgingly amassed at key crossing points over the Vanian mountains, and stood still in the interest of keeping the undead contained to Vania. 

Reports from the time suggest the Eresonain army was wildly uninformed. Members in key positions were given poor information, some suggesting that all of Vania was overrun and that they should await further instruction, other reports suggesting that all of Vania was safe, and a mix of inconsistent reports detailing the alleged exact list of overrun and free towns. Many army bodies acted on these reports only to find them false. The necromancer had set up and maintained ambush positions expecting the incursion, and the inconsistency of the reports held the army back from all of Vania. 

Retrospective reports highlight how there were attempts from the Vanian army to signal to the rest of the Eresonian army that the eastmost crossings were safe, but the necromancer had led incursions across the mountains to make it seem as if no crossing was safe. In addition to this, the Empire of Ralean refused to let the Eresonian Military pass through Nater's End, which was the safest and easiest way for the Eresonian military to re-enforce the border between Vania and the necromancer.

Luckily, local Vanian forces were able to hold the line as a band of local heroes went on an incursion into the Necromancer's land, eventually managing to defeat him and thus toppling the entire empire. Once the necromancer was dead and the military structure of the undead armies was disorganised, Eresonian military forces were able to enter Vania and help clean up the vast majority of undead, freeing towns from their rule. 

But general Vanian consensus is that the rest of Ereso had failed Vania - a true statement given the potential alleviation of loss had the Eresonian army committed to their involvement. The organisation to which the local heroes were associated, the secretive Versidillen now made public, began the campaign for the independence of the Vanian council from the National council. Such a motion was incredibly popular following the recent events and the independence vote passed without issue. The Vanian Council would separate in 3332, becoming its own national council.
## Vanian National Council, 3332-3450
The government of the Vanian National Council was politically identical in the 50 odd years after its foundation to the 32 years it existed as a part of the Nation of Eresonia. This is because the local councils generally only have a few ties to the national council: a delegation, a political obligation to adhere to national principles, and the receipt or paying of a development fund. Vania was a payee of the development fund, with the primary recipients being Kiu, Reipo, Ursan, and Raikeor. After the necromancer's devastation, Vania was set up to be a recipient of the development fund for a short while. The main reason given for independence was the political severance, which would let Vania manage its own reconstruction without the intervention of Eresonia.

Following the Necromancer, the economic structures west of the river Karel were completely deconstructed. This left a massive economic gap in Vania, the regions had previously made up three fifths of the Vanian economy and the vast majority of the region's food industries. Without any established working presence, the region was reconstructed by private enterprising entities. 

These people and companies were the generally wealthy of Vania. A mix of well-endowed workers, companies with surviving stake, companies based east of Karel, and wealthy guildspeople. As part of the reconstruction of Vania, the government often handed out grants to these companies and individuals for the express purpose of re-starting the Vanian economy. A large part of these grants was reparations handed to Vania for the lack of military intervention during the necromancer ordeal.

Labour council organisations slowly reformed in these areas as well, but with the destruction of their historic precedent, their re-establishment was not as smooth as that of the private individual. 

Around 3350 the economic role of the private entity in reconstructed towns west of Karel evolved. The Versidillen had managed to change it's role from a secretive supporter of the Kiyvan culture and its peoples to a powerful cultural organisation. It's contributions to the reconstruction were impressive. Their primary contributions were facilitating the spread of information and in establishing a company focussed on the reconstruction of their home home town, Aone. 

Leading up to 3400, several private companies made individual efforts to undermine the efforts of labour forces in the area, each individually coming to the conclusion that the presence of labour was a threat to their profits, and that undermining labour now would let them make more money in the future. There is some historical suggestion that this tactic was distributed by the Versidillen, but these claims are conspiratorial. 

By 3400, there were very few labour-based organisations in Aone and Ebysarsir. It was around this time that these private entities started to cooperate in their efforts to undermine labour companies. In 3410, they officially formed a political organisation: The Estateers. This was the first political organisation in Vania, and its name coincided with its political ideals: that the land of Vania should be divided into privately-owned Estates. 

The Estateers began as a conglomeration of delegates on the Vanian Naitonal Council. Throughout the next 40 years, the political goals of the organisation shifted, and they would come to propose a re-organisation of Vania as a whole. 

In 3450, the Estateers had formed a "Competition Support" fund which got its money from the companies in Ebysarsir and Aone. This fund then funnelled money into private companies that had been long-established in Karsir and had been operating alongside labour organisations for over a hundred years by this point. The goal of the competition fund was explicitly the undermining of labour operation through explicit outcompetition. 

This economic push on Karsir was largely successful. Company organisations were explicitly more efficient and profitable than the labour organisations present, in large part due to the competition support fund and the grants given out as part of the reconstruction effort. 

Around this time, after Vania's long period of economic instability, the Estateers proposed a re-organisation of Vania: The New Vanian Constitution. It would be ratified in 3456, creating the modern situation in Vania.

# History and Introductions
Eternity is populated by a number of gods. Some which were always there, others which seemingly came into existence. Some are mortal people who became gods through their dedication. This section is dedicated to introducing the gods in chronological order to our knowledge of when they appeared. It includes several noteworthy events, such as the creation of species of people.

## The First Six Gods
In the beginning of our known part of Eternity, there were 6 floating consciousnesses. As Adnabyd famously said, “We were merely aware of our own existence”. The gods were children without parents, forced to figure out their own existence. Eventually, these consciousnesses realised the godly powers they had. They found each other in Eternity and began sharing what each of them had learnt. The gods Adnabyd and Medr were the first to find each other, followed by the other four.

Adnabyd and Medr figured a way to create their own realms – a concept completely new in their eyes. In their realms, they played like children. Eventually, they realised that each of them have a particular interest. They came up with the premise of each of them having a domain, a concept they can dedicate themselves to. Following are the gods, in order of appearance after Adnabyd and Medr found each other.

### Adnabyd
Adnabyd is the God of Knowledge. He dedicated himself to the collection of knowledge and realised that his realm was already a vast library of things. Before books were discovered by Eternals, Adnabyd stored physical collections of things to represent their knowledge.

### Medr
Medr is the God of Skill. Doing things the best way was her obsession, and she managed to perfect her realm before any other. When new skills were discovered, she was always the first to claim its mastery.

### Ymdig
Ymdig is the Eternal of Behaviour. They were interested in their own behaviour and the nature of their own consciousness. Ymdig pondered existential questions that they had no way of answering at the time.

### Bwydo
Bwydo initially chose the senses as his domain. He was interested in the way that the Gods could perceive the world and was the first god to figure out light. Much later, Bwydo would find their true Eternity calling, food and taste. This happened later, after the advent of the Muz.

### Riw
Riw changed her domain several times, and originally dedicated herself to replication, creating self-replicating organisms. Later, after the Mortal Realm was found, she would discover that her interest in the domain of replication was truly for the act of procreation that many of the Mortal Realms’ creatures participated in. After, later than when Bwydo discovered his true calling, Riw realised her true calling of relationships.

### Cyfapheb
Cyfapheb decided to be the Eternal of Communication. She found the exact ways the Gods communicated to be interesting. She invented new and interesting methods of communication and developed the language that most Gods speak.

## The Gods and the Mortal Realm
After they had created their own realms within Eternity, the Gods found another realm. It had no god, and there was flourishing life within. They found the Mortal Realm, which revolutionised their ideas of existence. First, they realised that the Mortal Realm predated their own existence, dating it far further back than they knew they had lived. They examined the vast and interesting landscapes. Then, they found the creatures within it, many of which exhibited traits that related to the domains of the gods.

The mortal realm was alive, with self replicating organisms beyond those which Riw had previously created. All the gods were delighted. Adnabyd had an infinite pool of knowledge to learn. Medr had a wide range of skills to master. Ymdig had a huge set of behavious to examine. Bwydo had a range of senses to examine, although he found more interest in the act of assimilating other material to continue existence. Riw found out the advanced ways the creatures recreated themselves, and changed her domain to sex and procreation. Cyfapheb found the ways that creatures communicated a good base to understand their own godly communications.

Ymdig was the first to create their own creature that survived in the Mortal Realm. The creatures he created acted on raw behaviour, one thing happened and they responded in consistent ways. Ymdig created his own series of bugs. Then, Cyfapheb saw the communication in creatures like the wild wolves, and designed her own creatures based upon communication. Then, Riw created the rabits, creatures that could replicate themselves one to a hundred if supplied with good food. Bwydo saw the creatures that ate, the pigs, and iterated on them in his own ways, still believing that his domain was the senses. Medr also created their own creature, iterating on the monkeys already inhabiting the world to make them better at grabbing things.

Adnabyd also wanted to create a creature, but his ideal iteration would be that of the mind. He was fascinated on how the brain worked, and especially how it stored knowledge. He wanted to create creatures with the capacity of extreme thought and greater knowledge than those already occupying the world. For his project, he asked the gods to band together, and together they created the person. They created the first Muz, a primate-like people based upon the likeness of chimpanzees.

At first the Muz were naked and did not know the true power that Adnabyd had given to them. Adnabyd, pleased with his achievements, wanted to create more people with the capacity for the intense thought that the Muz could muster. Bwydo couldn’t join, he was too fascinated by the complex taste buds he had created, and soon found his true domain in food. Riw too was too preoccupied by the concepts of love that she couldn’t add to the new people. So, Adnabyd, Medr, Ymdig, and Cyfapheb created a new set of people, using the knowledge and skills they had gathered by creating the first.

They created the Dos, a raptor-like people based upon the likeness of birds. Adnabyd, Cyfapheb, and Medr wanted to create their own variants of the dos, so they did. Adnabyd made a creature that focused on the accumulation of knowledge, a smart creature with a quick whit. He created the Nidos, and based their appearance on the crow, an intelligent bird. Medr created a creature which would perfect a skill, a capable creature that he based off the falcons. Cyfapheb created the Nudos, and based their appearance from the tits of the world, creatures that talked every morning in song.

The Muz and the Dos were both created around the year -10000, and they are the only species of person known to have existed at this time.

## The Birth of New Eternals
The God’s created people started exhibiting behaviours that weren’t expected from them. These behavious were evil, selfish, without a care for their tribe. Some went mad, betraying the knowledge Adnabyd had gave them. Some went lethargic, lazing all day and withering away. Some were careless, overlooking simple things and causing massive damage. Some were gluttonous, eating everything before their eyes. Some were evil, taking pleasure from the harming of others. Some betrayed Cyfapheb, displaying harsh rejections to commonly accepted human communication.

It’s not fully understood, especially by the gods, how the mere existence of these behaviours made more gods appear. But, after the first six recognised these traits, their antonyms appeared.

### Gwalloph
This is the God of Madness. He tempts us all into forgetting our knowledge. Sometimes, he wins, driving people to do things completely uncharacteristic of themselves. He targets the old more often than not, causing us to lose the knowledge that we spent our lives gaining at the very end of our lives.

### Sirphni
Sirphni is the God of Lethargy. She coaxes us all to not provide anything to society, to become useless and a drain on others. Some, she wins over, but many perform better in fear of her.

### Diofalux
Diofalux is the God of Accidents and Disasters. Famines are often attributed to it. Smaller things like tripping on nails too. Diofalux lives in the misfortune of the world..

### Glinphe
Glinphe is the God of Gluttony. She coaxes us to eat food uncontrolably. She makes some selfish and pursue all of the things.

### Maleisus
Maleisus is the God of Evil and finding joy in other’s suffering. He relishes in hurting people and pushes others to cause pain.

### Gwrphcim
Gwrphcim is the God of Antisocial habits. He’s attributed to the antisocial people in every society, the ones who use social communication for their own benefit.

## Muz diversify
The gods didn’t intend for it to be a feature, but the Muz spread rapidly across the continent they were placed on. They managed to adapt to their climate far faster than any other species could, through the slow process of evolution and mutation. It is around -8000 that we see the first examples of a diversified Muz people, Adnabyd reporting that “the people of the islands have given themselves webbed feet.”

## The Everlasting War
Within Adnabyd’s libraries sits an old book discussing a series of ongoing events that began around -6800. The book is constantly updated by one of Adnabyd’s Esten. Relatively recently, one of these books was copied by a scholar at this university, then another scholar copied it to take it north to Lale Darie. This book, “The Everlasting War”, describes in great detail the creation of the Vugaix, and several events that happen in those lands and Kyenel.

Cynnag came to be after growth blew up for the Muz population, which occurred the same time as an explosion in the diversity of mushrooms in the region now known as Difath. Cynnag was found by the twelve gods and made them thirteen. He immediately chose growth as his domain and took a special likeness to the growth of the mushrooms in the mortal realm. He managed to design his own species that would grow well on sand. It quickly diversified and conquered large parts of what was then the Desert of Kyenel, and now the northernmost parts of Vugaix.

Then another God was found in Eternity. This god immediately identified with decay, seeing the decay in the world in all its glory. Although Pydred is conventionally seen as a bad god, where would we be without decay? Covered in piles of dead bodies, hoarding mounds of rich food in wait for the time to consume them? Most importantly, Pydred adored the desert as an example of decay at its finest. Not only did she find greatness in the abrasive sands, but even at the silver structures that survived them and perplex all who offer them time on the mind.

In her adoration of the desert came a hatred of the thing that seeked to wipe it of our continent, the mushrooms and their growth. So, although decay could not exist without growth, and growth without decay, these two gods began a petty feud across Kyenel. Cynnag creating mushrooms to dirtify the land and Pydred sweeping sand to destroy the mushrooms.

Also within this document are outlines on the creation of certain creatures to aid either side in their battles. Pydred creating giant tortoise to crush the tallest of mushrooms. Cynnag creating creatures that aid the mushroom’s spores in their travels. These are not the only creatures outlined by the section titled “Weapons of Nature”, as there are tens of creatures created by these gods to aid in their battles.

## Sea’s Storm
Around -6400, a huge storm raged off the eastern coast of Barogate, horizontal to the region of Kusao. Riw was the first to notice this, having just been in the region at the time, and she quickly beckoned other gods to watch this impressive event. The storm continued for weeks, slowly approaching the coast.

Then a new God appeared and was found in Eternity. Medr and Adnabyd gave them the essentials of Godhood, and when it became time for this new god to choose a domain, they chose the ocean. Their name was Caesi, god of the Sea, and as the almighty storm calmed in their presence, they got to work designing some of the most interesting sea creatures that proliferate the Mortal Realm to this day.

## Strength and Persistence
A tale perpetrates the Muz world, one of strength and persistence. This tale is the story of the first Mortal Ascension into godhood, a surprise to all especially the gods. The events of this story occurred around -5500. The version of the story held at my university goes as follows:

Cryver was a mortal human, and his house had been set alight. There, amidst the red painful heat lay his son trapped underneath a burning beam of wood. Try as he might with all the strength he could muster, he could not lift the beam. He watched helplessly as his son, crushed, burnt to death under an unliftable beam.

After that day, he dedicated his life to gaining the necessary strength to lift an equivalent beam to the one that had killed his son. He set aside all his life, work and relationships, and trained. Day after day he would build his strength, whether through direct training or the pursuit of chemical or hidol means. At the end of each day he trained, he would try to lift the very same beam. After decades of training, his body began failing him and his bones grew old. He realised that he was not as strong as he used to be despite training harder than ever. But still, in the face of absolute defeat, Cryver didn’t give up.

The gods found Cryver, impressed by his dedication.They saw an element of themselves within Cryver. His dedication to a single element of his existence was analogous to the way the gods chose their domain. As Cryver grew older, Medr manifested into the Mortal Realm to collaborate with Cryver in the skill of gaining strength. Within years, Cryver had finally reached the peak of mortal strength. When he went to lift the beam, it did budge, but it was not lifted.

Medr saw a potential spike in Cryver. Potential that no God had ever seen before. With one small push from Medr, Cryver could ascend and become himself a God. So, Medr asked Cryver if this would be something he wanted. Cryver accepted the proposal and swore to help mortals in their hardships for the remainder of Eternity. Medr had never ascended a mortal before, so with a little trial and error, Cryver ascended to Eternity. He became the God of Strength, not just in body but in mind.

## The Great Relics
Around -4600, when the Gan first found iron and the technology spread to the other side of Barogate, there was a Domuz smith living in the Lale called Tanea. They were a regular smith, creating a few extraordinary tools or weapons in their time. One dat while smithing, a passer-by noticed an odd behavour from Tanea. He had become erratic, and commonly spouted unintelligible nonsense. The only legible words people could make out were “they must be complete” and “I need more time”. Tanea did not respond to outside people, which scared the locals. The matter was escalated to the highest form of government, which decided to leave Tanea be. He was still producing his contractually obliged weaponry, accepting payments and giving payments for raw materials. People were saying that Tanea was struck by Gwalloph, gone mad pursuing the completion of an impossible project. Tanea was in a mad trance, tirelessly devoting himself to the forge when, one day, he dropped dead. People nearby at the time recall him shouting one last time before Eternity called for him. He bellowed “I must complete them!”. His life vanished from his mortal body, and the 75 items he had forged in his madness disappeared.

One time while in the mortal realm, Medr heard of Tanea and his dedication to the forge. Intrigued, she found her way over to Lale where she met Tanea, mad and at the forge. She saw the same growing potential she had recognised in Cryver before. Then, she left him to it, periodically checking in on him. Eventually, after he had completed 75 strange items, Medr manifested to talk to him. At first, communication was a struggle, but Tanea was not opposed to the idea to become a God. He was repeating that he could finally complete “them”, his 75 forged artefacts.

So, when Tanea’s time came, Medr helped him ascend to godhood, where his personality completely changed. After previously being a madman, with Tanea’s new expanded consciousness that mad part of him was a mere fraction. Using his new godly power, he enchanted his 75 relics and spread them across the mortal realm.

## The Temple of Healing
Adferiad was a person born into war. Her birth occurred around -2160, the culmination of 150 years of the War of the River, the last war between the Nidos and the Nudos. She lived her early life as a camp follower, seeing death and destruction with her own innocent eyes. But as she grew up, she chose a path of healing rather than joining the soldiery. When she came of age, she entered the political realm with one goal in mind, to stop the senseless struggle of war that corrupted her childhood.

In her career, she experienced the Nidos way of thinking. There was this assumption that the Nudos were a populous and uncivilised bunch, all accustomed to unlawful activities. So, the Nidos were fighting to protect their way of life and conquering to better the lives of most. From her firsthand experience as a camp follower, she healed many Nudos that had fallen during battle. She knew that they were a Dos like any other. She had conversations with them, and knew that they thought almost the same of the Nidos. They believed that the Nidos were a bunch of oppressive bureaucrats, and that life was a misery underneath the rule of the few. The Nidos were all mindless drones to them, so they were fighting for their liberation.

Adferiad had gained a multicultural following of people each dedicated to heling the hurt. She and her group placed armies that had fought each other together, forcing them to communicate. One by one, the peoples on both sides realised that the war was ultimately meaningless, and that their ways of life were almost identical. After a slow couple of decades, the fighting slowly came to a stop, and Adferiad’s heralds’ jobs were completed.

Adferiad managed to cultivate the extraordinary ability to bring back people from the dead, in mostly healthy conditions.

Adferiad dedicated her followers to the creation of a new organisation. A place of free healing, where all would be treated equally. The first building was constructed in the completely destroyed foundations of Cosaden, a town situated on the river with an indestructible crossing.

## Abandonment and Courage
Following is the tale of Ofidea, the snake god of courage. Her tale is a long one, dutifully copied by one of our scribes from Adnabyd’s libraries.

This tale is about Ofidea, a Difmuz of Sandcamper culture, who began life living in the great deserts of Kyenel. From a young age, she was accidentally left behind by her family, and wandered off in a random direction to find them. Unfortunately, she had gotten herself lost by falling down into a sandy ditch, so that when her parents came back they could not find her.

Down in the ditch all alone, Ofidea had nothing to do but wait, perhaps for enough sand to blow in so that she could climb out. Within the same day, a snake found Ofidea, this young girl stuck in a hole. It creapt up on her, ready to attack a free meal it found in the empty desert. That is when, Ofidea, in all her courage and wisdom, grabbed the snake from behind her by the neck. She looked at this snake, treating its struggling body with great care, and decided to name it Davin.

Ofidea’s first action was to use Davin as a piece of rope, using him to climb out of the hole she was in. Soon after wandering aimlessly with Davin in her hands, she got hungry. It was at this point Davin realised that Ofidea meant no harm to him, as he knew that if she wanted a quick meal she could have easily eaten him. Ofidea, assuming Davin was her friend, let him on the sands for him to find food for her. Instead of running away, Davin stuck around Ofidea without biting her, thinking that she’d be an indispensable companion.

Ofidea and Davin wandered Kyenel aimlessly for a day until Davin saw a vulture fly off in a direction. Usually, he would be too slow to reach the corpse in time with his own wriggling body, but with Ofidea’s legs he could crertainly make it. He pointed in the direction using his tail, and she took the hind, running after the vulture. Off in the horizon Davin saw the giant carcass of one of Kyenel’s many giant beasts. Using its carcass, they fed for several days.

Around the carcass gathered several snakes, feasting on large chunks of meat they could find. Davin approached these snakes and told them about Ofidea’s aid to him and how helpful she was. All the other snakes couldn’t quite understand what Davin was saying, given how they were illiterate, but they soon caught wind and started following Ofidea. She wandered north, advised by Davin, carrying him on her neck while the other snakes were hanging off parts of her body.

# Essays on the Gods

## Adnabyd
Adnabyd is one of the gods that people hear a lot about, especially in a university. In our own bid for knowledge we ask the one who has it all. As part of this paper I’ve done extensive research on much of the Gods, and my view of Adnabyd has changed significantly

### Adnabyd’s Realm
Adnabyd’s realm is described as an infinite library
