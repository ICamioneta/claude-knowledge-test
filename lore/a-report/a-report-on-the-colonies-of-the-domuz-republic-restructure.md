This is an attempted restructure of [A Report on the Colonies of the Domuz Republic](/wastelife/lore/a-report/a-report-on-the-colonies-of-the-domuz-republic). It's very incomplete because I lost interest in the restructuring shortly after beginning. Basically, it might be better to have the report presented per colony rather than in chapters, that way there is a clear narrative through-line on each of the colonies. 

# Prefaces
Before anything we must address the name of the nation, as internally it is referred to the Republic Union of Gazi, Lale, and Darie. Locals often refer to the country as Gazi Lale Darie while foreigners often drop the Gazi. Locals often use ‘the republic’ in reference to the government. This document will use "the republic" in reference to the homeland nation.

The native inhabitants of the Dithas are diverse themselves. The most common populations are the Dimad and the Buemad. The other subspecies of Mad are also present in Dithas. In addition to this, populations of Difmuz are quite common in Dithas, and are also called natives. Often, military interaction between the Mad and the Domuz also include the Difmuz.

# Introduction
This account delves into the colonial dominions of the Republic Union of Gazi, Lale, and Darie. Information about this nation seldom reaches Eresonian shores through the word-of-mouth of sea traders and the little information that crosses the many internal divisions of Kyeron. As naval technology is rapidly developing and our political spheres develop the educated public yearn for insights into the world at large. While the geographical mapping efforts of Jeisei Oera and the Survey Comittee of the National Council provides insights on the literal layout of the world, the actual structures of it remain mysterious.

I and my alumni, and a group of unaffiliated adventurers from the Adventurer’s Guild in Caroset (credited at the end) took it upon ourselves to expand the pool of public knowledge on the government of Lale Darie. We have spent much of our time confirming beliefs about the government and its structure that had reached Ereso through heresay. One element of the government we had not expected was the extent of its colonial reach. 

## Context and Ambitions
During 3189, Shildo was dominated by the Second Eresonian Empire and the Ralean Empire of the Reimig Dynasty. These two giants fought politically and territorially over Shildo, with the Terlayan kingdoms weaving their way in and out of each political sphere. In the far-off lands of Lale Darie, the ideas of the relationship between the capitalist and the king were shifting rapidly. Lale Darie underwent a ferocious political revolution in 3189 that saw the formation of a federated republic. Later, the country had another major shift in government structure, becoming a centralised republic in 3324 with a governmental structure similar to those proposed by the Polbegeo group during the Eresonian revolution of 3277, and the Ralean Republicans.

The Domuz Republic was a pioneer of the private corporation economical structure. This economic structure was measurably beneficial for many of its citizens, however its workers worked in workplaces with high mortality and exploitation of labour. In more modern years, life for the average Domuz worker sees as much comfort as modern day Eresonian life. This common experience of luxury at home is built on the back of exploitative colonial practices.

Exploitation is the context and the ambition of the republic’s expansion into other territories. Despite this common goal, each colony serves a unique socio-economic purpose to the republic. It is this socio-economic character that I will highlight throughout this document.

## Scope of the Report
This report encompasses a wide array of facets surrounding the colonial ventures of the republic. From the economic underpinnings that sustain these distant outposts to how the governance is woven across the regions. Moreover, our specific job at this bureau is to scrutinise the complex interplay of cultures and societies that emerged between the Domuz colonists and the indigenous lands. We will also confront the challenges and conflicts facing each of the colonial enterprises.

### Economic Activities
Colonial trade has become an important part of the Republic’s economy. Many tradespeople rely on colonial exports and imports to maintain their trade, and thanks to the special materials and minerals many trades are available in the Republic that there is simply no economic precedent for in Shildo. Although much of production from the colony’s resources is handled in Lale Daire, many of the trades handling colonial goods is handled in the colony that produces them.

Many of the colonies are not self-sufficient, especially those in regions with bad soil. Food in the Dithas colonies is mostly harvested to be exported, and much of the worker’s food is common grains grown in the agricultural powerhouses of Lale Darie. Food in the Llyhai colonies almost relies on imports with the harsh infertility of the grounds. Domestic Llyhai food production is almost exclusively fishing and hunting.

# Governance and Administration
The Government of the Domuz Republic is centred around a democratic central government similar to the modern government of Vania after the recent 3456 constitution. The Eresonain criticisms of the 3456 constitution are largely applicable to the Domuz Republic. One of the primary differences is the election of a head of government. While Vanian parties need to agree to put a head-of-state in charge (an event that is unlikely to occur until the complete collapse of Labour), the Domuz Republic's President is elected on a system best described as wealth voting, where individuals with more money are afforded more votes. This has lead to a president with massive favour to the rich, and the majority of Lale Darie's most wealthy people have huge steaks in the colonies.

As for colonial administration, there are four types of colonial government officially recognised by the government. These are Imperial Colonies, Self-governing Colonies, Frontier Colonies, and Autonomous Territories. These four government types generally encapsulate the Domuz's colonies in their entirety.

## Imperial Colonies
The two imperial colonies are Yphofthle and Mepheisatha. Imperial Colonies have locally elected councils where members are chosen from a colony-wide pool. Votes for these elections are only available to the colonial elite, determined based on colonial tax contribution. AA colonial governor is appointed by the president of the republic. Due to the wealth voting system, both the president and colonial governors heavily favour industrialist policies. 

The Imperial Colonies are integrated into the republic. Citizens of the colonies not only vote for local councils and the colony's own president, but also for the councillors who will join the republic's house of representatives. The imperial colonies are also under the jurisdiction of the Admiral of the Home Fleet.

Imperial colonies act a lot like devolved regions, managing local taxation, resource allocation for infrastructure, education and schooling, and policing and public order within the limits defined by national law. 

The President of imperial colonies is responsible for oversight of the local councils and can veto or override almost any decision with the caveat of two-thirds council approval.

Following are the interactions between specific imperial colonies with their native populations, as well as how many seats they have on their council.
### Yphofthle
Yphofthle has 17 seats in the house of representatives. 

The local Yphofthle government manages relations with 20 native settlements near its border. These native settlements were guaranteed independence and that the colony would not expand with a recent declaration made in 3454. Many Mad tribes live within the border. While their presence and lifestyle is often tolerated by the local government, these tribes have no guarantees and are sometimes forced to move to make way for plantations.
### Mepheisatha
Mepheisatha has 11 seats in the house of reprasentatives. 

Mepheisatha has a continually straining relationship between the government and indigenous population. The colony maintains a veneer of cooperation between indigenous tribes and the local government by allowing them private ownership of their own territory within the colony. Tribes who accept this deal often find themselves with private plots consisting uniquely of their settled territory, and the land that they forage and survive from is industrialised and turned into farmland, mines, and privately managed forage-land. 

Tribes that reject this offer are frequently misplaced, as the land they settle is sold to other private investors. Tribes on the colonial border are generally resistive to movement, but the small military units known as posses are highly effective in driving them out.

## Self-Governing Colonies
The self-governing colonies are colonies with economic and sovereign ties to the Republic. The two self-governing colonies are Cyrox and Asthenel. Although they often have their own government which manages matters of colonial development, the majority of local government is handled privately; corporations manage their own private armies that enforce a corporate law on their private land. 

The self-governing colonies have no council, instead the colony's own policies are managed entirely by the president-appointed governor. The governor is allowed to make huge legislative departures from the laws of the Republic.

The policies enacted by the colony itself are only enforceable in land directly owned by the colonial administration. Privately owned land is allowed to depart from colonial policies, corporate law is allowed to rule supremely. The colonial governance sometimes acts as a mediator between the complaints of people living under corporate law, but complaints are often ignored by the governor who is themselves a heavy industrialist.

The colonial administration is technically allowed to petition the central government for support in enforcing disputes between itself and private corporations, but this has only happened on occasion.

Historically, the self-governing colonies have been extremely hostile to native populations, showing no respect for the indigenous and violently throwing them out. 

### Cyrox
Cyrox's colonial government owns a very small portion of the land. The majority of the land belongs to private plantation owners who run company towns. These company towns are exclusively supplied by the private entity; prices are often gouged and workers paid so little that workers practically spend the entirety of their wage on rent and food, both of which are services provided by the company.

This relationship would be slavery were it not for the legal stipulation that the inhabitants of company towns are legally allowed to leave. The caveat to this legal right is that the company towns are usually completely isolated, and travel between them is dangerous. Without any spare money, workers seeking to escape their plantation have to walk for hours or days through the treacherous Dithas to reach the nearest colonially governed town. Even once they reach a colonial town, job opportunities are slim and food is still expensive. 

The private corporations of Cyrox have enforced the growth of their company and their private land by forcefully evicting native inhabitants. These inhabitants are always given the option to join the plantations as a workforce, sometimes with premium wages, but natives recognise this as a trap. Almost all natives retreat further into Dithas, further up the Cyrox river.

### Asthenel
Asthenel is in similar conditions to Cyrox, but many of the farms located here are owned by the colonial administration as they are used specifically to feed the Llyhai colonies. These places are often accepting to new hires who are escapees of the private farms, so mobility across Asthenel is more forgiving than in Cyrox.

As for relationships with the natives, Asthenel has a similar habit to Cyrox of using private armies to forcefully evict natives. However, in the southern part of the country, a union of Mad tribes and Okung Kinships (of Duela) has formed in attempts to slow down Asthenel's expansion, sometimes going on the offensive to reclaim some of their lost land.

## Frontier Colonies
The Frontier Colonies are the colonies of Llyhai: Isles of Mourn, Bhethfei, Fauson, Fanthel, and Phemfbharei. Given the hostile nature of the region, frontier colonies are military governed by commodores of the Colonial Fleet. They have near complete governmental control over the colony, but the economy is almost entirely managed by private corporations.

Commodores are high-ranking members of the Republic's military. They command squadrons of ships within the larger fleet, where fleets are controlled by the Admirals. The Admiral of the Colonial Fleet is responsible for colonial oversight and military cooperation in the frontier colonies, and has appointed their commodores as governors of Llyhai's five colonies. 

The frontier colonies are given this near perfectly autonomous stature because of the hostile nature of Llyhai. It is believed that an extremely high military presence is crucial for the survival of these colonies, and generally this is entirely true. frontier colonies are constantly under attack by the Armies of Ichmenre, local Ashlander tribes, and the hostile fauna of Llyhai. 

### Isles of Mourn
As part of a massive military-colonial project that began in 3290 to establish a beachfront on the Island of Mourn. Since, the colonial government has been highly focussed on safeguarding the island settlement, slowly eradicating the local fauna from the immediate area and then the island as a whole. Still, the island is dangerous, as the creatures of Llyhai are well known for being subterranean and aerial. 

The commodore has since managed to arrange a land invasion, taking full control of prospected high-quality copper and zinc resources. These mines are protected fiercely by the commodore and the admiral against frequent attacks by the armies of Ichmenre. 

### Bhethfei
Bhethfei's government isn't at all unique, and as a military-colonial project it was generally standard, with the military leading a huge landing party at the colony's foundation in 3310. Since, defensive efforts have focussed around the far-out mining operations of Hidol-sensitive materials, which the commodore and the admiral have used to fuel their military administration and defensive efforts. 

### Fauson
The fourth colony established on Llyhai as a military-colonial project, as this section breaks from the normal chronological structure to discuss the Llyhai colonies from west to east. Fauson is currently being governed by the Admiral of the Colonial Fleet, or their right-hand commodore when they are required elsewhere.

The military contribution of Fauson extends beyond the navy and includes private militaries. The resources extracted by Fauson are extremely wealthy, especially silver which is used directly as a raw resource as a currency in the Republic. This additional military contribution is incentivised by the increased hostility Fauson receives from Llyhai's dangers. 

### The Lost City of Fanthel
The Lost City of Fanthel has a large defensive wall build around the settlement for the express purpose of defending against fauna. Due to the city being positioned on an island, there is no threat from the armies of Ichmenre, so defensive efforts here are a minimum. The commodore governing this territory often leads expeditions into the silver ruin with select captains, high-ranking marine officers, and random adventurers. 
### Phemfbharei
Phemfbharei is the Republic's most recent military-colonial project. The military is currently launching naval campaigns to free the bay from dangerous creatures. The Admiral has assigned his Marine General (in command of the army directly under the admiral) to command land expansion of the colony.
## Autonomous Territories
The autonomous territories are entirely self-governing and completely disconnected from the Central Government in both governance and economy. They are, however, under the sovereign control of the Republic and frequently receive military and industrial aid from the mainland. The two autonomous territories of the Domuz Republic are Baron-Gazi and Oedreci.

### Baron-Gazi
Baron-Gazi's governance mirrors that of the main land. It has a single central governance with a president and a national assembly, except Baron-Gazi's national assembly only consists of a house of representatives without a senate. The President is also elected using a proportional system, different to the wealth system of the Republic. This legislative branch acts with full independence from the Republic, but the President of the Domuz Republic is still allowed to veto and propose laws for this territory.

As for interaction with native populaces, the Domuz lead a huge military campaign against the native Caran government, Kingdom Naxdyme, to solidify Baron-Gazi's foundation on the island of Acadym, part of the Llynllanes island archipelago. Since, many Caran have been forced to integrate into the Baron-Gazi culture (colloquially called Baron-Laleite), or they have relocated themselves to Kingdom Naxdyme territory.

There is an ongoing conflict between Baron-Gazi and Kingdom Naxdyme over ownership of the island of Texo, which is called "Blava" by the Baron-Gazi government. It is a small island situated in between the part of Llynllanes controlled by Baron-Gazi and the part controlled by Kingdom Naxdyme. Currently, the conflict is at rest, but this will likely change very soon. 

Baron-Gazi's own military is almost entirely defensive, with a strong focus on local milita, whereas Kingdom Naxdyme has a fully formed offensive navy that it has historically used to invade and influence other Raidal nations. The Domuz Republic's Admiral of the Trade Fleet regularly operates protecting trade on Barogate's west coast, and protection of Baron-Gazi is their responsibility. 

### Oedreci
Oedreci is more officially a city-state than a fully fledged autonomous territory. It's inherently attached and tied to its neighbour nation, the Wafthulham Principality. Its governance is almost entirely in the hands of its mayor, which uses governmental structures similar to those seen locally in Oranax. It is still sovereignly tied to Baron-Gazi.

Its formation and relationship with the native government began actively hostile, but throughout the landing war Domuz diplomats were able to convince the local prince (in charge of the Wafthulham Principality) to cede the town in exchange for positive relations and increased trade with Lale Darie. The Domuz's own internal reports have shown that the relationship has been beneficial for the Wafthulham, with Lale Darie having significantly more to give than it has to get from the economic relationships introduced by Oedreci. Although the trade relationship is comparatively more beneficial for the Wafthulham, it is still partially beneficial for the Domuz.

Relationships with the indigenous population have been incredibly stable. Local tradesmen have prospered with the newfound link between Oranax and Barogatian economies. Workers too have felt the weight of these benefits, as the mayor *seems like a nice guy*. I ultimately apologise for not being able to visit the colony myself, and having to rely on often biased internal reports, but I have no reason to doubt their accuracy.


# Yphofthle
The history of the Republic’s first colony actually predates its own existence by several centuries. The colony of Yphofthle has evidence of Domuz presence as early as the 1500s – as far back as Nao Techia. The first domuz to step foot in the land was unlikely part of an organised colonisation attempt and was likely a couple people looking for somewhere to live using tools they brought from their homeland in Lale Darie. The first evidence of the land being controlled by a government back in Lale Daire is a tablet charter dated to 2604 referring to the colony as a territory of a monarch who at the time held dominion over much of Gazi.

Since then, control of the colony shifted de jure between several monarchs from Gazi Lale Darie until the nation’s unification. However, de facto control was often handled domestically as frequently, monarchs weren’t even aware of the colony’s existence. Even under the unified Empire of Gazi Lale Darie, the region was left alone until 2731, when the monarch was informed of the colony’s existence and decided to send troops to it to ‘make the final move in unifying the Domuz people’

The colony from then would experience its trials and tribulations being part of the Empire of Gazi Lale Darie’s internal and external struggles until the Domuz Revolution in 3189, where it served as an escape for much of the old nobility to hide in. The revolutionary intent spread to the colony, and many nobles would land in Yphofthle only to be immediately assaulted and stabbed by the locals.

Since the Republic, Yphofthle has seen a massive population increase mostly due to its economic role in the Republic, which will be discussed in a later section. What will be said here is that the colony has territorially expanded onto the mainland due to the discovery of a few sparce resources.

## Economic Overview
Yphofthle’s economy comes in two parts: the islands and the Dithas mainland. On the islands, the economy is largely tourist-based as the islands serve a great vacation for the well-off of the Republic. Most of the people living on the islands are actually colonial investors with stakes in companies, or rich guild owners taking month-long breaks from their management jobs.

Local food production primarily consists of mushrooms and a few local fruits. Grains are a large import as home species struggle to grow in the soil. Delicate mushrooms are sometimes farmed, but these are difficult to grow and more often foraged.

There are rumors that during the Domuz’s first inhabitation of the island there were Mad tribes living on them, but any mad presence has been completely eradicated long before the colonial period of the Republic. There is a nearby settlement of Mad on the mainland who claim to descend from the Mad inhabitants of the islands. All foraging is kept strictly outside of these borders, although it is common for Domuz foragers to meet Dimad foragers on the job.

There are some efforts in the colony to grow the best of the mushroom delicacies on farms. Most of these efforts have limited success, and wild foraging remains a more economically viable way to obtain the delicacies. It has been confirmed that advanced enchantment-enhanced growth is crucial for farming these plants.

### Erigandra
In 3268, rare insulative fungal plants called Erigandra were discovered, so a colonial outpost was established on mainland Dithas and began exporting Erigandra to Lale Darie. Slowly, parts of the insulative process moved into the colony. First, the trunks were cut into smaller, manageable, and shippable trunks in the colony around 3271. Then, the grinding and milling process went to the colony which made the trunks ship as a fine dust. This section of the process required the trunks to be dry, so a small section of skilled worker was moved to the colony in order to construct the necessary facilities to do so. Soon after that, the entire refinement process moved to the colony in 3272.

## Social and Cultural Aspects
Having been the earliest established Domuz presence on Dithas, there is a population of Domuz considered native to the region. These people have cultural lineages tracing back to the 1500s, as part of a culture called Yphofth. 

In recent years, with the integration of Yphofthle into the Domuz economy, Yphofthle has received a massive migratory population from a migration wave beginning around 3328. This colonial wave has generally drowned out both native Yphofth culture and many native Mad cultures with a highly culturally mixed Domuz population. In terms of species, integrated Mad folk are part of a small minority in the population, with none inhabiting the islands that are part of the colony.

More waves of migration occurred in 3268, with the popularisation of Erigandra plantations. Further migration waves occurred in 3272 as workers moved to the colony for work in Erigandra refinement factories. Since, migration has been on the low, but since around 3380 the colony's islands have been viewed as a prime holiday home destination. Many wealthy business owners own holiday homes in the area. These people have main homes in Gazi Lale Darie, and often only live on the islands for a week or two in a year.

There is talk in the colony about full integration with the Republic as a devolved state. This movement generally comes from the small buisness owner and worker with the hopes of having a more representative government. Native Yphofth folk are making efforts to have their culture recognised as a minority Domuz culture with the hopes of any future devolved region having a Yphofth-representing senator in the National Assembly's senate.

The modern mixed cultural aspects of Yphofthle are beginning to coalesce into a defined and unique culture with aspects of certain Gazi Lale Darie cultures mixed in with native Yphofth and Mad culture. Many people proclaim this as the "real" Yphofth culture, often forgetting the definitively different native culture.

## Challenges and Conflicts
As an old colony, there is little record of conflict in its origin. The general belief is that early Domuz settled rather peacefully on the islands and in their portions of the mainland. As mentioned in another chapter, there is some evidence that there used to be Mad communities living on the islands. Given modern Domuz histories, it seems a likely assumption that these peoples were violently expelled.

### Medieval history
In terms of military conflict, Yphofthle has a long and patchy history of internal struggles and its involvement as a Gazi territory during Lale Darie's medieval period. None of these conflicts can be tied to modern colonialism and are much more traditionally aligned with feudal histories, therefore they are out of the scope of this report. 

The first conflict that could be considered under the banner of colonialism would be the 2731 Empire of Gazi Lale Darie's conquest of the region. In the conflict, the emperor's navy showed up near the colony's castle capital. The navy was equipped with naval trebuchets which fired for only a few days before the Yphofth monarch surrendered to the emperor. 

The histories of the Empire of Gazi Lale Darie are long and strenuous topics, and Yphothle was too insignificant a territory to seriously impact that history in any way. While there are several mentions of Yphothle during this time, they are insignificant to the purpose of this document. 

During the Domuz Revolution beginning in 3189, much of Gazi Lale Darie's royalty and nobility fled to Yphothle, seeing them as untouched and remote islands in which they could hide from the violent uprising. In 3197, the Revolution was nearing its bloody end and it was clear that the nobility had lost. In this year, a boat full of Laleite revolutionaries toured the islands of Yphofthle killing any known nobles they could find. They also toured mainland Yphofthle, which at the time was small and wild. It is likely that many nobles survived, but in fear for their lives they had integrated with local peasantries and workers. The local Yphofth people were, largely, collaborative with the revolutionaries.
