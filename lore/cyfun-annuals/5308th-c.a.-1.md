Topic of Discussion: Progression of Mortal Worship
Proposed by: Adnabyd

Opening statement.

**Adnabyd:**
It has been 303 years since the decision of the 5005th C.A. 41 was agreed upon and 300 years since the first "the True Nature of the Gods" pamphlets were distributed among scholars and leaders in the Mortal World. Over the 300 years since, the knowledge of this pamphlet has spread far and wide, and with little intervention has been adopted into common knowledge. 

This has had the desired effect: the popular churches established in our name have largely disbanded. The vast majority remaining act with the understanding that they are following their own goals inspired by our domains, and that we generally do not sponsor these institutions insofar as they contribute to the expansion of our domains. Few religions persist that continue to believe in the "divine", however these religions are remote or fringe. 

A not entirely unforeseen consequence of this change in thought is the change in the relationships between Eternals and Mortals. We are now seen in there eyes as a sort of force of nature, powerful beings that will do what we do regardless of mortal opinion or desires. The resurfacing of "morally objectionable" actions performed by "gods" formerly seen as benevolent have certainly contributed towards this shift. 

Among this change in the mortal outlook towards Eternals has been growing sentiments that we must be kept in check in some ways. There is a tendency in some mortals to attempt to "tame" the force-of-nature that is us performing the activities that enrich us and further our domain. 

A nearly global example of previously "benevolent gods" clearly demonstrating their apathy towards Mortal worship and care has been the co-operation between Medr's mortal manifestation and the mortal Leider. Although I'm certain all Eternals are aware, as too are most mortals, Medr was supporting a large "bandit" group in the Mortal Realm between the years of 17376 and 17435. The motivation for Medr was developing the skills involved in banditry, involving the martial arts, organisation management, and an exercise in terror. 

Previously, Medr would have well hidden her mortal identity while supporting a mortal. However, through the distribution of the pamphlet and the widespread understanding of our true nature, her identity was quickly revealed to the rest of the bandit group and then the areas that were raided by them. 

Prior to the 5005th C.A., Medr was often seen as a benevolent god who sends agents to the Mortal Realm to help those gain skill. She was often the subject of prayer or rituals where mortals would hope to gain some supernatural favour towards a skill. Mortal people, upon learning that Medr was an accomplice to the leader of the bandit group that raided, slaughtered, and burnt their village, tended to see her in a more negative light if they believed it at all. 

Now, Leider is present as the Eternal of Banditry, ascended by Medr as she does with any mortal who has the potential. The ascension of such a hated figure, to no Eternal objection, has certainly lowered the opinions of us among Mortals further. 

It is important to note here that Medr's actions have only been one of many contributing factors towards the degradation of Mortal-Eternal relations. In their movement away from centralised religious organisations, each individual mortal has accrued their own opinions of each Eternal they care for. While many mortals have open distain for us and our actions, many others view us in awe and as a force of nature, as previously mentioned.

With all of this information in mind, I find it important now more than ever to establish a more formal relationship between us Eternals and the Mortals of their realm. Perhaps for too long have we treated the mortal realm as a sandbox in which we explore the causal effects of our domains, in which we further our domains with the help of mortals who know no better. 

The relations will heal with time. Mortal figures previously universally hated have faded into legend. Although we will persist, our "morally objectionable" actions too will eventually fade into legend as we change alongside our domains. I still see it relevant to attempt now anything within our ability to repair relationships with mortals. 

Individually, we must seek our own relationship with the Mortals. Some may seek to begin cooperation, such as myself. Others, such as Maleisus, might revel in mortal hatred as they have done for millennia. The purpose of this discussion is to describe and perform actions that may help repair mortal relations with Eternals as a whole. Individual actions will contribute to the overall opinion, so it is encouraged they are shared so that we may all take inspiration for our own actions. 

I will begin with my own proposal, then we shall visit each Eternal in order. 

As the Eternal of Knowledge, or the God of Knowledge as the mortals see it, I will assess the actions my Esten and I commit on the mortal plain and involving mortals and ensure they are not morally objectionable when held up to modern mortal moral standards. I will continue to work directly with institutions of knowledge to further my domain. 

**Medr**
I hold no regrets to my work with the mortal Leider, however I will avoid working with controversial figures in the future. I will ensure that my Esten and I are viewed as collaborators in the mastery of skills. As a sparring partner rather than a mentor. I won't be able to stop myself and my Esten from working on skills that are morally objectionable, and I hope that mortals will accept that my domain transcends morals, and hope that in focussing on less controversial figures I will be able to repair the broken relationship. 

I also hope to join some grand tournaments under my own name rather than a mortal alias. In doing this, I hope to encourage the mortals in the skill of the tournament in seeing what the current limit of it is. Not only will this encourage mortals to further my domain through their inspiration, but it should also cultivate the idea of myself as a legendary figure. 

**Ymdig**
I have historically performed morally questionable experiments on mortals in attempts to explore the limits of their behaviour. I have since moved away from this, but I will formally declare that I am now focussing on observation rather than intervention. If, in observing the limits of mortal behaviour, I am observing a situation in which harm or torture is done, I will do nothing to intervene unless I see fit. 

**Bwydo**
My domain has never been subject to mortal challenge! Although many mortals blame me for starvation, they must understand that it is not in my power to intervene in this way. Following the 5005th C.A., master chefs have been more accepting of my cooperation and I no longer need to explain my motivations as they are commonly understood. 

**Riw**
Mortals have abandoned ritualised marriages and partnerships done in my name, and have often condemned my more deviant explorations of my domain of love and sex. Without mortal worship, it has been easier to engage with mortals socially. It is also easier to explain myself when I'm discovered. 

I will continue to be an observer and a subtle influencer. Mortals generally revere love and sex, so I am not a detested god.

**Cyfapheb**
I have done extensive work to encourage the development and documentation of language. My domain has never been hated, so I will continue as normal. Many empires seek to limit the methods of communication used by its subjects. Previously, I have been rather explicit in my interventions of these decisions, however I now see these interventions as unfit. Although I will continue to encourage the development of language everywhere, I will not oppose the law of empires in my preservation of it. 

**Gwalloph**
My actions continue, mortal objection or not. I am a purveyor of eccentricity, there are many who enjoy it and many annoyed by it. 

**Sirphni**
Yeah, I'll continue doing basically nothing. This whole thing has let me chill with mortals without being called "temptress", which is quite nice. 

**Diofalux**
Without mortals decrying me as the fault of all their accidents and bad outcomes, I've been given so much more room to study the causality of events! Sure, I do still sometimes set things up, but these are often indistinguishable from normal causal chains and is that really so bad? 

**Glinphe**
There was a time where mortals hated me for giving them greed. Now they know it is themselves that greed, I am almost given more power and freedom to purposefully tempt people, and to observe the limits of mortal greed. 

**Maleisus**
I have no contributions to this. I will continue to inspire hatred and suffering. I will continue to cause it. All that has changed is that the mortals no longer see me as an all powerful adversary but a force of malevolent nature. 

**Gwrphcim**
I, as Maleisus has already described, have only to benefit from the mortals ditching me as a mythical adversary. Many have even understood that breaking societal norms isn't inherently harmful. 

**Hell**
Mortals have indeed stopped worshipping the sun, but I know this blatant worship has only been replaced with glorious reverence! Farmers have nothing else to thank for their crops growing. Boaters have no others to thank for the ice melting. If anything, mortal understanding has moved reverence away from myself and towards the subject of my domain.

**Enw**
Mortals have slowly started to come to terms with their own identity. It was before influenced by the religions they followed. After these were dismantled, I have made efforts to help mortals explore identity, and with this I have raised mortal opinions of myself. 

**Dim**
I hate this. They actually understand the void now, and they look into it way more often. Do you know how hard it is to be nothing when mortals are constantly staring at it in mental crysis! I just hope I can stay quiet, or maybe figure something out, and this will all blow over. 

**Lleh**
There have been a few advancements in astronomy. To help repair Eternal-Mortal relations, I will encourage these discoveries and directly demonstrate to mortals the cosmology of their world. I will also do my best to accommodate mortals when I move them to the moon for fun. 

**Dyddiad**
I am a keeper of dates and records, and mortal collaboration is in my interest.
