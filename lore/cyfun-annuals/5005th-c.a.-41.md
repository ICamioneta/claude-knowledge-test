Topic of Discussion: Mortal Worship.
Proposed by: Resum

Opening statement.

**Resum:**
It came to my attention that the peoples of the Mortal Realm worshipped Eternals shortly after my introduction to the Mortal Realm in A16505. I have studied the rationale between this worship and it has been understood since A7826 that the rationale is one of misunderstanding and imperfect information. In A17012 the first mortal temple was established in my name, and my Esten report that they revere concepts of formal logic and champion it as some "divine truth". On A17012, I was informed by Adnabyd that this is "Just a thing the Mortals like to do". I have long since thought about this worship. In A17210 I approached the temple in person and informed the priests of my actual nature, and how this unwarranted worship was unnecessary, they believed I was not the real Resum and that I was a "false prophet". Upon providing evidence of my Eternal capability and a demonstration of my domain, the priest still did not believe me. They did not know me. 

Worship of my perceived character brought this priest to be illogical. For myself, the removal of mortal churches and the presentation of the truth would further my domain. It is my belief that this logic extends to most other domains. 

For Adnabyd, the Priests of Knowledge may do good in keeping records of history, pursuit of further knowledge is only hindered by the church. Prophets of Medr are focussed on the practice of pre-existing skills and not the creation of new ones or the expansion of existing ones. For other examples, worship of strength prays to an Eternal who is not listening, for strength that is not within the Eternal's power to grant. Mortals bless the forge rather than focus on the real processes of their creation.

To remedy this, in the interest of furthering our Domains in the Mortal Realm, I propose we educate the mortals about our true outlook towards them, and notify them that prayer is meaningless and it would be better for them to continue their lives without false religions, naturally performing actions of our domains rather than worshiping our fake characters.
