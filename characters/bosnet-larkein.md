Bosnet Larkein is a Vevran Kaemuz living in Eosyrdel.

Bosnet is an "artefact connoseur" who likes to keep track of Eresonain "artefacts" which are generally permanently enchanted items. He knows of the OPB as they have previously come in conflict with one another, as the OPB searches for artefacts to help their cause while Bosnet generally wants to keep them either hidden or in the hands of random individuals.

Bosnet is giving L from Death Note, where his enemy "Kira" is Kesnon Ivisel.
