The Continental Strait is the narrowest part of Ayangshula. It is between the southmost point of [Ralean](ralean.md) and some point on the north-western coast of [Neble](/wastelife/regions/anula/neble). Jia is an [Allyiscereen](/wastelife/creatures/misc/allyiscereen), a gargantuan creature causing wreck and havock on any large convoys travelling through the strait. They rush to the strait's shallows and tears apart boats with ease when they detect the convoys.

Jia is said to be as large as (small) cities with massive legs it stands on in the shallows. It is said to have massive tentacles the width and length of streets which it uses to smash boats. It is said to have a mouth large enough to swallow ships whole.

Jia usually appears in Vanian legend as one of a usually friendly disposition. Some of the tales suggest that Jia is Nater re-incarnated.

There is no recent record of contact with Jia (for thousands of years), but this is likely because of the caution people take around this tale rather than Jia not existing.

_FUN FACT_
If we crossed the shortest part of the strait (130km) at a good speed of 25 knots, we'd get to the other side in 3h. 

If Jia was chilling the furthest possible distance that she could be from this point (2300km) she would have to go 800 kmph to reach us in time. 

so fun fact, jia can't always be there to stop a massive convoy, but she does move really fast.
