Rhedzin is a 1300 year old sentient [Palug](/wastelife/creatures/ereso-native/palug) that lives on the top of Ereso's tallest mountain, Rouval. Rhedzhin was once a warrior wizard living in northern Ereso. On their adventures, they managed to either transform themselves into a giant Palug or come to inhabit the body of one. 

He is mythologised in Ursan culture as the "King of the Palug". Rhedzin does have many Palug companions, but most Palug do not respect him.
