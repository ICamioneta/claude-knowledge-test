Shibel is a character from Oranax whose worldwide claim to fame is the creation of her own type of crossbow. The bow part of the crossbow faces backwards, using wheels and pulleys to redirect the string. It is by far the best crossbow variant available in the world, but unfortunately it is also the rarest. There's only about 5 on Barogate, and allegedly less than two dozen of them in existence. Repairing these and knowledge on how to maintain them is almost equally rare, designated only to the most devoted owners of the weapon and some highly skilled crossbow smiths. One such smith is [Kyelee Ebhand](/wastelife/characters/kyelee-ebhand).

[Corvan Esata](/wastelife/characters/corvan-esata) owns one of these crossbows, but it is currently on display in the Allesoden University of Cultural Anthropology.
