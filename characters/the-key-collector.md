The Key Collector is a Reipan Kaemuz from Reipoten. Their real name is Ekith Nebythet.

They are part of a long series of key collectors dating back thousands of years. Their collection is the largest collection of keys on Barogate. The bulk of the collection focusses on all types of keys from Ereso. The non-Eresonian keys are generally notable.

The Key Collector hoards information on keys from everywhere. This notably includes information on the Key to the Heart of Hidoyad, and the location of the key to get the key, since the key is in a silver ruin locked behind a door that itself requires a key.

The Key Collector as an institute is an independent, who uses independent adventuring groups and other sources to get their collection and knowledge. They seem to have a lot of money when it comes to procuring things for their collection. The exact origin of this wealth is unknown, but it is fairly commonly understood that many of the keys in their collection locks old hidden treasures.

Ekith doesn't like to use their actual name, much preferring their hereditary title of "key collector".
