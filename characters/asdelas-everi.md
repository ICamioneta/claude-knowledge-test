Asdelas Everi is a [Domuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/domuz) Lale-Deornite worker at the [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md) Bureau of Transregional Exploitation, serving as its primary author. In 3479, they finalised a [A Report on the Colonies of the Domuz Republic](/wastelife/lore/a-report/a-report-on-the-colonies-of-the-domuz-republic).

The Bureau of Transregional Exploitation are a partially subsidised group of 12 individuals under the supervision of the Eresonian National Council, to whom it reports. Their primary activities as of late have been the creation of A Report on the Colonies of the Domuz Republic in 3479 and the earlier and much chunkier The State of the Terlayan Economy in 3472.

The bureau uses office and meeting spaces in government commons across the nation.
