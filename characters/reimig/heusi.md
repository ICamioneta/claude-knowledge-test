Heusi Reimig is the first properly recorded [Reimig](/wastelife/characters/reimig/index). During his lifetime, he became a popular figurehead in Ilcanthyn, eventually overthrowing the old dynastic rulers of the city. He then went on to take control of the entire Eysenre valley, solidifying his dynasty and its position as a power in Ralean.
