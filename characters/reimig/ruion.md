Ruion Reimig is the current Shalun (emperor) of [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md) and the head of the [Reimig](/wastelife/characters/reimig/index) dynasty. He is often criticized for his lack of strength, beginning with his lacklustre response to the independence of the Llofilot region and his gross mismanagement of the southern revolts. Supporters of Ruion claim the revolts were inevitable given the ambitious conquest of Terlaye, and that Ruion is a good diplomat and peace-keeper. 

Regardless, Ruion's modern legacy is the downfall of the Empire and a bitter end to his father's celebrated reign. s
