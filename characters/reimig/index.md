The Reimig Dynasty is the ruling dynasty of [The Empire of Ralean](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/the-empire-of-ralean.md), which was formerly known as the Reimig Empire prior to the crowning of some member of the family.
# Members
- [Heusi Reimig](wastelife/characters/reimig/heusi.md), leader of Illandus in 2889, died in 2938.
- [Tensar Reimig](/wastelife/characters/reimig/tensar-reimig), coronated 2938-2954

- [Canita Reimig](wastelife/characters/reimig/canita.md), The Conqueror of Terlaye, ?-3455
- [Ruion Reimig](wastelife/characters/reimig/ruion.md), coronated 3455, current emperor

# History
The public lore article for this is [A Brief History of the Empire of Ralean](/wastelife/lore/old-unfinished/a-brief-history-of-the-empire-of-ralean)

The Reimig family have somewhat mysterious beginnings, but the story promoted by the modern dynasty is that they descended from a family of master chefs in Illandus. Through luck, charm and wit, Heusi Reimig came to be a controlling figurehead in the city of Illandus, eventually coming to control the entire Eysenre valley.
