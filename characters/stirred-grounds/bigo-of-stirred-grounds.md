Bigo is a [Popens](/wastelife/species-of-person/ens/popens) from the [Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/index) of unknown age. His story is short, but basically, he was a nomad until his tribe kicked him out. He then went on to join the group in their endeavours. He’s just a strong man and a good longbow user. That’s all there is. Secretly, Bigo’s a real deep thinker and has many thoughts on many things. He’s probably the first to form an opinion on something, and he’s been more useful in identifying threats and issues than as a strongman.
