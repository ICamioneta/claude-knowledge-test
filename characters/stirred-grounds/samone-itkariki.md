Samone is a [Nidos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nidos) Fighter and a duellist in [Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/index). He is 32 years old. Samone is reasonable, meticulous, and quick-witted. It is for that very reason he’s a longsword duellist. Samone’s actually a bit short for a Nidos, but somewhat average height if he were a Muz.

Samone’s family moved to [[ralean#Southern Forest|Southern Ralean]] for work. They neglected Samone a lot, but he found a group to be with. Eventually, he met [Viera Atsucher](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/viera-atsucher), who he became close friends to. Eventually, they decided to go on adventures. It was Samone’s idea at first, and Viera only backed it up wanting to do something with his life. Samone didn’t nearly have as much use as Viera during their hiking days, but when it came for the adventuring group, Samone found a longsword easy to learn, perhaps because of his attention to detail.

Samone is a fighter, plain and simple. As has been mentioned, he wields a longswords, but also carries a crossbow for when it’s needed. Samone has gotten the group out of many difficult combats. His style is special in that he often focuses on inhibiting his opponent’s movement instead of killing them. He goes for the arms and shoulders, places where Viera can easily heal.
