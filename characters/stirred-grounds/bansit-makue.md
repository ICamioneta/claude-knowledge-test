Bansit Makue, is the de facto leader of the [Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/index). He is a [Kaemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/kaemuz) 36 years of age. He is strong, athletic, wise, and sometimes takes too long making decisions. Makue has a wide eye and almost never looses track of where he is. Makue has short-cut hair, a defined face with a stubble beard. He is of an average height for a Kaemuz.

Like many adventuring group’s stories, his starts in a tavern, where he met with two other members of the group [Viera Atsucher](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/viera-atsucher) and [Samone Itkariki](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/samone-itkariki). At this point in his life, Makue didn’t really know what to do with his life. He’d been pressured by his parents to make a career for himself. Both hopeful and drained, he met next to Siera and Samone hoping to meet some friends. He eventually learnt that they’d been on great adventures together. Not the bandit-slaying, dungeon-delving adventures but more the hiking and sight-seeing ones. He wanted to join them because it sounded fun. Away from the pressure of his parents, and perfectly fine. He did join them, and although his parents rejected, he snuck out and went with them anyway.

On adventure, he found his role as a sort of guide. He found himself with a natural aptitude for the forest, perhaps taken from his enjoyment in the woods as a child. With Makue at their side, Siera and Samone never lost themselves, and always had a way to go. When they decided to become a true adventuring group, which happened after they met [Silgori Solthun](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/silgori-solthun).

Makue decided to take a ranger’s role. He wields an enchanted longbow, and he knows enchantments himself for the arrows. Being the de-facto leader of the group, Makue often always says the thing that everyone does. He might not be the best at making these decisions, but his ability to listen to his teammates and consider their viewpoint makes him the best in the group.
