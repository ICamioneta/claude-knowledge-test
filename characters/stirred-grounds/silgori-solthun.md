Silgori is a [Korongan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/korongan) of the [Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/index). He’s 35 years old, hardy, stubborn, but ultimately loves the company and attention. Silgori plays into Korongan stereotypes by being an axe-wielding fighter. Silgori actually shaved his head and all his hair, and he likes to keep it that way.

Silgori, like most Korongan, grew up fighting the dangers of the Wastes. This is what made him into the battle-hardened soldier he is today. He grew tired of fighting and decided to wander alone. Eventually he met [Bansit Makue](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/bansit-makue), [Viera Atsucher](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/viera-atsucher), and [Samone Itkariki](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/samone-itkariki) in [Cronar](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/cronar), where he joined their group. He believed he needed to be with them if they wanted to go up to [Kusao](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/kusao). He thought it was his duty to protect them. In reality, he just didn’t want to be alone.

Silgori wields the Battleaxe and a basic shield. He’s a fighter, not a barbarian. Much like Samone, he’s been invaluable in combat scenarios. He also has a bit of an aptitude for conversion. He’s probably the best at speechcraft in the entire group. Even though he likes a good fight, he tries to avoid as many as possible with his speech.
