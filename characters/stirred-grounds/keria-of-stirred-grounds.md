Keria is a [Sosophax](/wastelife/species-of-person/phax/sosophax) of the [Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/index) with a hard-to-tell age. Much like any Sosopax, she’s quiet and doesn’t speak often, but likes being around people. There’s not much to say about her personality other than that she’s very curious.

Keria left a Sosophax town due to wanderlust. She wanted to see the world. She’d heard about lands where trees reach the skies, of lands where the ground eats your feet. She wanted to see it all. It’s convenient that as she was leaving Kusao, so was Makue and his group. She joined them, and has been quite useful to them as a mage of illusion.

Keria is a mage, a clapper, and she doesn’t like battling. It is for this very reason that she specialises in magic that repels enemies. Fear spells to make them run, paralyze spells to let her run, mirage spells to conceal herself and blind spells to give her the opportunity to escape. All of this of course benefits the rest of the group.
