Viera Atsucher, or [Siera when he wants to be](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_fluidity), is a [Danmuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/danmuz) healer and potion crafter in [Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/index). Who wouldv’e guessed that the Danmuz of the group did magic? Viera is 28 years of age, thoughtful, caring, and perhaps too cautious. Viera either does his hair in a low-hanging ponytail, or in interesting styles when he prefers to be Siera. Viera’s also of average height for a Danmuz.

Viera grew up in Southern Ralean. His family moved there as part of a wave of Danmuz who did during the Empire’s invasion of Terlaye. Viera lived a comfortable life, and eventually grew up with a natural aptitude for herbs and healing. From this aptitude came Viera’s potion making skills. Viera knew [Samone Itkariki](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/samone-itkariki) from his childhood. Samone was the most supportive of Viera, and could easily tell when he wanted to be Siera. Eventually, they decided to see the world, and began hiking across mountains and walking across lands. That’s where they eventually met Makue

Viera is a Wizard. He carries many bottles and potions of healing. He also just has a good ability to heal regardless. His role in the group is very clear. If someone breaks a bone, Viera’s there to do some healing. He wields a flangehead mace and a tower shield, which he uses to protect his teammates from projectiles. He uses a flangehead because he can easily heal broken bones and ribs.
