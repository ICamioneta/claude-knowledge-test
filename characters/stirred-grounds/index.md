The Stirred Grounds is a diverse adventuring group 
They’re quite experienced adventurers, and like to take up larger quests by the higher-ups of society, but they still find time for smaller jobs and dungeon crawling.

# Members
- [Bansit Makue](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/bansit-makue), de facto leader and wizard bowman
- [Viera Atsucher](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/viera-atsucher), healer and potion wizard
- [Samone Itkariki](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/samone-itkariki), duelist
- [Silgori Solthun](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/silgori-solthun), axe fighter
- [Keria of Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/keria-of-stirred-grounds), utility mage
- [Bigo of Stirred Grounds](/wastelife/characters/stirred-grounds/bigo-of-stirred-grounds), longbow user.
