The Peddler

Name: The Peddler, Stoot Feller

Likeness: A rather short (158cm) Difmuz who is well-fed

Personality Traits: Introverted and Sturdy.

Ideals: Individuality and Safety.

Bonds: Everything the Peddler does is for the people of Hegrel.

Flaws: Annoyingly pacifist and overly enthusiastic

# His Disclosed History

The Peddler talks to the people of [Hegrel](wastelife/factions/barogate-wastes/hegrel.md) and the people in its towns often. It’s how he gets his weekly social interactions. In these interactions, he talks a lot about what he does and his adventures, and only sometimes speaks of his backstory. This is what his fans know about him.

The Peddler was born in the lower parts of [Darie](wastelife/regions/barogate/gazi-lale-darie), in a small town near Port Checkr. The Peddler spent his early days as a young adult as a general trader’s assistant in a town, which is where he says he got his trading skills from. Why he left is one of his stories that he likes to tell. The boss of this general store, Daern, had a few regular customers who came into the place to sell all types of odds and ends, and sometimes people came in to buy trinkets. One of these regulars, a woman called Hegsy, often came in with ornate objects. Then Daern told the Peddler that she was a businesswoman, the Peddler began to question exactly how these pleasant jewelleries fell into her hands.

So, the Peddler began to watch her in order to learn exactly what her trade was that got these things into her hands. It turned out that she was a conwoman, coaxing poor people to get items that they can’t afford, giving it to them for free at first, then going to their residences later for payment. She then took things with lots of sentimental value from these people as payment for the objects, even though that wasn’t the original deal she gave the poor people. She sold these objects through Daern. The Peddler objected to her business much, and when he called her out for it no-one took his side, so in the night he went into her house and stole an object that meant a lot to her. He then left that town so that the guards wouldn’t track him.

He decided to wander the wastes for the rest of his adult life. He spent a large amount of time wandering [Dithas](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/dithas), which was easier because of the highly developed Domuz colony he could rest at any time. He learnt most of his skills that way. Eventually, he moved to Kusao, did some things there like settle disputes, then he decided to wander the Heabe taking in the culture and living with the nomads, which is where he learnt the many variants of Garas he can speak. Then, fascinated by the mushroom people, he lived among them being a somewhat hero solving issues for fun until eventually he went to Hegrel.

He says he chose to stay in Hegrel because it needed him. The first time he went there he saw a bog monster attack some innocent, which he saved by drawing the monster away from them and onto him, since he could outrun it. He saw all of the hostilities that Hegrel faces year-round, and saw that when he arrived it had multiple issues. He spent as much time as he could fixing them. Throughout all of his adventuring he always carried the trinkets of the last place he had been, and traded them for a trinket in the place he was at that time. He continued this trade in Hegrel.

The Peddler never got into any dungeons mostly because they weren’t his style, and that he wasn’t willing to risk his life like that. Instead, he trades with many [Lindi](/wastelife/species-of-person/other/lindi) and [Ens](ens.md) tribes that live in or wander through the area, giving their things to Hegrel and back. He likes telling stories in Hegrel, and just by doing that he gained sort of a celebrity status in the town.

# An adventure involving him

If the player ever manages to meet with him and convince him that they should go on a journey together, which he will be reluctant to do because of his independent attitude, but not opposed to the idea due to him assuming everyone he meets is nice, they will set off north-east out of Hegrel, or down that path regardless of where they are.

Sometime upon this path he will come across an adventurer or a otherwise innocent person being extorted by a group of hostile Lindi. In this scenario, there’s a single person on the road completely surrounded by 7-12 Lindi, the Lindi asking said person to drop their belongings or fight. The Peddler will quickly rush in to diffuse the situation by calling to both of them not to fight. He will ask the player to help him in convincing the Lindi to leave the other person be. He might try and offer the Lindi some of his own items, or intimidate them to leave. Whatever the tactic, if the Lindi do not leave then he will insist that no-one gets killed and he will deal as much non-lethal damage as he can, trying not to kill any of the Lindi. He will warn others in the fight not to kill the Lindi, and if they do then they will be left by the Peddler because of the harm that they’ve caused. The Peddler will make sure that none of the Lindi die or are crippled, medicating them afterwards. The attacked person will insist that they need to go to Hegrel, but might be persuaded on going with them.

Next, him and the player will arrive at a destroyed Lindi settlement. At learning this, the Peddler will be distressed, and might explain that he had befriended this settlement and enjoyed their company, and traded trinkets often with. He will be angry at whoever did this, and will want to track them down. Luckily, there will be tracks of some sort leading from the Lindi settlement to a camp, which also has a fire lit.

This camp will be a bandit camp. The Peddler will try to reason with the bandits, and they will say that they think that all Lindi were hostile, because they ran in to loads who just attacked them on sight. They’d explain how them doing that made them heroes, because they stopped another hostile tribe. If the party can’t settle the situation diplomatically, there’s about 10 of them and they might be worth running from.

After that, the Peddler will refuse to follow the player anymore, stating that they need to mourn for the dead. He will be seen in Hegrel from time to time, and may join the player if they can convince him that there’s something good for him. He won’t permanently join the player, and will only ever come on one-shot adventures with them, and he’s extra reluctant to enter dungeons.
