Aimer and Misha are two adventurers who travel all around Barogate. Aimer is a [Lemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/lemuz) who grew up in the [[ralean#Eysenre Valleys|Eysenre Valleys]] [[ralean#Eysenre Valleys]], quite close to the border with the [Vugaix](/wastelife/regions/barogate-wastes/vugaix). On one of his many wanders, he met a small [Cuemad](/wastelife/species-of-person/mad/cuemad) girl who clung to him. He now travels everywhere with this woman, as they’ve decided to explore Barogate. Misha was the name of this Cuemad.

Aimer and Misha have grown to be quite a powerful couple. They have felled many foes only with each other. Even though they are quite young, Aimer and Misha are quite powerful magic users.

## Aimer Resgu

Aimer is a 29-year-old Lemuz of average height. He is calm, reasonable, and a little forgetful from time to time. Aimer is a friendly character who can see the good in anyone. Aimer has short-cut hair but often wears a hat.

In his village, he was quite a bored lad. He was the only child his age in the village, and all the other kids were either too old or too young to get along with him. For this very reason, he liked to adventure alone or with an adult and make up stories about the wilderness. At the age of 17, Aimer was wandering close to the river that separated the Vugaix and Ralean when he saw Misha crying by the riverbed. Intrigued, he swam over to comfort her. Misha clung on to Aimer at that moment, and followed him back to his village.

Although initially cautious, the people of the village were happy that Aimer had something to distract him. Aimer felt complete too, as if he had his own child to look after. Over time, Misha learnt how to speak empiric and Aimer succeeded in learning Misha’s Vuga language. Misha began to open up to Aimer, and they began to understand each other more.

Aimer decided that he would continue on his childhood adventures, but up the scale. Misha was more than happy to go with him, and even asked him to go places sometimes. Aimer and Misha have since been to many parts of Barogate, often temporarily joining adventuring groups or tailing caravans.

Aimer became fond of the longbow. It stopped Misha from worrying that he would suffer a wound in close-up combat. Aimer learnt how to enchant arrow tips to make his attacks stronger. This became his main role in combat, although when Aimer and Misha were alone they would have to skirmish as they had no close-combat fighter.

## Misha

Misha initially kept her past a secret to Aimer until they had been together for about 2 years, and they could understand each other’s language. Apparently, her people were slaughtered by a Flutsxa. She ran south, and eventually found a river that she could not cross. She mourned for her family in the river’s sound, safe knowing that it would drown out her cries for the most part. Then, a Lemuz boy found her, and she was thankful to have someone to protect her.

Misha stayed with Aimer because he cared for her. She tried to learn how to speak Empiric by watching Aimer talk to others, and with Aimer’s help she could eventually speak fluently enough to teach Aimer her people’s Vuga language. She became comfortable with Aimer, and had moved on from the death of her family. She finally told Aimer of her history.

When Aimer grew bored of his home town, he proposed to Misha that they should travel all over and see the world. Misha barely knew the world, she had believed it to be an infinite span of mushroom forests until she met the river, and then she thought that it was just an infinite span of mushroom forests, regular forests, and rivers. Misha was more than happy to explore the world and see the beautiful sights.

Misha, on their travels, eventually learnt that she had amazing magical abilities. When meeting with a mage in an adventuring group, the mage pointed out that Misha should have some innate magical abilities. After Aimer realised these were unexplored, Misha learnt that she could clap with nothing in her hands to produce a strong offensive magic attack. It hurt her hands to do this, after all some of her matter was used in the reaction.
