Corvan Esata is a Lemuz from northern [Ralean](ralean.md) who has travelled almost everywhere around Barogate and Anula, as well as decent chunks of Mothdao, Oranax, and Raixzer.

# Early Life

Corvan was born in a large town in south-eastern Ralean. He ran around and explored his immediate environment. Then, he ended up joining the Army.

# Corvan in the army

Corvan was in the army just after Llofilot declared independence from the empire, so there was a lot of unrest. He was stationed at the border and trained there. He first met his friend Shames Veshici. He then was moved to Southern Ralean to fight an organised group of neer-do-wellers that actually was a catalyst for the revolution of the South. Then, he left the army and decided to make some money selling colourful sand from Cronar in Darie.

# Getting to Cronar

He left the army with Shames Veshici and travelled across Ralean to a port town to go to Cronar. Shames didn’t board the boat so Corvan started alone.

# Getting out of Cronar

Corvan followed a caravan, but it didn’t pass through Kyenel so he had to walk through it alone in one of the most difficult times of his life.
# Meeting a Domuz in Ralean

Corvan meets a Domuz in Ralean and travels with her until they reach Nater’s End.
