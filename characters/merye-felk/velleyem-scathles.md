Velleyem Scathles is a member of [Merye Felk](/wastelife/characters/merye-felk/index) who lives in Serven. They come from a relatively wealthy background, but by the time they joined the Merye Felk they had given away most of their wealth to those in need, particularly old Terlayan knights abandoned after the Ralean conquest of Terlaye.

Between the stories of [Robin Howitha](/wastelife/characters/merye-felk/robin-howitha) and the Soris of Jonati and Robin Howitha and the Soris of Fellan, Velleyem heard of the Merye Felk and was inspired to join, using the last of his wealth to support an underground network in the developed area around Serven which would help honourable outlaws. He personally sook Robin out and, although loosing in a swordfight against Robin, was offered to join. Velleyem then went back to Terlaye, setting up a small Merye Felk cell there, which Robin and the many Merye Felk in Northlake would often visit for large heists.

Velleyem's family, the Scathles, used to control a lot of wealth in Serven. However, a few decades ago, many of the family members were mysteriously killed. This challenged Velleyem and his conception of his family wealth, and he eventually chose that money could only lead to trouble. In this ethos, he chose to use his wealth to help the fallen knights of Terlaye, and eventually spent the rest to establish Serven's underground network for the Meyre Felk.

During the events of Robin Howitha and the Soris of Fellan, Velleyem learns that it was the Eyvindot Dynasty, particularly the Soris of Fellan, that murdered all of his family members in attempts to consolidate power in Maevesfellanterum following the Eyvindot appointment to positions of Soris in the province.
