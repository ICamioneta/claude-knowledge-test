Melin Mux is a significant member of [Merye Felk](/wastelife/characters/merye-felk/index) who joined after being rescued from a death sentence for poaching the local Dynasty's animals.

Melin is a very generous individual.

Melin Mux killed the Rostavar of Jonati in 3481, and was caught and executed during the following weeks by the Soris of Jonati. Although the Merye Felk bear no relation to the Union and the Southern Revolts, the action weakened a legion escorting a Dux to Deshegdinum, and this lack of a reputable leadership led to the army immediately surrendering to Union guerrillas.
