Little Jenna is [Robin Howitha](/wastelife/characters/merye-felk/robin-howitha)'s lieutenant in the dissident organisation [Merye Felk](/wastelife/characters/merye-felk/index). She is a Danmuz from Desheg

She is a relatively small individual who joined the group before Robin's leadership. She beat the then-leader in a quarterstaff duel over a river, and is known as one of the best fighters in the group.

She loves cunning and mind-games. She enjoys deceiving people and putting on a play.
