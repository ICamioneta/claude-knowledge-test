# Characters

## Soris of Caleheb
The Province of Calaehebadinum is the northmost province of the Empire of Ralean. There are no cities in Caleheb, its centre is a large town called Notami which is on a tributary to Rullan and the lake Fellan (which is on Rullan). Calaehebadinum is the only Ralean province to have a border with The Heabe. It's roads are crucial trade routes for all Heabe-borne trade.

The Soris of Caleheb cares a lot about the Merye Felk and their impact on his land. Caleheb's land is mostly Northlake with some Calaerula and Sefare Eysenre, but most if not all of the region's development is in Northlake. The Merye Felk usually go around bugging the Soris, his trade routes, treasury, robbing his tax collectors, and bugging the dynasties of his provinces. 

The Soris of Caleheb is hell-bent on capturing Robin Howithe and ending the Merye Felk in his area. He is cruel and arrogant, and certainly not cunning. His cunning comes from his advisors, who hope to balance his ill governance.

## Soris of Jonati
The provincial border for Jonatidinum are drawn to extend to Rullan. This is to give Jonati access to the river, and the Terlayan border, for trade. However, it does mean that a very rich province with all of its wealth in the Jonati valley has some stake in Northlake. 

The Soris of Jonati is a powerful person. The dynasties present in Jonati aren't very widespread, and are all only in the valley. Merye Felk's domain is Northlake, and given how part of Jonatidinum is Northlake, the Merye Felk and the Soris of Jonati come into conflict. The caveat to this is that the Soris of Jonati cares little about the ongoings of their Northlake territory and tolerates banditry to some extent. However, the Soris of Jonati and Jonati forces are incredibly rich and competent, so the Merye Felk have to do work as to not annoy the Soris of Jonati who alone could amass an army plenty large to demolish the group.

## Soris of Fellan
The Terlayan Soris of Fellan is a rather bitter member of the old monarch's family that was chosen by Ruion Reimig to lead the province. He is loyal to the emperor and completely clueless when it comes to economic governance. The east side of the province, the part adjacent to the lake Fellan, is well developped and a destination for caravans traveling through Northlake. The Soris of Fellan is aware of the Merye Felk and is close friends with the Soris of Caleheb. They happily oppose Merye Felk actions in the province in the hopes of pleasing the Soris of Caleheb. 

Serven is a large city and the area around it is highly developed, so it's quite hard for a band of outlaws to navigate freely.

## Soris of Nillan
The Terlayan Soris of Nillan is another member of the same family that rules the province of Maevesfellanterum. They dislike their neighbour. They also have a large city: Ateimot, which regularly trades with Jonati going across the roads in Jonatidinum. Although highly lucrative targets, hitting them would make enemies with the Soris of Jonati. The Soris of Nillan doesn't know much about the Merye Felk, but recognises that the Soris of Fellan doesn't like them and would covertly support them to the end of annoying the Soris of Fellan.

## Notius Dynasty
The Notius Dynasty is a minor Sefars dynasty present in Northlake and Calaerula. The Notis Dynasty's estates are across generally and poorly developed land, and a big part of this is how often they get robbed by the Merye Felk. Before Robin they had little qualms with the Merye Felk, but now they've gone stealing a lot thee Notius dynasty have been a massive target.

The Notius Dynasty aren't powerful at all. It's a relatively small family (say, 12 members) with little stake in developed land. I guess, in a direct matchup, they might make equal antagonists for the Merye Felk.

## Eyvindot Dynasty
The Eyvindot Dynasty is the result of the Maeves king in power being dethroned and the Reimigs deciding that Terlayan monarchy should look a lot like the Ralean dynasties. The Maveves king's family name was Eyvindot, and the Reimigs decided to pull the provincial managers (once the Duxes were unnecessary) from this family, essentially establishing it as a dynasty. 

There's a lot of inner conflict in the Eyvindot Dynasty, namely between the two Soris selected by Ruion Reimig to govern over Maevesfellanterum and Maevesnillanterum. The Soris of Fellan and the Soris of Nillan. The dynasty is almost split in two. 

They are fair targets of the Merye Felk. This has lead to the main antagonism between the Merye Felk and the Soris of Fellan, but the Soris of Nillan doesn't seem too annoyed by them probably because they're worse for the Soris of Fellan.
