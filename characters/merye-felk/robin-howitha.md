Robin Howitha is a [Kaemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/kaemuz) radical living in [[ralean#Northlake]]. He is the leader of the [Merye Felk](/wastelife/characters/merye-felk/index) insurgency group.

Robin Howitha is a masterful swordsman and bowman. He is also empathetic and kind, with a satirical view on life. Chivalric, to a fault, and very showy. He knows he's the best and he isn't afraid to show off.

He came to become the leader of Meyre Felk through charisma. He gained the trust of many of the group's members and announced his radical ideas for the group, which many of his friends (the majority of the Meyre Felk) supported.
