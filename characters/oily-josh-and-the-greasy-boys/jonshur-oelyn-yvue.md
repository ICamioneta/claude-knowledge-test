Oily Josh is the leader of the Greasy Boys. He is 31 years of age [Lemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/lemuz). He is thin, kind, surprisingly wise, and a healer wizard. Jonshur only carries a mage satchel, and it is often empty at that. Jonshur isn’t a mage, and only carries ingredients to make a living. Jonshur is a great healer. With enchantments only, he has cured all ailments from scratches to blindness. He does all of this without the need of potions, somehow directly enchanting the bodies of people. He’s also cured people of more godly illnesses such as clumsiness, schadenfreude, madness. He’s even brought people back from near-death. Some of his greasy boys would say that the people he revived were entirely dead, but Jonshur does not make these claims.

Jonshur could be extremely powerful with his power. It’s already known that his torfol is massive, stretching four meters from him. If he wanted to, he could directly enchant people in negative ways, such as making them explode. He doesn’t do this, instead he heals. There are non-healing things he’s done, such as his signature turning water into an alcoholic beverage otherwise known as wine. He’s saved a few port towns by bringing fish to the shore somehow. He’s somehow multiplied food as well. He’s made a tree wither, one of his weirdest magics.

Perhaps his most famous show of his ability was him walking on water barefoot. Usually, someone with that enchantment would need to enchant some boots or shoes, but Jonshur with his ability enchanted his feet. Many of the Greasy Boys say this is why he is called Oily Josh, because of his ability to be above water like oil. The name is also influenced by Jonshur’s middle name, Oelyn.

Some of the Greasy Boys say that Jonshur once died and then came back to life, but Jonshur and other Greasy Boys say this never happened.
