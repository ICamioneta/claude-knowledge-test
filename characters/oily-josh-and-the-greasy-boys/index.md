Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys is a group of peoples that wander all over, but originate from [[ereso#Northern Planes]]. Oily Josh, real name [Jonshur Oelyn Yvue](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/jonshur-oelyn-yvue), is a Lemuz and his followers, collectively known as the Greasy Boys, are 12 men of several species and races.

The 12 greasy boys are:
- [Pevir Nimtet](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/pevir-nimtet)
- [Shames Isph](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/shames-isph)
- [Uvhydtop yh Ikchyt](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/uvhydtop-yh-ikchyt)
- [Andu of the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/andu-of-the-greasy-boys)
- [Nath Indith](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/nath-indith)
- [Shames Ustei](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/shames-ustei)
- [Mamlel Yancon](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/mamlel-yancon)
- [Chad Bixbi](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/chad-bixbi)
- [Levi Entu](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/levi-entu)
- [Sumishu Shara](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/sumishu-shara)
- [Zeatet Mishimy](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/zeatet-mishimy)
- [Maenie Posartem](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/maenie-posartem)

Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys travel all over. Oily Josh has the unique ability to use enchantments directly on a person, such as healing without the need to enchant liquids or equipment. Many people in the Greasy Boys don’t actually fully understand Jonshur’s ability and they think he’s performing acts of miracle. Jonshur is a healer and he really just travels for the fun of it, helping anyone he can. The Greasy Boys just follow him for whatever reason.
