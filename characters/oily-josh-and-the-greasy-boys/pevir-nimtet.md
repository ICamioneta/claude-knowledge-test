Pevir Nimtet is a 32 year old [Kaemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/kaemuz) and is part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index), and known as the smart one in the group. He’s solved many problems himself through his own ‘miracles of the mind’. He’s constructed amazing mechanisms to save beings, like taking beached whales back to the shore using pulleys, a method still in use by the people of Darie. Pevir Nimet was one of the first people to follow Jonshur, and is Jonshur’s close personal friend. Pevir shares all of Jonshur’s ideals.
