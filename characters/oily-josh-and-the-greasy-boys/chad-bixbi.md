Chad Bixbi is a [Korongan](/wastelife/species-of-person/gan/korongan) and a seasoned veteran warrior who is part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index). He doesn’t want to fight anymore, and as Jonshur saved him from a conflict without anyone getting hurt. Chad realises Jonshur’s other ability to completely avoid conflicts. Chad is one of the Greasy Boys who are more religious about Jonshur’s existence.
