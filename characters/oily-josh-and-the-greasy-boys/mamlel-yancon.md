Mamlel Yancon is a [Kaemuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/kaemuz) 30 years of age and is part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index). He was  cured by Oily Josh earlier in life and since decided to follow him in all of his healing endeavours. Mamlel is like a therapist, helping people with issues of the mind rather than issues with physical health.

Oily Josh helped Mamlel Yancon with an issue with his physicality. Mamlel was born as a man but given a female body for [whatever reason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transgender). Oily Josh used this predicament to perfect an enchantment he made that would change people’s bodies to that which they want.
