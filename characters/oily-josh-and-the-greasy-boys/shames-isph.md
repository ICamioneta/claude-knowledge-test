Shames Isph is an [[Coesgan#Akiiogan]] 40 years of age. He is an expert sneak and covert operationist, which might make it seem as if he’s out of place in the group. As a covert operationist, he is actually very helpful. When Jonshur is in a settlement, Shames Isph watches the people around and studies the town. He does this to find people who need healing, which is because that is what Jonshur requested he do, but he also finds people who seek to assassinate Jonshur for whatever reason. This barely ever happens, and when it does, Shames brings them in front of Jonshur so that they can figure out their differences.

Shames was one of the original people in Jonshur’s group. He does not share the exact ideology of Jonshur but is extremely satisfied with the way he helps Jonshur in his task.
