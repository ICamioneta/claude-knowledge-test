Shames Ustei is a [Nudos](/wastelife/species-of-person/dos/nudos) and part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index). He subscribes to a religion in following Jonshur, but he helps Jonshur and likes seeing the world. Ustei is a fairly accomplished warrior too, but once again the Greasy Boys almost never get into combat due to Oily Josh’s really good charisma and persuasion.
