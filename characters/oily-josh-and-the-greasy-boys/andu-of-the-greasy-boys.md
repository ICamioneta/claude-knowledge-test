Andu is a [Ximuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/ximuz) and is part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index).

Andu is a bit of a religious fanatic and believes that Oily Josh is the messiah spoken of in the Ximuz religion. Andu is 29 years old, and mostly follows Oily Josh to be part of ‘history’. He is overall a good person; he also wants to experience the world. Andu is one of the Greasy Boys who keeps a diary of Jonshur’s endeavours.
