Nath is a [Domuz](/wastelife/species-of-person/muz/domuz) 19 years of age and part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index). As Jonshur’s youngest follower, he is following to learn the healing ways. Nath helps heal people, but without Jonshur’s abilities he has to use potions and liquids. Even though he has to enchant liquids to heal, he has the same healing capabilities that Jonshur had when he was 19.
