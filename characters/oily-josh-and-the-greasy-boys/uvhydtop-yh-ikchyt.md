Uvhydtop yh Ikchyt is a Mothdaoan of an unknown age, and usually goes by the name Yuvi. He’s part of the original group that joined Jonshur as part of [Oily Josh and the Greasy Boys](/wastelife/characters/oily-josh-and-the-greasy-boys/index), and is the last one stated here. He is a warrior, and uses blunted chakrams to injure people. He only does it in extreme circumstances. Other than that, he’s like a nurse to Shames's doctor
