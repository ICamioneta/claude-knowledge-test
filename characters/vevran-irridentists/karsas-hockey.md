Karsas Hockey is a higher up in the Mia Mesten Mining Company. He is the leader of the Vevran Independence movement and part of the [Vevran Irridentists](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/index). He is incredibly pro-active and popular among small business owners in Vevran areas.
