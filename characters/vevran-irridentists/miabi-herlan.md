Miabi is a field operative of the [Operatives of the Pierced Back](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/operatives-of-the-pierced-back) who specialises in assassination. They are highly proficient in rounded dagger usage, and likes to disarm opponents. She likes to kill opponents with their own weapon. She is also a master of improvised weaponry, capable of making a weapon out of any common household item, from utensils to improvised clubs. 
In WTTTRPG, she wields a rounded dagger of quality 5, with a +10 attack bonus.

She is very noticeably a sadist and a psychopath. She is very clearly incapable of empathy with others. She enjoys her job, which is killing people, so she enjoys killing people.

Her brother is [Makvel Herlan](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/makvel-herlan)
