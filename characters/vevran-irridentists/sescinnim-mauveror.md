Sescinnim is a Laurafall and a field operative of the [Operatives of the Pierced Back](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/operatives-of-the-pierced-back). He is a mage with a supply line established with their home tribe in Duela. This supply line gives him powerful Duela ingredients, which are crucial for his signature illusion-based clap style. These are useful as saviours of a plan if something goes wrong. Other than his claps, he wields a shortsword.
In WTTTRPG, this shortsword is of quality 4 and has a +9 attack bonus.
