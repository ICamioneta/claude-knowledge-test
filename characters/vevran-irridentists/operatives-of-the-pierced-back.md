The OPB are a highly skilled covert operations group acting in the interests of [Vevran Irridentists](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/index). Because of [Karsas Hockey](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/karsas-hockey)'s leader position in the irridentists, the OPB is essentially under his command. Its main base of operations is located in Eosir, and they also have a secret base in Galresir.

These are the operatives:
- [Makvel Herlan](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/makvel-herlan), planner and leader
- [Kesnon Ivisel](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/kesnon-ivisel), planner and experimental psychologist
- [Miabi Herlan](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/miabi-herlan), sadistic assassin
- [Etkai Mabtet](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/etkai-mabtet), arrow watchman
- [Sescinnim Mauveror](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/sescinnim-mauveror), illusionist
