Dosben Maslei is a diplomat in service of Karsas. Under Councilism, his role is highly diminished and he merely represents the [Vevran Irridentists](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/index) in relevant councils. He is also a small buisness owner in [[Lesten]], and wishes for the end of Councilism and its government structure presumably so that his company and all companies alike can prosper in profit without having to compete with the working councils.
