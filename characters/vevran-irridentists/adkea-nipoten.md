Adkea Niopten is a skilled town administrator on the [[Lesten]] city council. She has secured a role in Lesten performing some simple administrational and managerial tasks for the council, but wishes more for herself. In addition to being a part of the [Vevran Irridentists](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/index), she wants the new government to change the role of the City Council and return to old and familiar mayoral systems of governance. She is a little annoyed at the requirement of accountability, and believes it holds her administration back.
