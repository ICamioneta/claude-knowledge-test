Kesnon is a planner of the [Operatives of the Pierced Back](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/operatives-of-the-pierced-back) who specialises in psychology. Being a burgeoning field of expertise, Kesnon enjoys the experimental nature of his suggestions. His activities often constitute the spread of misinformation and leading investigators into dead ends. He also has a knack for framing folk.
