Makvel is a planner of the [Operatives of the Pierced Back](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/operatives-of-the-pierced-back), and Miabi's brother. He scopes and plans assassinations. Information in his plans include time of death, location, method, and good places to hide and stay concealed. He is a bit worried about his sister's clear sadism, as well as her infrequent psychotic episodes. It was however his idea to use his sister's nature and found a covert operation group whose activities often involve killing. 
In WTTTRPG, he wields an obsidian halberd of quality 5, with a +9 attack bonus.

Makvel's sister is [Miabi Herlan](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/miabi-herlan)
