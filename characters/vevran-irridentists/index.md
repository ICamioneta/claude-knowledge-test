The Vevran Irridentists are the people involved in the Vevran Independence plot against [Eresonia](wastelife/factions/barogate-lifeland/eresonia.md), specifically those aligned with Karsas Hockey and his idea for an extended Vevran border which covers the [[ereso#Iavan Coast]]. They are:
- [Karsas Hockey](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/karsas-hockey), the main guy
- [Adkea Nipoten](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/adkea-nipoten), an administrator for Lesten
- [Dosben Maslei](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/dosben-maslei), a diplomat on the national council that represents the independence movement and the irridentists
- [Iketa Bandel](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/iketa-bandel), a military official who believes he deserves command of an army

[Operatives of the Pierced Back](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/operatives-of-the-pierced-back) is a group of covert operatives supporting Karsas.


Although Vevranian independence is a somewhat popular movement among the Vevran wealthy. The most moderate of the independence movement only wants independence for Vevran itself, the land between the rivers that define the Daraerula region. Karsas is a bit of a fanatic in Vevranain independence, seeking to restore "old borders" and bring the Iavan coast into this new Vevranian nation.

This irridentist movement hinges on somewhat false historical information that the Iavan coast was once a Vevran cultural area. The truth in this belief is that the Iavan Coast was once the location of a "lost" Eresonian culture that was more close in tradition to the Vevran than the other closest Sapyat, but this culture was somewhat lost as the area became culturally mixed during the Second Eresonain Empire. The original culture was lost as its unique traditions were overcame and regularised into the Vevran peoples starting to inhabit the area.
