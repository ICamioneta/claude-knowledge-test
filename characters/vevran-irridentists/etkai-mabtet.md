Etkai is a field operative of the [Operatives of the Pierced Back](/wastelife/characters/vevran-irridentists/operatives-of-the-pierced-back). He is a wizard with a shortbow, using offensive enchantments to enhance the effectiveness of his arrows. He specialises in hidden recon, often watching important areas from a concealed position to enable the effective operation of the remainder of his team. His arrow enchantments help secure him quick and quiet kills. 

In WTTTRPG, his shortbow is of quality 5 and an attack bonus of +4. He uses Rhenium-tipped arrows, which themselves have a +5 attack bonus.
