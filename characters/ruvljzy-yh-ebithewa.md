Properly called ɾʉʋɮɤ ɶ eʙɪðʍə and called "Ru" by everyone, ve is a Haerulian from a nation called Ivshahn in Haerul. Ve's known for being a case-study by [Corvan Esata](/wastelife/characters/corvan-esata) on the effectiveness, and existence of, the chakram. In addition to the chakram, which are quite common in Mothanial and Aerlada, ve is also an expert in the sabre.
