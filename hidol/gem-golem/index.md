# Gem Golem Hidology
I've covered in Hidology how torfol are creations of multiple hidol atoms aligning and concentrating with motion. Gemstone torfol are also a result of this process, but they take yonks to form. 

Basically, the movement of atoms arranging into repetitive structures also accumulates hidol atoms, generating over hundreds of millions of years. Because this process also takes a shit ton of time, you can't artificially make gemstones. 

The best that can be done for artificial gemstones is an Eternal making their realm's time dimension be a lot faster than the mortal realm, then with this speed having the gemstones form naturally over their hundreds of millions of years translating into a few decades in the mortal realm.
