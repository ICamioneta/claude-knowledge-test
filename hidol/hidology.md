Hidology – Hidol Sicence

The Hidol in Wastelife is something not well understood by mortals. However, they still try, often with the assistance of the Eternals who interface with it on scales unimaginable. Thanks to the Eternal understanding of the Hidol, mortal understanding is way more advanced than it could have been alone, but mortals are still striving to construct their own independent understanding of the Hidol, independent of the explanations provided by Eternals.
# Eternal Understanding
The Eternals have offered their own understanding of the Hidol to research institutions. This pertains to things that mortals currently have no way of observing or measuring themselves. 

The Eternals describe the Hidol as a multi-dimensional substrate of energy with no space or time. Realms, like the God's realms or the Mortal Realm, tie the Hidol to a space and a time. When tied to space and time, the Hidol is full of a bunch of "atoms". Note these are not material atoms, but Hidol atoms, expressions of hidol energy. It is with these atoms that Hidosis occurs, when they are brought into the mortal realm through manifesting. 

Hidol atoms have space and time, but also an extra-dimensional vector tied to the Hidol at large. The atoms coalesce when matter is in motion, like when there is life. Through enough coalescing and vector alignment, a Torfol exists. 

It is through these Torfol that mortals manifest the Hidol. Eternals can create direct interfaces with the Hidol, allowing them to use as much energy as is available in that time at whatever area they can tear their Hidol. Through Torfol, manifesting happens through atomic reaction. In spells, this is through the overlapping of two different Torfol to create some spontaneous event. In enchanting, this is through changing the Hidol vector of one's own torfol to produce some unique manifestation. These are limited to the strength of the torfol, determined by the properties of its atoms. 

Eternals transport things between realms by creating interfaces in the destination and location that align in their hidol vector, then they can safely bring matter through the Hidol. They state that their consciousnesses are made from the raw stuff of the Hidol, when compared to mortal consciousnesses that are fundamentally matter. They exist within the Hidol, but must manifest themselves a physical body if they wish to "exist" in a realm. These bodies often come with their own torfol.

Without a physical body, they still exist and can see into the mortal realm by existing in a certain area within the hidol. They can exist across all of their own realm, and thusly have access to the interfaces that let them draw as much energy as they want into it. The constraints that exist between Eternals and the Mortal Realm also exist with eternals and other eternal's realms, however with another eternal's efforts they can be given their own interface from which they can manifest or draw energy confined to the will of the granting eternal. 

# The Torfol
As Classical Hidology goes, every living thing has a Torfol.

Essentially, every living thing has this ‘bubble’ that occupies a space in Hidol. The area of this bubble is like a mass, that being it is derived by the Hidol Density of the region multiplied by the volume of Hidol it takes. It is, however, not mass. Mass is a ‘quantitative measure of inertia’, where inertia is how much a body opposes changes in velocity. The movement of these bubbles don’t depend on forces, because they stick to a central focal point.

These bubbles are called by the public as either ‘magic fields’ which is wrong by description, or ‘hidol bubbles’ which is significantly more accurate. When you look more into it, they are better described by ‘Hidol Masses’. Hidol Masses are called a Torfol, so as the saying goes, all living things have a torfol.

The word Torfol is a singular referring to the bubble created by all living things that occupies hidol. It follows the plural rule for all words derived from Gazish, adding -ae.

Torfolae have a focal point. The exact location of this focal point might not be exact for a specific creature, but if that creature has a creature stone, then that stone is the focal point of their torfol. In humans, the focal point is usually between the lungs close to the diaphragm.

The existence of torfolae gives us a quantifiable measurement of life. Life in the most basic of sense, that is, since wizards and mages are known to increase their torfol, but may not have ‘lived’ as much as other creatures.
## Measuring the Torfol
The amount of torfol something has (like the amount of mass) is Ffalds (ф), which uses the ffald unit ф. This is a base unit for Hidology. Doised is a measurement of density for the Hidol. It is often expressed as ρ<sub>д</sub> and has base units фm<sup>3</sup>. Doised can be expressed in this metric manner, but usually it is expressed using relative doised units.

The average adult human who does no magic has a torfol of 0.8 Ffalds, and a torfol volume of 0.072m<sup>3</sup> in doised normal environments (basically central ralean). This would mean doised in a normal environment would be 0.0576 ffalds per metre cubed. This is 1 relative doised unit, or rdu (sometimes expressed with a P).

Many microorganisms don’t have a torfol, but they might attract the subatomic idea of what torfol is made from. Torfolae begin appearing on creatures when they approach the size of a paedocrypis, which have mass 25 milligrams.
# The Heiine Model
The Heiine Model is a model described by the Ralean scholar Heiine Mespil to attempt to describe and mathematise the observable phenomenon of torfol coherence, torfol repulsion, torfol compression, and torfol overlap. The central idea of the Heiine model is that the Torfol’s surface consists of points (called “Heiine points” by peers. These points exert forces on other points that cause them to move. 
### Torfol radius
Something that pops up once in a while, especially in the Heiine model, is the idea that the torfol is ideally a sphere. While this is true for torfol larger than the life that it is surrounding, when a torfol is about the same size or smaller, it somewhat “clings” to the surface of the living thing that’s creating it. Regardless, the ideal torfol tries to be a sphere, and the exact size of this sphere depends on how many ffalds the torfol is and the doised of the hidol around it. 

The actual calculation is for the neutral radius of the torfol, which is given with the torfol mass and the doised (density of the hidol).

$${r}_{τ}=\sqrt[3]{\frac{3ф}{4\pi{p}_{\text{д}}}}$$


Where ф is the strength of the torfol and ρ<sub>д</sub> is the density of the Hidol.

For an example calculation, r<sub>τ</sub> for a normal person’s torfol under normal hidol density would give about 1.5

You will see r<sub>τ</sub> in equations that don’t want to write the full thing.
### Heiine Force Constant
Throughout Heiine’s equations, the presence of a single force constant pops up. This constant, known as the Heiine Force Constant and denoted as И, is a value that hasn’t quite been determined by Hidologists. For all we know, the constant could be different for every equation. What we do know is that it is somewhere inbetween 0.44 and 0.82. 
## Torfol Coherence
Torfol coherence is the observation that the torfol generally stays a fixed distance away from the focal point. In Heiine’s model, the points are fixed to their distance by a “spring” force that attempts to equalise the doised inside the torfol and the Doised outside the torfol. Heiine proposed a  function for the force on points, called the Heiine spring function:

$$F_k(d)=И(d-r_τ)-ρ_{\text{д}}v$$

For some explanation:

- d is the distance from the focal point
- И is the Heiine force constant
- r<sub>τ</sub> is the neutral radius of the hidol.
- ρ<sub>д</sub>, the doised in the area
- v, the velocity of the heiine point

The first part of this equation is formally the spring part, whereas the second part ρ<sub>д</sub>v is a dampening force that acts on the points that’s strength depends on the doised.
## Torfol Repulsion
Torfol repulsion describes how the surfaces of two torfol repel each other. This is observably a simple inversely proportional function. This equation is known as the Heiine Repulsion Equation, and acts between all Heiine points on the surface of a torfol.

$$F_R(d)=\frac{фИ}{d^2}$$

Where:

- d is the distance from the focal point
- И is the Heiine force constant
- Ф is the strength of the torfol the Heiine point represents
# Planar Theory
A Torfol is a type of mass in the Hidol. Planar theory describes how this mass produces phenomena, describing a property of the Hidol called the Planar Vector, also called torfol vector or just vector. This vector contains a magnitude, which is a value often attributed to the mass (or strength) of the torfol, and a direction. The planar vector is theoretically any-dimensional, but for any practical and conceptual purposes it is a three-dimensional space.

The vector is often represented with (μ, θ, φ) where μ is magnitude, θ is latitudinal rotation and φ is longitudinal rotation. For adaptive torfol and for the purposes of enchanting, often only the angle is described in the form (θ, φ). The angle position (0, 0) is set defined as the default vector for adaptive torfol, which is a type that normally forms around large, conscious creatures such as people. The angle is measured in milliradians.

The physical difference between types of torfol is their vector direction. Different 'types' lie within certain vector boundaries. The properties of torfol are usually physically undetectable, with the exception being torfol reactions. These vary between different types of torfol, but reaction types are theoretically transferrable between types.

Categories for types of vectors have been created in line with classical hidol theory. These categories are adaptive, reactive, and expansive. In modern hidology, these groups are defined by vector boundaries.

The adaptive category describes vectors around the origin. This category spans all directions within 100 miliradians of the origin. Adaptive torfol are the torfol seen around conscious beings, and many people have cultuvated the skill of 'rotating' the vector, which can cause an enchanting reaction.

The reactive category covers a circular range of 160 miliradians radius on the centre point (653, 0). The centre point of this category defines the 0 angle longitude. This category contains the vector directions of most torfol, centred around plants, small creatures, organs, and every living thing from a certain size. They are most often used to cause spell reactions using claps.

The expansive category covers a circular range of 20 miliradians radius on the centrepoint (224, 267). Gemstones often have torfol in this category, and spell reactions using them cause the emergence of golems through casting. 

These three classifications cover the vectors of all known torfol. Theoretically speaking, there are torfol vectors outside of this but there is no physical precedent for how these would act. Some speculation involves untouched categories used by the god. There is a dubious account of an extremely powerful wizard altering his adaptive torfol vector past the boundaries defined by the adaptive category, and his body collapsing in on itself. There is theoretical speculation that the vector contains more than three angular dimensions.
