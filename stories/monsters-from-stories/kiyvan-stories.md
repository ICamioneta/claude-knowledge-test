As Daklyn walked down the west coast of DeMoch, he came to the peninsula now controlled by Nixiana. Before the settleing of Ouranx refugees, however, this land was controlled by the Kithral Kaer. The Kithral Kaer managed to control the entire area surrounding lake Daini until the establishment of the DeMoch Tribunal, where Adal saw it fit to place that land in the hands of Daenlle. Kaerllan was compensated by a lot during the partition of DeMoch, as they were granted control over a large portion of Dithas despite not having a border with it.

Although the culture there has since been erased, fragments of it were kept by other cultures, especially by Daklyn on his journeys. In Wastelife, he decided to populate the peninsula by a Kaemuz subculture called the "Kiyvan", and their country was to be called "Vania", using the Eresonian ending for a passive nation -nia and the last syllable of their culture's name.

Vania itself is just the region beyond and including a fictional mountain range. Before this region, Daklyn placed a large wetland where the water from the lake seeps down into the sea. In reality, the peninsula is pretty flat land, neither marshy nor hilly. The legends from the region all seem to play on a fear of the horison, or fear of something right in front of you that you can't see. A certain strain of legends go another way, playing on how the land is low and feels out-of-place. In those legends, the land is but a thin layer of dirt and rock sitting atop a deep ocean, and there are certain oceanic creatures that decide to poke up from the dirt and attack people.

Many of these legends Daklyn used to populate the Kiyvan plains, and many were used for the wetlands and the kiu strip as well. These two legend types have lead to a thematic split between the region's indigenous creatures.

The ddogoli is a creature from the first type of legend, it's name literally translating to "no sight" (Ddogolni, ouranxised) It's a sort of snake-dog, sharing many physical features of both. It's a scaled creature, often coloured brown, with a snake-like head held low, but a body structure similar to that of a dogs. The mouth still dons the sharp inward-facing teeth that are as long as hands when extended, but also contains several other sharp teeth made for ripping apart flesh and swallowing it without much chewing. Its body is not quite a tube but similar, a ribcage persisting past the chest and down the entire body. The legs are somewhat dog-like in shape, but allow for the body to sit close to the ground while moving, by moving the thigh joint outwards at a horizontal angle to the body. In legend, the ddogoli cannot be seen by those it looks at, its eyes allegedly concealing the rest of the body. The tale is a warning to always watch out for your friends. In Wastelife, the ddogoli is present in the sparsest parts of the Kiyvan planes, and the Kiu wetlands & strip.

The saxym (literally "not there") is an elusive creature, one that isn't aggressive nor friendly. It's a cyote-like animal with a beak. The creature is told to only be seen on accident, as its a herbivore. Sometimes it appears in the corner of your eyes, perhaps watching from the grass, but the moment its seen it scurries off. In legend, the Saxym is supposed to be a play on that thing that watches you. In Wastelife, the Saxym are slightly different in that they might attack, but only if you can't see them. They contain very good ingredients too, but you have to fight them without your sight as the second they're seen they dissapear.

The Dsaelg in Wastelife is quite differnet to the Dsaelg of Kaer lore. In Kaer lore, the Dsaelg is a flying monster that looks like a human head with bat wings. However, when it wants, it can project itself a huge neck reaching down to the floor and onto a fake human body. This is an illusion, and it only persists as long as the viewer doesn't see the head, so all they notice is a body with a very tall neck. In Wastelife, however, the whole idea of a floating human head has been dropped in favor of a giant centipede that has as many body portions as any other centipede, but can make it look like it goes on forever, wrapping around the landscape. These critters occupy the Kiyvan planes and mountains, and not the wetlands nor the strip.

Next on the list of creatures from that sector of barogate lore is the Hoxelgolne (name from temporary sight "Hoxyzeddgolni") While a saxym can only be fought when blind, and the fighting can be ended whenever just by seeing it, the Hoxelgolne blinds its victims. It's described in Kaer lore to be a deer-like creature with six legs and a spider-like mouth. When it hunts, it spits a toxin that blinds a person no matter where it hits. The toxin seeps in the skin and somehow affects the eyesight. In Kaer lore it's more of a story for the blind to tell, like how they fought the creature blinded and survive. It's also a bit of a horror story, scaring children. I'm sure the village blinds would like to hear their terrified cries. Eitherway, in Wastelife it's all the same, except sight isn't really taken permanently. The sight takes weeks to recover alone, but with any kind of healing potion it recoveres either instantly or within a day. The recovery is slow, but eventually regular eyesight is restored.

The final creature on the list of vision-related Kaer monsters is the Xzeddrye (name from temporary person "Hoxyzedd rynen"). In Kaer lore, it describes a wild person who wanders like a zombie until it hunts. The lore describes this person blinking in and out of reality, able to make deep incisions with its sharp claws only when it's in this reality. It doesn't choose when it wants to attack, either. In the legends this creature was told as being on the verge of Life and the Works. Every time it blinks it feels pain. The creature was used as a threat, like a "this is what you'll become if you don't eat your greens!" but instead of lack of vegetables it would be lack of humanity because the person who becomes the creature murdered someone. Eitherway, in Wastelife it's appearence is simmilar, humanoid but less Muz. It's body is paler, head completely different, and limbs much different to distinguish it from the Muz. Its head is still ape-like, but the jaw is much larger and usually open, either because it's screaming in pain or because it wants to eat you.
