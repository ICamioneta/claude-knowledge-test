On DeMoch, Llyhai might not be an actual ashen wasteland (although it does look like it in many places), but one feature rings true about the region - Ich Mena Re and Hidoiad itself.

Mount Hidoiad is an active volcano, one of several in the region. Hidoiad towers above the rest. When the kithral of old sailed above the barren desert, they came across Hidoiad, and several stories about it were told. These stories were reiterated, and eventually passed on to Daklyn, who wrote them into Wastelife.

First is a concept that Wastelife is familiar with: the heart of Hidoiad. Legend tells that if you step into the heart of hidoiad you will be met with your own godlike ascension, and gain an infinite playground to satisfy you for the rest of eternity.

The Lords of Ich Mena Re
For those who adventured the entire mountain range, they saw the several active volcanoes and made up stories of the lords of them. The lords of Ich Mena Re acted like a pantheon in many places, each character having their own set of stories and dramas. In the legends, they are genderless monarchs whose bodies are made of rock (but obviously they can appear human to meddle with regular people) and bound with fire.

Daklyn appropriated these into Wastelife. In fact, it's a bit of a secret so try to keep it down. There are fourteen volcanoes in Ich Mena Re, five of which are very small and one of which is Hidoiad. The Lords of Ichmenre in Wastelife are as described, beings made entirely of stone and bound by fire. Their size reflects the prowess of their volcanoe, the five smaller ones being no larger than your typical Muz or Dos. The regular ones tower over buildings, while the Monarch of Ichmenre is taller than the tallest spires. In Wastelife, they are known to have their own drama, but most importantly they command their fiery armies. many of Llyhai's most dangerous creatures come from the Lords of Ichmenre. The Ashmuz, who are foot soldiers. The Kaddkenf, which are rock beasts who are somewhat bat-shaped in proportions but they don't have wings. You have the Burnspitters, who are beings of fire who throw magma at their enemies. There's the airborne Kaddedan, who are like giant vultures with a Lovyn's wings. I could go on, but this really doesn't matter unless you're in Kusao, in Kyenel next to Ichmenre, or in Ichmenre or Llyhai.
