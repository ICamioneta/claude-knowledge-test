Wastelife as a fantasy fiction is buried as the second layer of fiction underneath another layer of fiction. Concepts from Wastelife are not based upon those found in Earth, but instead on those found in Xeiat, the realistic world in which Wastelife was originally written. For this reason, creatures in Wastelife are not inspired by stories from Earth but instead stories from Xeiat, which in turn were inspired by stories from Earth. It is this extra layer of abstraction that I believe makes Wastelife a truly unique world, unlike any other fantasy in existence.

- [Emeniaddol](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/emeniaddol) is fundamentally a story about how everyone in a society is important and has their impact.
- [Kithral Kaer stories](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/kithral-kaer-stories) cover topics including drought and bad harvests.
- [Kiyvan stories](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/kiyvan-stories) are thematically about the horizon and things that are hard to perceive. 
- [Landith and the Caterpillars](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/landith-and-the-caterpillars) is a bittersweet story about death and reincarnation
- [Portal on a Castle](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/portal-on-a-castle) was a dream I once had and kinda wanted to add to Wastelife in some capacity
- [Sheres Do](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/sheres-do) are about new flora and contains potential development for Raikeor.
- [Llyhai stories](/wastelife/stories/monsters-from-stories/llyhai-stories) is basically just the first development of the Lords of Ich Mena Re.
