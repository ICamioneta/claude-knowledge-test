the Lindi from Wastelife are a caterpillar-like people who live willingly in neolithic conditions. Their inclusion in Wastelife comes from an old Yari story that follows a character called Landith.

---

Landith was wandering through the forest when he uncovers a caterpillar that can speak. This caterpillar explains himself to Landith as a wizard, and welcomes Landith to the society that he participates in. The wizard caterpillar shrinks Landith down to his size, and Landith is led by him to the caterpillar civilisation. 

The wizard caterpillar says to Landith that he actually went out that day to die. He says that every caterpillar in this society has their time, when they know they must leave and die. He explains that everybody is a little scared, but they know that they must go outside and follow their instincts, and cease to be. Landith questions this, asking how could a caterpillar be so ready to accept their death? The caterpillar shrugs. He says his last goodbye to his family, and sets out with Landith again. 

After the wizard returns Landith to his normal size, the caterpillar cocoons. Landith stays with the cocoon until it emerges as a butterfly. Landith attempts to speak to the butterfly, celebrating its emergence from the cocoon. But the butterfly asks if he knows Landith. The butterfly has, in fact, forgotten all about caterpillar society, and is eager to be part of whatever Butterfly society has to hold.

---


This is a spoken word story mostly. This is what story tellers would remember.
### main plot points:
- Landith wanders through a forest
- A caterpillar is revealed to him in some way, usually by him lifting a leaf.
- The caterpillar talks to Landith, surprising him.
- The caterpillar says he is a wizard and asks Landith if he wants to experience Caterpillar society
- Landith agrees, and the caterpillar shrinks Landith down
- The caterpillar shows Landith around caterpillar civilisation
- The caterpillar, while showing him Caterpillar civilisation, explains to Landith that every caterpillar has a time when they must leave caterpillar civilisation and go out to die. The caterpillar explains that he actually went out to die that day.
- No-one knows why this is, but it is part of the natural cycle of life.
- The caterpillar leaves caterpillar civilisation again and returns to the forest
- The caterpillar says goodbye to Landith, growing him back to normal size.
- The caterpillar cocoons
- Landith stays with the caterpillar until the caterpillar emerges from the cocoon as a butterfly
- Landith celebrates the caterpillar's emergence, but the butterfly doesn't know anything about Landith
- Landith tries to explain Caterpillar society, but the butterfly has forgotten all about it.
- The butterfly shows eagerness to engage in whatever Butterfly society has to hold.
