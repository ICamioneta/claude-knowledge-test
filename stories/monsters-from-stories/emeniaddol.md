So let me tell you a story of a village called Emeniaddol.

Emeniaddol is a busy town. In one part of the town, miners sweat as their steel picks crash against a quarry's rocks. In another part of town, dock workers sprint to hoist sails, load ships, and push them from the docks. In the middle of town, the markets bustle with chatter as the townspeople look for fresh products and haggle with prices. On one edge of town, people gather around a fire talking their hearts out. 

One day as someone was walking from the fire to the middle of the town, they went missing. Next time people gathered around the fire, they noticed the missing person, and this made them concerned. But the town was still busy and these people had work to be done. 

As they worked, they couldn't shake the missing person from their minds. What had happened to them? Should they go looking for them? What if they got kidnapped? they spent so long thinking of these questions that they sold much less at the market than they should have.

The next day, the market merchants couldn't buy all of the goods from the dock workers because they hadn't sold all of yesterday's goods. Because of this, the dock workers didn't fully unload all of the ships. The miners had always mined enough stone to fill the ships, but since the ships were still full of today's goods, the miners couldn't get rid of all of their stone.

Because the miners couldn't get rid of all of their stone, they didn't get as much money as they should have gotten. Because they couldn't pay the merchants for all of their goods, the merchants had more goods left over. Because the merchants sold less of their goods, less of the ship's goods were given to the merchants. Because of this, the ships had less room for stone, meaning the miners couldn't sell all of their stone meaning the miners had even less money

Soon Emeniaddol came to a standstill. no-one could do anything because the merchants were too busy thinking of a lost person, and because of this the dock workers and miners couldn't make money to give to the merchants. Life in the town was sad for a while as no-one could do anything but wonder about this lost person.

But one day, that person came back. They only went on holiday, but everyone at the fire was too busy to pay attention to them when they said they would be on holiday. Now that this person was back, all of the merchants were so much happier. They sold their goods at a much lower rate that the miners could afford. Because of this, the docks opened up again, the miners could sell all of their rocks, and the town was back to normal.
