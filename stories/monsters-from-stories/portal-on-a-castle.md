The portal stood on the largest balcony of the castle, overlooking the burnt, war-torn lands. The castle's massive stone towers climbed even higher into the sky, and the Dragon lurked among them.

The dragon was unlike any other, taking more the shape of a gecko with short arms. But still, it flew. Its scales were as thick as the castle walls, portruding from one another making the dragon's skin look like giant steps. Its head featured two massive false eyes, hiding its small real eyes just beneath. These real eyes were orange with fire.

The portal was a doorway, and through it another balcony. Not of the castle, but to a massive, dark, underground place. Another realm, perhaps? You could see through it nothing but another balcony, unlike the castle it had a black floor, and instead of crenalations it had sick black fences.

Walking through the portal I faced my first foe: a weak lich.
The balcony overlooked a massive plane of lava. The room was gargantuan: as large as a city. Directly in front lay a symphony of small cubical islands, populated by the monsters of this place. The balcony sit forwards in the room; the highest point by far as the ceiling was visible. It was extended from the wall.

Behind and underneath the balcony stood a stranger structure. A huge corridor against the wall, with massive doors on either side. But instead of walls, it had a specific arrangement of pillars. They shot up about ten metres. They alternated heights. Against the corridor, the pillars alternated from tall to short, with the short being about three metres shorter than the tall. The pillars were on a grid: three extended out from the corridor with the second out being short and the furthest immitating the short-tall pattern of the first. After two short pillars in the second row, there would be no pillars, leaving an infinite abyss. The faces of these pillars would give you a 5 metre cube of space to walk on.

Directly below and behind the balcony was a massive platform, extending far either side of the balcony. More cubical structures stood without meaning. Two of these cubical structures slowly rose into the air, what looked like the faces of automata on top of them. These were gargantuan automata: as tall as a clocktower.

I assessed the room's dangers.
Liches were common. These awful beings come in many themes, all decipherable by their mask. They are a mesh of torn and burnt rags flowing from the mask. Their arms are long and skeletal. Its hard to tell if these creatures even have bodies, as tehy don't walk but float across the ground. They can't fly, though, as I see them climbing the arrangement of pillars.
The automata come in three forms. The first, a human-like torso above a sphere. This torso holds a crossbow and a sword. The sphere itself is as big as I am, each crossbow bolt as long as my arm.
The second are the guardians: purely human-like and made to kill. Thrice my height, they don a variety of weaponry on their artificial arms. The third I reffer to are the gargantuans: they had just come out of the floor. Their heads seem to be the same as Guardians, but their bodies are made out of the same cubical thing that makes up the room. They are humanoid, made in a similar shape to the guardians.
Littering the room are a number of less dangerous beings, like scamps and demons. Not that they aren't dangerous, but comparetively liches can demolish entire regions.
