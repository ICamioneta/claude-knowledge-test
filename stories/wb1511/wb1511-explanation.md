Okay so this document is just an explanation of what the fuck is going on here. 

It's currently 2am on a sunday. Primary thinking time. 

I found a file called wb1511.txt in my stories folder. I had forgotten about it. Not only since the obsidian port, but way before, sometime after I initially wrote it. I wrote it almost exactly 1 year ago from now (19/11/2023 according to my laptop backup). I've put the unedited contents of this file into [wb1511](/wastelife/stories/wb1511/index)

I probably initially wrote it as a light-hearted story with a particular setting and introducing some concepts of Wastelife through a Kaemuz who for some reason doesn't know this stuff already.

Opening it in modernity and re-reading it, I have completely recontextualised the piece with my modern understanding of Wastelife.

Then, I hyper-analysed, analysing my analysis and the idea that only I could recontextualise my old work like this. I explore what it is to be a world builder, and my deep and internalised loneliness.

I now kinda want to make a youtube video on this. It would be a neat way to introduce people to Wastelife, then explore deeper into Wastelife, and then finish on a deep exploration on what it means to be a worldbuilder using my own psyche as a case study.

I have written the script, so all I really need now is review, filming, and extra-production (music and props)
