Notes on Characters

Ria's ideas and morals are in conflict to Iana.
Siai is going to be built up by the group and challenge her traditionalist views.
Corvan is going to be supportive and nice, and ultimately prove to be a powerful leader and a good combatant.
Gareey is head-first and resourceful.
Eariph is too humble, and must learn to take credit for her successes.
Hodeso is a subversive person who is in cahoots with Ria, but won't reveal it until the last second.

Ideas for Character Devlopment

Ria is eventually going to appear villanous, telling tales of misdeeds as if they were accomplishments and introducing conflict within the group, potentially performing villanous acts like being rude to natives, or even abandoning the group.

Siai must come to realise that her Master is not the only important one, and that she needs to see herself as a person rather than a tool. She will become independent, and promises to demand better respect from her Master.

Corvan is a wide-jawed adventurer who is just starting off. He holds 1 adventure from Cronar to the city they began in, and he loves the event-filled lifestyle. He'll come across as jealous of Ria and Iana, and he holds them in high regard.

Eariph is there for a purely scientific purpose, but even though she's part of a highyly acclaimed underwater univercity in the underwater city, she doesn't actually know much. The others assume she's smart.

Hodeso doesn't say much, because he has nothing to say. His story of why he's there doesn't hold up because a small village wouldn't see demand for such a rare ingredient. In reality, he's a bit of a sociopath who bands with Ria if it ever comes to that.
