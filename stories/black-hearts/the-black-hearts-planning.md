# Origins & motifs

So let's start at the beginning. Why did all the characters sign up for the expedition? What exactly do they seek to gain from it?

## Iana Easa

First, Iana, who is the story's first person. It's important to give her character because i've come to hear that Everymen are kinda boring. To think of her character, i've first got to think of her role in her own adventuring group.

Iana Easa is a mage, and the only one in her group. There’s also a wizard who acts as the group’s leader, a sword & shield, a spearman, and a crossbowman. Iana is the person in the group that tends to push things forwards. While other members would rather sit back and think, she dives headfirst into doing what she can. The only time she’d wait is if she’s genuinely helpless.

In combat, she’s more of a support specialist using illusion-based claps to make combat easier. She’d use fear claps at the beginning, trying to wane the aggressors into yielding or otherwise giving up. She also keeps sleep, muddle, paralyze, and several detriment effects on hand as well. All of this isn’t in attempt to hurt people, as Iana prefers to avoid, but to end the combat with as little casualties as possible, on both sides. She works with her wizard in this regard, although the wizard does know plenty of damaging enchantments.

Iana’s group came to the city with her group just as a stop. They mainly operate in Lale Darie and it’s nearby areas so they are familiar with its cities and towns. Then, she overheard a conversation about the black heart ingredient, of which she wants greatly for its strong fear property.

## Ria

Ria acts as the antagonist, making clearly villainous decisions. But, to her, she’s the most important person. She, like Iana, comes from an adventuring group. She’s a mage as well, they’re all mages.

Ria’s group acts differently. They all have a “every man for themselves” mentality, which would make them disorganised and defeatable if it wasn’t for their individual prowess. When they do fight, they do it together with glee. Ria’s a damage dealer, as are all in her group. Each of them have small aspects of other classes though, as they each often need to fend for themselves. In the end, it’s a group of individuals only together because they’re less likely to die.

Either way Ria actually does want a black heart. It’s got an overwhelmingly strong air property that can topple buildings. Although, after finding Hodeso on the journey, she might just take more than she came for.

## Hodeso

Hodeso is a bit of a sidekick to the antagonist, Ria. In the story, it’s not explained that they know each other. That’s because Hodeso doesn’t like to reveal his intentions, even if they’re mostly innocent. Hodeso’s got a similar mindset to Ria, although he’s not as loud and boasty about it. He’s more a scheming and knifing foe.

Hodeso is from a small island in the north, and he actually knows about Siai. In fact, although he does intend to get the Black Hearts and sell them to a trader in a town, his secondary objective is to kidnap Siai, which he’ll think of doing after they’re closer to society. He won’t get much character development..

Hodeso’s packed with disabling spells, namely lightning claps and the odd sleep clap to keep his opponent’s unconscious.

## Siai

In the normal story, Siai is the “employee” of Nilif Tenia, but it does seem that their relationship is significantly closer, so I might make her the adoptive daughter of Nilif instead. Either way, she’s on the adventure because Nillif wants some black hearts, and trusts Siai to get them for him. Siai’s relationship with her father is rather important in this, because in the original script she seems a bit brainwashed.

Nilif’s very powerful and influential, but an authoritarian father. He’s controlling of Siai, and generally she only does what he wants her to. This time in particular, he trusted her to get some black hearts, giving her a few rudimentary claps to get going. He did this also because he’s got some important meetings coming up, and wanted Siai out the house.

Throughout the story, as this is one of Siai’s first interactions with anyone, she’ll be reclusive, apologetic, a people pleaser. She’ll continue to have a submissive disposition with her father, but Iana, Eariph, and Gareey will seek to change that. They’ll subtly encourage her independence, so much so that she’ll carve her own path away from Nilif when she returns.

## Eariph

Eariph is from the Odea Yemon, which is an underground city with the largest university in the Mortal Realm. She’s smart but humble. She’s a scholar and is mostly on this adventure to take notes not just about the Black Hearts but on everything, the Vugaix and the Heabe. She’s a bit of an underdog at her university because her mindset doesn’t play well with the study focus of the university. When others are studying, she’s itching to get out and do some practical work.
