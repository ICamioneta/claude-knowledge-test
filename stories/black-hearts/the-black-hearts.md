Of Iana Easa, Domuz Mage

"I don't need a redcrest salmon, they hardly react with ceirpeth werdd at all!" I exasperated, this tired shopkeeper had been trying to convince me to buy her overpriced magery ingredients.

"You've got this all wrong, their 51.6 44.3 tensor perfectly aligns with ceirpeth's 32.7 44.8. It'll do you well in the field, the science is there." The shopkeeper responded. 

"Don't bother with your tensor talk around me, I know what I've tried! I'm not getting the salmon, if you don't have Tincrer Tendril or Gerthiliumin then I'll find somewhere else to source them." I hade little time and little patience to take this long to shop between stops on my and my party's adventures.

"We do have Gerthiliumin, one eighths a bag." The shopkeeper said excitedly. 

"Well I'll have 6 bags some of that then" I said reaching into my purse. 

Leaving the shop I couldn't help feel defeated. Sourcing ingredients for every adventure can be such a challenge, I'd often hope I had the time to go and get them myself. I wandered the round cut brick roads of Espetha. As useless as its markets were, at least the town was scenic. I had left the shop into a public square, where the blue tinted rocks came up into a tower of a fountain in the middle, spraying waters several metres down from the top where it would splash people standing too close to the base. 

I was entering the next street when i overheard a very specific word at the end of presumably a full sentence, "Black Hearts". 

I stopped dead in my tracks. 

I listened.

"Why didn't you go?" One conversational voice in the crowd said to another.

"I'm just a bit washed ashore, you know? The publication's soon, not to mention my partner's medical condition" the other said back. I would not be getting my answers from eavesdropping.

"Hi, " I said ramming myself into the conversation, "I couldn't help but hear you mention black hearts. It wasn't metaphorical, was it? You were talking about the ingredient?"

"Er, hi." The man stumbled. "Yes, there was an expedition I saw posted in the forum, I..."

"An expedition? It was in the forum. I'll have a look." I turned quickly and almost shot off.

"Wait, no, it was leaving today. Three forum post won't be there." 

"Oh." I said, defeated.

"You might be able to find them in the docks? I'm not sure when they'll leave but they do this every year or so." 

Off I went. Dodging and weaving through people in these crowded streets.
