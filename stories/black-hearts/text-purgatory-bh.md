# Rush
I was wandering Esphetha’s markets, the noise of bountiful Domuz trade echoing from the blue-tiled floor, when I started listening in on a random conversation. It was a passing interest until I heard talk of the Black Hearts. I knew them to be a rare magery ingredient, powerful and notoriously hard to acquire. 

“Why didn’t you go?” one said to the other.

“I’m just a little backed up right now, I’ve got the publication, not to mention my partner here…” The other responded.

I butted in.
“Hi! Sorry, I overheard you talking about the Black Hearts? An expedition did you say? What more can you tell me?” I intervened. Both were surprised.

“Oh, hello, sorry. You heard right, but they’re leaving the next hour.”

“Do you know who’s doing the trip?”

“Yes, a Colsens called Gareey, he’s done a couple trips like this.”

“Where?” I asked.

“If you rush, you should be able to find them at the docks.”

“Okay, thank you!” I said, making a hasty escape.

I weaved out of the central market, clashing shoulders with the crowds on my way out. I ran onto the wide streets, walled like a corridor with tall houses either side. Running further I made my way down to a tavern, burst myself through the door, and promptly walked to the table at which my group was sitting. I sat myself down.

“Venthi!” I addressed my group’s leader, “There’s an expedition for the Black Hearts and I have to go.”

“hi, the Black Hearts?”

“Yes, an ingredient. It’s more than worth it.”

“Right, well I’ve already sussed out a job for us, it should be easy and we should manage without you, so feel free to go yourself. Here, “ he rummaged a coin purse, “take these and good luck Iana!”

“Thank you, thank you, thank you!” I slid away waving.

As quick as I ran here, I ran back to the market. I bought some dried foods in a pinch. I ran down another street, approaching the tall and decorated walls of the city. I went through the city’s gigantic eastern gate. My shoes clopped on the paved and winding stone road. The docks were in sight: a geometric mix of Esphetha’s iconic blue-coloured stone. Huge and impressive ships, embarking on all types of journeys. Then, a smaller cargo vessel with a Colsens and five others talking on its pier.

As I was running, they began boarding. I was approaching their pier when one of the sailors unmoored the ship. I was nearly out of breath when I jumped onto the ship, causing some confusion in the sailors. Landed on the deck, and out of breath, I was confronted by the Colsens and his group.

"Hello" the Colsens said.

“I…” I took a breath. “I’m a mage and I…” and another breath “I couldn’t pass the opportunity.” I caught my breath.

“Ooh, another!” a Lemuz said.

“What’s your name?” Gareey inquired.

“Iana.” I said under my breath.

“I’m Corvan, and I’m sure you’ll have the chance to meet everyone else.” the Lemuz said.

“Gareey.” Gareey the Colsens said.

“Hi, I’m Eariph!” A Lephiolite Domuz said.

“Siai.” A Gazite gave a repressed wave. The other two present were Oenesite, amidst their own conversations.

The sailors finished unfastening the ship. My arrival was tight they still took their time.  I took the moment to farewell the City of Esphetha. The boat floated away from its pier and eventually out of the dock’s huge gate. The walls of the city and those surrounding the dock looked like several consecutive pillars connected by thin walls, each about fifteen metres tall off the ground. Atop the dock’s gatehouses shone our glamorous flag.
