The Necromancer is called Tiebeo Desaniete, but nobody really cares about him so carrying on

There are no undead up the stairs as if there were there would have to be more than before and they would have to pose an additional challenge to the group, and they cannot really handle a challenge just before the "boss room" so there are no undead there to give them a breather

Tiebeo is a mad necromancer. There is no real disguising this. In the origional he's a bit common and comic. "As you've been so kind to show up here unannounced, do come in" is a bit stereotypical, so I have to mix up everything about him.

Tiebeo does wear a dark robe, but that's really just because robes are common wizarding clothes and Tiebeo wants to hide in the shadows of the fort, so bright robes wouldn't be too great.

His staff is described as enchanted "with a round gem placed on the top". In function, this enchanted staff allows the necromancer's self to move to the nearest conduit (within a range, not that it matters). This action is not too dissimilar to something powerful gemcasters can do, so it only really makes sense that Tiebeo is using a similar method. A staff to do it might be required, but the inclusion of a gem stone doesn't make too much sense.

Instead, his staff should really include an enchanted trinket on the top that Tiebeo made himself that allows for this transmissibe power.

Secondly, he should really have some attacks of his own. He could use the staff, but as it does not move with him when he transfers, someone else could pick it up so he wouldn't be great in close quarters combat. Magic, however, makes sense. His primary methods of attack are going to be wizard rings and other enchantments because he's a necromancer who already has many of the wizarding skills.

Onto Tiebeo's tactics. In the origional, once the group open the door to him, and after they say what they're here for, he throws the boy to them. I reckon this is actually quite a fair tactic, as he is just about to call his conduits and their groups to the area. Giving the boy to them would mean they have to work to protect him as well as themselves. If he held the boy, he wouldn't be able to finish turning him into a conduit, and the group wouldn't have to look after an extra person. 

Then, he does call the hordes, but the group retreats into the room to bottleneck the hordes through the two doors. Maris closes the stairs door and braces himself against it. They are initially fighting undead coming from the hall, so Maris has time to do this while Guiese braces the other doorway with his shield.
