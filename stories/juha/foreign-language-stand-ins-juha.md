Lesliye after the Shory contains modified Picard french as a stand-in for Seaspeak.

### Lesliye in Eswin
Lesliye in Eswin contains modified Archi to stand in for Bharkent, here's the phrases.

Stop! Who is\[be] here?
Oucis! Cyiri id is?'

Greetings! I am\[be] Meibin. I know Codia. Here, I have a pendant\[thing].
Jarsami bos! Mum id Meibin. Mum sini Codia. Is, mum i hecllona.

Who are\[be] these\[your] people?
Cyiri id usu ʕalaam?

Friends I have with me. They are\[be] good people. I trust them.
Dosurscellu mum i lullaq mum. Jaamur id hibaat ʕalaam. Mum icyorcis jaamur.

If they break our trust, you break our trust.
Baalki jaamur li as uluttu wixcul, un li as uluttu wixcul.

un cyashas id Meibin. Mumat t'avlas c'it un, osgwn dannase Herews Codia bosiri un ryara acllis.

Yes, that is\[be] me.
hu, tuw id mum.

A war is coming. Foul, ruccus, all parts of Ralean will be part of\[in] it. Eswin is not safe.
anx id aclliscyish. Iqit'uttu. Hapucapur. Obsi ditalurces Ralean ryara id dital qʕit jem. Eswin ryara id zaral.

Understood. Goodbye
Jaq'an. Qʕes.
