This document is supplementary for [Juha](/wastelife/stories/juha/index)

I'm removing and re-writing a lot of the foundational sauce to clear things up a little, to _sauté the sauce_ if you will.

The story has five main characters who I've already written the key moment in their lives during which they decide to become an _adventurer_, by perceived necessity or just wanderlust. I've also decided on the main setting for the first story, as well as set up _loads_ of potential for a prequel with Serdeo and Meibin in Ereso. I've also generally laid out the structure of these stories. 

They come in "books". The first book I'm not going to write right now as it's just Serdeo, Meibin, and some other characters some of which survive onto the second book for a bit like Yosher who serves some undecided but important role in book 1. For some light planning, Yosher's current involvement in book 2 is as a simple friendship with Meibin and Serdeo, but he leaves on political grounds _despite_ allegedly joining during an Ereso arc. His political leanings would have to be vehemently present in book 1 and a major cause of group conflict.

Also Ethenri doesn't show up until book 3, so basically I'm just writing with who are by far my most interesting characters. 

# Characters
Juha, once it _actually_ forms as a S-AG, consists of five main members. I have written the pivotal short stories for each of these characters in [Shories](/wastelife/stories/juha/shories).
## Serdeo Aufenyth, Scientific Gemcaster
The general idea of Serdeo Aufenyth was a gemcaster who had managed to bring a gem golem to personhood. They come from a rather conservative academic institution in Easea (also called Easa) which is on the mainland of Oenes. As in the shory, once discovering that gem golems are capable of personhood and presenting the immediate scientific proof to the upper academic staff at their college, they reject them and the experiment as being deviate and unsuitable to modern life.

Serdeo is an academic and curious character often forgetting about the common sense. He is also logical and loving, coordinating utility and emotion.

Serdeo Aufeneth was born 3457, making them 31 as of 3488. They should be referred to as they or them.

**Dress:** Typical Oenesite dress with a chainmail layer: colourful and flowing with enough strips to be considered gambeson.
**Utility:** Serdeo brings a satchel full of gems and ingredients to cast them, as well as a reserve of the ingredients required to re-cast Kyroh. These gem golems represent his practical utility alongside Serdeo's unique logical outlook.
**Combat:** Serdeo carries a shortsword and is aware of offensive claps they can perform with the ingredients in their satchel. They are also equipped with a thumb pouch usually empty.
### Kyroh
Kyroh is the sentient gem golem Serdeo cast that got them expelled from their college. Kyroh is a Spessartine golem, cast from the Spessartine gemstone which is a Manganese Aluminum Silicate Garnet. Their golem is humanoid and most notable for their employment of thin and sharp javelin-like weapons which they can synthesise a finite number of times usually depending on the strength of the cast. 

Kyroh is essentially a child. Although their body is adult-sized (roughly 1.6m tall) they haven't been sentient for long. Kyroh is observant and usually silent, following their busy parent around and often adding nothing to conversations. 

Kyroh is emotionally intelligent and exhibits very person-like behaviour (because they are a person). Still forming their character, Kyroh performs a wide range of behaviours and gauges the responses of their friends and parent.

Kyroh was casted in 3483, making them basically a baby in book 1 and a child in book 2. They should be referred to as they, them.

**Dress:** Kyroh wears a padded tunic with metal plates sewn onto the places most likely to get hit, forgoing obvious protection like the chest and face. 
**Utility:** Serving as more of an accessory to Serdeo as Kyroh starts to understand the world, Kyroh's actual utility is limited to the materials they can conjure. 
**Combat:** Being a gem golem, Kyroh is well equipped for combat with their crystalline javelins, which they stockpile in a quiver on their side. Kyroh is also significantly more resilient to attacks, but the threat of Kyroh receding scares Serdeo. 
### Kodi
An often unmentioned character, Kodi is a pack mule that Serdeo bought when they chose to adventure after being expelled. Kyroh's often in the background being lead by Serdeo. Even I need reminders of his existence, I should probably add Serdeo or other characters interacting with Kodi often, to get him moving or to get things out of his pack.

Kodi was probably born around 2478, making him about 6 years old in book 2.
## Meibin Bitric, Eresonian Velews
When constructing Meibin, the general idea is that he was carrying a potted plant gifted to him by Codia during his service to it. His shory is fairly simplistic and short, it's an account of his last work for Codia before setting off to do his thing in Ereso. During this shory, he is characterised as ruthless and loyal. As he serves Codia, and since I want him to have this extra characterisation, he also has strong egalitarian political convictions.

During book 1, I intend to focus on his development as a character from a follower to a leader. Initially unsure about what actions he should take, often shaking and wavering under pressure. But by the end of the first book, Meibin would have learnt to take initiative before it is taken by the enemy. He becomes a strong leader and a friend to Serdeo, who is enamoured by his strong and egalitarian political convictions.

Meibin is a leader, strong in his egalitarian political convictions, ruthlessly loyal to Codia.
 
**Dress:** He wears a stylish green mobile gambeson with fairly padded trousers. 
**Utility:** He is intimately familiar with the utility of claps he can perform with ingredients in his satchel, but his utility within the group often lies in his acrobatic prowess and physical ability, as well as his leading potential.
**Combat:** Meibin is ruthless with the longsword and isn't afraid to kill for Codia, but knows it should only be done when necessary. He also has a number of offensive ingredients within his satchel that he incorporates into his longsword fighting style.

### Meibin's Plant
Meibin carried around a plant on his back when he's introduced in Book 1. However, following his and Serdeo's meetup, the plant has since taken a place on Kodi's pack. 
The plant takes little to no role during books 1 and 2 aside from Meibin's protectiveness of it and constant Ews recognition of it and what it means for Meibin's status among fellow Ews. 

## Lesliye Thenxal, Calapeth
Lesliye Thenxal built upon an idea I put into Wastelife because of the insistence of a WTTTRPG player: a ghost thing with freaky shadow abilities. This turned into the Calapeth who aren't often thought about but are generally discriminated against due to their abilities and the roles of a few bad actors in the species of person. Lesliye's shory goes over how she was abused and practically thrown out of her old village. She vows to be a new person. 

Lesliye is cautious but eager to prove herself. She struggles to feel comfortable, often trying to socially mirror people she interacts with. 

Lesliye Thenxal was born in 3465 making her 23 years old and incredibly young for a Calapeth. She is a her. 

**Dress:** Coming out of the forest she wears little but a few cloth garments she could save and put together. She relies on Kyroh's covering clothes most of the time. She would choose to have a thin, manoeuvrable, quiet cloth garment with a niqab-like face covering.
**Utility:** Lesliye's inherent abilities as a sneaky rogue come both from her past as a seclusive member of a village and her abilities as a Calapeth, who are literally light on their toes. 
**Combat:** Lesliye would rather remain out of combat as it has a high risk of revealing herself. She would rather hide and, if possible, do damage from afar.

## Arkha Vthnieolksh, Cursed Soldier
The general idea of Arkha was a cursed soldier and to explore whatever ark might emerge from it. Their shory shows little character and goes over their backstory, but their inner character is in constant conflict between a side that dreams of the joy it would get from hurting others and a side that hates killing and only wants peace. 

Arkha struggles with this curse. He struggles to manage his impulses, and reacts strongly to them. They are hesitant to talk and overthink every word.

Arkha Vthnieolksh was born in 2459 making them 29 in 3488. Their proper pronouns would be xe and xem, but in a normal English setting the gender neutral (they and them) are used.

**Dress:** Arkha dons a solid chest plate with heavily padded limbs. 
**Utility:** Despite one of their personality's faces hating it, Arkha lets go during combat and makes a ruthless, competent, and experienced fighter. 
**Combat:** Arkha carries a longsword for protection and they are the most competent fighter in the group.

### Maleisus' Curse
I will probably adress it in its own ark in a future book, but getting rid of Maleisus' curse and re-entering society is one of Arkha's main wishes. It's the main reason they stick around Juha, as Juha accepts the curse without discrimination.

## R.2: Ilcanthyn
My general idea for Ilcanthyn was the establishment of some insular clique in all positions of council. I've done a little bit of work on exactly _who_ this clique is and _how_ they managed to secure power for themselves, but their whole thing is dutifully incompatible with the political machinery they're trying to take. 

The general history is that a few guild leaders found out about the spread of councilism in their workers and artisans. Instead of combatting it as councilism is generally opposed to guild leaders like them, they went on to join it and become _incredibly_ prominent leaders on their own regard, eventually gaining seats on the local _revolutionary council_ and often acted delegate for the councilists of Ilcanthyn. Then when the union invited the guards, these leaders invited the guard captain into their plan and he too joined the council.

Come revolution, they likely had a good kerfuffle with the local dynasty heads and any _loyalists_ and likely had some struggle in securing their power. They likely just got _that_ much support from their workers and the head of guard, still holding onto state machinery of oppression, managed to gain the popular favour alongside the other people in the council. 

Predisposed with their normal work lives, the artisans and workers were contempt with wealth pilfered from the exiled nobility and other government chambers. But, some people who weren't associated with them definitely aren't happy with their complete control of the council. And sure, they're gonna make their opposition known in this _democratic councilist_ system but to be completely honest, between the guard, sulfur mines, the masons, and the forestry they themselves have enough support to hold council.
