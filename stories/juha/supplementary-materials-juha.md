# Chapter 1: What is up with the Cut?

Written by Sotweya Colienthey

Hi, I’m Sotweya Colienthey, a member of the Dirurd Institute of Local Affairs. I and my colleagues realised that knowledge on the Cut among the people of the Western Neble Imperium This pamphlet serves as a general introduction to this curious species.

First, lets address why exactly the Cut are unknown to the people of our nation. Usually, when people know of a rarer species of person, it is due to exposure. All manner of peoples come to Western Neble, from the near Lemuz to the distant Laurafall. These species make it to Western Neble through direct, unobscured pathways to the country. The barrier between Barogate and Western Neble is only the strait Jangshula. The barrier between our country and the Cut lands, however, is Codia.

To form this pamphlet, I and some of my colleagues boarded a boat to UTHEC, which borders many Cut nations and houses a population of Cut as well.

## Cut and their Subspecies

To begin, the Cut are a bipedal species of person created by the gods Riw, Adnabyd, Medr, and Cyfapheb, and Bwydo. Much like how us Nebdos were somewhat made from eagles, the Cut were made from the Cavy. They are furless creatures. Their physical bodies are almost tube-like, hosting thin arms and legs that are still strong. Their faces are long, mouths are small, and they have great cheeks with whiskers. They are short, commonly being around 1.2 metres tall. The different subspecies of cut don’t differ as much as subspecies of Dos do, as their differences are often in natural physical and mental capabilities.

There are the Diacut, Chôcut, Lescut, Cafcut, and Dancut. Each of these subspecies are mostly confined to a certain country’s borders, mostly due to liveability and exclusion from other societies. The Lescut are the most regular and populous cut subspecies
