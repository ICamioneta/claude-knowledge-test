This document is supplementary to [Juha](/wastelife/stories/juha/index)

I think I'm in an ambitious state of mind, I just revised some of the light prehistory world-building and realised how absolutely crucial all of the Silver Ruins are to the philosophy and story of Wastelife. No doubt they will be important end-game items in any tabletop roleplay experience set in Wastelife. No doubt much later in the development of other interactive media they will have a focus. But they are, by design, end-game focusses. 

My current focus on Juha is very much in the present. Their formation, the foundational introductions of Serdeo, Meibin, Lesliye, Arkha, and that fourth one who doesn't show up until after the Revolts arc. I don't really know who these characters are. That's because I care a lot more about the worldbuilding, why the revolts are happening, and the politics of Ralean and Shildo. 

I also don't know where their future takes them, but there are a lot of worldbuilding places that they should interact with. 

For example, I'm not sure if they should go to Vania. Ultimately, they won't be able to change much, but when can they ever? It might be more fitting for them to explore central and northern Ereso, and engage in growing separatist movements there. 

I've written some interesting story opportunity in Carehope and Neble, and I could maybe write a grander story set there, but it's very much home for Meibin and Arkha, especially Meibin if it's set in Carehope. 

Then, Llynllanes, where they can intermingle with the Blava/Texo stuff and have a significant change. From here, the arcs are pretty easy to connect. They do some stuff in Llynllanes, visit the other Domuz colonies, maybe do some freedom fighting in Cyrox before moving on to the Llyhai colonies and have their first proper interaction with a silver ruin. 

It would also be nice to have arcs on other continents, like Mothdao, Ouranxo, and Elvas Eldu at least. Raixzer and Annwvyn would also be nice, but I think arcs on the others would be easier to write. 

Following these, they'd be really old and seasoned adventurers, probably in their 50s. (3520!). Industrial technology in Ereso and Annwvyn would be kicking off. The intercontinental arcs would have direct interactions with the gods cementing this group's influence. Maybe they do a Crawl, collect one or two of Tanea's artefacts. At this point, they could have their final arc delving into the mystery of the silver ruins, starting in Acvidaras with Gatesweep, moving south to the Cosbon Complex and maybe they realise how big the whole thing is and how there are in tact transit networks. 

It would be fun if they took the HSR to Costrutica and then managed to re-surface there, where they find themselves "impossibly far away" when they go up ten degrees north and are 2450km away from where they began in less than a day (only 6 hours!!)

The end of Juha is probably finding the Heart, but it's hard to imagine any engaging story that surrounds this, so maybe it just ends with them reaching the lower levels of Costrutica. I can't imagine any interesting story lines involving them finding the Heart and especially not in activating it, but we'll see I guess. This is already like 10 LOTR-length epics for six people in one adventuring group, whose members likely won't remain static apart from Serdeo and Lesliye. 

# Juha Arcs List
These are my ambitions for arcs. They can and will be abandoned or completely changed.


1. The Ereso Arc. Serdeo meets Meibin in northern Ereso. Yosher must also become an important member, and his departure in the second arc must be forshadowed so it doesn't just feel like "getting rid of the old crew". Another transient member may exist. The arc likely revolves around Vevran nationalism. Raikeor may wish for Meibin not to get involved, or to support Vevran independence because of promises Vevran elites have of improving relationships and the standing of Raikeor, but Meibin will betray Raikeor's wishes in favour of his own personal beliefs, inspired by the events of the arc. Raikeor will be forgiving. (3483)
2. The Southern Revolts. The second arc and the first one I'm writing. Meibin and Serdeo meet with Lesliye, abandon Yosher, and then meet with Arkha. Meibin, partially regretting abandoning Raikeor in favour of his own beliefs, cooperates with Eswin and attempts to give it a solid position in the emerging political landscape during the southern revolts. (3484)
3. Carehope Arc. Some arc set in the cult of Carehope, a country-sized cult. I have no idea what the exact plot would be, but it's a good place to introduce the religious worldbuilding and perhaps show the Eternal's interest in Juha to forshadow their later collaboration. (3486)
4. Llynllanes Arc. The Archipelago of Llynllanes, part of the larger Raidal island chain, has a political story line revolving around the colonialism of the Domuz Republic. They are settler colonial and culturally erasing. The island of Blava/Texo politically comments on disputed territories and the politics of my time, such as Ukraine or more properly Palestine. (3489)
5. Cyrox Arc. After making progress fighting against Domuz colonial oppression, Juha find themselves in another of their colonies and tied up in organised resistance movements. I need to find more for this arc to be, since Juha can't really make a theme of fighting colonial oppression. (3492)
6. Fanthel Arc. They must somewhat reconcile with the Domuz Republic as an entity, which will be challenging to narratively justify (and may require showing that Juha is structurally flawed despite its member's idealisms). The Lost City of Fanthel is a silver ruin and a story may be constructed here for Juha. The arc of Fanthel serves the meta role of introducing Juha to the Silver Ruins, but must also stand on its own like every other arc. (3495)
7. Terlaye Arc. Juha must have been going back to Shildo regularly to collect S-AG payments and give items they have collected. After the Cyrox arc, one of the artefacts they collected was historically significant to a dynasty in Gordol. At the same point, the southern revolts are solidifying themselves as political entities and Terlayan independence is brewing following the murder of the Emperor of Ralena. At the same time, the Nethyl-Vethonthye's sponsorship of Juha is made public by a private detective. I think this is plenty for a story revolved around Nethyl-Vethonthye and from the perspective of Juha to write itself. The Nethyl-Vethonthye family, all at the same time, makes a bid for Terlayan leadership while protecting their prestige from their revealed secrets. I think it must end with a Nethyl-Vethonthye victory, the permanent exile of Ethenri. It's in future lore territory, so I cannot determine the outcome for Terlaye. (3900)
8. Set in Mothdao, it would be nice to have an arc that involves itself with themes I may yet construct from the Pri, Cin, the Silath, and Crecyvdaith. The Silath would introduce the silver ruin's underground inter-connectivity in a way that Juha might just not engage with meaningfully at this point (since it needs to appear later). Probably needs to include Domuz colonial outposts.
9. An arc set in Elvas Eldu that is less about the setting and more about the affairs of Eternals. I've no clue how the Eternals would involve Juha in what they get up to. Maybe this is the Crawl arc, where their dungeon crawl must carry some additional weight as perhaps another Eternal has some stakes that the Eternal of the Crawl refuses to cooperate on. Probably subtly foreshadow Annwvyan colonialism with a small colonial entity relevant in the plot.
10. Annwvyn Arc. No clue here, it's a clean slate. Probably explore an emerging Annwvyan colonial power. This is really far in the future from where I want to develop the lore to, but I know that Annwvyn will emerge with colonial powers that will eventually colonise Shildo in a very British Raj style of way.

The final arcs have them follow the silver ruins in Barogate, perhaps during the final post-Terlaye arc they are informed that not even the Eternals know of the silver ruins due to their inaccessibility. They are asked personally by an Eternal (or a group of them) to discover their secrets, the eternals inspired by Juha's impeccable experience portfolio.

ON this revisit, I might not give Juha the wastes arcs? I might keep them as a group in Shildo with a few expeditions to lands far off in favour of reserving some of these story hooks for a different group entirely. 

11. Acvidaras. Juha first goes to Hegrel and experiences some grand political scheme while simultaneously exploring the surface layers of Gatesweep. Gatesweep is ultimately cut off from the under-layer, so they abandon it as a front for exploration into the silver ruins. Perhaps the political scheme is the Keron conquest. After all, the world is always changing.
12. Cosbon Complex. Brought to the Cosbon Complex, Juha find themselves wrapped up in the End of the Aredvics. It must end with their discovery of the under-layer which Cosbon Complex is built on top of, and perhaps inspired by their past experiences with the Silath they take the HSR to a future unknown.
13. Emerging in Costrutia on the opposite side of the continent, Juha finds themselves once again wrapped up in the colonial future of the Domuz Republic, fighting with another colonial power in Kusao and Cronar. At its conclusion, Juha convenes with the Eternals on their progress
14. The final arc of Juha. They convene with the Volcano Lords, find the HSR from Costrutica to The Heart after engaging with Mettaton (an active entertainer AI in Costrutica). They activate the heart, and the unsatisfying end of Juha occurs.

The activation of The Heart (which, by the way, is a portal between the Mortal Realm and the Sanded Realm) is not meant to be a satisfying end. While there are many recurring themes of colonialism especially with how prevailing the Domuz Republic is to Juha, that is ultimately just a consequence of the setting rather than an intentional theme. There is no "final battle", no "ultimate change" caused by Juha. All they ever achieve is small-scale heroism in face of the progress of history.

The Domuz Republic vs the Annwvyan colonial power is a battle against evil and evil. It will definitely have some glorious ultimate battle, but no side wins, and Juha does not take part but instead does damage control for local communities. 

Once they activate the Heart, nothing fundamentally changes about the world. Colonialism still occurs, people still suffer. The Eternals stay as they are. All that has changed is that Juha, and therefore the readers, have experienced the World of Wastelife.
