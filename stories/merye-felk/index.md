# Trilogies
There's six planned stories each narratively separated but serve as nice groupings of the six.

## Trilogy 1
The first trilogy is supposed to include everything I think is super important for Robin Howitha's story. So, it involves the Soris of Caleheb as a constantly mentioned antagonist, the Soris of Jonati to bring in wider Ralean political dynamics, and the Soris of Fellan to introduce wider Terlayan polotics and the Soris of Fellan as a recurring villain. 
### Robin Howitha and the Soris of Caleheb. 
This story is supposed to introduce Robin Howitha, his demeanour, the Merye Felk, and his overwhelming competency. 
- The first scene is an introductory robbing scene. Robin Howitha and Little Jenna rob a small caravan of rich folk moving through Lakeland. The robbery goes well, as Robin and Jenn are able to disarm the caravan's guard without any trouble. The caravan guard recognises Robin and demands a duel, claiming he knows he can win. Although Robin appreciates the challenge, they are only here for the riches. 
- Robin and Jenna head back to the camp to celebrate the successful robbery. He shares the spoils among his men, telling them to only take what they need, for the rest will be given to some local family struggling with paying the Soris' taxes during their bad harvest. Among the spoils are a piece of legal paper describing the movement of a tax wagon to Cania, the Capital the Empire. The paper claims that the caravan will be relatively unguarded. Robin celebrates this find, and takes Melin Mux with him for the heist, who volunteered eagerly with the hopes of redistributing the tax funds back to those who paid them.
- Approaching the site of the tax caravan, Robin and Melin quietly await the caravan's passing, but it never comes. Instead, they are ambushed by the Soris of Caleheb's men. Laughing at the situation he's in, Robin distracts the guard with a call to duel, which lets Melin escape. The Soris's guards say to let the him go as it's Robin they're after anyway. Robin accepts he is outnumbered and honourably surrenders. He is sent north to a nearby fort, and awaits the arrival of the Soris of Caleheb who will decide what to do with Robin.
- In his cell, Robin is approached by the Soris of Caleheb who celebrates Robin's capture, claiming Robin and the Merye Felk has been a pain in his side for too long. The Soris is ready to send Robin to Notami for immediate execution, but his present advisor says that holding onto Robin in this secure location would be better, as without him the Meyre Felk are sure to collapse into disarray. The Soris agrees with the premise, ensuring Robin that his public execution will be a spectacle. Robin agrees with the Soris, laughing in the face of his death.
- Back in the Merye Felk camp, news of Robin's capture hits and there is a lot of panic and discourse. Many people are distraught on the loss of the relatively new leader (the events are in 3480), but Little Jenna stands on a box and manages to get the people's attention, with some help from Melin Mux who leverages that he stayed after "escaping" to hear where they were taking Robin. Jenna says that the fort has some serious weaknesses and claims that they could put together a plan to save Robin.
- Jenna's plan makes heavy use of a guard on the inside that's sympathetic. He will tell the gate guards that he is expecting a delivery wagon, which in reality will be a bunch of Merye Felk in disguise as caravan guards and caravaners. The delivery wagon will also hold Jenna, Melin, someone who knows the layout of the castle, and another highly skilled Merye Felk who will be hidden underneath the goods and be able to drop out from underneath the cart. The guard will have the cart sent to a predetermined destination, while the tradesmen will make a fuss. During this, the four infiltrators will get out of the cart and split up, Melin and the person familiar with the fort will go to the dungeon and rescue Robin while Jenna and the other will attempt to cause a fire and explosion using hidosis. During this distraction, the disguised soldiers and tradesmen will make their way to the gatehouse and open it, letting the rest of the Merye Felk in to attack the fort to cause as much of a distraction while Melin and the other fetch Robin and escape out the main gate as the rest of the Merye Felk retreat.
- As the wagon approaches the fort, everyone is tense. Melin, Jenna, and the two others hide in the cart underneath a mediocre shipment of vegetables. As they approach the gate, the guardsmen on top of the gatehouse claim that the fort is not accepting any traders or deliveries on special circumstances. Tension is high a the disguised tradespeople perform a vocal fuss about this and the guards are having none of it. The sympathetic guard rushes to the gatehouse and urges them let the delivery in, using their personal relation with the gate guards to manipulate them into letting the caravan in. 
- Once inside, they are led to the predetermined spot by the guard. The Sepervar of the fort storms out of the main building and demands the guards tell them what is afoot. The tradespeople and the sympathetic guardsperson bargain about how important the cart is while the Sepervar shouts at them for thinking a bunch of vegetables could be so important. The four slip out of the cart as soon as they can, and as the Sepervar demands the tradespeople and their guards be kicked out, Jenna sabotages the wheels of the cart so that it breaks when moved. She and her companion rush to somewhere to make the distraction, while Melin and the other hide and wait for a clearing to enter the dungeon.
- As the tradespeople are forced to pick up their cart, the wheels break off and the tradespeople make a fuss about how they must now repair their cart before leaving. The Sepervar screams at them and orders they lift the cart themselves out of the fort. The tradespeople struggle to lift the cart when a massive explosion is heard coming from the stables, causing the wood to fly across the courtyard. The guards of the main building rush to the commotion, letting Melin and other inside. The Sepervar, clearly extremely irritable, screams at the guards to impound the tradespeople's cart and throw them out. The sympathetic guard goes back to the gatehouse.
- As the caravaners reluctantly leave the fort, the hidden force outside of the fort take the gate opening as their sign to attack. Upon seeing the forty people rushing towards the gate, the gate guards panic to close it but the sympathetic guard has lodged a piece of wood into the mechanism, forcing it open. The disguised tradespeople and soldiers are approached to be arrested, but they brandish their swords and reveal themselves as the Merye Felk. The Sepervar comes back from the stables to witness their guards in conflict with the Merye Felk as forty men rush towards the gate. They express their immense disappointment before rushing up to the gatehouse.
- Upon reaching the gatehouse, the Sepervar discovers that it is locked open by a piece of wood. They shout from the gatehouse to the gatehouse guards, now fighting the formerly disguised caravaner, on how incompetent they are. Just as the Merye Felk approach the gate, the Sepervar removes the gate blockage, lowering the portcullis and blocking most of the Meyre Felk from entering. However, ten make it through. Guards rush out of the main building to deal with the commotion in the courtayrd as the remaining Merye Felk outside of the gate panic and fall back, being fired upon by guards rushing to the walls.
- Back to the infiltrators, Melin Mux and the other hide as guards rush past them leaving the dungeon. They hope that everything is alright in the courtyard but fear the worse as they hear the commotion. They rush to Robin and successfully release him from his shackles. He's two days starved, and Melin realises he is in no condition to fight. They hurry him out.
- In the meantime, Jenna is desperately trying to reach the gatehouse in the commotion, using the battling in the courtyard to carefully sneak across the wall. Once she gets to the gatehouse, the Sepervar notices her and recognises her. The Sepervar's frustration reaches a climax as Jenna draws her thinsword, and the Sepervar gladly accepts the duel. Throughout the duel, Jenna contends with her short stature, closely dodging attacks that were practiced for a taller opponent. Jenna manages to stab the Sepervar in the kidney, causing the Sepervar to collapse. Jenna opens the gate, and the Merye Felk rush in once again from the ditches they hide in, but they take a while to reach the gate
- As Jenna re-opens the gate, Melin, Robin, and the other enter the courtyard. Guards immediately engage Melin and the other, and Melin instructs Robin to make his own way out the gate, saying that they'll follow him. Robin, only two days starved, still has plenty of energy and courage to start walking. However, on his way to the gate, a familiar face blocks his path. The caravan guard from before, wearing Sebhar armour. Robin picks up a nearby sword from a fallen Merye Felk and prepares himself in a healthy stature to give the Sebhar the fight he wanted.
- During Robin's fight with the Sebhar, the rest of the Merye Felk make it through the gate and the battle turns more even. Robin has his back and forth with the Sebhar, before gaining the upper hand with a tricky parry. The Sebhar is disarmed and his hand is cut non-lethally by Robin. Robin instructs the Merye Felk to retreat, who group up and hold back the guards. 
- Before Robin and the Merye Felk can make it out, the Soris of Caleheb reveals himself on top of the fort's walls, holding Jess in his arms with a knife to her throat. The Soris demands Robin turn himself in or lose his companion. Robin picks up a rock and throws it at a support to the platform that the Sebhar is standing on, causing the platform to collapse and for Jenna to break free. 
- The remaining Merye Felk escape the fort and run into the forest. Robin scouns Jenna and the rest of the Merye Felk for performing such a reckless attack, and takes a moment to mourn the losses of the dozen Merye Felk lost in the conflict. After this short scorning and memorial, Robin returns to his cheery personality, thanking the Merye Felk for rescuing him, and hoping well for the future of the Merye Felk.


- The next scene would be the Merye Felk in complete disarray, however Little Jenna manages to take charge and stage a competent rescue of Robin.
- During the rescue, Little Jenna would have to contend with the well prepared Soris, who himself will have to contend with the decently prepared Jenna. After using a little deception and whit, Jenna would make it to Robin and free him.
- Having been starved for a day or two, Robin would be in no position to fight, but still eagerly takes up the sword when the group is faced with an opponent that only he could take care of. Perhaps this is the person he robbed initially, to lead to a dynamic highpoint.
- Before Robin manages to escape, Jenna is captured by the Soris, but this situation is resolved quickly using Robin's wit. 

In general, the story should start at a midpoint: Robin robbing a military officer
Then it needs to reach an emotional highpoint: Robin discussing the next job with the Merye Felk
Then it reaches a lowpoint when Robin is captured and the Merye Felk are in disaray, followed by a midpoint where Jenna manages to stage a successful rescue.

The rescue itself needs to have a highpoint where all is going well, perhaps in the initial deception. Then, when the deception is revealed, it is shown that the Soris's guard were well prepared leading to a low point for the Merye Felk. Then, the emotional tension would look up when Robin is successfully rescued by Jenna, with another lowpoint when he is revealed to be unable to fight followed by the struggle of getting him out. Then, the story reaches climactic tension as the capable fighter blocks their path, and a heroic end when Robin takes up the sword and defeats the fighter. 

Then I get to milk some tension by having the Soris capture Jenna before she is released by the wit of Robin. Then that's the end.

The initial rob should go well with a little tension. Then, another caravan of soldiers enters the scene and starts attacking the Merye Felk in hopes of protecting the caravan. Then, in some accident, Melin Mux kills the leader of these military chasers causing the rest to disperse. Looking at the uniform of the soldier, he realises it was a Rostavar of Jonati. A soldier has to get away to tell the Soris of Jonati about this quickly.

The camp doesn't pack away in time and the army storms it, killing many Merye Felk members as they scramble away. They overhear the message for themselves, and the Soris of Jonati claims that he is tired of the Soris of Caleheb's incompetence in dealing with their own banditry group and will continue to take matters into his own hands. ### Robin Howitha and the Soris of Fellan
This story will widen the context of the Merye Felk into Terlaye, showing their bounce-back and how they operate on the other side of the lake. Also, it will introduce the Soris of Fellan, a bitter and cunning fellow who is friends with the Soris of Caleheb. 

- The opening scene needs to be a daring burglary on some rich house in Serven. This should showcase the supportive underground network that the Merye Felk utilise in their urban operations. Perhaps the burglary starts smoothly before Robin and co realise that there are unexpected guards on duty, and although the heist initially goes smoothly someone in the group, maybe Robin himself, alerts a guard and has a messy battle that makes a lot of noise, alerting more guards and forcing them to make another escape. Durign this scene, Velleyem should be introduced as the leader of the Serven underground for the Merye Felk.
- Robin is approached by a member of the underground who says that a personal friend of his wants to join the Merye Felk. Robin say that this is not how he recruits for the Merye Felk, but the underground member pushes Robin by suggesting that the promising candidate is an excellent fighter, and that Robin should at least humour a duel. Robin, torn between his honour and wanting to show off, agrees to meet with this member. 
- Robin enters a public duelling club that the underground member said the candidate was with a few Merye Felk. Upon entering the building, he and the Merye Felk find no-one but the Soris of Fellan who thanks Robin for humouring his request. Robin, sensing a trap, makes some witty remark against the Soris before ordering a retreat, but it is too late. Him and the Merye Felk he came with are faced with an ambush of Terlayan guardspeople. The Soris orders that Robin duel him, and Robin gladly accepts the request.

Robin eventually overcomes the advantage of the trick by carefully watching the Soris' hands. He almost wins against the Soris. In a panic, the cell cart turns around and flees with a few members of the Merye Felk chasing after it. Back at the bridge, it is revealed that the bridge collapsed under the Soris of Fellan himself, and Velleyem Scathles must duel the Soris. ## Trilogy 2

The Hunter of the Merye Felk is supposed to be a re-enforcement of the setup of the original trilogy. I'd have to make it some sort of narrative departure to fit in the themes of trilogy 2. It also reels in the Soris of Fellan as a recurring villain, setting up a good villain hierarchy. Then, Merye Felk and the Pirate of Fellan could be a final departure as an action packed swashbuckling finale for the trilogy, showing the group being more audacious after the events of all previous stories.
