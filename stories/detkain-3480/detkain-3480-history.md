# Quick historical overview
Shalun Ruion Reimig was declared Emperor of Ralean in 3455 following the death of his father, Canita Reimig, to natural causes. While demonstrably competent at first, Reimig was critisised by the Rostavar, Soris, and dynasties of the homeland provinces.

The Empire maintained a fairly normal state between 3460 and 3470, although criticisms of Reimig were becoming increasingly popular, His seeming inability to involve himself in the many disputes between the Kidhmatac, Soris, Rostavar, and dynasties of the provinces made him unpopular to all of them. 

In 3470, the Terlayan province of Llofilot would declare independence from the Empire of Ralean with the support of Eresonia and Vania. Ruion was sure to be swift in his response, attempting to re-invade the region with the nearest legion. His efforts were crushed by an indomitable Eresonian defence, at which point Ruion gave up on Llofilot in hopes of securing control over the more cooperative regions of Terlaye. His image, however, would be entirely ruined.

Throughout the next decade, a coalition of revolutionaries would build up. In Rejedmana, an anarchist cell known as the Union proactively cooperated with those in other provinces who had issues with the Shalun's rule. The Union would unite all who would see the Shalun gone, despite the overwhelmingly different views of certain member groups. 

Notable members of the union were dynasties who wished to be rid of the Rostavari and to re-establish their historical military dominance over their own land. They were large Councilist and other anarchist cells. They were bureaucrats and provincial managers fed up with the government and believed they could do better if they consolidated power. The end result of this coalition proved to be decisive and fatal for the Emperor. 

By 3480, members of the union invited their local guard into the budding revolution. The union had been operating largely underground, however as it had accrued members of the dynastic nobility and even many rostavari, it was known to the Emperor. Notably, Tadcraldinum and Leuvednodinum guards were already part of the union. This date only marked the extension of the invitaiton in other provinces, including in Detkaindinum. This publicised the union, and its intention to lead the coalition against the Emperor sometime soon.

And in fact, in 3484, the southern revolts would occur as Emperor Ruion Reimig declared nearly half of all homeland dinae to be dissident provinces, effectively swearing in martial law for two thirds of the Empire. Specifically, 12 of 23 Ralean dinae were declared dissident.

The first Dux to be issued was the Dux of Deshegdinum, who upon travelling across the border was quickly ambushed by a councilist-lead army. News spread quickly, and those loyal to the Emperor were expelled, imprisoned, ignored, or killed. 

# The Situation in Detkain
In 3480, detkain was anything but politically homogenous. It's present forces are councilist, anarchists, monarchists, capitalists, republicans, empiricists, and meritocrats. Much like the prevailing ideology of Detkain, the individual characters and their ideologies change. 

Detkain specifically is mixed between a strong anarchist cell, the meritocrats lead by Calis, and the rest of everyone who work for their own interests. We have a few "groups", each represented by a different name. 

First and foremost, you have the workers and labourers of Detkain. The names are Aelia the builder, Nosric the carpenter, Hemery the smithhand, Carlea the printer, Soren the gardener, and Althe the cook. Although each of these people are lead by their own ideals, throughout the events of d3480. 

Carlea is from Ereso, moving to Detkain after she fell in love with a Londa student studying in Ereso. She is well educated in the ideas of Councilism and democracy, and is fascinated by old anarchist ideals in the CTP and Lauvedno culture. She now works in a printing shop. Aelia is a builder who initially only campaigned for construction safety laws before being radicalised by Carlea and becoming the most motivated forces of labour in Detkain.

Alongside the workers are the artisans, who are practically self-employed with involvement in some trade guild. They are Lorcan the Wizard, Celyb the Smith, Mena the Weaver, Meichi the Potter. The artisans generally take sides throughout the situation.

The physical power in Detkain is divided between the Guards and the Garrison, the forces of the Soris and the Rostavar respectively. The guard people are the captain Liora, and the guardspeople Elara and Cin. The garrison people are Caelen and Lensen, and their captain Nilsyt. The Soris and the Rostavar themselves are a bit above the situation, and although they are aware of the union as characters in this story they do not need to be named.

Finally, there are the leaders. Calis is a meritocrat, who owns a large company in Detkaindinum and is personally motivated by a system of governance he calls a "pure meritocracy". Seria is the Soris-appointed mayor, who believes she is too competent for the role and aspires to be the next soris. Jarvid is an influential peasant liaison, he is an important part of the union ensuring cooperation between the city politics and the politics of its villages.
