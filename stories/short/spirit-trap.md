Observed by Edreth Gwelshith, Esten of Raifel.

A slight cracking sound was heard. A usual ambiance for a patrol squad going through the dense forests of Codia. This was a group of seven. Although they would try to stay quiet, the unkept natural ground would disagree with this mission by scattering brittle branches across the bumpy ground. This cracking was that of a branch, but it wasn’t from a squad member stepping on one. It came from afar but was loud enough to reach. Animals lived in this forest undisturbed, lightly treading over grass and hopping over tree roots.

Members of the group slowly unsheathed their weapons, spearmen bringing their spears to face forwards. These were Diacut, their industrial steel weapons making up for their short arm’s reach. One of them was a weapons specialist handling a Springburst Pneumaflux Confluence Catalyst, the SPCC, to the other soldiers its just a springburst firearm. Its hard to imagine a tree-like object at the end of the rifle’s long barrel surviving the explosion of fire that a T-d266 cartridge creates.

The specialist raises their weapon. The crack of branches in the distance was too loud to be any animal. The soldiers all knew what it meant. None of them were combat veterans, but they believed they were prepared. Combat drills and golem fighting makes you feel like a veteran when you come out of the training camp, only experience can prepare you for the forces of Silpt.

Suddenly, a tree trunk revealed itself as a huge Ews. Twice the size of any cut, it swung its massive metal hammer at the patrol. Every cut fell prone, avoiding the attack.  The soldiers scattered. The three spearmen surrounded the huge caddews. Four Codews pounced from among the trees. Without a thought, the three swordsmen ran in to protect the spearmen’s flanks. The specialist stood and took aim at the caddews.

On the left flank, a single soldier fought two codews. He hastily swung his shield, blocking both of the codews’ attacks in one defensive sweep. In the same motion, he extended his sword forwards. It slid through the bark-like skin of one of the codews. The sword slid just as easily outwards, and that codews fell to the ground.

On the right flank, two diacut clashed with two codews. The leftmost codews thrust their spear forwards. The diacut parried the blow, guiding it gently to her side. The rightmost codews took initiative and swung their sword. The codews was unprepared and immediately had a sword lodged in the side of their head.

In the centre, the diacut stabbed the caddews’ chest. The caddews stepped forwards, forcing that diacut backwards and tripping them on a branch. The caddews raised their hammer. The diacut winced before being crushed by the hammer’s immense weight.

On the left, the fallen Codews corpse began to glow blue. It twitched, then stood back up again.

“We need to kill the big one!” the cut faced with two Codews again exclaimed.
