Pamphlet 
To be printed and placed on seats. This provides additional world-building context, but is not necessary to enjoy the play.

**Welcome to Haerul**
You find yourself in a harsh desert land, dominated by the life-giving flow of the Nila river. Here, the **Almaic** hold control, a powerful class who maintain their wealth through taxes on houses, farms, and crucially bridge tolls. 

In our story, we observe the friction between the classes:
- **The Almaic:** The ruling class. We meet the respected **Polithe** and the ambitious **Mesir**
- **The Nahr:** The servants who keep society running. **Daki** is a steward of immense capability, while **Muharie** is a messenger of immense hunger.
- **The Jari:** The military captains. Represented here by **Reiun**, whose authority is as loud as it is innefective.
- **The Jisur:** The dramatic extended family of the almaic.

**Casting roles**
- **Polithe.** Almaic. Respected, wealthy, and calm.
- **Mesir**. Almaic. Ambitious, arrogant, and stingy
- **Daki.** Nahr. Polithe's underappreciated steward.
- **Muharie.** Nahr. A messanger, perpetually starved and confused.
- **Reiun.** Jari. An overbearing captain whose arrests carry no threat.
- **Esuha.** Jisur. A lover who quarrels as much as they love. Mesir's niece.
- **Linsa.** Jisur. Esuha's partner, equally prone to petty squabbles. Polithe's child.
- **Danial.** Jisur. Mayt's bitter rival; infinitely lucky. Mesir's child.
- **Mayt.** Jisur. Danial's bitter rival; talented, smart, but subject to Danial's luck. Polithe's child.

## Costume
**Aesthetic**
Authentically Egypt-Ottoman fashion. 

**Worldbuilding specifics**
The Chakram is a symbol of Haerul culture; a brutal weapon only used when conflict is seriously lethal. It must be worn by all actors (make a cardboard one - it will probably just look like a random silver circle). 

Reiun should carry several but poorly, maybe they fall off in some scene.

Muharie should have some colourful puffy pants though.

## Props
- **Backdrop:** The Redspan Bridge over the River Nila. Desert scenery with a guard tower. Bright sun, refreshing water.
- **The Table:** Littered with keys (modern is fine), coins, and tokens.
- **The Tokens:** Large, hand-width cardboard tokens. Text: "Bridge Token: Valid Yesterday.
- **The Ledger:** Thick, leather-bound. Needs a latch to prevent opening when tossed.
- **Scrolls:** Long paper or sticky-taped A4 sheets. Wax seals edible or removable for Muharie.
- **Script:** A prop script for Daki to pull out. Should probably have "SCRIPT" in big letters on the back for the audience.
## Prologue
(Muharie tumbles onto the stage)

Muharie:
Good folk of Haerul! I bear a message- 
-or did bear it-
-or perhaps I ate it?
(Muharie checks bag)
No, no, I did not eat the message,
only the wax. And only a little.

"By order of the Council of Haerul,
the toll upon Redspan Bridge is hereby- "
(Muharie licks their finger, the scroll sticks to their face)
"-hereby postponed, preponed, reconsidered, recanted, reinstated, and reviewed, pending review of the postponement-"
(Muharie droops)
-of the reinstatement
-of the reconsideration...

(Muharie reads ahead)

So... the bridge. Yes.

(Muharie leaves)

## Act 1 - The Toll
(Redspan bridge, a painted backdrop of the Nila. MESIR stands besides a table piled with keys and toll tokens. REIUN looms nearby.)

**MESIR**
A caravan delayed? A toll unpaid!
Do they wish the bridge not to stand? 
For the river's flow to halt?

**REIUN** 
(slowly walking up to the table and enthusiastically pounding their fist on it)
Name them, my lord, and to the dungeons they shall go!
All of them! For loitering as they check their full coin-purses!

**MESIR**
(knivingly)
Yes, yes. Arrest the river if you must. 
But allow them to pay the toll first. 

(DANIAL and ESUHA enter)

**DANIAL** 
(to Esuha, without thinking)
How lucky I am to pass by as the guard ties his shoe!

**ESUHA**
If only my lover were to show for me the way untied laces show for you.

**MESIR**
My son, A toll-dodger? Insolence! Reiun!

**REIUN**
(grabbing Danial)
You are under arrest for-
for-
What's the word?
(Reiun thinks uncomfortably long)
-for structural dissent!

**DANIAL**
Reiun you ass! You shall be my new rival!

(REIUN carries DANIAL offstage. ESUHA follows)

**ESUHA**:
Don't be so cruel, uncle!

## Act 2 - The Tax

(POLITHE and DAKI enter)

**POLITHE**
Peace, peace. There shall be no arrests here today. I am here to collect what is due.

**MESIR** 
(hiding wealth)
Due to whom?

**POLITHE**
To the structure that allows us both to exist.

**DAKI** 
(pleasantly)
I have already calculated the arrears, interests, penalties, and the polite fiction that reduces them to nought point seven two per-cent.

**MESIR**
Have you calculated my accounts?

**POLITHE**
Only the ones I know you can pay.

**MESIR**
Outrageous! I own the bridge!

**DAKI**
Then according to your contract, you are...
(Checks notes)
Liable for maintenance, obstructions, collection, mis-collection, 

**POLITHE**
And excessive righteousness.

**MESIR**:
You dare audit me in public!

**POLITHE**
We dared this privately first. This is merely the encore.

(MESIR lunges for the ledger in DAKI's hands. DAKI calmly steps to the side. DAKI and POLITHE play piggy in the middle with the ledger, where MESIR is the piggy.)

**MESIR**
That book lies!

**POLITHE**
Not on my account.

**MESIR**
I will not be reduced to a line-item!

**POLITHE**
You already are. Page four.

**MESIR** 
(freezing)
...Subsection?

**DAKI**
Page four, section three. 
"Bridge lords with delusions of permanence."

**MESIR**
I object to the plural!

(Piggy in the middle continues, REIUN re-enters)

**MESIR**
My captain! 

**REIUN**
Shall I arrest the book, my lord?

## Act 3 - The Lovers

(ESUHA and LINSA burst in mid-argument, LINSA chases ESUHA.)

**ESUHA**
You said you would meet me before the bridge!

**LINSA**
I did! Yesterday! Tomorrow! Always! 
But today your uncle's toll demanded proof of my love in exact change!

**MESIR**
(loudly)
No crossing without the toll!

**POLITHE**
No loan without its payments.

**DAKI**
(quietly)
No effort with recognition. 

**MESIR**
The toll is just! Linsa knows perfectly well of it, that they may not cross without a token or a coin.

(They start speaking over one another)

**LINSA**
Oh, but I brought a token! Why must you be so stingy and cruel? Have you no respect for love?

**MESIR**
An expired one, and who is to say I do not respect love, if I do respect the toll more.

**ESUHA**
You should have prepared for every eventuality! Brought every token and coin, is this not the process of a lover? To do everything in their power to see their person? You should have sneaked past the bridge.

**MESIR**
No they should not have! You pay the toll!

**ESUHA**
Have your toll, you cruel uncle!

(Esuha throws a token on the floor)

**REIUN**
Esuha! You are under arrest for disrespecting the toll!

**MESIR**
Yes, Reiun! Put them on the naughty step!

(REIUN escorts ESUHA out)

## Act 4 - The Death of Mayt

(DANIAL and MAYT enter and disrupt argument, swords drawn)

**DANIAL**
At last! Our fated duel! 

**MAYT**
Only a fool believes in fate! 

**DANIAL**
Then by Medr, you will find my skill far greater than your own.

**MAYT**
Is that so, rock-jointed one? We shall see if your luck gets you out of it this time! 
Ha-Ya!

(MAYT lunges but slips on the token. Falls, and dies dramatically. DANIAL gasps, runs to MAYT, checks a pulse.)

**DANIAL** 
(looking at audience)
He's dead!
(Danial gets up, brushes his shoulder)
A calculated manoeuvre, on my part.

(MUHARIE re-enters carrying a scroll)

**MUHARIE**:
Message! Message!

(MUAHRIE trips on MAYT. MAYT twitches but remains dead. MUHARIE does not realise MAYT is there.)

**MUHARIE**
The toll has been abolished!
(Muharie checks scroll)
Or doubled? Or postponed?

(MUHARIE looks around and notices MAYT)

**MUHARIE**
Mayt is dead? I have a new message to send.

(MUHARIE exits and passes REIUN as they re-enter)

**REIUN**
Fear not! I am here to restore order! Who needs order?

(Everyone points at everyone. REIUN follows the pointing around and notices MAYT)

Reiun:
Mayt is dead? 
Ah. (Reiun backs away slowly)
Danial, you are doubly under arrest this time!

(MAYT suddenly gasps and sits upright)

**MAYT**
Did I win?

**DANIAL**
My sharp wit and sharper sword bested you rightly.

**POLITHE**
It did not! He slipped on a token!

**MAYT**
(Jumping up athletically)
Then we must duel again, and prove our honours!

(Both re-draw their weapons. DANIAL makes a clearly lacking lunge that MAYT side-steps. MAYT prepares to strike DANIAL, but slips on the same token)

**DANIAL**:
Dead once again, bested by my excellence!

## Act 5 - The Resoution

(DAKI re-enters.)

**DAKI**
I have calculated. If the toll is suspended, the tax is adjusted, the lovers allowed passage by credit. 
The bridge remains, the river flows, and no-one dies.

(DAKI notices MAYT)

**DAKI**
No-one will die hereforth.

**MESIR**
Passage by credit? You mean to say that they must not pay?

**POLITHE**
Credit, my friend, is what has you owe me so much.

**MESIR**
Then it is good for some to be indebted to me. 
For this temporary solution.

(MAYT suddenly gasps and sits up)

**DAKI**:
Ah! Mayt! You've returned.

**MAYT**
(checking hands)
So it would seem.

**DAKI**
Worry not, for according to my figures-
(checks script)
-that should be the last time today.

## Epilogue

(MUHARIE addresses the audience, gnawing on a scroll.)

**MUHARIE**
Thus ends the matter of the toll, 
the tax, love, death
and the bridge, which still stands,
may now charge on credit?
A solution sure to cause more trouble. 
To be re-evaluated, recanted, reconsidered. So forth.
But for now, Haerul rests.

(Bow, curtain. MUHARIE rapidly eats the scroll just before the curtains close.)
