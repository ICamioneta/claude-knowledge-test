All day it glares from above, a white ball of death in a sea of boiling sky. It casts rays of light that subdue the ground. The grains of sand in the desert flatter the sun by becoming suns themselves. You're not safe from it in the shade, and you're not safe from it indoors. Torches, candles, glowlights. They are all micro-suns, and it keeps us alert.

Its scorching eye peers over the horizon. It cloaks the sky in pumpkin hues, the same shade of askootasquash, blending seamlessly with the orange sands. The horizon is hard to find, the sun delights in our confusion. It moves to terrorize another part of the world. The daily dictator.

There is no respite. It kills in its absence. Cities fall silent. Grand animalia fall to the ground. Personkind falls moribund. 

Though flawed, daytime's strengths are clear: wakefulness, alertness, and productivity. The night carries the terror of unproductivity. The might of all Kan Koronar's children snuffed out by the absence of a sun. 

This is a waste; 
_hir yw'r dydd a hir yw'r nos, a hyr yw aros Arawn._
_The day is long and the night is long, and Arawn waits._

The sun rises weakly over Rahoamor, the angry sea. Its violent rampage worn out from a nightly battle. It limps over the horizon. Journey across the Rahoamor will break a god's ship, but Rahoamor was the salt in a twisted knife wound. The attacker with the knife was Annwvyn, a land of excess and delight. A land where the day and the night are one in the same. 

There is a proverb my culture sings of Annwvyn.

_Deffron a dechreuwn, gwnawn ni ein bythol dydd. Bydd ein teirnas fythol gynhyrchiol ymhlith sêr gwnaethom ni ein hunaun. Fydd yr nos dydd tawelog._

_We are to be awake, and make our days eternal. Our kingdom is forever productive among the stars we make ourselves. The night becomes a silent day._


## Afterword
it's January 2025 and I've done some climate modelling for the world of Wastelife and I've realised that all the prevailing winds are going in the wrong direction, so I've had to reverse the direction of the world. The sun now rises from the west and sets in the east. This hasn't come with many issues, but this story. This story explicitly talks about the sun moving east to west. Annwvyn is supposed to be east of Cronar, but this story very explicitly references the sun leaving barogate westwards and terrorizing Mothdao, then getting beaten up by Annwvyn.

I don't really know what to do, this sun reversal completely destroys this story. I'll leave it for now, but use this afterword as an important note that the sun in this story is moving in a non-canonical direction.
