title: Thraechos learns about the outside world
description: No description.

An adventurer hikes across the harsh grey volcanic sidings of Ichmenre. The rocky ground beneath their feet has not provided restful stops, but their hard shoe's soles are built for purpose. The adventurer can still smell the Duela's petrichor. From the high point they can see far across the mangrove treetops, a bright and luscious green that they'd struggled to imagine while bogged down in the brownish mudded waters. They were looking forward to the, though still wet, solid ground of Edlyue, the trees of which they could recognise in the distance. 

A feint smoky smell tore their mind away from the view of Duela, and from above a discordant voice sounded.

"What are you doing here!"

"Uh, exploring?" The adventurer timidly responded.

Turning to see the source of the voice, the adventurer saw a towering red and black figure.

"On My active volcanic mountain! You have trespassed against me!" The figure marched forwards with purpose, a burning fury of black charcoal in the shape of a person, nearly twice as tall as the adventurer. The figure withdrew a metal sling, the string of which seemingly also a metallic alloy.

"I don't know what to say, I've got this thing. I see a mountain and I just need to climb it." The adventurer dropped their thoughts. 

The giant pulled back their sling, the metallic alloy bending as if it were string and a black ball of fire forming in its centre. 

"You have climbed your last mountain!" the giant bellowed.

"Wait wait wait!" The adventurer cried in plea.

"What? Are you going to plead for your small life?" the giant hesitated on their sling's draw. 

The adventurer noticed the full stature of the figure, now two dozen paces away. Fire-clad beings like this were fabled to live in the volcanic kingdom of Ich Mena Re. The adventurer had seen from afar a town of dancing fire creatures earlier in the mountains, and reckoned they had come into contact with a ranger or a guard.

"Small?" The adventurer atoned, "Life is anything but small."

"If you're not to plead, then you shall die!" The charcoal giant decreed and pulled back their sling further.

"Hey!" The adventurer screamed, "What for! All I did was wander!" The charcoal giant seemed hesitant.

"You wandered onto _my_ mountain. My sacred mineral pits, home of my great fire-towns." they said. The adventurer at this point had realised that this was no mere guard but a notorious Lord of Ich Mena Re, rulers of the aforementioned volcanic kingdom.

"I'm not a threat!" They pleaded, "I don't want to go near your fire. I only want to near the summit and pass through."

The charcoal lord released their sling's draw. "You... don't want to go near my fires? My great and bountiful towns?"

"I can't, even if I wanted to." The adventurer worriedly explained

The charcoal lord raised their arm to their chin, fire dripping from their blacken figure. The adventurer mistook this action for an aggression, and flinched in fear.

"Why can you not near my voluminous fires?" The lord exclaimed.

"Are you joking?"

"You insult me you SMALL thing?" The lord raising their voice, "I shall have you killed for that alone! Prepare to-"

"No no, sorry my big and firey guy-" 

"LESSER LORD THRAECHOS OF SIWGRGWYN, LORD OVER CHAR COAL! KNEEL!" Thraechos bellowed, the fire from their body exploding metres into the air. Sweat dripped from the adventurer's forehead.

"I apologise, my lord.", the adventurer said barely holding back tears, shooting down to a kneel,  "I should've recognised a noble soul."

The charcoal lord Thraechos eyed the kneeling adventurer.

"Wait, lesser?" the adventurer said.

The fires of Thraechos grew ever taller as he frustrated further, angrily breathing before ultimately beginning to calm down as the adventurer waited, kneeling in fear.

 "Yes, lesser." Thraechos explained. "My glorious mountain. It is... geographically smaller than my peers, thus I am a..." he hesitates "_lesser_ lord."

"I am sure your fiefdom is as brilliant as any other." The adventurer said with the pahoehoe of confidence they had left.

 "It is, small person." Thraechos assured the adventurer. "Why do you not seek to steal my riches? Why can you not?" he asked.

 "I'd die" The adventurer said simply.

"I and my Kaddenf guard would make sure of it!" Thraechos proudly exclaimed, voice carrying far throughout the mountainous terrain.

"No, I mean I would literally die. It's too hot for me. I saw one of your towns earlier on from afar. I didn't know what it was but it was beautiful. Creatures of pure fire, towers of the shiniest of metals, all working harmoniously to build something greater than themselves. There's nothing prettier on the continent." The adventurer explained with beauty.

Thraechos' solid stature began to waver, molten tears pooling in his eyes. "My towns... the most beautiful... And you cannot enter them fearing you'll boil!"

"It's a bummer, but it's fine. I get to see loads of nice places." The adventurer casually responded. 

Thraechos composed himself, "Such as my grandiose volcano! The prideful Siwgrgwyn. And my proud neighbour's Tanwyn!"

"Yes! And the Spire in Ereso standing so impossibly tall you worry it's scraping the stars. Or Nater's glistening river sparkling so brilliantly as it cascades from lake to lake. And the beaches of Kyeron, where warm sands stretch as far as the eyes can see in a way that makes you feel so small but so alive. It all makes you appreciate the sheer scale of life and beauty and everything in the realm."

Thraechos hesitated, seemingly enamoured in the adventurer's descriptions. "You mean to say there is more beauty outside of my realm?"

"Yes, but it is your prideful volcano that I find the most impressive!" The adventurer worried.

"No. It is okay adventurer. You need not flatter my meagre mountain. Tread across it how you please, for I know you cannot approach my fire-born communities. I too know what it's like to see places so beautiful that one themselves cannot hope to reach." Thraechos almost teared again, but stood strongly in front of the adventurer.

"Thank you, Lord Thraechos!" the adventurer said with glee before continuing their hike across the harsh, stony face of Siwgrgwyn.
