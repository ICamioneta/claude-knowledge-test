For a world called Wastelife, everything seems awfully alive.

I have done a lot of worldbuilding in the inhabited areas of Wastelife. So much so that the wastes in which there exists life (the namesake of wastelife) have received relatively little development.

This is most honestly because there simply isn't as much going on in the wastes as far as society, history, lore, and culture are concerned. 

However, as the namesake of Wastelife, the wastes deserve their story hooks. 

In the Heabe we have a few things. Silver ruins in Gordolmonre such as Gatesweep, the lonely City of Hegrel and it's little basin. The conflict of the Aredvics, and Aredvic trade and politics more generally. The lived-in silver ruin of Cos on Complex, whose surface secrets are a part of everyday life. 

In Dithas, we have the colonies of the Domuz. Exploitation, resistance, capitalism. Plus, probably, a silver ruin or two. 

In the Vugaix we have the incredibly powerful ingredients (see Black Hearts), and a few clearing Mad towns. But, primarily, we have the forested and lonely city of Nadsarch with its elite foraging Duporea. 

In Kyenel not only do we have the highest concentration of silver ruins such as Costrutica and The Cube, but the Obsidian Road and the sand camper cultures.

Ichmenre has the Lords of Ichmenre in their infinite drama. It is of course the source of the obsidian road.

Cronar and Kusao are technically wastes, but to give and how they're inhabited they don't act like ones. Kusao has the Fyn water sources, which are an extension of Costrutica. 

Duela has the okung kinships, the conflicts of the tribes in Edlyue and Barthe. 

Llyhai has a motherload of silver ruins, incredibly dangerous fauna and flora, a number of Domuz colonies holding on including Fanthel. 


Maybe I should write stories for a mythical character and their involvement in all of these hooks, as they wander the wastes and eventually become a legend of it. 

# Bryn the Wanderer
Bryn is a Difmuz who grew up in Hegrel. They were training to be a farmer. In Hegrel, farmers are wizards with enchantments that can improve the quality of soil and support plant growth in the poor soil of Acvidaras. 
## Prologue
Bryn explores Gatesweep with some friends. They are pushed deeper into the ruins by the more daring of the group. Bryn quickly becomes infatuated with the silver mystery, and enables some sort of automatic defence system that calls on Gatesweep's hostile automata. Bryn makes it out alive, but one of his friends is mortally wounded. Bryn manages to stabilise him by using the magic he learnt as a farmer and can only carry him out by convincing his other friend that they should stay together. 

## Chapter 1: The Heabe
Fascinated by the ruins, Bryn decides to explore more than Gatesweep. He picks up a book and learns of the locations of nearby ruins across Gordolmonre, but these are difficult to get to and far from civilisation. He discovers that the most accessible ruins are likely those in the Domuz colony of Mepheisatha. Bryn sets for Mepheisatha.

While crossing The Heabe, Bryn stays in a small nomad camp. During this stay, the Deksie Aredvics arrive and demand taxes from the nomads. One nomad who cannot pay is assaulted by the Deksie and Bryn tried to intervene, but gets hurt himself.
