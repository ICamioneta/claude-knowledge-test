I really like some of my stories. Often, the other ones are shit. None of the long ones are any good, they take way too much effort to produce when short stories are just one and done writing sessions. 

Anyway, here's the stories I'm most proud of. 

1. **Thraechos Learns about the Outside World**
I think I knocked it out of Cannon Hill Park with this one because its dialogue has both emotion and emoting, something most of my other writing is missing out on and something I really need to add. 

2. **Demons**
This story actually makes me tear up a little when I read the mental breakdown part of it because it comes both from a place of empathy and sympathy. I was inspired to write it after watching a video about some American Christian cult and how awful they can be. I already had Carehope set up for that kind of thing, so this was the perfect opportunity to develop it. The parts with screaming, both internal and external, I wrote while thinking about the mental anguish of A level exam prep.

3. **The Monster at the Door**
Might be a bit too early to rank this one, but I think its alright. It's got atmosphere, subversion, and most importantly a genuine meaning. I'm not entirely sure how people understand it, but _I'm the monster at the door, I'm not the girl_. The story's inspiration wasn't one about overcoming fear to make friends or some thing like that. The monster represents my body dysmorphia and how I can see myself. A horrific, "too tall" creature with a disproportionately large chest. Albeit, I've taken some exaggerations with my own body dysmorphia. The story is about my hope that people will look past what I see about myself that's horrific. 

4. **Lesliye Thenxhal's Shory**
For Juha (which is much lower on this list) I wrote short character pieces for each of the members of Juha S-AG that explains what events inspired them to become adventurers. Lesliye is definitely the coolest character, with heavy themes of both autistic and trans masking. Her shory has emotion, intrigue, and action between dialogue, being kind of my best work for a while. Plus, it leads directly into the start of R.1. 

5. **The Sky**
This one was inspired by my need to produce some outlet about the cosmology of the Mortal Realm, and a gay romance fit into that best. I do need to touch up the dialogue a lot. Reading it very awkwardly in open mic taught me that. Nevertheless, I think it works.

6. **My Sword!**
Again, there's emotion and meaning. The main issue is that it's one of the first short stories I wrote inspired by the concept of colonial reparations. All it's missing really is a more fair defence by Hanver Rebbish and more action in-between dialogue. 

7. **The Eternal Day**
Inspired by when I had to pull an all nighter to finish some programming coursework, projected onto the desert of Cronar and influenced by Welsh mythology. It's a short poetic piece, and I'm not really a big fan of poetry.

8. **Canal Privateers !🏴‍☠️!**
Just a fun short story. It's in a similar vain to the 3394 Report from Northern Terlaye. Usually, I try to keep Wastelife as grounded as possible. I make it as realistic as it could possibly be. Sometimes, I just like to loosen that a little and be a bit silly with Lindis with Guns, or 18th century piracy performed on a canal network. 

9. **Black Hearts**
We're entering unfinished territory here. The first draft is very shallow and empty, but the story as a whole has serious potential if I actually ever bunkered down and re-wrote it. When I originally wrote it, I was inspired by my narcissistic friend (who I cut off) , who's habits and brain chemistry inspired Ria from Alinreg's selfishness and wanton uncare for pretty much everyone else in the story.

It has a lot of potential, I just need to rewrite character conflicts to both somehow ease it in and make all characters have some conflict with each other, before the two cliques form in the climax with the kidnapping. Also, it needs emotion and action between dialogue, which is somewhat challenging to do when all of the important dialogue happens during the walking.

10. **Juha**
I really like the idea of Juha. I could spend a lot of effort actually writing it, refining R.1 and actually writing R.2 and R.3. They are also conveniently sorted by how proud of them I am within the shory document. I like Juha because it has potential, not just for the characters but for me to genuinely show off my impressively grounded worldbuilding with the southern revolts.

11. **Detkain 3480**
I'm basically only proud of this one because of the worldbuilding I did for it. I do think a play or story centred around the revolution in Detkain would be interesting, but it doesn't really have good enough characters for it yet. I haven't written a word of the actual thing for a while, the planning is definitely more engaging to me.

12. **Merye Felk**
This is a story that I'd love to exist but almost definitely can't write. At every turn, I'm given the challenges of storytelling that I am not paid enough to solve. Despite this, the planning for the two trilogies was really fun and I think it's got pretty good trilogy arcs too. Inspired, of course, by Robin Hood. 

13. **Necromage**
The only non-short story I've ever completed. It's alright, but it could use action between dialogue, and maybe an actual theme. Currently, it's just a bunch of mildly interesting adventurer characters solving some random video-game quest. A third draft might make it more pride-worthy, but can I really be bothered to write that.

14. **wb1511**
Okay, so the actual original wb1511 probably deserves to be higher because it's an alright story that involves two interesting characters who discuss what amounts to species-ism. All of the surrounding detail? Not sure. I might have been crazy. It's more an essay on the potential of worldbuilding because yeah, sure, Wastelife is _potentially_ as deep as the real world. But it's not. Materially speaking, it's a bunch of neurons in my head and a bunch of 1s and 0s stored on some computers and a cloud drive. 

**Honourable Mentions**
The Silver Construct. 
The first story I ever wrote for Wastelife. It was really shit, and still is, but its plot is simple and probably retconned by newer lore. Magic comes up out of nowhere without foreshadowing, characters don't have character, shit just kinda happens without explanation. It was, however, a great first step.

Potion Seller
I do actually quite like this one. It's got emotion and action between dialogue (the two things I've identified that make my stories good) but it's not original. Maybe that's fine, perhaps my re-setting of an internet joke and re-contextualising its emotional impacts are enough to spin it into originality. But I did not write any of the dialogue, just everything around it.

Five Storey Tower
Don't look at this if you're reading this document and you're not me. It's highly private. I'll even tell you why: it's smut. The main characters are a hermaphrodite and a slime girl, and they have sex 3 times. It has its own end note that goes into my post-process feelings of it, but I can summarise for this document. I am actually impressed I got a full 14000 word story done, and I'm upset that it was only through the process of being horny that such a feat could be achieved. I think the story's alright, although I'm not convinced there's much character or character development. I am, however, extremely proud of all the innuendo in the chapter titles _and_ the title of the story.

Luckily, now that it exists, I have no desire whatsoever to write more Wastelife smut, as much as I'd like to see the characters of Evsel and Nesieze again.

## Other Prides

Within Meta:
Most characters aren't well defined, but it's an important list to have.
My creatures are actually really good and inspired. I like all of them and I'd place them above Lesliye Thenxal's Shory in the pride list.
I really like the lore behind Eternity, but currently most of the gods have a meaningful personality or even a word in their name.
My factions writings are generally really good. I've spend a lot of time on the government systems present, and I'm especially proud of Vania and the Empire of Ralean.
I really like my magic system. I think it suits the grounded requirements of the world.
Every single special object (like magical artefacts) is beautifully inspired and truly unique. There's only 16 of Tanea's 75 but not rushing them is proving to be incredibly worthwhile as I continue to come up with bangers like the Precise Boule and Jobi well after the initial hits of The Cauldron and the mid-hits of the Telescope of Time and the Emotional Banisher.
I also really like my prehistory worldbuilding because it doesn't matter at all but it lets me satisfy the need for lost lore.
The regions and geography of Wastelife is also pretty good, but I would love to talk to an actual geologist to fix all of the issues or at least reconcile them.
I love that my world is in depth enough to spend 1000 words on one random city.
Every single species of person is created with care and beauty, and I'm scared I'm going to have to abandon that when I develop the species of Mothdao and Ouranxo.
