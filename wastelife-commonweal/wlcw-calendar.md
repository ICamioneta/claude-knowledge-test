This is an explanation of the common date calendar in Wastelife, for players of Wastelife Commonweal.

There are six days in a week, four weeks in a month, and fourteen months in a year.

The week is divided into half-weeks, where two long work days are traditionally followed by a short work day. In this system, the half-week is a single discrete unit of time used in resting.

The months are named as follows:

1. Hantenia
2. Hantenxa
3. Hantenli
4. Hantenma
5. Roxia
6. Roxxa
7. Roxli
8. Nelenia
9. Nelenxa
10. Nelenli
11. Dekia
12. Dekxa
13. Dekli
14. Dekma

The days are named as follows:

1. Chodin
2. Dinvi
3. Edindina
4. Bisdin
5. Chidin
6. Edinbethna

To write the day in words, it is most common to write it as follows:

week-day-month-year

Week is written ordinarily, for example:

The *third* Chodin of Roxia, 3488.
