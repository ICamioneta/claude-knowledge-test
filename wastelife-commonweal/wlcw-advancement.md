In Wastelife Commonweal, advancements are the core unit of progression. They provide abilities and actions. They must be learned in a game session. 

# Classes

Classes in Wastelife provide a clear progression of advancements and focus on certain roles. They aren't exclusive paths: Wastelife Commonweal explicitly promotes mixing paths to create your own "class", something that would be regarded as multiclassing in other systems. 

Wastelife has six classes. 

## Caedyll

Primary Skills: Athletics, Stealth, Awareness, Empathy. 

Weak Arena: Combat

Caedyll are forces against adversity, devoting their variety of skills to defend the innocent. Throughout history, they have fended off the foes of society that threaten settlements. The caedyll prioritises cunning over strength, outmanoeuvring a stronger foe and carefully waiting for openings. 

They are the scout of the group, excelling at reconnaissance and first contact. 

## Fighter

Primary Skills: Athletics, Endurance, Motorics, Leadership. 

Weak Arena: Discourse 

Veteran soldiers, military officers, trained bodyguards, and chivalrous knights. Fighters are masters of weaponry and the body, training since childhood to excel at the martial arts. Their strength and endurance comes in handy in a number of scenarios. 

Fighters are the front line damage dealers, excelling at controlling the battlefield. 

## Jack

Primary Skills: Stealth, Logic, Charm, Empathy. 

Weak Arena: Combat

Missing flavour text. 

Jacks are the utility, steering conversations and being a little bit good at everything the party otherwise lacks entirely.

## Mage

Primary Skills: Motorics, Academics, Logic, Charm. 

Weak Arena: Dilemma 

The mage is the ultimate spellcaster. They are a conglomeration of skill and knowledge, feeling the very essence of the hidol to enhance their powerful claps. They maintain a myriad of powerful and rare ingredients in their satchels. 

They are the burst and artillery of the group, capable of producing high energy reactions if provided with the limited resource of ingredient. 

## Velych

Primary Skills: Endurance, Willpower, Memory, Leadership. 

Weak arena: Dilemma

The Velych seek to tap into the same near-inexhaustible power that makes the Luminaries so powerful. Aligning themselves to domains of freak but gradual change, they seek to bridge the gap between people and nature's power. 

They are the anchor of the group, enduring damage and leading through adversity when they're the last one standing. 

## Wizard

Primary Skills: Awareness, Academics, Memory, Willpower. 

Weak arena: Discourse

Wizards define themselves by their enchantments. Enchanting is power within yourself, and with this power it’s unlikely that a wizard will ever live a life close to normal. They seek knowledge, and wherever they are they can be found improving and expanding their knowledge. 

Wizards are versatile in their enchantments, and can fit a number of party roles from healers, support, or their own hybrid damage dealers. 

# Requirements

Advancements have two different types of requirements: prerequisites and training. Prerequisites are other advancements required before this one may be taken to training, and training is a number of study, practice, and trial marks required to finally earn the skill.

Each advancement will describe how many of each training marks it needs, and what those marks look like. For example, studying manuscripts, practising by sparring, then fighting a real opponent.

## Weapon Proficiency Skills

Weapon proficiency skills are sorted into classes by similarity of use. Weapon proficiency levels follow the same pattern of requirements. Trial marks are gained by using the weapon in an active arena. Each arena occurrence only provides 1 trial mark for weapon usage. Every proficiency level requires the previous level.

A manuscript or an instructor with the advancement is required for study. Practise requires a sparring partner.

## Durability

Advancements that focus on resilience to damage

### Second Wind

- Prerequisites: Endurance 1
- Study marks: none
- Practice marks: none
- Trial marks: 4, gained by being reduced to 0 save points without going beneath 4 vitality in the same combat.

Once per day, you can use this advancement to one of the following effects:

- Regain 1d6 save points up to your save point maximum. 
- Regain 1d4 composure up to your composure maximum.

### Indomitable 

- Prerequisites: Endurance 2
- Study marks: none
- Practice marks: 4
- Trial marks: 10, gained by being reduced to 0 save points without going beneath 4 vitality in the same combat.

Permanently increase your save point maximum by 3 points.

### Survivor

- Prerequisites: Endurance 3
- Study marks: none
- Practice marks: 4
- Trial marks: 15, gained per point of vitality lost

When you have 1 or 2 vitality remaining, roll a d6. On a 6, regain 1 save point up to your save point maximum.

## Enchantment class proficiency

Although the skill of enchanting is one thing, the enchantment skill classes each vary wildly on their post-cast behaviour. 

The enchantment proficiency skill is itself a separate thing which, in addition to being a prerequisite to the enchantment class proficiencies, provides some extra abilities.

The enchantment classes have a shared requirements profile per proficiency level. Each proficiency level requires its previous level, and the Enchanting proficiency of the same level. Study marks require a manuscript, practice marks consume 1 torfol energy. Trial marks are gained by enchanting with an enchantment of that class during an arena. 

| Level | Requirements | Study | Practise | Trial |
| ----- | ------------ | ----- | -------- | ----- |
| 1     | only default | 2     | 2        | 1     |
| 2     | Willpower 1  | 4     | 4        | 2     |
| 3     | Willpower 2  | 10    | 6        | 4     |
| 4     | Willpower 3  | 18    | 14       | 8     |
| 5     | Willpower 3  | 30    | 18       | 10    |

The enchantment skill classes are: 

- **Aeroesence**, utilisation of the flexible material.
- **Bindermancy**, Hidol binding.
- **Cinderance**, creation of heat and fire.
- **Rotchemy**, corrosion and decomposition.
- **Magnesis**, light, zaps, and magnesis.
- **Mirageing**, perception-altering effects.
- **Pushcraft**, moving objects and projecting force.
- **Crashmancy**, sound and pressure.
- **Vitescence**, potions and health.

Individual enchantments belong to one or more of these classes. In the case where an enchantment belongs to multiple, the lowest class proficiency applies.

## Enchanting

This is the enchanting proficiency skill. It provides abilities and is a prerequisite to enchantment class proficiencies, which actually provide proficiency die during enchantment.

### Enchanting 1 - House wizard

- Prerequisites: none
- Study marks: 6
- Practice marks: 2
- Trial marks: 1, gained by succeeding a trial cast.

House wizards are common enchanters. They are the healers of towns, having spent the effort to gain some basic enchanting competency.

### Enchanting 2 - Wizard

- Prerequisites: Enchanting 1, knowledge of 4 enchantments
- Study marks: 4
- Practice marks: 10
- Trial marks: 4 successful casts during an arena, fully deplete torfol energy once.

With more practice, house wizards may become proper wizards, prepared for scholarly or practical study of this branch of hidosis. 

Your maximum torfol energy increases to 8. Once between rests, you may instantly recharge 2 energy levels.
