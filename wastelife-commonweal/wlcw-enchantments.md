# Aeromancy



### Wall

| Type | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration   | Cost |
| ---- | ---- | --------- | ----- | ---------- | ---- |
| Misc | 1    | 4/3/5     | 1 use | 10 minutes | 4    |

Enchanting a length of a surface no longer than 6 metres, an impassable wall of Aeroquartz that extends 4 metres perpendicularly from the enchanted surface. This wall can absorb 50 damage before collapsing, and has an armour save of 4+ against all damage types except force. The wall weighs very little, and cannot be moved relative to the surface it was cast on. When it breaks, the wall dissolves into nothingness.

### Spike Pit

| Type | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration   | Cost |
| ---- | ---- | --------- | ----- | ---------- | ---- |
| Misc | 1    | 4/3/6     | 1 use | 30 minutes | 2    |

Enchanting a surface up to 2 metres squared, uneven Aeroquartz spikes and thorns erect from the surface. Walking on this surface unprotected can deal piercing damage. If they pierce, the spikes deal a minimum of 2 piercing damage. The spikes can pierce armour with enough force, and can extend up to 0.5m from the surface.


# Bindermancy

### Quick Glue

| Type   | Tier | Movements | Uses      | Duration | Cost |
| ------ | ---- | --------- | --------- | -------- | ---- |
| Potion | 0    | 5         | 1 portion | 1 minute | none |

Enchanted liquids become more viscous, like glue. The liquid can be comfortably applied to broken objects, such as torn fabric or a cracked sword. Once the duration is up, the liquid will conform to the shape of the broken object filling these holes and cracks. The liquid hardens, and tears filled by it become structurally sound.

### Oilify

| Type   | Tier | Movements | Uses       | Duration | Cost |
| ------ | ---- | --------- | ---------- | -------- | ---- |
| Potion | 0    | 6         | 5 portions | 2 hours  | none |

The enchanted liquid becomes highly flammable and will combust upon contact with fire, flames, and sparks. The liquid is also very slippery. Enchanted objects are not combustible, but get quite slippy.

### Lock Hinge

| Type | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration | Cost |
| ---- | ---- | --------- | ----- | -------- | ---- |
| Misc | 1    | 4/4       | 1 use | 1 hour   | 1    |

Enchanted mechanisms lock up and cannot move unless enough force is applied. The lock is as strong as the material itself, as if it were fused. The enchanter of this lock may unlock and re-lock this enchantment at will when it is within their enchant range.

### Alarm

| Type | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration | Cost |
| ---- | ---- | --------- | ----- | -------- | ---- |
| Misc | 0    | 3/3/5/5   | 1 use | 8 hours  | 1    |

Enchanting a doorway, window, or any gap smaller than 3 metres squared, the air in that gap holds still. Creatures that pass through the area will cause a loud alarm to ring, which sounds like a large bell. The sounding will last 12 seconds, and its loudness can disorient creatures near the gap. Once the alarm is tripped, the enchantment wears off.


### Disenchant

| Misc | 1    | 1         | 1 use | n/a      | 1    |

This enchantment removes any bindings and dissolves transmutation materials such as Aeroquartz

# Magnesis

### Legerdemain

| Type | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration | Cost |
| ---- | ---- | --------- | ----- | -------- | ---- |
| Misc | 0    | 6         | 1 use | 1 hour   | none |

This enchantment is an easy trick used by novices for practice. It's intended to enchant the air, something generally difficult but useful for practising focus. Legerdemain is a grouping of 5 simple enchantment effects:

- The enchant produces a harmless sensory effect such as a shower of sparks, faint sounds, or an odor.
- The enchant lights or snuffs a candle.
- The enchant heats a small area 3 degrees above its current temperature.
- The enchantment cleans or stains an object no larger than 0.5 metres cubed.
- A colour, small mark, or symbol appears on an enchanted surface.

| ---- | ---- | --------- | ----- | ---------- | ---- |

# Crashmancy

### Sonic Instability

| Type   | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration   | Cost |
| ------ | ---- | --------- | ----- | ---------- | ---- |
| Weapon | 0    | 3         | 1 hit | 30 minutes | 1    |

When the weapon deals bludgeoning damage to a target, a sonic wave explodes from it. This sonic wave deals 1d2 + 1 thunder damage to the target, and 1 thunder damage to any creatures within 2 metres. The weapon's structure is stressed by this thunder wave, and weakly made items will break apart. 


### Explosive

| Type   | Tier | Movements | Uses  | Duration | Cost |
| ------ | ---- | --------- | ----- | -------- | ---- |
| Weapon | 3    | 2/1       | 1 hit | 4 days   | 3    |

When the enchanted weapon deals bludgeoning damage, that weapon explodes with force, sending shrapnel from the explosion. 

The target receives 1d8 thunder damage. All creatures within 5 metres who are not completely behind cover receive 1d3+2 piercing damage.

The enchanted weapon is completely destroyed in this process, being reduced to shrapnel.

| ------ | ---- | --------- | ---------- | -------- | ---- |

### Death Ward

| Type   | Tier | Movements | Uses      | Duration | Cost |
| ------ | ---- | --------- | --------- | -------- | ---- |
| Potion | 2    | 0/6       | 1 portion | 2 hours  | 2    |

The liquid greys. On ingestion, creatures have a measure of protection from death. They are also immune to necrotic damage. The enchantment ends if the first effect is triggered, or after 50 points of necrotic damage is negated.
