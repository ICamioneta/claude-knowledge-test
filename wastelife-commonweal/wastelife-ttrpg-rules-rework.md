Wastelife: Commonweal is the brand new title for Wastelife: The Tabletop Roleplaying Game, abbreviated to WLCW because WC is a toilet. Feel free to pronounce it "wool cue". It is a complete departure from the D&D rule set, with progression represented by advancements and gameplay shifted from generic d20 rolls to specially crafted scenario "arenas". 

# Attribute set

**Body** - physical strength, endurance, toughness, and speed

**Mind** - knowledge, wit, perception, logic.

**Spirit** - willpower, charisma, social presence.

then lean heavily onto the achievement-style skill / ability system. I think I'll turn away from a dice rolling attribute system where attributes influence skills since I can't find any skill/attribute groupings very useful. But, skills are still necessary...

**body skills**

- athletics - climbing, swimming, running, and other generally cardio exercises. 
- Endurance - resisting bodily damage through fitness like disease, poison, actual physical damage. If you're healthy, you're healthy.
- Stealth - with control over the body comes ease in moving as conspicuously as you want.
- Motorics - Your ability to handle objects with precision, your hand-eye coordination

**Mind skills**

- Academics - One's learnedness as it pertains to study. Represents a certain alignment with academic rigour and ability to study.
- Awareness - Not only perception and the senses, but wit and certain quick-thinking social skills.
- Logic - reasoning and sense, applied to planning and deduction, both natural and psychological.
- Memory - Your ability to recall and recognise patterns. Also builds an instinct for solving certain problems.

**Spirit skills**

- Charm - persuasion, etiquette, and even seduction.
- Leadership - inspire others, command, and make your presence known.
- Willpower - mental fortitude, your ability to resist fears and other mental attack
- Empathy - Emotional intelligence in one word, among other things your ability to feel for others and relate. 

# Advancement System

Attributes start at 1 and reach 10 (or above). They, in conjunction with skills, are used as requirements to learn advancements. 

Advancements have attribute requirements and prerequisites. They also have a practice requirement, this is what is required to have happened in a game for them to justifiably be applicable to a character. Skills are advancements that form a base layer for other advancements to grow from. 

In this roleplaying system, the "base" person is a person who isn't capable of any of the defined skills. Since most people have acquired some of the skills, the average person has at least a few skills and attribute points.
